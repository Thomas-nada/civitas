Info Actions: 0 of 23 Passed (0% Pass Rate)

All 23 info actions submitted between epochs 507 and 619 failed to achieve ratification. This is not a coincidence — info actions are constitutionally non-binding signaling mechanisms, and DReps overwhelmingly treat them as advisory polls rather than governance instruments. Notable examples include the NCL proposals (350M, 300M, multiple iterations all failed as info actions), the Cardano Budget Process Framework, and the Cardano Foundation/Catalyst management proposal.

In a striking paradox, the ₳275M Ecosystem Budget (the largest single spending authorization in Cardano's history) was submitted as an info action and also officially failed — yet the ₳39 individual treasury withdrawals it authorized proceeded successfully. This confirms InputEndorsers' characterization that info actions are "performative theater mandated by the constitution" with the real governance work happening at the individual withdrawal level.
