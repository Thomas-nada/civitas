The Cardano Foundation acknowledges the need to bridge the gap between resignation of the Cardano Atlantic Council and the next committee elections in September 2026 with a new committee member appointment. This will ensure that critical governance actions between now and September 2026 can be voted on by the CC, which will be restored to a functional state.
We trust that the new elected CC member Cardano Curia will vote promptly and conscientiously, with full consideration for the Cardano Constitution and the proper role of a Constitutional Committee member. 
Note: While this is not a counterargument per se, we also note that this NewCommittee governance action suggests that CC consideration is needed in respect of Net Change Limit governance actions. While consideration can of course be given, we do not think the CC needs to vote on such governance actions per the wording of the Constitution, which contains no such requirement.
