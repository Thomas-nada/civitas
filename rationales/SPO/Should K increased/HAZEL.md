# HAZEL

**Proposal:** Should K increased
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `pool1h6q8jj55dn6727yydlypu0rz4sflf2enxhs0thqydmddgu3shl5`

---

Our vote towards adjusting the k parameter is yes. This has been a topic of some discussion and magnitude for a long time in the community. In the last (pre-Chang #1) poll HAZEL voted no on changing k to 1000, and yes on decreasing the min fee to 170 ADA. Our reasoning back then was clear, and has not changed since then. We do not believe k should immediately be raised to 1000, but instead to an intermediate 750.

We believe that recent events around large retiring stakepools and the slow flow of delegation to dReps clearly show that a large portion of held ADA is unused and must be considered sticky. With doubling k, this would mean a large number of ADA holders would lose their rewards, often over extended periods of time, potentially reducing their trust in Cardano.
While we cannot hold off these changes forever just because holders are complacent or inactive, we can reduce its impact by increasing k in smaller steps.

Generally, we support raising k, but urge everyone to understand that we need to track the outcome of such a parameter change in detail to evaluate its success (or lack thereof) with regards to improving the ecosystem.
