そのため、今後は技術レポートの本数・テーマ・公開時期、独立技術レビューの対象選定基準、利益相反管理、関係者が判断から外れるrecusal記録、Intersect本体予算との責任分担を継続的に公開することを求めます。

I support this Treasury Withdrawal.

Intersect Technical Steering Committee Support is important public infrastructure for Cardano’s technical governance. The Parameter Committee reviews network settings, CIP editors help organize Cardano improvement proposals, and the Hard Fork Working Group helps coordinate major upgrades safely. These functions help DReps and the community understand complex technical proposals and make evidence-based decisions.

This proposal is not just committee operating cost. It strengthens Cardano’s technical accountability and improves the ability of DReps to evaluate network parameters, upgrades, improvement proposals, and core infrastructure proposals.

I also value the fund management design. Treasury funds will not simply be paid out all at once. The proposal includes Smart Contract Disbursement, milestone-based payments, external oversight, Appold assurance, and the return of unused funds to the Treasury. This is appropriate for responsible public fund management.

At the same time, we should be careful that the TSC does not become perceived as a central authority that decides the “correct” technical answer. The TSC should support, not replace, the judgment of DReps and the community by providing transparent and verifiable technical information.

Going forward, I expect continued public reporting on the number, topics, and timing of technical reports, the selection criteria for independent technical reviews, conflict-of-interest management, recusal records, and the separation of responsibilities from the main Intersect budget.
