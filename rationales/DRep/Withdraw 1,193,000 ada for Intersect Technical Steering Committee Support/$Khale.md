I am voting NO because, although technical governance capacity is important, this proposal funds an additional administrator-centered committee layer under Intersect rather than directly strengthening open, decentralized infrastructure. The work packages focus on event attendance, commissioned reports, specialist groups, and a pilot review program that extend Intersect’s footprint across protocol governance, while related security and workshop functions are already funded elsewhere in its budget.

Under my rubric, this structure carries higher structural-risk and centralization concerns than I am comfortable supporting, and it does not deliver enough direct, Cardano-facing infrastructure or user benefit to justify another Treasury stream for Intersect and its committees in a tightening NCL environment.
