We have voted Yes for the proposal ₳5M Loan for Cardano's Global Listing Expansion – Powered by Snek. This initiative stands out because it is not a grant but a structured loan, complete with interest, audits, and repayment commitments. It represents a new, more sustainable way of deploying treasury capital where the community sees tangible benefits while ensuring accountability for the funds used.

What makes this proposal particularly compelling is its ecosystem-wide impact. While SNEK may be the direct beneficiary, the infrastructure, frameworks, and legal groundwork created will open doors for other Cardano tokens to access Tier 1 exchanges more easily in the future. This is how Cardano can begin closing the gap with ecosystems like Ethereum and Solana, where community-driven tokens already play a powerful role in liquidity and user acquisition.

Finally, the structure of oversight through Intersect and a board of respected industry figures provides the governance confidence needed for a proposal of this scale. The reporting, auditing, and repayment mechanisms ensure that the community retains visibility and influence over outcomes. For these reasons, we support this initiative as a bold but measured step toward scaling Cardano’s global visibility, liquidity, and adoption.
