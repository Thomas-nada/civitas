特に重要な変更は次の3点です。
Budget Info Actionの廃止と全件監査の義務化
　一部の大きな支出だけでなく、すべてのトレジャリー引き出しを監査対象とし、透明性を強化。

　投票中に内容を変更できないよう固定化し、信頼性を保証。
非拘束的な期待・推奨条項の削除
　助言的な条文を削除し、実効性あるルールだけを残して明確化。
v2.3は、「ルールを簡潔にし、誰もが同じ条件で、信頼できる形でガバナンスに参加できる」改訂です。

Cardano Constitution v2.3 introduces several refinements,
but the three most significant updates are:
Removal of the Budget Info Action and universal audit requirement
All treasury withdrawals, not just large ones, are now subject to equal auditing for greater transparency.
Codification of proposal immutability
Proposals can no longer be altered during voting, ensuring integrity and trust.
Deletion of non-binding expectation and encouragement clauses
Removed advisory language to keep only enforceable, practical rules.
v2.3 is a reform that simplifies the rules and enables everyone to participate in governance under equal and trustworthy conditions.
I support the rationality of these three changes and vote Yes.
