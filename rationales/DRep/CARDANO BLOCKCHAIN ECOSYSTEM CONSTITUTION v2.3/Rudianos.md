The changes to the constitution being made by this proposal are well thought through and there has been extensive community input. The result I believe is a better, more concise document. The most important change is the removal of the info action requirement for budget withdrawals. I believe this to be a good change to try and make the governance process less onerous on both DReps and proposers. It's important to note that some of the requirements that were part of the info action process still exist but are now part of the withdrawal request process. This should maintain the safety of the system while improving its agility. I find the changes here to be positive and therefore I am voting in favor of this proposal. Thank you to everyone who has put in extensive effort into this action.
