I approve the proposed changes to the Cardano Constitution because they meaningfully simplify governance, improve consistency, and strengthen accountability.

Simplified Governance:
The removal of aspirational “Expectations” and “Encouragement” clauses makes the Constitution leaner and more enforceable. It removes redundant mechanisms like the Budget Info Action, reducing bureaucratic overhead and potential confusion.

Clarity and Consistency:
The introduction of unified terminology (e.g., “Ada Holders”, “Treasury Withdrawals”) and new definitions ensures precision and interpretive consistency, which seems critical to me. 

Enhanced Accountability:
By requiring that all proposal documents are immutable and by extending treasury audit safeguards to all withdrawals (not just large ones), the proposal enforces greater transparency and protection of treasury funds.

Operational Focus:
By shifting procedural details (like codes of conduct) out of the Constitution and leaving them to community practice, the document maintains constitutional clarity without overregulating implementation details.

In summary:
These changes make Cardano’s governance framework more transparent and user-friendly while removing unnecessary complexity. I believe approving v2.3 strengthens the ecosystem’s ability to evolve sustainably. Furthermore, Version 2.3 incorporates widespread feedback from reviews, indicating an effective iterative governance process.
