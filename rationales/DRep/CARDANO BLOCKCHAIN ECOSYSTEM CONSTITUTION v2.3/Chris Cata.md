I am in favor of this constitutional update to make improvements to how governance operates in Cardano.  It simplifies process, adds clarity to some definitions, and attempts to firm up the immutability of what is done on-chain.  While I would have liked to see some of this broken out, particularly changes to budget info action process, consensus based on feedback from many throughout the community suggests this will ultimately be a net positive and I agree.  It's an iterative leap but one that's needed as over time I have growing concerns of governance participation waning due to process fatigue and confusion/interpretation efforts due to these improvements being needed.
