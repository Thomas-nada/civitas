# STORM Partners

**Proposal:** Dingo a Production-Grade Block Producer in Go by Blink Labs
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1yf0s5guxklwlqn7ttdpfwmmkaucwaqkkm6hzncy8nful5fgemurcj`

---

We vote YES. Client diversity is a genuine strategic priority for Cardano, and treasury investment in teams that can deliver on it is how that priority becomes real.

Blink Labs has been funded across multiple Catalyst cycles and has a reasonable delivery record to date. Dingo itself has made meaningful progress, with chain synchronisation, UTxO tracking, Plutus execution, and block production logic implemented and running on a private devnet alongside the reference node. Mainnet block production is not yet achieved, and this proposal funds the work to get there.

Go is a deliberate choice with real implications. It is the dominant language in cloud-native and enterprise engineering environments, and a production Cardano node in Go opens operator profiles that are harder to reach through Haskell alone. For an ecosystem that has named institutional adoption as a priority, that matters.
