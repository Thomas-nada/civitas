# Big Lazy Cat

**Proposal:** Dingo a Production-Grade Block Producer in Go by Blink Labs
**Vote:** Abstain
**Voter ID:** `drep1yffcjs0njfea09xhs6up429rhya5pxd7qz7hvmwq6vqy2nczwpj3g`

---

I support the idea of multi-client implementations for Cardano, and I’m also supportive of exploring a Go-based node.
However, since Amaru is already underway, I think it makes sense to first observe the progress of existing multi-client efforts before committing additional treasury funds.
Taking a bit more time may help ensure the treasury is used in a more efficient way. I also want to acknowledge the contributions Blink Labs has made to the Cardano ecosystem.
For these reasons, I will abstain on this proposal.
