An audited contract library and solid reference implementations would be a real gain for Cardano builders. OpenZeppelin has the track record to deliver them. My No vote is about how this proposal is put together, not about whether the work is worth doing.

The budget is pegged to ada at US$0.16, but the price has moved well above that since submission. As written, the full delivery amount is converted to stablecoin when the contract is signed. At current prices that would be far more than the US$1.83M budget. I couldn't see a clear statement of what happens to the surplus. I'm not comfortable approving a treasury withdrawal where the amount leaving the treasury doesn't match the stated need.

I also have smaller concerns. The proposal came forward outside the normal budget process, and Intersect is administering it without endorsing it. The smart contract language is still to be decided. 20% is paid upfront before any delivery, and the milestone dates are already out of step with when work could realistically start.

If OpenZeppelin resubmits with the amount re-priced closer to current ada, a clear commitment to return any surplus, and the language question settled, I expect I'd support it.
