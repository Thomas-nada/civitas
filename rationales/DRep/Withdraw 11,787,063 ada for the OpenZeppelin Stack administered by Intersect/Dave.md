I appreciate OpenZeppelin's desire to bring their industry-leading expertise to Cardano. However, I am voting NO due to the exceptionally high 11.8 million ADA ask and a lack of clear strategic direction. There is currently insufficient evidence demonstrating that the ecosystem actually requires or will adopt these specific blueprints to justify the cost. Treasury commitments of this magnitude must be driven by proven demand. I am not convinced the high ask, proportionally represents the value that is proposed to be delivered.
