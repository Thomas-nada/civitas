# Cardanians

**Proposal:** Withdraw 11,787,063 ada for the OpenZeppelin Stack administered by Intersect
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1yt4lyf0fwlrz8k6j5evd3rxw0sqp67qkh36su98a97q3qsc0h03y4`

---

We welcome OpenZeppelin’s involvement in Cardano and recognize the value of secure, reusable open-source contracts and developer tooling. However, the current proposal does not sufficiently justify the ₳11,787,063 commitment.

Related infrastructure is already being developed within the ecosystem. Before approving this expenditure, we need a public gap analysis and a concrete division of responsibilities showing which existing components will be reused and what additional capabilities this engagement will deliver.

The smart-contract language remains undecided, and the budget provides insufficient detail on staffing, rates and costs by workstream. The acceptance criteria primarily demonstrate technical delivery, with limited requirements for independent adoption.

We acknowledge the milestone-controlled payments, public evidence and return of unused funds. These safeguards are valuable, but they do not resolve uncertainty about scope, demand and value for money.

We would welcome a narrower proposal with settled technical choices, a granular budget, measurable adoption commitments from Cardano teams, clear conversion-surplus handling and maintenance responsibilities beyond the first year.

We remain open to supporting a coordinated engagement with demonstrable public-good value.
