I am voting No on the Withdraw 11,787,063 ada for the OpenZeppelin Stack administered by Intersect proposal.

I recognize OpenZeppelin's capability and its reputation in security. But I don't see this proposal addressing Cardano's real bottlenecks.

First, it is not what Cardano developers need. The 2025 Cardano Developer Survey's main pain points were documentation, transaction-building tooling, and the eUTxO learning curve, with virtually no one citing the lack of a standard contracts library.

Second, it does not remove the barrier for outside developers. The core challenge for EVM developers is the paradigm shift to eUTxO, which a library does not solve. The proposal's only Cardano adoption criterion is feedback from a single independent developer.

With the reserve shrinking, I believe Treasury spending should prioritize projects that directly drive network adoption. I don't think this proposal meets that bar, so I am voting No.
