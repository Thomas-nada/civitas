Voting NO. I would like to see this resubmitted with a narrow scope.  

This proposal has aspects that help address gaps identified in Cardano's ecosystem. A recent ecosystem audit points to specific things addressed here in OpenZeppelin's proposal.  However, after review, there is some overlap with past ratified proposals with committed spend.  It may be worth OpenZepplin having that discussion the Alpha Growth team on what to resubmit to close up the remaining gaps that IO did not cover in their own already passed proposal.

As is, we would be paying ~$1.8M for a bundle whose first workstream is named the same thing as an already funded IO workstream.

Suggestion - Resubmit a more focused, follow-up proposal to IOs - Pay OpenZeppelin to ship a liquid-staked ADA receipt other apps will take as collateral, a shared vault plug those apps can reuse, one live tokenized money-market product with a named issuer, and an audit of the IO contract library already funded.
