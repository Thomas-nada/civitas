# Rudianos

**Proposal:** Reduce minimum Constitutional Committee size (committeeMinSize) from 7 to 5
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1yfv77z2fsz7nucy59lx2pkafamathf8q783lalkr3quju0gv93lq2`

---

I am in favor of this change. The current min size of 7 paired with an actual committee size of 7 creates an inflexible point of failure for Cardano governance. The departure or resignation of a single member immediately pauses our ability to pass important actions. This operational fragility could force the community into rushed elections, which compromises candidate vetting.
In my view the community has a choice between two reasonable paths: maintaining a min size of 7 with an actual committee size of 9, or reducing the minimum to 5 while maintaining the committee size of 7, as is suggested by this proposal. Either would work but I prefer the latter. Seven members to me is optimal and the risk of temporarily operating with 5 or 6 members is negligible compared to the very real risk of governance paralysis or the poor decision-making that stems from emergency appointments. Either way the main risk I see here is failure to move off of the 7 and 7 scenario we are currently in. If this action does not pass I would gladly support a change to a min size of 7 and a committee size of 9.
