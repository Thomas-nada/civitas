I will vote YES on [Reduce minimum Constitutional Committee size (committeeMinSize) from 7 to 5].

Current Risk 
At present, the actual number of Constitutional Committee (CC) members is equal to the minimum required size. In this situation, if even one CC member resigns, the committee would fall below the minimum threshold and become non-functional. This would prevent not only ordinary governance actions but also critical actions such as urgent security updates. This represents a serious structural risk that must be addressed urgently.
