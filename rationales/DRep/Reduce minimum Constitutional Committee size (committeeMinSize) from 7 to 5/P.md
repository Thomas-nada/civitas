**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1yftc8zs7gjcj4a9nxzplz4wg6cwweya0kxp8adnw59vsyrqvrysud`

Guardrails are good. Having more members on the CC means more eyes are on Governance and the treasury, helping ensure they are safe.

On the other hand, we are creating more work for people in the ecosystem. Reducing the number can increase participation, but it also centralises control.

Because I'm for decentralisation and representation, I'll vote NO and keep the number at 7. Keep us in check and ensure we are operating within the bounds of the Constitution.
