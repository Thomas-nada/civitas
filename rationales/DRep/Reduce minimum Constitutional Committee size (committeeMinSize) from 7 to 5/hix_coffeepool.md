I am voting clearly against the governance action to reduce the minimum number of Constitutional Committee seats to five. While I support discussing the problem of governance halting when a single seat becomes vacant, reducing the minimum to five seats does not address the root cause. It would only lead to greater concentration within the Constitutional Committee. Under this proposal, if the number of seats were to decrease further and one of the remaining five members were to resign, governance would still grind to a halt in exactly the same way. Furthermore, reducing the minimum to five seats carries the significant downside of increasing centralization beyond what we have today. Rather than pursuing this path, we should be discussing an expansion of the Committee to something in the range of twenty seats.
