# Ha-Nguyen

**Proposal:** Reduce minimum Constitutional Committee size (committeeMinSize) from 7 to 5
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2z8gktqj27kwmmxesd0y484f7p4z9tjf5xu8m4sucstm3gvtywlp`

As a DRep, I vote NO on this GA.

While I understand the intention to improve operational resilience, reducing the minimum Constitutional Committee size from 7 to 5 introduces greater governance risk than benefit. The Constitutional Committee serves as a core constitutional safeguard, where representativeness, diversity of perspectives, and mutual checks are essential to maintaining legitimacy and community trust.

Lowering the minimum threshold weakens collective judgment and increases the risk of power concentration at a critical layer of governance. Operational challenges should instead be addressed through timely elections, clearer succession planning, and improved processes.

Rather than reducing the committee size, I believe a more robust approach would be to expand the number of Constitutional Committee members, supported by clear selection and transition mechanisms, to improve resilience without compromising constitutional integrity.

For these reasons, I do not support reducing committeeMinSize at this time.
