# Peter Horsfall

**Proposal:** Reduce minimum Constitutional Committee size (committeeMinSize) from 7 to 5
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1ytfas4axhm8yzrqqh4ru2uuqesu87zeq6fwg5pq8azqf7ecu4nu8s`

---

I am voting against the proposal to reduce the minimum Constitutional Committee seats to five. While governance stagnation due to vacant seats is a valid concern, lowering the threshold fails to address the underlying issue and risks diminishing collective decision-making while concentrating power at this essential governance layer. A larger committee enhances oversight of governance and the treasury. I advocate increasing membership and replacing inactive members rather than reducing the minimum. This was something I was quite vocal about in the 2024 governance workshops.
