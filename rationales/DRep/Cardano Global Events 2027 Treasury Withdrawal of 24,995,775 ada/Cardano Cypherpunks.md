# Cardano Cypherpunks

**Proposal:** Cardano Global Events 2027 Treasury Withdrawal of 24,995,775 ada
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y23wk4w6f2aumc92vl9m3x74xzm6jh4ha9wdq09yfq2sl6q8zlv4l`

---

The proposal has a credible track record: the 2025–2026 programme reportedly exceeded its targets, engaging 1,169 developers, generating 219 collaboration discussions and giving 79 ecosystem projects access to major industry events. The 2027 proposal also improves builder access by funding travel and accommodation, introduces quarterly reporting and gives the Cardano Foundation clear accountability for delivery.

However, I do not consider the evidence sufficient to justify another ₳25M withdrawal. The programme's KPIs remain heavily weighted towards booth contacts, social engagement, merchandise, wallet sign-ups and conference attendance. These are awareness metrics, not reliable indicators of commercial adoption. The proposal does not adequately demonstrate how the substantial expenditure will translate into active users, production deployments, enterprise customers, transaction volume, DeFi liquidity or recurring ecosystem revenue. The projected cost of almost US$5M also makes the opportunity cost significant.

Cardano needs stronger commercial traction, not simply greater visibility at industry conferences. A more compelling approach would allocate funding to measurable business development and adoption outcomes, with a direct link between expenditure and live deployments, paying customers, sustained on-chain activity and revenue. The previous programme's performance is a positive signal, but it does not establish that this scale of event sponsorship is the best use of Treasury capital. I therefore vote NO.
