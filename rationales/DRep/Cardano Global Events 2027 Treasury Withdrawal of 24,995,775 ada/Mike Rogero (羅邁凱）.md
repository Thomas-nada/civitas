I can not support this for the following reasons:

1. The Cardano Foundation has not been acting like an organization which is incentivized to benefit the Cardano ecosystem, but more like a short-timer organization looking to coast and milk their self-defined salaries until the Foundations allocation is bled dry.  They are not managing the organization to be an ever-green foundation, but have defined the mandate as time-limited, and not specific to Cardano.  This being the case, finding high-ticket marketing events is too likely to turn into friends-and-family paid junkets for the executives rather than actual ecosystem and adoption benefiting events.  While I do not state that the Foundation has been acting in bad-faith, I do believe that the outcomes are more likely to be suboptimal than not, and thus this expense, now, with this team is more likely than not to not generate value exceeding its cost to the Treasury.   This is more than anything a trust issue that the CF is acting with prudence and fiduciary duty in their spending decisions.  It also reflects lack of over-sight and ability to punish bad behavior if they do spend without results.  Thus down-side risk with such high ticket asks with sub-optimal previous performance, by a team we can't change or influence speaks to it not being smart until they start showing trustworthy behavior and results.

2. I don't believe that paying all costs for all participants at these marketing events puts enough skin in the game and proper incentives for the participants.  Subsidizing part of expenses for teams that have logical business based reasons to participate in conferences makes more sense.  However, Cardano primarily needs non-crypto industry adoptions by non-crypto based users.  Thus, focusing only on crypto events and current crypto users, seems poorly chosen strategy, and unlikely to lead to valuable outcomes.

A good portion of my career was in marketing  I am very attuned to how events like these are more often junkets and perks for those going rather than actual business outcomes.  As the people deciding what to spend, where to go, what the budgets are, don't seem to have proper incentive to focus on outcome above personal benefit, nor oversight, I can't support giving them more budget power and decision making until this situation changes, or they build a track record of success with smaller asks more appropriate for Cardano's economic situation and needs.

That the requestor is an organization which hasn't downsized to reflect the economic value of the money which  supports their organisation's operations is a final red flag that they are not focused on the long term value of the organization to the ecosystem over personal benifits.
