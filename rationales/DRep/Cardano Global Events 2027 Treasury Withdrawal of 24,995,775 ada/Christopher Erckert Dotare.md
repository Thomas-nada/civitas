I support a unified Cardano events program, and the 2025-26 results earned a renewal. I am voting No on this version because it spends heavily on crypto-native mega-conferences, duplicates regions, and funds builder travel. With Cardano expected to be ready for institutional adoption by the end of 2026, the 2027 program should put Cardano in front of banks and financial institutions that can adopt it directly or through ecosystem projects. As a builder myself, I want this program to succeed, and I would support a revised proposal with the following changes.

1. One event per region, weighted to financial institutions

Africa: Africa Tech Summit, Nairobi
Southeast Asia: Money20/20 Asia, Bangkok
US East: Consensus, Miami
US West: Rare Evo and Dev Gov Day, San Francisco
Europe: Signal Week, Paris (in place of Web Summit, for its bank and asset manager audience)
LatAm: LABITCONF, Buenos Aires
South Asia: India Blockchain Week, Bangalore
East Asia: TEAMZ, Tokyo, only with a plan for meetings with Japanese financial institutions

Remove TOKEN2049 New York, TOKEN2049 Singapore, the Australian Crypto Convention and Web Summit. TOKEN2049 New York is an unproven first edition at the highest cost in the proposal.

2. Builder access, not builder travel
As a builder, I strongly support putting Cardano projects in front of these audiences. A booth slot, a speaking opportunity and a seat in the room with financial institutions is the real value. For any serious project, that access should be plenty of help. Each project gets a maximum of two event tickets: the project pays for one and the program covers the second. The Foundation coordinates group rates for flights and hotels. The Treasury should not fund project travel or accommodation. Staff travel is budgeted and reported separately.

3. Executive meetings at financial events
At Money20/20 Asia and Signal Week, replace the networking session with invite-only executive roundtables built around a named list of target institutions.

4. KPIs built around financial institution meetings

Meetings with banks, asset managers, payment providers and other financial institutions, at decision-maker level
Meetings sourced for ecosystem projects
Follow-up status at 90 days, and resulting pilots, MOUs or integrations
Targets set per event, reported quarterly, with institutions named where agreements allow

Other KPIs should at least match the 2025-26 results.

5. Budget
These changes bring the request to roughly US$2.3M to US$2.8M, about half the current US$5.0M. The final figure depends on staff travel costs.
