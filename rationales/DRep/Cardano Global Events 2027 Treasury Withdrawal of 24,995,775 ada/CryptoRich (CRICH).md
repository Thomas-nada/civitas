If Cardano had a group chairman, this request would have been sent back

I first posted this in Korean on X on Nov 23. Here is the English version. A few readers asked whether I'm against events. I'm not. My point is that, in this market, this doesn't need to come from the treasury.

The Cardano Foundation (CF) has asked the treasury for 25M ADA (about $5M), paid in one go, to cover 12 overseas conferences next year. That is 59% of the remaining treasury withdrawal budget limit.

As a DRep, I vote No. Not because events are bad, but because we should first ask whether this is the right strategy in a bear market like this one.

What the proposal is

1\. Joint "Cardano" booths at 12 conferences, from Nairobi in February 2027 to Bengaluru in December, plus a side event at each (four of them hackathons) and builder travel.

2\. The reason given: unlike other major layer 1s, Cardano showed up not as one clear presence but as several small ones. In other words, there was no group pavilion at the trade show, and affiliates and partners each went with their own small booth.

3\. I share the concern. The Foundation pays its own staff costs for the trips (about $640K), and there are clauses for returning unused funds and for an audit.

The starting point: is now the time?

4\. Conferences pay off in a bull market. Attention is high, conversations with new institutional clients grow, and business progress turns into real results.

5\. Right now the whole crypto market is in a downturn, and ADA fell to $0.149 on Nov 15, close to its low for the year.

6\. That is exactly when we should step away from "we did it last year, so we do it again this year" and ask whether these events need to continue right now.

Looking at Cardano as one group

7\. Cardano has the "Pentad" idea, grouping five entities: the Foundation, IO, EMURGO, Intersect and the Midnight Foundation. At the end of last year, the "Critical Integrations" budget the five put forward together won over 85% DRep approval.

8\. The five are most efficient moving in one direction. If there were a holding company and a group chairman over these five affiliates, that chairman might well have made the strategic call, in a market like this, to pause outside events and focus on technology, internal strength and fundamentals.

9\. Because Cardano is decentralized, there is no such chairman. I believe DRep votes are what make that call instead.

And the Foundation has its own reserves

10\. The Foundation received 648.18M ADA at launch, and its 2025 report, published in April this year, shows total assets of CHF 287.5M (about $360M). This $5M request is less than 2% of that.

11\. Yet while those total assets fell about 45% in a year ($660M to $360M), total operating expenses stayed almost flat at CHF 46.05M (CHF 46.14M the year before).

12\. Personnel costs were cut 25%, but external services (+15%) and other operating expenses (+83%) rose, so the overall books can't yet be called belt-tightening.

13\. I'm not saying don't hold events. When the group is tightening its belt, an affiliate should also cut spending and cover something this size from its own funds or outside funding. Reaching into the shared vault (the treasury) for all of it at once right now feels a bit tone-deaf, doesn't it?

The money flow and the targets

14\. The largest line in the $5M is sponsor packages paid to the conference organizers, $1.99M (39.8%). Builder travel and side events/hackathons, the parts that go directly to builders, add up to about 28%.

15\. The targets are also set below last year's results. The 2027 targets are 955 developers and 910 wallet sign-ups, while the 2026 series (20 events), as published by the Foundation in September, delivered 1,169 and 1,045. (With fewer events, that is higher per event.)

The order and method of the request

16\. The Foundation proposed the 2027 budget process (CEBP) together with Intersect, and is a candidate steward for the "marketing and events" area within it. Yet it submitted this request before that process has even reached the chain.

17\. Payment is "upfront funding" in the proposal's own words: paid in full in advance, with no tranches. The receiving address is "single signature on-chain" too, so 25M ADA would go into an account that a single seal can move.

So my vote

18\. No. I'll look again if the Foundation uses its own funds or outside funding (investment or loans), or, if it must come from the treasury, brings it back for approval inside the 2027 budget process with split payments. Deadline: Dec 20, 21:45 UTC (epoch 675).

19\. As of the morning of Nov 23 (KST), Yes 3.03% (15 DReps), direct No 15.99% (24 DReps), and the Constitutional Committee has not voted yet.

If you were the group chairman, would you sign this tone-deaf request?

PS: I'm not saying don't hold events. (They're free to plan them; given the situation, they should just fund them from the Foundation's own budget or outside funding.) Events can come back bigger in a bull market. I think saving the treasury now is how we get a bigger group pavilion then. 👍
