I don't think the giant ask translates to material benefit to holders, and this is coming from someone who has had strong experience in organizing and attending events for top tier musicians, and also as someone who has attended multiple cryptocurrency events and NFT events in Toronto, Austin, Miami, and more, both as a creator, as a community member, and as press. 

Events are interesting on the surface, but the metrics and KPIs tracked for growth don't necessarily translate to a material benefit for ADA holders, in that there is a large amount of expenditure, but a small amount, a negligible amount, that translates to real networking, or long-lasting marketing benefits. In the age of modernity, the Internet connects us to more people in an organic manner, and the face-to-face communication is fantastic, albeit superior in organic settings like afterparties or dedicated dinners, etc.

In the absence of highly competitive products, the conversations at the expense of ADA holders are also less impactful, as the best marketing is a fantastic product, that then has its marketing accelerated by said events.

We need to be frank, and when it's all said and done, we'll be looking at massive expenditure without any significant boost to marketing, metrics, or upside to ADA holders. In my personal opinion, the events should be fully subsidized by the founding entities, or not done at all. Sometimes inaction is the best course of action, especially with the massive negative price volatility across the board.
