**Two key questions for me. I vote NO.** There may be urgency on the part of the proposer, but that is no reason to compromise proper scrutiny of other people's money.

**First, whose responsibility is this expenditure?** CF was endowed with nearly 648.2 million ADA to support Cardano's development and adoption. It retains substantial reserves and earns staking income, including through its own private pools. Why should the Treasury pay for network-level branding and promotion that fall squarely within CF's mandate? **CF should finance at least the roughly $1.99 million in conference sponsorship packages from its own resources, or explain why it cannot.** CF contributes staff time, but no direct cash towards the programme's external costs.

**Second, has this level of spending demonstrated real value?** Contacts, sign-ups and collaboration discussions are not proof of sustained economic benefits. I want an **independent impact evaluation** of the 2025–2026 programme delivered by CF, EMURGO and Rare Network and previous large-scale events organized by the CF. It should verify claimed outcomes with counterparties, examine six- and nine- or twelve-month results where possible, and compare them with projects or markets that received no subsidised participation.

The proposed 2027 events also need an **independent value-for-money assessment**. What would Cardano actually lose without spending $882,200 on TOKEN2049 New York or $671,000 on TOKEN2049 Singapore? **Competitors spending money is not itself a reason to match them.** Independent evaluation should recognise palpable non-economic benefits too, but only if they actually exist.

CF proposes to administer the funds, deliver the programme and report its own performance. It also proposes financial auditing. **A financial audit may establish proper accounting of expenditures, but not whether the strategy generated value worth its cost.**

I do not mistrust CF, but I remain cautious about its approach. Retail adoption is/was evident; a valid path towards sustained institutional adoption at scale is much less clear.

There is a need for DRep scrutiny of CF that was endowed to serve the ecosystem. Before asking for another 25 million ADA from a separate Treasury, **it should demonstrate more skin in the game.** CF does not bear a defined share of the external financial cost of the proposed programme, but in my view it should.
