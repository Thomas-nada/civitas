I am voting ABSTAIN on Cardano Global Events 2027, primarily because I am a recipient of Cardano Foundation delegation and wish to avoid any perceived conflict of interest. This proposal has merit, and I appreciate the Foundation providing tangible performance data from its previous program, including 8,191 booth contacts, 1,169 developer engagements, 219 collaboration discussions, and exposure for 79 ecosystem projects. I also recognize the value of the numerous events the Foundation attends without Treasury funding, where meaningful industry discussions, panels, private meetings, and relationship-building contribute to positioning Cardano within the broader financial and technology sectors. Not all of these activities produce immediate or publicly quantifiable results, but that does not diminish their strategic importance.

Where I believe we can improve is in developing a more comprehensive framework for measuring how these engagements translate into commercial opportunities, executed partnerships, sustained adoption, and economic activity on Cardano. My abstention should not be interpreted as a lack of confidence in the Foundation's ability to execute or the importance of strategic marketing. Rather, it reflects my commitment to transparency and governance integrity, while encouraging the continued evolution of marketing KPIs that demonstrate meaningful progress toward Cardano's Vision 2030 objectives.
