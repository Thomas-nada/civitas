I vote NO on “Cardano Global Events 2027.”

I recognize the value of a shared Cardano presence and support for ecosystem builders. My priority, however, is whether these activities lead to Cardano adoption, production deployments, and sustained on-chain demand.

The published materials report contacts, collaboration discussions, and wallet registrations. They do not provide sufficient evidence to assess how many of these opportunities progressed to production deployments, or how much sustained usage and additional transaction activity followed.

The proposal commits to tracking and reporting post-event on-chain transactions attributable to event leads. However, the measurement methodology, follow-up period, and specific evaluation criteria remain insufficiently defined.

Before supporting a further allocation of approximately 25 million ADA, I need verifiable evidence connecting previous spending to adoption and real-world usage, alongside a budget allocation informed by those findings.
