I’m voting NO on the 24,995,775 ADA withdrawal for Cardano Global Events 2027. If I’m being completely honest, I think it’s time for a change in leadership at the Cardano Foundation. The Foundation is asking for more treasury funding, and from what I’ve seen, its past programs have delivered too little lasting value for ADA holders to justify another request of this size.

The treasury put 6 million ADA into the last joint events program. I give the Foundation and its partners credit for getting 79 Cardano projects in front of people they might never have reached on their own. My question is what came out of the 219 collaboration discussions they reported. How many led to a project landing a customer, signing a partnership, or launching something people are using on Cardano today? That would help me judge which events are worth paying for again.

I’d also like to know which events still go ahead and how many builders still get to go if ADA trades below the $0.20 assumed in the budget. I’d be open to covering early deposits, protecting builder travel, and releasing more funding once we see what those events deliver. As it stands, I can’t justify committing the full amount upfront.
