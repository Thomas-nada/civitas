As a DRep, I have decided to vote NO on the proposal: Cardano Global Events 2027: Treasury Withdrawal of 24,995,775 ADA.

My rationale:

First and foremost, I would like to thank the Cardano Foundation for their willingness to represent Cardano at crypto events and for their contributions to the ecosystem.

Cardano certainly should be represented at crypto events. The question is: what is the cost, and what exactly should the Treasury be paying for?

The Treasury should not be covering travel expenses for builders. Builders either have a revenue-generating product, meaning they can cover their own costs, or they are willing to invest in their own product.

Investing in crypto events during a bear market is not an ideal strategy. Attendance may be low, rendering the impact negligible.

An important question is whether these ₳25M could be invested more effectively. Cardano has been represented reasonably well in recent years. Moreover, the Cardano Foundation has organized the Summit.

Yet, the ecosystem still lacks major stablecoins and tokenized assets. There are no vaults, AI activity is low, the user base is small, developers are leaving, and key products have recently shut down.

I have to ask: what exactly are we trying to showcase at these crypto events? 

Is it "old-school" DeFi applications that have no users? Is it a governance system where DReps are losing the motivation to vote? Or is it a decentralized network from which small stake pool operators are departing? 

Cardano’s presence at crypto events has failed to secure the strategic partnerships needed to keep the project in the top 10. I therefore view these as a neutral investment with an impact that is difficult to measure.

The ₳25M should instead be invested in a team tasked with bringing a major stablecoin and tokenized assets into the ecosystem, integrating Chainlink, building vaults, offering incentives for liquidity deployment, fostering AI-related activity, or applying blockchain technology in a novel and original way.

Given the current low price of ADA, we must prioritize and carefully consider where we allocate our investments. In my view, the coming year is not the right time to invest in crypto events. We should invest in the ecosystem instead.

Finally, it is worth noting that the Cardano Foundation has its own resources that can be used to cover the costs of attending crypto events. In fact, as the owner of the Cardano brand, the CF has a responsibility to utilize its funds for such purposes.
