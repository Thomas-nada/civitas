Voting yes. The Dolos and UTxO RPC proposals are the two sides of the same infrastructure bet: one funds the interface standard, the other funds the open-source implementation that makes the standard real. If UTxO RPC adoption grows, which the separate UTxO RPC maintenance proposal is designed to drive, then Dolos becomes more critical, not less. Investing in both is internally consistent.
The lightweight node case is worth stating on its own terms. Full cardano-node operation is a non-trivial infrastructure commitment. For the large class of developers and operators who need chain data access but can't or don't want to run a full node, the alternatives have been commercial API providers (with their attendant cost, rate limits, and third-party dependency) or not building at all. Dolos is the open-source answer to that problem. It's not replacing commercial providers in every context, but it makes self-hosted chain data access viable for teams that would otherwise take the commercial dependency.
Year 2 of a proven Intersect arrangement, on schedule, from TxPipe's consistent delivery track record. The ask is the same as Pallas at the same cost. Both are justified.
The note I'll carry forward: future proposals for tools with open-ended "enhancement" scope, Dolos and Oura, should specify what enhancements are planned and at what adoption baseline. The maintenance case is clear; the enhancement case needs more specificity to be evaluated properly.
