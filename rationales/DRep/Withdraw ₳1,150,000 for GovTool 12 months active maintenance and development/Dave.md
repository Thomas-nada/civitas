While I appreciate the recent improvements to GovTool and recognize the expertise of vendors such as LidoNation and Dquadrant, I cannot vote yes on this proposal. The requested amount is not justified by the current quality, performance, or user experience, both from my own experience and from feedback within the community. The reported metrics, including “50,000+ unique mainnet users across 170+ countries,” are not sufficiently supported by transparent data or verifiable evidence. For a request of this scale, I would expect stronger validation, clearer performance metrics, and a higher standard of UX, performance and functionality. I would however welcome a reduced budget that better reflects the current stage of the product and its demonstrated impact.
