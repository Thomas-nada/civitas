We, the Dutch DRep, vote YES on the Govtool budget proposal by the Govtool consortium.

Transparency Disclosure: One of our Dutch DReps represents Dquadrant, a Govtool consortium member, in community forums. While this relationship informs our understanding, we believe the proposal stands on its own merits and advances Cardano’s governance goals.

The Govtool consortium—coordinated through Intersect and backed by trusted community teams—has a proven track record of delivering accessible, constitutionally sound governance tools since CIP‑1694. Their proposal is transparent, well‑structured, and firmly rooted in Cardano’s principles of accountability and community‑driven decision‑making.

Govtool’s planned enhancements will:

Guarantee open access for all ADA holders, lowering barriers to participation.

Foster community ownership through open‑source code and public APIs, enabling developers to build complementary governance solutions.

Support a distributed development model by funding multiple teams and encouraging community contributions.

Ensure the roadmap remains responsive to ADA holders’ needs via transparent, community‑driven prioritization.

Together, these improvements will strengthen Cardano’s decentralized governance, boost transparency and efficiency, and align with our long‑term vision of inclusivity and innovation.
