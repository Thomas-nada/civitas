This is an expensive price tag, but as the only open-source and community-owned tool for proposing and voting on governance actions, I'm willing to vote YES on this proposal.  However, I expect to see notable improvements to the platform as a result of this funding.  Namely the ability to vote on multiple governance actions at once, a crowdfunding mechanism for governance action deposits, and the addition of all remaining governance action types to the list of proposals that can be submitted through the GovTools UI.  At a bare minimum I think these changes should be made before submitting for any further treasury funding.
