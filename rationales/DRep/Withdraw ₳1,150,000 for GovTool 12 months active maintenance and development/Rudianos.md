I am in favor of approving this funding request. The amount requested is significant and I believe that GOV TOOL in general has quite a few improvements to make, however it has one important reason why we absolutely should fun it: It is community managed and open source. It is great to have multiple tools from multiple teams out there to handle governance but we also must maintain enough tools that are in the control of the community if we want to remain fully decentralized. Community managed projects come with a certain set of difficulties and are generally a little more expensive but that is certainly worth the cost.
