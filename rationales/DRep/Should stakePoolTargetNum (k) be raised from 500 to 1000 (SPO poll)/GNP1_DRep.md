I voted YES on raising k from 500 to 1000 as both a DRep and an SPO.

Don't let anyone bamboozle you with tech talk. The numbers are simple: stake has stopped moving. I checked them on the chain itself:

- Only 0.16% of stake changes pool each epoch. In 2021 it was 1.36%.
- 60% of the stake delegated to launch-era pools in 2020 is still with launch-era pools six years on.
- Just 36 to 58 operators hold over half of all stake and SPO voting power.

Raising k to 1000 halves the point where a pool is full, to about 38.9M ADA. Big pools would have to shed stake, and that stake has to go somewhere, giving new pools a real chance.
