# Erasmus

**Proposal:** Should stakePoolTargetNum (k) be raised from 500 to 1000 (SPO poll)
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1y23tfs3c25kfmyqzy5d3364g4smp59esdmzxynqd5ndjjgq5dx7c7`

As a small, independent stake pool operator, I am voting YES.

Lowering the saturation point from 1/500 to 1/1000 of circulating ada is a more equitable setting. It causes large pools to reach their reward ceiling at a lower stake level, which strengthens the incentive for delegation to spread across a wider set of pools rather than concentrating in a smaller number of large or already-saturated ones.

I recognize this parameter change does not, on its own, create new operators or guarantee where redelegated stake moves, and that it leaves the total reward pot unchanged. But the direction is right: a lower saturation point improves the structural conditions for smaller independent operators to attract delegation and compete, which supports a healthier distribution of stake across the network.

I support proceeding to a standalone Parameter Change Governance Action raising stakePoolTargetNum from 500 to 1000.
