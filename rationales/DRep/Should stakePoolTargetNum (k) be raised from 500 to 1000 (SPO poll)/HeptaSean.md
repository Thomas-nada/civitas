Not an SPO, but voting as dRep anyway: PoolTargetNum should not be raised. We have far too many SPOs as is. We cannot afford them with the little profits that this barely relevant ecosystem generates. And we don't need them. For enough decentralisation (and to outbalance the centralised exchanges and the wallet multi-pools), a few dozen SPOs are more than enough. That amount could _maybe_ be financed by transaction fees _if_ Cardano gets/retains some relevance. Moreover, the saturation limits generated by PoolTargetNum do not achieve what they want to achieve and just lead to lots and lots of multi-pool operations. If an SPO can attract that much stake, they should just be allowed to operate with that in a single pool and not be forced to artificially distribute over multiple pools. PoolTargetNum should ideally be 1 even if that requires a change to the guardrails.
