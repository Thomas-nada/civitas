The Parameter Committee meticulously drafted a plan to ask ALL of the voting community (SPOs and DReps) about raising k, in the most informative way possible. They prepared the arguments for, and the arguments against raising k and these series of arguments were to be linked in a neutrally formulated overview document, all so that voters could have a balanced view of both sides, with the intent of everyone being able to make an informed, educated decision. 

Since stakePoolTargetNum is not part of the parameter group that would constitute SPOs to vote on a Parameter Change directly, the TSC, PC, members of other committees, and many more community members collectively agreed to  go above and beyond what the protocol and the Constitution require and went out of their way to give SPOs a voice on this.
The Parameter Change should be preceded with an info action that asked SPOs whether the Governance Action that would change the parameter, should be submitted subsequently, following an approval to the info action. 
This is a question concerning the authors alone - "Should they submit something else, or not". Further, since this is asked in an info action, which has the CIP-1694 defined purpose of not being able to be ratified or enacted, since they do not have any effect on the protocol (https://cips.cardano.org/cip/CIP-1694#enactment), there exists no adequate or correct approval threshold. 
Knowing that a large part of SPOs voting power is locked in custodian's and infrastructure providers' client's wallets, where they evidently from previous Governance Action's lack of participation, do not engage in Cardano Governance actively, the Parameter Committee decided to use a threshold which would produce a definitive view of the active SPOs' opinion, while not needing to wonder why SPOs did not vote (implicitly no or explicitly not knowing that they need to vote?). 

This part is, among other things, apparently something that the Author Chris did not agree with, and he took it upon himself to submit an altered version of the draft, changing the intended approval threshold for the un-enectable info action, and omitting documents designed to inform the community. 

Apart from the fact that taking other people's work, altering it, and submitting in a manner that suits their own narrative and intent, is extremely disrespectful and damaging to the community as a whole, which has to now question which documents to share publicly, very much not in the spirit that has bloomed within the ecosystem since years, and apart from questioning whether this action is Chris directly and deliberately trying to bypass the notion of informing the community, the debate on k may realistically continue with this action just as well. It is just sad to see a chance to get neutral empiric evidence on the wishes of everyone be clouded by a single opinion, causing a storm of confusion and mistrust. 

I hope the SPOs consider the many informative documents that are available to them, which were created by the Parameter Committee, and vote with the best educated decision they can make - in plenty numbers. 

Even though I am not being asked, I voice my opinion and vote YES to submit the parameter change governance action that asks DReps to raise stakePoolTargetNum to 1000.
