I support raising stakePoolTargetNum (k) from 500 to 1000 as a step toward greater decentralization, but I cast an Abstain vote because this Info Action is explicitly framed as an SPO mandate poll under CIP-1694 semantics. DRep votes are not included in the proposer's stated threshold for determining sufficient SPO support. Rather than add noise to a stake-weighted SPO calculation, I record my participation through Abstain and use this rationale to signal my position.

Lowering the saturation point from ~75M to ~37.5M ADA creates stronger economic incentives for stake to flow toward smaller and newer pool operators. This is directionally positive for Cardano's decentralization and long-term network resilience.

However, I share the concern raised by other DReps and SPOs that k does not operate in isolation. Stake pool economics are shaped by the interaction of minPoolCost, minPoolMargin, pledge influence, and future changes like CIP-50. Changing k without a coherent review of these interdependent parameters risks unintended distortions.

My support for k=1000 is therefore conditional: I would more confidently endorse this change as part of a comprehensive parameter package with published impact analysis, rather than as an isolated adjustment. If a subsequent Parameter Change Governance Action is submitted following clear SPO mandate from this poll, I would evaluate it on the merits of the full proposal, including whether related parameters are addressed holistically.
