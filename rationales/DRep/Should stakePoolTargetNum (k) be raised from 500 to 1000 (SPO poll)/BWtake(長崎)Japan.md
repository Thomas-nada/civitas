I vote YES on this Info Action.

stakePoolTargetNum (k) was increased from 150 to 500 in 2020 and has remained unchanged for about six years. Raising it from 500 to 1000 would lower the reward saturation point from about 75M ADA to 37.5M ADA, encouraging stake concentrated in larger pools to move again.

While it has become easier to start an SPO, the burden of stable operation has increased due to higher node resource requirements, redundancy, monitoring, security, and governance responsibilities. At the same time, staking rewards from the reserve are designed to decline over the long term, making access to delegation increasingly important for new and smaller SPOs.

k=1000 alone will not guarantee decentralization, but encouraging stake redistribution is a reasonable step toward improving Cardano’s decentralization.
