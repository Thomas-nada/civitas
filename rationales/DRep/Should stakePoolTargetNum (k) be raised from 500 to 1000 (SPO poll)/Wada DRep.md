As Wada DRep, we choose to abstain on this governance action because it specifically seeks to establish the opinion of Stake Pool Operators (SPOs) on whether the stakePoolTargetNum parameter should be increased from 500 to 1,000. Since SPOs are the primary stakeholders whose perspectives this poll intends to capture, we believe the decision-making process should be led by them. Our abstention reflects our intention to respect their role in expressing the ecosystem's position on this parameter change, rather than influence the outcome through a DRep vote. We encourage SPOs to participate actively and express their views through the designated polling process.
