I am voting Yes on this Info Action. Raising stakePoolTargetNum (k) from 500 to 1000 would lower the saturation point from roughly 75 million to 37.5 million ADA. This change creates a clearer economic incentive for stake currently held in larger pools to move toward smaller and newer operators.

Greater distribution of stake across more pools strengthens decentralization. When stake is less concentrated, the network becomes more resilient and a larger number of independent operators can meaningfully participate in block production. Supporting smaller pools also improves the long-term health of the ecosystem by making it more viable for new operators to attract delegation and earn sustainable rewards.
