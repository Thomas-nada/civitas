I vote ABSTAIN on "Should stakePoolTargetNum (k) be raised from 500 to 1000? (SPO poll)" because this action seeks an SPO mandate and DRep votes are excluded from that assessment. I support consulting operators, but substantively I do not support moving to k=1000 at this time. Doubling k would materially change saturation and delegation incentives, while I do not see a sufficiently clear problem that this change would solve or convincing evidence of a net benefit for independent operators. I prefer to preserve the current setting until the need, operator economics and effects on existing pools and passive delegators are better demonstrated. My abstention respects the purpose of this poll and does not endorse a future parameter change.
