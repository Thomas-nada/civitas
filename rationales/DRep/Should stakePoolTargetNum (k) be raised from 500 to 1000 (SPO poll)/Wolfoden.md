**Proposal:** Should stakePoolTargetNum (k) be raised from 500 to 1000 (SPO poll)

**Voter ID:** `drep1y293f6495fvld5zqv0uqsjpjt25zzdkjglys7ykc678jtkghet05n`

---

I do not believe that increasing k from 500 to 1000 meaningfully addresses the underlying challenges faced by stake pool operators.

Raising k effectively distributes the same reward pool across a larger number of pools. For many operators, this means either operating additional pools, increasing operational complexity and infrastructure requirements, or accepting lower rewards per pool. In practice, operators may face reduced profitability, a higher operational burden, or both.

While the proposal is often presented as a decentralization measure, I am not convinced that stake will naturally flow to the smallest operators. In fact, if I am wrong, my own SPO would likely benefit from such a change. It is equally possible that stake simply redistributes toward medium-sized pools closer to the new saturation point.

In my view, the long-term sustainability of independent SPOs is better addressed through improvements to stake pool economics rather than further partitioning the existing reward structure. Increasing k changes how rewards are distributed, but it does not create additional value for operators or delegators.

For these reasons, I do not support this proposal.
