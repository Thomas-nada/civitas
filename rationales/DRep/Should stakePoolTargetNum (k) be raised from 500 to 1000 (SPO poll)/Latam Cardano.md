Raising stakePoolTargetNum ($k$) to 1000 is a necessary evolutionary step for Cardano’s consensus layer. While $k$ parameter adjustments alone do not solve multi-pool consolidation without future updates to pledge weighting ($a_0$), lowering the saturation threshold to $\sim 36.4\text{M ADA}$ provides an immediate structural advantage to independent pool operators. It incentivizes stake migration away from centralized entities and creates space for reliable, smaller operators to become economically viable. The technical capacity of the network post-Chang upgrades is well-equipped to handle 1,000 active nodes, making the operational trade-offs acceptable in pursuit of deeper decentralization.
Impact on the Latin American Cardano Ecosystem:Lowering the saturation cap directly empowers Latin American SPOs—many of whom operate as single-node entities—by making full pool saturation significantly more achievable with regional delegation pools. This strengthens local infrastructure, increases sustainable reward generation for LatAm community operators, and drives broader regional participation in securing Cardano's global consensus network.
We are voting "Absain" due to the way this proposal was built on other's work without their consent.
