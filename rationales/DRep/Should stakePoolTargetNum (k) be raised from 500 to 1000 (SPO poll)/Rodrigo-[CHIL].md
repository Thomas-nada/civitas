**Proposal:** Should stakePoolTargetNum (k) be raised from 500 to 1000 (SPO poll)
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2j2q9pcl855969ea9csrhdta6slnamctgzuvuqnnkl6fusqyjy36`

---

I support the goal of increasing decentralization and I am not fundamentally opposed to raising k to 1000. However, I do not believe this is the right time.

Cardano is facing declining reserve-funded rewards, limited transaction fee revenue, and increasing operational costs for SPOs.

Raising k will not increase the number of blocks produced or the total reward pot. Furthermore, there is insufficient evidence that the resulting stake redistribution would meaningfully benefit independent operators rather than existing multi-pool entities.

Before making this change, we should demonstrate that it improves decentralization without compromising the long-term economic sustainability of stake pool operators.

**My vote is NO under the current conditions, not NO to the idea itself.**
