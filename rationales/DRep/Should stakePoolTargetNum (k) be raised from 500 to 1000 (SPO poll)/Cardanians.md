# Cardanians

**Proposal:** Should stakePoolTargetNum (k) be raised from 500 to 1000 (SPO poll)
**Vote:** Abstain
**Voter ID:** `drep1yt4lyf0fwlrz8k6j5evd3rxw0sqp67qkh36su98a97q3qsc0h03y4`

---

Cardanians also operates stake pools and therefore has a direct economic interest in changes to saturation and staking incentives. Our DRep abstention does not endorse increasing k to 1000 or reject further consideration of the change.

We believe further research and stronger empirical evidence are needed to properly assess any future Parameter Change action. This should include analysis of independent operator concentration, likely redelegation behaviour, delegator rewards, operational sustainability and interactions with pledge and pool-fee parameters.

Any subsequent proposal should demonstrate how the expected benefits outweigh the risks and distinguish an increase in pool count from a meaningful improvement in independent control.

Our objective is a sustainable network with broadly distributed control and reliable independent operators.
