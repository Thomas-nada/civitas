I support clear consultation with SPOs, but this Info Action seeks a mandate to proceed specifically toward increasing stakePoolTargetNum from 500 to 1000, and the evidence does not sufficiently justify that direction. Halving the reward-saturation boundary does not directly increase small pools’ block-production opportunities or available rewards; any benefit depends on delegated stake moving to genuinely independent operators rather than being redistributed among additional pools controlled by existing groups. The proposal acknowledges these uncertainties but does not provide current comparative modeling demonstrating improved entity-level decentralization and sustainable operator economics. Although parameters can be voted on separately, k, pledge influence, fixed costs, margins, and reward availability must be evaluated as interacting components of one incentive system. I therefore do not support establishing a mandate for 𝑘=1000 at this stage.
