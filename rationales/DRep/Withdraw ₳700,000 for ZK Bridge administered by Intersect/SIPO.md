SIPO supports the ₳700,000 treasury withdrawal for the ZK Bridge project because it addresses one of the most critical gaps in the Cardano ecosystem: secure, trustless, and modular cross-chain interoperability.

Currently, Cardano lacks native infrastructure for zero-knowledge (ZK) bridges. Most existing bridges rely on centralized or multi-signature schemes, which have proven vulnerable in other ecosystems. This proposal offers a fundamentally different and decentralized approach by implementing a ZK bridge that allows secure messaging and asset transfer between Cardano and any isomorphic blockchain.

The benefits of this proposal are multifold:
	1.	Security and Trust Minimization: By using zero-knowledge proofs, the bridge eliminates the need for centralized validators or long challenge windows.
	2.	Liquidity and Composability: It enables seamless integration with external ecosystems and access to their liquidity and tools.
	3.	Modular Interoperability Framework: The design allows other blockchains to easily integrate with Cardano by implementing the counterpart smart contracts.
	4.	Future zkRollup Enablement: The bridge infrastructure lays essential groundwork for native zkRollups on Cardano.
	5.	Open-source and Reusable Components: All code, circuits, and documentation will be published to enable reuse by other projects.

SIPO also recognizes the credibility and deep expertise of the vendor, Eryx Coop, whose team has contributed to multiple ZK projects, including Plonky2 and Aztec’s Noir. Their prior work in Aiken and EUTxO ZK integrations demonstrates strong alignment with Cardano’s architecture.

Moreover, the proposal is contractually governed and managed by Intersect with robust on-chain treasury controls, milestone verification, and third-party assurance, all of which contribute to transparency and accountability.

For these reasons, SIPO believes this proposal is a strategic investment that will significantly enhance Cardano’s interoperability, scalability, and competitiveness in the zero-knowledge space.
