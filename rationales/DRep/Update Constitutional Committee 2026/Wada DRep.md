# Wada DRep

**Proposal:** Update Constitutional Committee 2026
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2jnqfamg4tq5aepxhggzhqg7pq5gunph4wypkyh9q3qt2cru5m2j`

---

We vote yes on this governance action because we view the renewal of the Constitutional Committee as necessary to preserve the continuity and functionality of Cardano’s governance system. Four existing Committee seats are reaching the end of their terms, and failing to replace them would reduce the Committee below its minimum required membership, potentially preventing it from performing its constitutional oversight role.

We are also comfortable supporting this action because it implements the outcome of a competitive Constitutional Committee election rather than introducing an unelected appointment. The election provided DReps, of which we are part of, with a choice among multiple candidates, and the results were independently audited, giving us reasonable confidence in the integrity of the selection process.
