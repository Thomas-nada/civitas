As a DRep, I decided to vote on the proposal: Update Constitutional Committee 2026

My rationale:

This action faithfully implements the independently audited result of the 2026 Constitutional Committee election by appointing Philip DiSarro, Leandros BSP, Marek Mahut and Cardano Curia. It preserves the Committee’s staggered rotation, leaves the remaining three members and the ⅔ quorum unchanged, and complies with the current maximum term length. The re-election of two existing members provides continuity while the addition of two new members supports renewal.

I nevertheless have reservations about the process. Participation was limited to 38 DReps representing ₳2.332B, and the fourth-place margin over AACC was approximately ₳146M. The approval-voting model also allows large DReps to apply their full voting power to several candidates, increasing the influence of concentrated stake over the resulting slate. These aspects should be reviewed before the next election.

The enactment design is also unnecessarily fragile: because the proposed terms use the full 146-epoch maximum, the action cannot be ratified until Epoch 653. Future committee updates should leave sufficient term-length headroom to avoid dependence on the final epoch.

These weaknesses merit improvement, but they do not invalidate the published result. Without evidence of manipulation, an audit failure or an incorrect on-chain implementation, the legitimate outcome of the election should be respected.
