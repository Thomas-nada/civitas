I am voting yes because this governance action is fundamentally about honoring the outcome of Cardano’s 2026 Constitutional Committee election and ensuring continuity of constitutional governance.

The four incoming members — Philip DiSarro, Leandros BSP, Marek Mahut, and Cardano Curia — were selected through the established public election process, with candidate registration, stake-weighted DRep voting, independent auditing, and publication of the final results before this update action was submitted. Approving this action therefore respects the legitimacy of that process rather than reopening the candidate selection after the election has already concluded.

I also believe continuity matters. The Constitutional Committee plays a necessary role in reviewing governance actions for constitutional compliance, and the expiring seats must be renewed to keep the committee functioning properly. This update balances continuity through the re-election of existing members with new representation through incoming members, while preserving the existing quorum and established term limits.

For me, the key principle is simple: if Cardano establishes a transparent, auditable election process, then governance should faithfully enact its results unless there is a material procedural or constitutional defect. I see no such issue here. For that reason, I am voting yes to approve the elected committee update.
