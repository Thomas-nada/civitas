### 🌐 **About the Proposal**

The Cardano Foundation proposes registering the top-level domains **.ada** and **.cardano** with ICANN — the international organization that governs internet domain names.
This will establish Cardano’s official digital identity and strengthen the bridge between Web2 and Web3.

* Builds a strong, recognizable presence for the Cardano ecosystem in the global digital infrastructure.
