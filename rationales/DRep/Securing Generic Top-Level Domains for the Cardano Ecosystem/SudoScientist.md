While I can see the interest in obtaining such domains, I do not believe that leveraging such "unique" domains achieves any meaningful outcome.  The task of expanding our ecosystem, in my opinion, is not achieved by showcasing domains that would indicate exclusivity.  There are cases where others may want to obtain vanity domains, but this is not a pursuit that provides many benefits to the ecosystem as a whole, and therefore, should not use treasury funds to pursue.
