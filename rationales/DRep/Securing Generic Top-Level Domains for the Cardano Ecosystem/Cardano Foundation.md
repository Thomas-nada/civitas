This info action, submitted by the Cardano Foundation, serves a clear purpose: to gauge on-chain community sentiment and gather a formal record of support for a potential ICANN application for the .ada and .cardano gTLDs.

Our decision to vote "YES" on our own proposal is based on the following:
- **Our Role as a Community DRep:** The purpose of this info action is to measure community support. As a DRep, the Cardano Foundation represents not only its own organizational stance but also the will of the community members delegating their ada to our DRep. We are a part of the community, and we are voting for this strategic initiative on behalf of our delegators as we believe it would be a positive course of action.
- **No Cost to the Treasury:** This is not a request for treasury funds. The Cardano Foundation has committed to using its own financial resources for the entire ICANN application and management process. This info action is purely a request for community endorsement, making it a simple and high-value proposition for ada holders to support. 
- **Strategic Importance:** Securing the .ada and .cardano gTLDs is a rare, time-sensitive opportunity that aligns with the upcoming ICANN application window in Q1 2026. These gTLDs are vital for the ecosystem's long-term brand protection, digital identity, and ability to foster Web2-to-Web3 innovations.
- **Commitment to Community Governance:** The Cardano Foundation, as a non-profit dedicated to the development of Cardano as a public digital infrastructure, is the right long-term steward for this project. The proposal's plan to establish a dedicated Community Advisory Group to guide the gTLDs' development ensures this project will be managed in the ecosystem's best interest.
