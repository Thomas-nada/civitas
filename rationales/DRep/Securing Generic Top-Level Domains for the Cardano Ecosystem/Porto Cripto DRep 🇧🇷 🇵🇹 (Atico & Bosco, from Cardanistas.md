We chose to vote "YES" on this governance action (gov_action15dh2lt4qskmhl97vat8s0l552ruvddrlasa0jndg7lg43g0uju3qq78hvja), because it aims to strengthen the digital identity of the Cardano ecosystem, ensuring that the .ada and .cardano domains remain under community management.
