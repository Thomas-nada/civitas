I am voting No on the “Top‑Level Domains for Cardano Ecosystem” Governance Action. I acknowledge the potential benefit of Cardano having its own domain namespace, and I agree that the Cardano Foundation could be a suitable organization to manage such a system. However, this initiative does not represent a strategically significant, emerging, or essential investment at this stage that justifies allocating a dynamic budget of this scale. Currently, Cardano’s ecosystem is falling behind Ethereum and Solana in terms of user growth and activity. The likely use of such TLDs would be limited to a small portion of the existing community, making the impact minimal. Moreover, community members may face unnecessary costs — for example, pressure to switch or register new domains despite already operating functional websites. Additionally, if TLDs are introduced without strong governance or identity verification, high-profile community names may be preemptively registered by third parties. This could lead to impersonation or scam risks, especially if original creators are unable to secure their own domain identities. At a time when core infrastructure and user-facing utility still require funding and development, it is more prudent to direct resources to fundamental needs rather than optional branding initiatives. That said, I recognize the need to protect critical namespace assets such as “.ada” or “.cardano” from misuse or squatting.
