The Cardano Foundation recognises that the minimum required size for the Constitutional Committee (CC) is seven members, and that the appointment of Christina Gianelloni would increase the total membership to eight. 

We acknowledge that increasing the total CC size to eight members has implications for CC approval thresholds, as the two-thirds majority requirement increases the absolute number of aligned votes needed for CC decisions, from five aligned votes, to six aligned votes, thereby marginally increasing individual members' veto power and may introduce additional coordination challenges, especially in scenarios of lower participation.

Notwithstanding these implications, the Cardano Foundation supports this governance action based on the qualifications of the proposed candidate. Christina Gianelloni brings a strong track record in stewardship, operational leadership, and open-source governance through her roles as COO of Blink Labs and Head of the Board at PRAGMA. Her experience in governance processes is well aligned with the demands of the CC member.
