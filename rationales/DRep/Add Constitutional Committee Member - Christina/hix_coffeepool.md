I am voting No on this Governance Action to add Christina as the 8th Constitutional Committee member. While I initially supported the idea, constitutional concerns have since emerged. Article 7 Section 3 of the Constitution clearly states that the Cardano Community shall \"establish and make public a process\" for the election of Constitutional Committee (CC) members. The Snap Election process was publicly announced and structured for the selection of one seat. Submitting a GA after the election to retroactively add a second seat appears inconsistent with that established and published process. Voters cast their votes under the assumption that only one seat was being filled. Retroactively modifying that outcome undermines procedural integrity and risks setting a precedent where off-chain elections can be overridden via Governance Actions. Such practices could lead to confusion and erosion of trust in governance mechanisms. While it's possible to interpret the addition as part of a new process, doing so without establishing and publicly communicating such a process raises questions about constitutional consistency. I support committee expansion in principle, but such a change must follow a clearly established and public process. I urge that any future expansion be preceded by an Info GA that sets expectations and allows the community to align.
