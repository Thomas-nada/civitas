As a DRep, I vote YES on this governance action.

I previously raised concerns regarding **procedural clarity**, as the recent snap election was publicly framed to fill a single Constitutional Committee seat. Those concerns remain valid. However, after further consideration, I believe **the risk of governance disruption due to an insufficient number of active Constitutional Committee members is more immediate and significant.**

I also support Christina as a candidate. Christina has demonstrated strong commitment to Cardano’s decentralized governance and received meaningful community support during the election process. Supporting capable and motivated contributors is especially important given that this role is **unpaid and responsibility-heavy.**

This YES vote **should not be interpreted as endorsing the current process as ideal**. Rather, it reflects a pragmatic decision to preserve governance continuity while acknowledging the need for improvement.

I strongly encourage the community and relevant bodies to follow up with an **Info Action** to clearly define:

* When and how the Constitutional Committee size may be expanded,
* How off-chain election outcomes should be interpreted in such cases,
* And how to avoid ambiguity or retroactive expectations in future appointments.

Strengthening procedural clarity alongside operational resilience is essential to maintaining long-term trust in Cardano governance.
