**Socious DRep** votes **NO** on the governance action: *“Add Constitutional Committee Member – Christina.”* While we hold the candidate in high regard and recognize her as exceptionally well-qualified for the Constitutional Committee (CC), we believe that structural changes to the committee must follow a legitimate and transparent process to maintain the integrity of Cardano’s governance.

### Rationale

**1. Candidate Merit vs. Procedural Integrity**
We wish to state clearly that **Christina is well-qualified**, and we would likely support her candidacy in a standard election or a specifically designed expansion process. However, a "NO" vote is necessary here because the process used to propose her appointment bypasses established community expectations.

**2. Adhering to the Election Mandate**
The Intersect snap election was held to fill **one** specific vacancy. Cardano Curia was selected by the community through that competitive process. To appoint a second member from the same election—without a pre-defined "runner-up" rule—sets a precedent where the scope of an election can be changed retroactively.

**3. Strategic Expansion Requires Consensus**
If there is a strategic need to increase the CC size to mitigate operational risk, that decision belongs to the ecosystem. We believe an **Info Action** should be the first step to:

- Formally propose an increase in committee size.
- Gather community feedback on the criteria for such an expansion.
- Establish a fair and predictable process for filling those new seats.

### Conclusion

**Socious DRep** remains supportive of qualified individuals like Christina joining the Constitutional Committee in the future. However, we cannot support a governance action that prioritizes expediency over a robust, community-aligned process. We encourage a return to a structured approach that ensures all structural changes are backed by a clear ecosystem mandate.
