# SIPO

**Proposal:** Withdraw ₳266,667 for Cexplorer.io -- Developer-Focused Blockchain Explorer
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh`

---

SIPO supports this proposal to fund Cexplorer.io with ₳266,667 for the following reasons:

1. Essential Developer Infrastructure

Cexplorer.io is a high-performance, developer-focused blockchain explorer that offers advanced insights and tooling for developers, SPOs, and users within the Cardano ecosystem. In a decentralized ecosystem like Cardano, such infrastructure is critical to building trust and enabling transparency. The platform’s uptime, speed, and real-time responsiveness are vital for on-chain dApp development, delegation monitoring, and token analytics.

2. Proven Track Record and Community Utility

With over 50,000 monthly users and millions of processed requests, Cexplorer.io is not a speculative product but a proven and relied-upon platform. Its predecessor, Adapools, has already established credibility and technical maturity. This usage validates both its value and necessity within the ecosystem.

3. Sustainability through Treasury Investment

This proposal emphasizes sustainability and continuity through two key deliverables:
	•	Maintenance and stability of existing infrastructure (server costs, bug fixing, and backend/frontend work).
	•	Enhancements with new features and a better UI/UX by expanding the development team.

By securing this funding, Cardano ensures that core user and developer infrastructure remains robust, accessible, and responsive to the ecosystem’s evolving needs.

4. Alignment with Cardano’s Governance and Oversight Framework

This proposal is submitted through Intersect’s structured and transparent treasury process. It includes legal contracts, milestone-based disbursement via audited smart contracts, and oversight by third-party entities such as Cardano Foundation, Sundae Labs, and NMKR. This offers both legal and operational assurance to the community regarding the responsible use of treasury funds.

5. Encouraging a Developer-Centric Ecosystem

Cardano’s success depends on accessible, powerful tooling for developers. Cexplorer.io directly supports this by offering deep data integration and flexible tools. Continued improvement of such tools is essential to attract and retain builders and foster innovation.

Conclusion:
SIPO believes this proposal will strengthen Cardano’s developer tooling, uphold transparency, and contribute to the long-term resilience of the ecosystem. Therefore, we vote Yes on this treasury withdrawal.

この資金により、Cardanoは重要なユーザーおよび開発者向けインフラを持続可能かつ柔軟に運営していくことができます。

Cardanoの発展には、開発者が活用できる柔軟で強力なツール群が不可欠です。Cexplorer.ioはその中心を担う存在であり、データアクセスの向上や新機能の追加を通じて、開発者の参入障壁を下げ、イノベーションを促進します。

SIPOは、本提案がCardanoの技術基盤の強化、透明性の向上、そしてエコシステムの持続的な成長に寄与すると判断し、「Yes」と投票します。
