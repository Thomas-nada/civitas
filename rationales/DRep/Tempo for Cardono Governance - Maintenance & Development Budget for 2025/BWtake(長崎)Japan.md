I support (Yes) this proposal.
Tempo.Vote is one of the few tools enabling governance participation from mobile devices, bringing Cardano’s on-chain governance closer to everyday users. Its independent operation of nodes and indexers—without reliance on centralized infrastructure—aligns with the principles of decentralized governance.
In particular, its seamless mobile experience for submitting proposals, voting, and checking results is highly beneficial to the broader ADA holder base. Given that the official wallet, Lace, currently lacks a mobile version, Tempo plays a critical role in filling that gap and encouraging broader participation from the community.
Additionally, the Lending Pool concept holds promise as a mechanism to amplify the voices of smaller holders in the governance process.
To make Cardano governance truly global and inclusive, the evolution of user-driven tools like Tempo is essential.
