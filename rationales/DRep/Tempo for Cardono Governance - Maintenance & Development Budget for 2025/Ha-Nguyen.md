I vote YES for this budget Info Action.

Tempo operates its own Cardano node and indexer, ensuring data integrity without relying on third-party infrastructure. Many DReps have used Tempo and found it offers useful governance features, a user-friendly UX/UI, and full independence from other governance tools or APIs.

While this proposal does not include a detailed budget breakdown, it specifies that Selfdriven will serve as the administrator for fund management. Compared to other proposals, this adds a layer of accountability. I expect a clearer budget and financial oversight process in Tempo’s follow-up Treasury Withdrawal proposal.

I support this proposal based on Tempo’s tangible value to Cardano governance today and its continued development goals.

This support acknowledges both Tempo’s current contributions and the areas that require further refinement.
