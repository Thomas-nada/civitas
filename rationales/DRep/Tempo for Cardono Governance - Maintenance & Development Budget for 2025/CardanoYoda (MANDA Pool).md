I do have a profile on Tempo.Vote and genuinely appreciate the service. I believe in supporting platforms I actively use. I also like the idea of offering a short-term loan of 100K ADA to help users submit governance actions and earn a modest return. Still, there are alternative ways to make this mechanism work.

However, the requested 200K ADA to cover six months of infrastructure costs—roughly 33,333 ADA per month, or around $25,000 USD—feels excessive. Additionally, the proposal allocates 180K ADA to implement three new features, which also seems overvalued. I believe another team could deliver similar results more cost-effectively.

In my view, a comparable proposal could be executed with a budget up to ten times lower.
