I vote YES on this proposal, with some conditions.

Currently, Cardano governance relies on only two active user interfaces: GovTool and Tempo. Depending solely on one tool—whether it's GovTool or Tempo—would undermine resilience and decentralization. Tempo offers a lightweight, mobile-friendly experience, which I personally find intuitive and easy to use. Its mobile accessibility lowers the barrier for broader participation, especially for users who govern on the go.

That said, some parts remain unclear—particularly the fund management structure (delegated to Selfdriven, but without details on whether smart contracts or multisig are used) and the development roadmap (which lacks concrete milestones or deliverables).

I support this proposal based on current utility and intent, but I strongly hope that future Treasury Withdrawal proposals will include clearer financial structures and more transparent development plans.

Supporting this proposal contributes to keeping Cardano governance open, accessible, and resilient.
