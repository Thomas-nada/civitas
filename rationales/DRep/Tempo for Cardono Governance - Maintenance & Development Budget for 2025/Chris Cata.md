I want to support Tempo for another year and see where they go with development.  I think we're still in a crucial time to see these tools get built out and offer different takes on the overall UX. There's the added benefit that the total amount of ADA asked for is to support a fee-based lending pool for submitting future gov actions. The 2026 plan for Tempo is still ambitious at an estimated 400K (per their feedback), however that assumes further development of the portal.  It would be half as much if it was just for maintenance. Some more time will tell us whether there's demand for conintued support as other APIs are developed, additional open-source tooling becomes available, and community feedback on what ultimately users want to see. I have found Tempo useful as a DRep, and am voting in favor of their budget.
