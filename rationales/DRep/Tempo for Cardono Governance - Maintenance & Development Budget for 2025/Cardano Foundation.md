This is a pragmatic decision based on the strategic importance of fostering a competitive and diverse governance tooling landscape. While the proposal has flaws in its quality and detail, we believe the risk of creating a tooling monoculture currently outweighs these concerns.

### **Rationale Statement**

This decision reflects a careful balance between the project's potential strategic value and the valid critiques of the proposal itself. Our support is conditional and comes with clear expectations for future funding requests.

#### **Reasons for a 'YES' Vote**

1. **Strategic Importance of Ecosystem Competition:** The primary driver for our support is the need for healthy competition in the governance tooling sector. tempo.vote provides another option for the ecosystem as a governance interface, allowing users to perform core governance functions like DRep registration and proposal submission. Having multiple options for such tooling supports decentralization, encourages innovation, and can help ensure tooling costs remain reasonable over the long term.  
2. **Demonstrated Value and Community Use:** The tempo platform has demonstrated its value and agility, for instance, by quickly shipping a bulk-voting feature helpful to the community. This responsiveness helps justify the requested ₳380,000 for six months, which, while high, we view as a pragmatic investment to sustain this valuable tooling solution option.  
3. **Supporting Ecosystem Diversity:** This vote serves as an important political signal that Cardano's governance and treasury are accessible to smaller, independent development teams. Fostering a diverse ecosystem of builders beyond the large, established entities is essential for Cardano's long-term health and decentralization.
