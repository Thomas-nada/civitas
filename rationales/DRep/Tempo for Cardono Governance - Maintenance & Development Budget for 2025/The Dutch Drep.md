# The Dutch Drep

**Proposal:** Tempo for Cardono Governance - Maintenance & Development Budget for 2025

We, the Dutch DRep, vote No on this Budget Info Action.

While we appreciate the early contributions of the Tempo.Vote team—especially during a time when governance tooling on Cardano was still nascent—we believe this proposal does not meet the standards now expected for public funding.

The quality of the current tooling remains limited, and there is no commitment to open-source the platform. In a decentralized ecosystem, transparency and composability are critical—especially for infrastructure intended to serve the governance process. Closed-source tools create silos and limit community trust, innovation, and auditability.

Furthermore, the gap Tempo.Vote initially helped fill has now been addressed by other governance tooling efforts. Proposals such as the gov.tool Info Action and the AdaStat/Cexplorer Treasury Withdrawal present more robust, transparent, and collaborative pathways for advancing Cardano governance infrastructure. These initiatives are better aligned with the community’s evolving standards for openness and usability.

We recommend that Tempo.Vote consider submitting a proposal for Project Catalyst, with a focus on improving its open-source approach to better align with community values and expectations.

We believe the Treasury should prioritize open, auditable, and broadly integrated tooling. As such, we cannot support this proposal in its current form.
