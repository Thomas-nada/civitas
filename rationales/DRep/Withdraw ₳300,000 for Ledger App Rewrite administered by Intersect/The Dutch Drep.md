We, The Dutch Drep, vote Yes on this withdrawal governance action for Scalus - DApps Development Platform administered by Intersect. Scala is a popular functional and object-oriented language with proven capabilities, and Scalus offers a comprehensive, practical approach that could lower barriers for developers by enabling them to use Scala across the entire Cardano stack. However, we have questions about the project’s unknown aspects, its adoption, and why developers would choose it over existing alternatives, which warrant further clarification.
