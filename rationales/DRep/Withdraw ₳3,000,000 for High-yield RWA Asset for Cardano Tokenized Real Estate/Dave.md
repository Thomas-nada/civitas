I am voting **NO**, based on the strong principle that our treasury must fund public goods, not the expansion costs of successful, for-profit companies.

The Haus team's impressive track record is the very reason they should self-fund their migration to Cardano, not ask the community to pay for it. This proposal socializes their business risk while privatizing the profits, which is contrary to a fair and **sustainable** ecosystem. We should attract projects on our technical merits, not by giving grants to private ventures.
