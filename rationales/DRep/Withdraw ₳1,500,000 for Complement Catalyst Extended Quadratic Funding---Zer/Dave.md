I strongly support Quadratic Funding as a tool for **decentralization**, but am voting **NO** for now.

My decision is reinforced by the fact that Fund 14 includes a QV proof-of-concept at the *tallying stage*. This is the perfect, low-risk experiment. The most responsible path is to analyze the data from this POC before funding a separate, large-scale system. For the sake of a sustainable, data-driven approach to governance, let's learn from this live test first. I look forward to supporting an evolved proposal based on these valuable findings.
