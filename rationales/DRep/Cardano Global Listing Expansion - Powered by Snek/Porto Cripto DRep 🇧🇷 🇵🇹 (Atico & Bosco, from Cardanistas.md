We chose to vote "NO" on this governance action (gov_action1fl6r784t2ffw7q96du2znhprw90r3xvrfugvqelgqewgxex42kdqq9tgrd5), because although the SNEK project is the main memecoin and the leading project by market cap on the network, it is already listed on some tier 1 exchanges, and the proposal seems generic by not specifying which exchange the funds will be allocated to. That is our suggestion.
