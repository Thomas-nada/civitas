Vote: NO – Snek Global Listing Expansion (5M ADA)

I appreciate the Snek Foundation’s achievements (Kraken, Crypto.com listings, $4M self-funded investment), but I cannot support this treasury withdrawal for the following reasons:
	•	Single-token focus – Funds are concentrated on SNEK, with benefits to the broader ecosystem indirect and uncertain.
	•	High cost vs. ecosystem ROI – 5M ADA is a significant treasury outlay for CEX listings, a marketing strategy that often produces short-lived volume spikes without sustainable adoption.
	•	Centralized exchange dependency – This approach prioritizes CEX access over strengthening decentralized infrastructure, DeFi liquidity, and developer tooling that benefit all projects.
	•	Opportunity cost – These funds could back initiatives that provide shared infrastructure, onboarding pipelines, and reusable tech for every Cardano builder.
	•	Market risk – Memecoin-driven strategies can amplify volatility and reputational risk for Cardano if sentiment turns.

I believe treasury funds should focus on network-level adoption drivers and foundational infrastructure—not single-token promotion—no matter how strong its community or track record.
