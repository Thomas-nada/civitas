Many Cardano dApps rely on Blockfrost for accessing on-chain data. While Blockfrost has served the ecosystem well, its centralized nature creates a potential bottleneck and single point of failure — something that contradicts the long-term vision of Cardano's decentralized infrastructure.
This proposal introduces an open-source, decentralized alternative built on top of Blockfrost. By enabling the community to access on-chain data without relying solely on a centralized service, it strengthens the ecosystem’s resilience and promotes healthy infrastructure pluralism.

While I believe the requested amount is relatively high — especially considering that Blockfrost has received prior investment from IOG and operates on a revenue model — I still vote YES because the strategic value of decentralizing access to on-chain data outweighs the cost concern. The proposal contributes directly to the robustness and sustainability of Cardano’s developer ecosystem.
