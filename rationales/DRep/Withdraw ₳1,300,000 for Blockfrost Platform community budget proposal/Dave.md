I am voting **NO** due to the severe centralization risks this architecture introduces at the transaction submission layer.

entralizing the `/tx/submit` endpoint through a single gateway creates a privileged "pre-mempool." This gives one entity the power to censor transactions, front-run users, and perform MEV attacks. This architecture fundamentally undermines Cardano's core promise of being a **permissionless and censorship-resistant** network. The risk to users and ecosystem integrity is simply too great.

Disappointing proposal.
