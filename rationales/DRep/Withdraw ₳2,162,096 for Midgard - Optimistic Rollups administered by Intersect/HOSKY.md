## Rationale for Voting in Favor of the Midgard - Optimistic Rollups Proposal

### Cardano First: Decentralization and Integrity

HOSKY’s DRep platform prioritizes Cardano’s long-term health over short-term gains. At the core of this philosophy is **uncompromised decentralization**. Midgard matches this commitment:

* **No centralized sequencers**
* **No custodial multisigs**
* **No challenge-response fraud proofs**
* **Open, permissionless fraud proofs anyone can submit**

Midgard reinforces Cardano’s decentralization by using the **Extended UTxO model** to build rollups that cannot exist on other chains. This protects the network’s integrity while enabling scalability.

---

### Scalability Without Sacrificing Security

Cardano needs to scale, but not at the cost of centralization or security. Midgard is consistent with the HOSKY DRep Platform’s position that scaling solutions must **benefit Layer 1**, not siphon activity away from it.

Midgard accomplishes this by:

* Using **ADA as the only transaction fee token**
* Publishing every Midgard block to Cardano Layer 1
* Keeping rollup activity linked to Layer 1, increasing utility and throughput

This ensures scalability that strengthens Cardano’s core chain, not bypasses it.

---

### Responsible Treasury Management

HOSKY’s DRep Platform commits to **fiscal conservatism**. Treasury funds must be spent wisely, with clear oversight and measurable outcomes. The Midgard proposal meets this standard by:

* Structuring payments in **milestone-based tranches**
* Utilizing **third-party assurance** from Sundae Labs, the Cardano Foundation, NMKR, Xerberus, and DQuadrant
* Using **smart contracts for disbursement and auditing**
* Including legal contracts for dispute resolution and delivery accountability

This reduces delivery risk and protects community funds from waste.

---

### Real Adoption, Not Theoretical Solutions

HOSKY prioritizes solving **real problems**, not building tech for its own sake. Midgard has tangible benefits:

* Unlocks new Layer 2 use cases unique to Cardano
* Supports advanced dApps without compromising decentralization
* Demonstrates the power of Cardano’s EUTxO design over account-based models

By focusing on **real utility and deployment**, Midgard helps drive adoption through practical, scalable solutions.

### Conclusion: A Vote Consistent with the HOSKY DRep Platform

HOSKY’s DRep Platform commits to:

* **Decentralization without compromise**
