SIPO’s Voting Reason: Yes Vote for “Midgard - Optimistic Rollups”

SIPO has voted “Yes” on the proposal for the treasury withdrawal of ₳2,162,096 for “Midgard - Optimistic Rollups”.

The primary reason is that this is one of the first Treasury Withdrawals in Cardano’s new governance era. Approving this action signals our collective intention to break through the delays and move the process forward. The budget has already been approved, and now is the time to release the funds and enable execution. By voting Yes, SIPO expresses a clear will to advance the timeline and prevent further stagnation.

On the substance, Midgard represents a significant and uniquely Cardano-native approach to Layer 2 scaling via optimistic rollups. Unlike most existing rollup systems that rely on centralized sequencers or custodial multisigs, Midgard leverages Cardano’s EUTxO architecture to enable a fully permissionless, censorship-resistant, fraud-proof system — a feat not feasible on account-based blockchains like Ethereum or Solana.

While Hydra moves transactions off-chain, Midgard compresses and posts them on-chain, ensuring that economic activity contributes directly to Cardano L1’s fee revenue. This architecture not only preserves but enhances the security and decentralization of Cardano.

In terms of governance integrity, this project follows a clearly defined legal and administrative framework under Intersect, with milestone tracking, third-party assurance, and smart contract-based budget disbursement, ensuring accountability and transparency.

In summary, SIPO supports this proposal both to move the process of execution forward and to endorse a technically sound, Cardano-native scaling solution that aligns with the long-term vision of a robust, decentralized ecosystem.
