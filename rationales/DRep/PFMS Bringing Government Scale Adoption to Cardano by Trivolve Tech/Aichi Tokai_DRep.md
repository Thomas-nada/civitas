I recognize the strategic importance of this proposal and believe it presents a credible path toward meaningful government-scale adoption of Cardano. The team has demonstrated prior delivery, and the public-good value of the project is clear. Under different budget conditions, I would be inclined to support it. However, with the remaining Net Change Limit headroom now very limited, I believe Treasury priorities must be applied more conservatively. My vote should not be interpreted as a rejection of the project's merits. If resubmitted in a future NCL period with greater fiscal capacity, I would be strongly inclined to support it. Therefore, I vote No at this time.
