Trivolve has done the hard part already. FMS is live on mainnet, past 156,000 transactions. PFMS has milestones anyone can check: 100,000 live transactions by month 12 and 390,000 over three months by month 15.Love all that.
My hesitation is about who pays. The Telangana government wants this system, and Trivolve gets maintenance fees from the state under a separate agreement. We'd cover the ₳9.6M build. Those agreements are confidential, and the only outside party that has read them is the Cardano Foundation, which co-wrote the proposal and would also administer the money. ₳1.8M goes out at contract signing, before any work. And the "multi-sig" in the text is a single-key address on-chain.
Good team, awkward deal. Show the state's side of the bargain publicly and sort out custody, and I'm likely a Yes. Until then I'm sitting this one out.
