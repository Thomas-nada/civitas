**Vote: YES**

PFMS is a compelling commercial adoption proposal with an existing Cardano deployment and a concrete government use case. The project aims to secure the chain of evidence across Telangana’s criminal justice system, with the state government covering ongoing transaction fees, infrastructure and maintenance. The existing Forensic Management System reportedly processes approximately 11,000 mainnet transactions monthly, providing evidence of real usage rather than a purely theoretical application.

The requested ₳9.6M funds an open-source system designed for wider reuse, with projected first-year activity exceeding 1.5 million mainnet transactions. The proposal also allocates 70% of paid verification revenue to the Treasury and 20% to core Cardano library maintenance. Milestone-gated payments, Cardano Foundation administration, independent assurance and provisions for returning unspent funds provide meaningful accountability.

The main risks are the scale of the deployment, the 15-month delivery timeline, and the fact that projected transaction volumes and adoption beyond the initial deployment remain unproven. Nevertheless, this proposal directly addresses Cardano’s need for real-world adoption, sustained transaction activity and recurring revenue. I support funding, with success measured by verified production deployment, actual transaction volume, government-funded usage and realized Treasury revenue rather than projections alone.
