Vote: NO

Governance Action: PFMS: Bringing Government Scale Adoption to Cardano by Trivolve Tech

Treasury Withdrawal Requested: ₳9,600,000

I recognize the work Trivolve Tech has undertaken and acknowledge the potential value of bringing government-scale applications to Cardano. However, after reviewing this proposal, I cannot support the requested Treasury withdrawal.

My objection is not to the technology, its intended purpose, or government adoption of Cardano. It concerns the responsibility for financing its development.

The proposed Police Force Management System is intended for deployment within an existing governmental framework, serving the operational requirements of law enforcement and judicial institutions. The government stands to receive substantial direct benefits from its implementation. It is therefore reasonable to expect the governmental agency commissioning and utilizing the system to assume primary responsibility for its development costs.

Cardano is a permissionless blockchain. Its infrastructure is available to governments, businesses, and individuals without requiring financial assistance from the Cardano Treasury. The technological rails already exist, and adoption should be driven by genuine institutional demand and a willingness to invest in that technology.

I acknowledge the proposal's commitment to open-source development, anticipated transaction activity, and potential revenue sharing. However, these considerations do not, in my judgment, sufficiently justify an expenditure of 9.6 million ADA.

Open-sourcing portions of the technology does not automatically establish that the broader Cardano ecosystem will derive benefits proportionate to the requested funding. Nor should projected transaction fees or verification revenue substitute for a compelling demonstration of financial sustainability and measurable returns to the ecosystem.

There is also an important distinction between developing foundational infrastructure that benefits the entire network and financing an application whose primary deployment serves the operational requirements of a particular government.

The Cardano Treasury should not become an alternative procurement budget for governments or private enterprises seeking to develop systems for their own use.

If the government recognizes the value of this technology, it should be prepared to finance its implementation directly, potentially in partnership with private investors and technology providers.

I support meaningful adoption, innovation, and responsible development on Cardano. But those objectives must be balanced against our obligation to preserve Treasury resources and prioritize investments that demonstrate broad, sustainable benefits to the ecosystem.

My responsibility as a DRep is to Cardano and those I represent. That responsibility requires financial discipline, careful scrutiny, and the willingness to distinguish worthwhile technology from justified Treasury expenditure.

For these reasons, I vote NO.
