I vote NO on "PFMS: Bringing Government Scale Adoption to Cardano by Trivolve Tech". A police evidence system could strengthen Cardano's credibility and generate useful activity, but this 9.6M ADA request does not demonstrate enough ecosystem-wide value to justify the Treasury funding the full build for a state government. I prefer the team and beneficiary government to fund a smaller pilot first, prove actual usage and costs, and establish an enforceable revenue-sharing arrangement with the Treasury before seeking larger support. A smaller pilot through a competitive programme such as Catalyst would be more proportionate than this direct withdrawal, particularly under budget pressure and at a low ADA price. I remain open to future assistance supported by independently verified results and clearer public benefit; the unresolved funding and adoption risks are too substantial for me to approve this request.
