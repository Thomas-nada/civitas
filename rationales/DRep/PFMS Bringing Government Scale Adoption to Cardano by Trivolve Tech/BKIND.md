What I support. Real adoption on Cardano has value. This team points to a prior deployment on Cardano, its FMS, whose transaction record can be checked on the public leaderboard. The action is milestone gated with an independent assurer, names the Cardano Foundation as administrator, caps spending at a fixed rate with the excess returned, and routes most of any future verification revenue to the treasury. My No is not to the project. It is to the treasury carrying a vendor's commercial build.

A build for a paying client. The proposal states that "the state government has committed to paying for the Cardano transaction fees resulting from PFMS usage and operating the off-chain deployment infrastructure", and that "Trivolve Tech and its subcontractors will receive maintenance fees from the state government under a separate agreement". The client exists, has committed to pay for operation, and holds a maintenance contract with the vendor. Yet the one time build is placed on the treasury. A company normally builds a product at its own cost and carries the risk. Here the build and the risk move to ada holders while the client relationship and the maintenance revenue stay with the vendor.

A cost basis that is not shown. The budget is given only as work package shares of the total. It names no person months and no rates. The work is built in India, a low cost engineering geography, yet the request is tied to no rate and no quantity of work that would let anyone place it against that cost. The ask is "9,600,000 ADA over 15 months", about 2.2 million dollars at the planning rate and capped near 2.9 million. Ada holders are asked to approve a figure they cannot check against the work it pays for.

No downside risk for the outcome. The schedule releases 1,800,000 ada at kickoff and 2,000,000 ada at each of the next three milestones, 7,800,000 ada or more than 80 percent, before the final 1,800,000 ada at month 15, the only milestone tied to use, "Sustained Usage". Milestone gating protects against failure to deliver and against fraud. It does not protect against a system that is built and not used. If use does not follow, the treasury carries the loss and the builder does not.

A custody plan that does not match the credential. The account named to receive and hold the funds is controlled by a single key at the protocol level. Whether the administrator moves the funds to a multi signature address after withdrawal cannot be read from the action as submitted.

A justification that cannot be verified. The agreements with the state are not public.
