Trivolve Tech has demonstrated its ability to deliver real-world applications on Cardano through its existing Forensic Management System. This proposal builds on that foundation and presents an opportunity to expand institutional adoption.

While the ₳9.6 million request is substantial, and there are legitimate questions about whether the Telangana state government should fund development itself, the benefits extend beyond a single deployment. The projected 1.5 million annual transactions would generate sustained network activity, while transaction fees and 70% of paid verification revenue would provide ongoing economic benefits to Cardano. If successful, these revenue streams could recover the treasury’s initial investment over a number of years. The open-source infrastructure also creates opportunities for wider adoption.

Cardano needs to attract and retain real-world users, and I believe the value of demonstrating what's possible with Cardano as a secure, reliable foundation for critical infrastructure should not be underestimated. A successful government deployment at this scale could demonstrate its capabilities to other institutions and encourage adoption beyond this project. Although future revenue and expansion are not guaranteed, I believe the combination of proven delivery, recurring economic activity, and long-term potential justifies the investment.
