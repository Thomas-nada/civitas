I am voting YES on PFMS.

My decision is grounded in documented prior delivery. Project Catalyst records the FMS project as completed, and the Cardano Foundation reports its operational use with police in Uttar Pradesh. These provide relevant evidence of the team’s ability to deliver and a foundation for the proposed expansion.

PFMS proposes an open source core and staged funding. Following an initial advance, subsequent payments would depend on milestone verification, with later milestones requiring live mainnet activity and sustained usage. I see value in developing reusable infrastructure that could support broader institutional adoption of Cardano.

The 9.6 million ADA request carries significant financial and execution risks. Projected transaction volumes, verification revenue and adoption by additional jurisdictions remain outcomes to be demonstrated. On balance, I consider the existing delivery record, the proposed milestone controls and the potential benefit to Cardano sufficient grounds to support this proposal.
