---

I believe we should completely flip the current **Cardano Governance model** — from today's developer-driven approach:

> Hi DReps! I'm a developer. Here's my proposal. Please review it quickly.

to a **DRep-driven** one:

> Hi developers! We're the DReps, and here's what we need.

Instead of starting with "Here's the NCL — now let's find projects to fill the budget," we should begin with **strategy**:

#### 1. Define clear objectives

First, define clear, time-bound objectives for the next budget window (6 or 12 months). A Strategy Committee or Intersect working groups could draft these initial goals and submit them for DRep ratification before any detailed costing begins.

Examples might include:

- Increase DeFi TVL by X% with Y% organic retention
- Reach Z key infrastructure milestones (Mithril upgrades, scalability releases, wallet standards, etc.)
- Deliver specific adoption outcomes (enterprise pilots, real-world payment volumes, etc.)

#### 2. Turn objectives into deliverables and budgets

Then break each objective down into concrete deliverables, define timelines and success metrics, and estimate the costs realistically.

This would give us clear budgets for each category, for example:

- Core Infrastructure & Security: ₳11M
- DeFi Growth & Liquidity: ₳22M
- Tooling & Developer Experience: ₳33M
- Adoption & Real-World Use: ₳44M
- Governance & Coordination: ₳55M
- Strategic Reserve / Carryover: ₳66M

#### 3. Approve the budget and set the NCL

After review by the Cardano community and DReps, we would approve the overall budget. The NCL would then become a straightforward technical enforcement tool — automatically set to the approved budget plus a 10–15% buffer for emergencies or unforeseen needs.

#### 4. Additional improvements

- Rolling 6-month windows with mid-term reviews and the ability to reallocate funds
- Mandatory post-spend reporting tied to KPIs and continued funding
- Clear carryover rules so unused funds aren't lost and there is no incentive to spend just to use up the budget
- Different guidelines or percentages for bull and bear markets

#### Framework first. Outcomes first.

I really think the Cardano community must develop a more coherent approach — one that combines clear strategic direction, stronger execution capacity, and effective accountability mechanisms.

The current "set the NCL first, then fill the pot" approach was understandable when the system was new, but it's exactly why we keep running into these capacity crises. Moving to strategy-led budgeting would make discussions about increasing treasury capacity far less controversial — because spending would be justified by real outcomes, not just available headroom.

Given these fundamental concerns, I, as a DRep, do not wish to continue lending legitimacy to a treasury process I consider structurally flawed. Until the treasury budgeting process undergoes fundamental reform, I will vote **Abstain** on all **Treasury Withdrawal** proposals.

This decision is not an abdication of responsibility, but a deliberate exercise of it — I will continue to vote actively on all other governance matters, including protocol parameters and constitutional issues.
