I am voting No because the proposal has not sufficiently demonstrated why ₳9.6 million from the Cardano treasury is needed to solve a problem already addressed by existing evidence-management systems.

Cardano could provide an independent record for detecting changes after evidence is recorded, but it cannot establish whether the original evidence was truthful or properly collected. The proposal needs a clearer comparison with existing alternatives and measurable benefits that justify the cost.

I support practical adoption, but projected transaction counts alone are not enough. Treasury funding should deliver clear, proportionate value to the broader Cardano ecosystem.
