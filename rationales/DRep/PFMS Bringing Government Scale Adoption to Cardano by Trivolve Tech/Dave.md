I really like the idea of this proposal, and Trivolve Tech's work integrating Cardano into the Indian justice system is a compelling enterprise use case. However, I am voting NO because a single ask of 9.6 million ADA is simply too significant. With the ADA price currently so low, our treasury simply cannot sustain multi-million ADA grants to subsidize enterprise software for a sovereign state.

Instead of relying on a massive treasury withdrawal, I strongly suggest the team explore ecosystem bootstrapping initiatives like PRIME. In fact, this proposal's rigorous focus on projected transaction metrics, revenue return models, and strategic alignment is exactly what those targeted funding initiatives look for in a strong candidate.
