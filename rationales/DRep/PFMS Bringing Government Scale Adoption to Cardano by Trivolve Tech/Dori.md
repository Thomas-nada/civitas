I'm voting No on the PFMS proposal.

First, I recognize Trivolve Tech's track record. FMS is already in steady use on mainnet, and the structural safeguards are solid, with the Cardano Foundation acting as administrator.

But a fundamental question remains. Why should the Cardano Treasury pay to develop a police system for a single state government?

The beneficiary of this system is the state government. When a government needs a system for its own operations, it normally commissions it out of its own procurement budget. Here, the Treasury pays for development, while Trivolve Tech signs a separate agreement with the state and earns maintenance revenue. In effect, the Treasury is covering a company's cost of winning a government contract.

If this passes, any developer that lands a government or enterprise client could ask the Treasury to fund that development. Institutional adoption matters, but the cost should be carried by the institution using the system and the company profiting from it. 

With little room left under the withdrawal limit, I believe Treasury funds should go where they benefit the Cardano ecosystem as a whole, so I'm voting No.
