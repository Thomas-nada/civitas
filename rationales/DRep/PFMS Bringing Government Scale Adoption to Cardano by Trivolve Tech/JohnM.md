# JohnM

**Proposal:** PFMS Bringing Government Scale Adoption to Cardano by Trivolve Tech
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2hky8fgx58luuuz5at5dr4xw0jzlre87wwyw3agra9f8js5eu9hp`

---

Trivolve has put working systems on Cardano mainnet. FMS has been live since October 2025 and is in the top ten by transaction count, and its Catalyst grant is closed out. The design is sensible too. Only hashes go on chain, funds after the kickoff are tied to evidence a third party can check, and the core is to be released under Apache 2.0.

My No rests on the government commitment, which nobody outside the proposal can check. The case for 9.6M ada is that a state government is commissioning PFMS for daily use and will pay the fees, infrastructure and maintenance afterwards. The proposal says those agreements are confidential and have been seen by the Cardano Foundation, which is also a co-author and the paid administrator. I found no public statement from Telangana's police or government about this system. FMS was also built for Uttar Pradesh, not Telangana. That may all be fine, but the track record and the new customer are not the same thing, and I am being asked to take the larger part on trust.

Then there is the money up front. 1,800,000 ada, close to a fifth of the request, goes out on contract signing, before the assurer is procured and before any deliverable exists. The budget is set out by line item, but there are no roles, headcount or rates behind the 2,688,000 ada for core infrastructure or any other line.

Some of the numbers run ahead of the evidence. The proposal says PFMS would add 18.2% to Cardano's monthly transactions. On the leaderboard's own figures, 127,500 a month would be nearer 5%. The 30% cut in case resolution times has no source I could find. None of this is disqualifying, but it is a pattern I would want fixed before a request this size.

There is also an obvious conflict in how this is set up. The Cardano Foundation co-authored the proposal and would then be paid 200,000 ada from the treasury to administer it. The same party should not be both sponsor and supervisor of a withdrawal, and overseeing adoption work like this is close to what the Foundation already exists to do. If it believes in PFMS, it can co-author it or administer it, but not both, and not for a fee.
