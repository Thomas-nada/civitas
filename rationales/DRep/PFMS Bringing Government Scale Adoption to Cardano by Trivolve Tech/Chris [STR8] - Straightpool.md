# Chris [STR8] - Straightpool

**Proposal:** PFMS Bringing Government Scale Adoption to Cardano by Trivolve Tech
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1y27qjjnz9a7q6zmvpajzqpqylnka6lpkvcm9sgjrdheqt0qq6pchw`

---

I vote YES because I see significant strategic value in bringing real-world government adoption to Cardano, particularly through a use case as meaningful as securing the chain of evidence in criminal justice systems.

Unlike many adoption proposals, PFMS builds on an existing production deployment with verifiable on-chain activity and a team with demonstrated delivery experience. I also had the opportunity to see the team's work presented at TUM blockchain conference 2026, which reinforced my confidence in the project's potential.

The ambition to scale this into a reusable, open-source solution for broader institutional adoption is particularly compelling. I appreciate the government's commitment to covering ongoing infrastructure, maintenance, and transaction fees. I also noticed Trivolve's [Cardano–Midnight ZK bridge exploration](https://github.com/Trivolve-Tech/cardano-midnight-zk-bridge), demonstrating an interest in privacy-preserving technologies that could become valuable for sensitive criminal justice applications.

The Cardano Foundation's administration, independent milestone verification, and expenditure safeguards provide meaningful oversight, although they cannot eliminate the financial and adoption risks.

I share several concerns raised by other DReps, particularly regarding the substantial ₳9.6 million request, limited budget transparency, historical adoption forecasts, and the absence of government co-funding for development. I encourage the team to address these concerns through more transparent cost calculations, clearer separation of reusable infrastructure and government-specific development, independently verifiable adoption metrics, and meaningful financial participation from the beneficiary government.

I recognize that direct Treasury fee revenue alone is unlikely to recover the investment within a reasonable timeframe. However, I do not believe Treasury investments in strategic adoption should be evaluated solely on direct financial returns. Establishing Cardano as trusted infrastructure within real government operations can create long-term ecosystem value beyond transaction fees, although these broader benefits must ultimately be demonstrated.

Cardano needs meaningful real-world adoption, and I believe promising initiatives with demonstrated technical progress deserve consideration even when their outcomes remain uncertain.

Despite my reservations, I am willing to support this initiative based on its strategic potential, existing delivery record, and oversight structure.

**I therefore vote YES.**
