# Cardanians

**Proposal:** PFMS Bringing Government Scale Adoption to Cardano by Trivolve Tech

Cardanians votes ABSTAIN, while leaning towards YES.

Trivolve’s existing government deployment on Cardano provides a credible foundation for PFMS. We see value in expanding real institutional adoption and delivering reusable open-source infrastructure, supported by independent milestone verification and Cardano Foundation administration.

However, a ₳9.6M commitment requires stronger cost justification, reconciliation of previous adoption forecasts, clearer government commitments and enforceable revenue-sharing terms. Success should demonstrate meaningful institutional usage and evidence coverage alongside transaction counts.

We see a proposal worth supporting, but these gaps need to be addressed before we can confidently vote YES.

If the proposal does not pass in its current form, we would welcome Trivolve to consult with the DRep Council to address outstanding concerns, strengthen the proposal and improve its prospects of gaining support upon resubmission.
