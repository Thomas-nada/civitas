# ADAtainment

**Proposal:** PFMS Bringing Government Scale Adoption to Cardano by Trivolve Tech
**Vote:** Yes

---

Trivolve’s track record is visible on mainnet. On the [cardano.org transaction leaderboard](https://cardano.org/apps/leaderboard), FMS by Trivolve ranks #10 with 19,643 transactions over the last 30 days, and Indianchain by Trivolve ranks #11 with 13,694 (Sep 16 to Oct 16, 2026). Both are government deployments in daily use. The leaderboard counts only transactions it can attribute on-chain, so anyone can verify these numbers with a chain indexer.

PFMS builds on FMS, which already covers one of the five stages and has run for over a year on the state’s own infrastructure. The team is extending a working system for a customer it already serves.

4 of the 5 payments are made only after an independent assurer has checked the milestone. Spending is capped at $0.30 per ada, and anything above that goes back to the treasury. After launch the state pays transaction fees and operations.

I have two reservations. The 1.8M ada kickoff tranche is large, and the 1.5M transactions a year are a projection. (will compare the milestone reports with the leaderboard numbers, and I expect the open source core to be published as promised)
