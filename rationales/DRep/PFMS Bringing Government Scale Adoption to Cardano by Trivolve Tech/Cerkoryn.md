I’m voting No because I don’t think the projected return to the treasury justifies the 9.6 million ADA request. At their projected 1.53 million transactions per year, using the previous FMS project’s observed average fee of approximately 0.227 ADA and the treasury’s current 20% share, the treasury would receive roughly 69,400 ADA annually. That would take about 138 years to recover the requested amount through transaction fees alone. Repeating FMS’s actual first-year volume of 134,924 transactions would extend that to roughly 1,569 years.

The commitment to return 70% of paid-verification revenue could improve those figures, but the proposal does not forecast paid-request volume or average pricing, so I cannot substantiate a shorter overall payback period. I would encourage the team to return with a smaller, staged funding request supported by realistic adoption and revenue projections, demonstrating how the treasury could recover its contribution within a reasonable timeframe.
