Key Factors Supporting This Vote

The projected ~1.53 million mainnet transactions annually (an estimated ~18% increase over current baseline network activity) and the registration of ~5.1 million DIDs demonstrate immediate enterprise utility while preserving data privacy by strictly anchoring zero-knowledge cryptographic proofs off-chain.

Sustainable Economic Design:
The financial terms protect the Cardano Treasury while generating long-term protocol value:

The state government covers all ongoing Cardano transaction commit fees and off-chain operational costs indefinitely.

The project introduces a direct value-return loop: 70% of fast-tracked verification service revenues flow back to the Treasury, and 20% is routed to open-source Cardano core library maintenance.

A hard expenditure cap of USD $0.30 per ADA guarantees that any surplus value from ADA appreciation is returned to the Treasury.

Robust Governance, Custody, and Oversight:
The proposal adheres strictly to constitutional guardrails. Funds are held in a multi-sig custody wallet by the Cardano Foundation (delegated to Auto-Abstain to avoid vote skewing) and are disbursed strictly across five milestone-gated tranches. Each disbursement requires verification by an independent third-party auditor and the Foundation, significantly de-risking Treasury exposure against non-delivery.

Public Good & Open Source Multiplier:
By developing PFMS as an open-source chain-of-evidence layer, the Cardano ecosystem gains a reusable institutional framework. This allows other states and international jurisdictions to onboard onto Cardano without incurring redundant initial build costs.

Funding production-ready enterprise infrastructure executed by a battle-tested team delivers on the Cardano 2030 strategic objectives of adoption, provenance, and sustainable protocol revenue. This proposal sets a high benchmark for future Treasury actions.
