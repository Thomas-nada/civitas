Trivolve Tech proposes an open-source evidence layer that anchors police records on Cardano mainnet, beginning with the Indian state of Telangana.  
Our decision is driven by the following factors:

* **Team track record** Trivolve Tech's FMS has processed 161,070 transactions at its script address since September 2025, and its Plutus validator ran on 19,513 transactions in the last 30 days. FMS and IndianChain both rank among the leading applications on the \[cardano.org transaction leaderboard\](https\://cardano.org/apps/leaderboard/).  

* **The treasury funds a reusable build, the state funds its operation.** After the initial treasury-funded deployment, the Telangana state will cover transaction fees, infrastructure and maintenance after launch, and the core ships under Apache 2.0. The proposal describes PFMS as an evidence layer for justice systems globally, which makes other governments potential adopters. Importantly, this withdrawal provides that a percentage of future revenue for certain applications will flow back to the treasury.   

* **Transaction potential and long-term opportunities.** Telangana state alone is projected to generate 127,500 transactions per month, roughly 18% of current mainnet volume. The proposal describes this forecast as conservative: it is built from the state’s own first-year record volumes and counts only the sources and record types live at launch, so volume in Telangana should grow as further districts and record types are onboarded.  India’s other 35 states and union territories could adopt the same open-source reference implementation, and every additional adopter would bring its own volume to Cardano. Long-term sustainability depends on several sectors each generating meaningful volume, and public-sector record-keeping produces steady daily activity that does not follow market cycles. We see PFMS as an opportunity to establish Cardano as critical infrastructure for this and similar public-sector uses.  

* **Funding follows verified delivery.** Four of five tranches, totaling 7,800,000 ada, are released only after the independent assurer and the administrator verify the deliverables, with reports matched to tagged releases and onchain data. Expenditure is capped at USD 0.30 per ada, and any amount above the cap and any unspent funds return to the treasury. The ask is roughly a third of the €6.83 million spent on the EU's research-grade LOCARD chain-of-custody platform, and about 11% below plan at spot. We therefore view the budget as tight rather than inflated.

To ensure transparency, we note that the Cardano Foundation is a co-author of this proposal and its named administrator, and receives the 200,000 ada Administration budget line to cover its administration expenses and taxes.
