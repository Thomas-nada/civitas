# Wolfoden

**Proposal:** PFMS Bringing Government Scale Adoption to Cardano by Trivolve Tech
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y293f6495fvld5zqv0uqsjpjt25zzdkjglys7ykc678jtkghet05n`

---

FMS has demonstrated real mainnet usage. Its original proposal anticipated roughly one million transactions annually without setting a deadline. Trivolve later described that figure as maximum capacity. The new PFMS milestone of 390,000 transactions over three months is consistent with the projected 1.53 million per year. Before approving 9.6 million ADA, I want to know who will verify usage after the final payment and what happens if it falls short. 

An open-source core can be reused, but each jurisdiction still has to assess its evidence rules and integrate the software into police workflows. Trivolve cited interest from German police. As a Bavarian resident, I would count Bavaria as a prospective adopter only if a named authority confirmed substantive discussions. An unanswered email, on its own, would establish only that outreach was attempted. 

I would also ask Trivolve to explore German development-cooperation funding and co-funding from the authorities that would use PFMS. Before asking the Cardano Treasury to finance the full build, the team should explain which public funding options it explored and why the requested share should fall to Cardano.
