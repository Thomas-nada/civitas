I am voting YES on the IO: Cardano Upgrades proposal.
This proposal introduces ideas that align perfectly with Midnight and future Layer 2 solutions. By enabling more predictable fees through Babel Fees and the Multi-Asset Treasury, it makes financial planning and decision-making much easier for users, DApps, and the Treasury itself.
Importantly, it directly addresses one of Cardano’s biggest current weaknesses: limited stablecoin adoption and usability. Babel Fees allows users to pay transaction fees in any native asset (such as USDC or bridged BTC) without needing to acquire ADA first. This removes a major onboarding barrier and opens the door to the massive stablecoin market, bringing new liquidity and real activity to the network.
These upgrades are essential for accelerating mainstream adoption and are fully aligned with Cardano 2030’s goals for Adoption & Utility (Pillar 2) and Ecosystem Sustainability & Resilience (Pillar 5).
