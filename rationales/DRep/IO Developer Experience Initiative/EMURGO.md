Summary
EMURGO as a DRep votes ABSTAIN on the treasury withdrawal titled "IO: Developer Experience Initiative", with rationale outlined below.
Rationale
Improving developer experience is one of the most direct levers available to grow the Cardano ecosystem, and this proposal correctly identifies fragmented tooling and poor onboarding as primary barriers to builder adoption. The analysis grounding the proposal, including survey data from Cardano developers and a comparison of developer growth rates across ecosystems, gives it a stronger empirical foundation than is typical for initiatives of this kind.


The concern is not with the goal but with certain structural elements of the delivery. Several of the key outcomes, including adoption of the Developer Hub as the canonical entry point and integration of cardano-init across the ecosystem, depend on third-party decisions and community coordination that fall outside IO's direct control. The 30% developer growth target, while meaningful as a directional commitment, is difficult to attribute cleanly to this initiative alone given the number of parallel efforts underway.


EMURGO would find it easier to support this proposal if the success metrics were more tightly scoped to deliverables within IO's direct control, and if the coordination approach with existing community efforts were more explicitly defined. The direction is right, and we would encourage a resubmission that addresses these structural questions.
