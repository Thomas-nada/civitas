# Terminada

**Proposal:** Cardano Budget Process Framework (facilitated by Intersect)
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1ytf9f78k7rkwk4cu6l2nvna22keu9yus4kqzc0hqspd36uszu0xc2`

---

This framework would position intersect as controller of the treasury pipeline, undermining DRep authority. Concerns: 1. Intersect role in proposal sequencing and bundling concentrates power. 2. Bundling forces DReps to approve unwanted initiatives to support prefered ones. 3. Net Change Limit creates artificial scarcity. Recommendations: 1. Eliminate proposal bundling. 2. Abolish NCL. 3. Restrict Intersect role to administration with no authority over proposal prioritisation or sequencing.
