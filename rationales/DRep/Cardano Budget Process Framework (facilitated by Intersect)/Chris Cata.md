I support this as a useful coordination, transparency, and planning framework for the 2026 budget process. My support should not be read as treating this action as a constitutionally operative budget approval or treasury-spending authorization or process. Under the Constitution update effective January 24, 2026, Budget Info Actions may remain on-chain as records of sentiment and guidance, but Treasury Withdrawals must stand on their own and satisfy the constitutional requirements directly. So I really view this action as a helpful reference point rather than a constitutional prerequisite for future Treasury Withdrawal Governance Actions.
