# Erasmus

**Proposal:** Cardano Budget Process Framework (facilitated by Intersect)
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y23tfs3c25kfmyqzy5d3364g4smp59esdmzxynqd5ndjjgq5dx7c7`

---

I am voting NO because this framework creates an exclusionary 'pay-to-play' system. The 1,000 ADA entry fee and 100,000 ADA minimum request size protect the interests of large organizations while shutting out the grassroots developers who are the heartbeat of Cardano. Furthermore, I oppose the use of off-chain filters to decide on-chain outcomes. Governance should remain as direct and permissionless as possible, without institutional gatekeepers.
