Over the years, the Cardano ecosystem has seen several proposals targeting supply chains in different areas. However, none of these projects has produced visible results to date.
From our perspective, it seems questionable whether a profitable market for solutions in this sector even exists. Not everything has to be put on a blockchain.
That being said, this proposal includes excessive upfront payments, a high execution risk with ambitious scaling targets, questionable commercial claims and only indirect ecosystem contributions as well as structures that do not yet exist.
As a result, AtlasHub does not support this proposal.
