I fully support the integrations and the strategic direction behind this proposal. USDCx, Pyth, LayerZero, Dune, and Fireblocks are all valuable additions to the Cardano ecosystem.

However, before approving additional funding, I would like to see either the full delivery of CCI V1 or a clearer accounting of the V1 budget, including committed, spent, and remaining funds. Without that visibility, it is difficult for me to properly evaluate the necessity and timing of CCI V2.

For now, I will vote Abstain.
