I am abstaining on this proposal for the following reasons:
The three components — data availability infrastructure, Hydra production hardening, and Midgard rollup development — are relevant to Cardano's scalability. I am not voting No because their development is necessary for the ecosystem.
However, I cannot vote Yes under the current conditions:
On Midgard: This proposal requests an additional ₳10,425,871 while Midgard previously received ₳2,162,096 via treasury withdrawal and ₳500,000 via Catalyst, with milestones still incomplete. Approving additional funding without clear evidence of delivery on prior commitments weakens governance accountability.
On urgency: The proposal frames L2s as an immediate necessity, but the current production adopters — Delta DeFi and Masumi — are already building. The L1 improvements referenced within the proposal itself (Leios, Peras) are not expected until 2027. The ecosystem can sustain a resubmission of this proposal with better structure and accountability without collapsing.
On bundling: The proposal combines early-stage research, ongoing infrastructure development, and previously funded but incomplete work into a single vote, making independent evaluation of each component difficult.
I am willing to vote Yes on a future proposal that demonstrates delivery of prior milestones and presents each component with clear accountability and defined deliverables.
