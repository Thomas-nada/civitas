I’m voting NO on this. Here is my rationale:

On its surface, this proposal sounds appealing as it will lead to an increase in staking rewards. Unfortunately, I also view this as a shortsighted reach for immediate enrichment that shortchanges the long-term goal of building out an attractive ecosystem that “earns” increased staking rewards via increased liquidity and use. 

The timing of this governance action is also inauspicious: the budget has not been ratified yet and therefore there is no compelling data to determine if taking this action is actually warranted and that it won’t have a detrimental effect on Cardano’s ability to continue to grow and develop.

Lastly, proposing to halve the tax is more than ambitious. It would have been more appealing to propose a smaller reduction, for example, 3% or even 5% vs 10%.
