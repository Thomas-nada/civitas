# Kit Willow

**Proposal:** Decrease Treasury Tax from 20% to 10%
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1ygk7qax0ztrv7a7unzgs4apj5mhk5a0058ne3fgfftj4tmgvuk5a7`

---

I voted NO because reducing the tax significantly impacts the replenishment of the treasury negatively.


While it is true that deserving and very hardworking SPOs will earn more, however, I believe the Cardano Blockchain ecosystem right now needs sufficient replenishment to the treasury to be able to make the necessary investment needed for growth and advancement. At this point, I believe reducing treasury tax by half will significantly inhibit that, thus my rationale for voting NO. 

It’s possible in the future that we may be in a better position to implement this ti the ease of every ADA holder, SPO and community member.
