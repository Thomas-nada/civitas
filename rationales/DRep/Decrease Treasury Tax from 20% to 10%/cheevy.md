# cheevy

**Proposal:** Decrease Treasury Tax from 20% to 10%
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1y25rpurh73warkm4mggq9hzqm9x48x7j4zwhpyu72tcufhcx6e4r2`

---

I believe that, if you do the maths, the "budget" would be less than the amount of tax allocated to fund the treasury for 2025. Regardless, reducing taxes is appealing and could encourage increased (and new) activity on the network. With the declining staking rewards, a lower tax would incentivise newcomers to take advantage of staking on Cardano and secure those who already participate. Since the 20% rate was arbitrarily set, I propose reducing it and observing the outcomes. This rate can be adjusted in the future if necessary
