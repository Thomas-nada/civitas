---

We are against the cut, because the proposed change is sudden and drastic. If the community deems the cuts necessary, it is preferable that they be small and gradual - if more intense changes are to be made - so that the market can appreciate the network, finding the greatest possible predictability.

Although lower treasury rates promote - as alleged - higher dividends, they also promote a greater volume of currency in circulation - which promotes depreciation of the asset. Therefore, since we do not find any technical or operational benefits, we see the proposal as purely speculative.
