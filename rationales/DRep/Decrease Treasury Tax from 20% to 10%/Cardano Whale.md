# Cardano Whale

**Proposal:** Decrease Treasury Tax from 20% to 10%
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1yfnuetvunwfl83qaq4e7vlg5w8dwwjmtt4j6tr9fegqvgmgtq5scg`

---

1. Taxes have originally been set on the high side, I’d like to see them lowered, and the recent Intersect budget proposal has made this all the more pertinent - if there’s a tendency to want to spend too much, one thing you can do is reduce the ability to spend.

2. I’ve always seen the treasury as a sort of tax on not being economically sustainable from fees just yet. If at some point in the future Cardano staking rewards are too low to pay for network security, we can tap into the treasury as a sort of delayed emissions mechanism (ignore for a moment how that would technically happen).

3. The treasury is effectively a temporary burn mechanism. Reducing the tax now will de facto increase emissions (reduce the burn rate), thereby we would be doing the opposite of what keeps coins like BNB and Tron afloat at a potential bull market inflection point. Risky business.

So while 1 is a reason I might at some point (after a year or so of seeing how treasury actions fare) want to campaign aggressively for this same thing, for now I want to give us all the benefit of the doubt. 2+3 are reasons to vote against this proposal, for now.
