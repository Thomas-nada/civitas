# Nicolas Cerny

**Proposal:** Decrease Treasury Tax from 20% to 10%
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1yttcav7gh3xlkqd876gmgma32c7qj555ajnn3fnp9kl6l4g97vcyz`

---

As a Delegate Representative (DRep), I am open to supporting adjustments to economic parameters, such as reducing the treasury growth rate or even the monetary expansion rate, provided they are grounded in rigorous, evidence-based analysis. However, I must vote no on this governance action (gov_action1js2s9v92zpxg2rge0y3jt9zy626he2m67x9kx9phw4r942kvsn6sqfym0d7) to decrease the treasury growth rate from 20% to 10% due to significant deficiencies in its supporting data and economic modeling.

### Insufficient Evidence and Analysis

The proposal relies on optimistic assumptions, specifically, significant ada price appreciation and a substantial increase in Annual Percentage Yield (APY), without providing robust evidence to support these claims. It lacks:

- Detailed multi-year treasury projections to assess the long-term sustainability of the treasury under the proposed change.
- Comprehensive simulations covering bullish, bearish, and stagnant market conditions, which are essential to evaluate whether reduced inflows could deplete the treasury or hinder critical functions.

Without this data, DReps, like myself, cannot confidently determine if the treasury, the Cardano ecosystem’s safety net, will remain adequately funded for ongoing maintenance and future development, especially during bearish markets when resources are most vital.

### Constitutional and Risk Management Gaps

The Cardano constitution mandates a thorough technical review (Article III, Section 5) and a reversion strategy (Appendix I, Section 2.6) for all parameter change governance actions. This proposal lacks the former and entirely omits the latter. These omissions prevent us from:

- Assessing potential adverse effects on network stability or economic incentives.
- Preparing for unintended consequences if the change proves detrimental, especially regarding the long-term sustainability of the Cardano Blockchain.

Approving this proposal without these constitutionally required components risks setting a dangerous precedent, lowering the bar for evidence and due diligence in future governance actions.

### Recommendations for Improvement

I urge the proposer to strengthen their case by including:

- Detailed Financial Models: Projections of treasury balances over time, expected funding for development projects, and potential shifts in staking rewards.
- Staking Simulations and Inflation Assessments: Data-driven insights into ecosystem-wide effects.
- Clear Risk Mitigation Measures: Including a reversion plan to safeguard against failure.

Additionally, I strongly recommend off-chain socialization. Engaging the community via platforms like X, the Cardano Forum, or the Cardano Sub-Reddit is a best practice that can refine proposals through diverse feedback, significantly enhancing their quality before submission. While Cardano’s permissionless system allows ada holders to submit governance actions with a 100,000 ada deposit, this step can elevate the proposal’s robustness.

I would also encourage the proposers to review EarnCoinPool’s Parameter Change Proposal (PCP) for the treasury growth rate parameter, available on the Cardano Forum (PCP_TreasuryTax-tau-parameter_EarnCoinPool).

### A Balanced View

I recognize that decreasing the treasury growth rate could offer benefits, such as reducing the tax on rewards, which would increase staking rewards for stake pool operators and delegators, potentially incentivizing greater participation in the network. However, without comprehensive data to weigh these advantages against the risks, particularly the threat to our safety net in adverse conditions, I cannot support the change.

### Conclusion

For these reasons, insufficient evidence, constitutional noncompliance, and the absence of risk planning, I vote no on this governance action. I encourage the proposer to resubmit with the necessary analysis and community input to enable a more informed decision.
