# Daniel_Sullivan_(pyraic3)

**Proposal:** Decrease Treasury Tax from 20% to 10%
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1yfhusw50g9ujptwg028zp38pmnnq80xa7l60g0vyhrsngsqns67xn`

---

Treasury funds ought to be used only for technological development of the chain, and integrated apps/dapps. With over a billion dollars current value of the treasury, and with the amount that continues to pour into the treasury, there is way more than enough resources to fund development for the chain and chain projects. There is not enough resources for wasteful spending, which is a good thing, and we should not allow for that opportunity to be taken advantage of.

Higher taxes encourage more waste, which massively decreases effectiveness, efficiency, and speed of development. We have seen funds wasted on Intersect employees, events, etc. Some of the employees never heard of Cardano prior to being hired, let alone even had wallets and contributed to the ecosystem. We should be prioritizing community members and including them in the process, and doing so in a manner which is not easily "grifted" upon, as we have seen with Catalyst, and will certainly see with the treasury. I understand it has and will continue to be iterative, but it would be much better if the mistakes were far less costly on the ecosystem.
