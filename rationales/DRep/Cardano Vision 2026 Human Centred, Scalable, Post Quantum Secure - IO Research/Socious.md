Socious abstains. Cardano Vision 2026 is a 32,916,000 ADA (about $7.9M) research programme — among the largest single asks in this budget cycle — funding the front of Cardano's innovation pipeline: 42 planned outputs across papers, problem statements, prototypes, and CIPs, delivered by a nine-partner consortium of 36 researchers under Aggelos Kiayias.

Socious does not vote No. Cardano's research-led foundation is a genuine differentiator, the post-quantum workstream is long-horizon insurance almost no other chain funds seriously, and cutting upstream research would be a short-sighted economy.

Socious's hesitation is about assessment, not merit. A programme of this size whose deliverables are largely papers and prototypes is hard to hold to the same value-for-money yardstick as engineering proposals that ship deployable features, and the headline evidence — CV25 exceeding its targets — is the proposer's own measure. In a constrained treasury cycle, whether $7.9M of upstream research is the right allocation against more directly deployable work is a legitimate prioritisation question, and one Socious would rather see settled by broad DRep consensus than by its own decisive vote. Socious abstains: it supports the research mission, but cannot fully underwrite the largest research line item at this scale without clearer independent grounding.
