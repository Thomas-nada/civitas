SIPO supports the proposal titled “MLabs Core Tool Maintenance & Enhancement: Plutarch” because it directly contributes to the health, resilience, and innovation capacity of the Cardano smart contract ecosystem.

Plutarch is a production-grade eDSL (embedded domain-specific language) in Haskell, widely used by Cardano developers for building robust, type-safe smart contracts. As a foundational tool, it must be maintained continuously — especially during network upgrades and hard forks — to ensure developers have access to secure, up-to-date infrastructure.

This proposal, submitted by Intersect on behalf of MLabs, addresses critical needs including:
	•	Regular bug fixes and compatibility updates for Plutarch
	•	Feature enhancements based on real developer feedback (e.g., GitHub issues)
	•	Ensuring alignment with the pace of protocol innovation
	•	Supporting both core teams and new dApp builders with a reliable development stack

SIPO recognizes that without sustained funding, even essential tools like Plutarch may degrade, undermining developer productivity and ecosystem growth. MLabs, a trusted and capable engineering partner with proven expertise (e.g., in Cardano.nix and budget contract audits), is well positioned to deliver this work.

The proposal is also supported by Intersect’s smart contract-based treasury governance system, including audit-backed disbursement and milestone-based accountability with oversight from independent third parties such as the Cardano Foundation, Sundae Labs, and others.

In short, maintaining Plutarch is not just a technical necessity — it is a strategic imperative for sustaining the quality and velocity of decentralized application development on Cardano.

SIPOは、「MLabs Core Tool Maintenance & Enhancement: Plutarch」という提案に賛成票を投じます。本提案は、カルダノ・スマートコントラクト開発に不可欠な基盤ツール「Plutarch」の維持・強化に直結するものであり、開発者の生産性やエコシステム全体の健全性を支える戦略的施策です。

本提案は、以下の課題とニーズに対応しています：
	•	プロトコルのアップグレードやハードフォークに対応するための互換性維持・バグ修正
	•	GitHub等を通じて寄せられた開発者の声に基づく機能拡張
