I am deviating here from my previously communicated stance, where I had conveyed I would likely support the IO proposals for continuity's sake despite their extremely hefty asks. 

If we look realistically at the state of Cardano today, we observe a few things: Smart contract functionality was brought in 2021 in a halfhearted way: lacking development tooling, an application backend, documentation etc, causing development issues for years. Cardano has not scaled since inception, two minor block size tweaks don't really count. The ecosystem has no major stable coins, low volumes, low TVL and dapp count, which all lead back to the above issues. To add insult to injury, IO has started pursuing a new blockchain called Midnight which the Cardano community is forced to accept.

I am not convinced that this new era of governance brings in a new IO or Charles Hoskinson. A track record of at best "overpromise and underdeliver" or at worst non-delivery is not about to change overnight. I think it's time we rip off the bandaid and seek out new development firms to take the lead here.
