Rationale
This proposal represents a structural and long-term investment in the future of Cardano. It goes beyond isolated technical upgrades and instead outlines a holistic strategy to enhance Cardano’s scalability, decentralization, and utility. EMURGO sees this as a continuation of Input Output Engineering’s critical role in shaping the protocol’s foundation, particularly through initiatives such as Ouroboros Leios, Hydra, Mithril, and the modular Acropolis node architecture.

We also recognize the significant emphasis on knowledge transfer, maintenance, and supplier diversity, which aligns with Cardano’s goal of open, decentralized development. With clearly defined deliverables, strategic alignment with the Product Roadmap, and a deep history of proven delivery, we believe this proposal is essential to prepare Cardano for real-world, high-scale use cases. Therefore, we cast our full support for this proposal.
