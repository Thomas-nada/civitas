Cardano needs to grow, and one of the things that it has been missing for a long time was stablecoins in sufficient amounts. Over the time for a number of reasons Cardano was unable to onboard USDC or USDT, which left the ecosystem hungry for solutions.

To the challenge rose Cardano native projects with innovative stablecoin solutions and with possibly better approach and technology. What is still lacking is accelerated growth and adoption of it.

In this sense, I believe we as a community can use treasury to push this through the early stages and provide deeper liquidity on DEXes and thus enabling a more vibrant ecosystem. Stablecoins are not usable just for trading but for all sorts of lending protocols, DePIN projects and other utility focused projects.

There are risks in properly executing movements of such a large amount, but the people in the committee are well known in the Cardano community as well as to be personally. Failure in execution will be visible and reputations will be tarnished forever.

I vote YES, because Cardano needs stablecoins like any other L1 ecosystem in any future scenario so lets give it a flying start and boost it with our own war chest.
