I voted "ABSTAIN" to the Stablecoin DeFi Liquidity Proposal. I'll split my rationale into "Things I like" & "Things I don't like", to which I can neither fully approve nor disapprove this proposal.

Things I like:
- I like and support community's initiatives in trying to solve one of the fundamental issues for Cardano, which is stablecoin liquidity.
- 15% to 35% composition in ADA-stables liquidity pools is a pretty risk-balanced portfolio that reduces the effects of impermanent loss, while other stables can be put into lending platforms to earn interests & provide additional revenue back to the treasury.
- I like the idea to set aside some funds for legal & audit fees. This will provide a temperature check on the committees for managing the stablecoin liquidity responsibly.
- Amaru smart contract setup is something that I'm interested in seeing them developed as I haven't seen other Cardano DeFi protocols developing smart contracts with it. Not an expert, but I'm not sure of the difference between the advantages of Aiken & Amaru on the smart contract designs.

Things I don't really like:

1) The committee profiles are decent enough, but I preferred to have some people in the committee with deep knowledge in not just the DeFi landscape, but also with macroeconomics knowledge that also ties to crypto economics, and also someone with crypto trading experiences that execute stablecoin trades based on strong fundamentals & technical knowledge. 

2) I don't think 49.5 Million ADA is enough to bring sufficient users from outside the Cardano ecosystem into Cardano to perform DeFi activities. Current stablecoin price slippages are > 7% for 100k ADA trades. If you take 15% to 35% (7.4 mil ~ 17.3 mil) and put them into LPs, assuming they are deposited equally to ADA-USDM & ADA-USDA and we're only taking Minswap's LP metrics, you can increase the Stablecoin LP liquidity by around 2x to 3x, thus the 4 ~ 6% slippage mentioned in the proposal is quite accurate, but if you compared with blockchains with very high stablecoin liquidity like Ethereum & Solana, similarly 100k trades will only have < 0.5% price slippages.

Meaning for Cardano, with this budget proposal passed, it can be a small boost for retail users that perform 4-fig trades with lower slippage, but large institutional or whale traders will still avoid using Cardano DEXes due to relatively high price slippages + DEX fees.

Cardano Foundation's recent announcement of an additional 8-figure stablecoin liquidity injection on top of this proposal will definitely boost Cardano's stablecoin liquidity from < $40 million to somewhere around $150 million, to which according to DeFiLlama, will put Cardano in the top 30 chains with the highest stablecoin liquidity, but still a far-fetched from being in the top 10 with > $1 billion of stablecoin liquidity, so there's still a lot of work to bring liquidity to Cardano besides this proposal. Thus, a good start, but I predict it won't bring meaningful institutional adoption unless other institutions beside this proposal & CF putting 8 to 9 figures worth of stablecoins into Cardano.

3) Maximum of 2 protocols per DeFi category is not very ideal. For DEX's case, most probably the largest DEXes (Minswap & Splash in terms of TVL & Trading Volume) will get preferential treatment for this liquidity. I would prefer 3 - 5 AMM DEXes & 2 orderbook DEXes to have a much fairer share of the liquidity. Plus, we already have DEX aggregators like Steelswap to find the best prices for swaps, and also lending aggregators like Danogo too, so it doesn't make sense to only restrict to 2 protocols.

4) There's a lack of details on how the direct sale of TradFi & OTC deals will be done, as there's not much CNT-native stablecoin listings on the large exchanges like Binance & Coinbase, there's a question on how the ADA will be converted to native, fiat-backed stablecoins like USDM & USDA via these exchanges unless if they are converted to USDC & USDT, and deploy to Wanchain or Mynth and bridge them to Cardano. As far as I know, only NBX & Coinstore list Cardano native stablecoins in their exchanges

For OTC deals, I can recommend using VyFi's Safe Swap solution here: https://docs.vyfi.io/vy-finance-products/safe-swap
But will also need the other party to be familiar with VyFi and execute the deal.
