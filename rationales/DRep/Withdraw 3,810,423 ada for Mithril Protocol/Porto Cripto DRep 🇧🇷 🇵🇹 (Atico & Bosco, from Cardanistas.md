[English]
We chose to vote "YES" on this governance action "Withdraw 3,810,423 ADA for Mithril Protocol" (gov_action1k02990lhw6wh74t7c6ufw3mqaek9ujtvyan99dj5qv5kvcs7pn8s24l0u4y), because we believe Mithril is a strategic piece of infrastructure for the Cardano ecosystem, enabling faster, more secure, and more decentralized blockchain state synchronization and verification. This technology reduces reliance on centralized services while making it easier to build lightweight wallets, bridges, and other applications that require efficient and trustworthy access to blockchain state. Although the requested ₳3,810,423 is substantial, we believe it offers good value considering the project's technical importance and its potential to deliver long-term benefits across the entire ecosystem. Strengthening Mithril directly supports Cardano’s scalability, accessibility, and decentralization, providing a stronger foundation for future applications and network growth. We also view the proposal’s governance, oversight, and transparency mechanisms positively. These include milestone-based disbursements, independent supervision, multisignature authorization, auditable smart contracts, and public on-chain monitoring of both project execution and Treasury fund usage. Together, these measures provide strong accountability and allow the community to transparently verify progress and resource allocation.
