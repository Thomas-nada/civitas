This would shift Cardano funds away from community governance to a VC firms discretion with limited transparency and no guarantee of returns. Issues: 1. Moral hazard: Draper Dragon has no incentive to be conservative, they profit form any outcome where the fund generates returns, even if reckless. A VC firm risking their own capital would be more careful. 2. Misaligned risk: The entity making decisions, Draper Dragon, doesn't bear the failure consequences. Yet the entity bearing the risk, Cardano community, doesn't make the decisions. 3. Precedent: If this passes then other firms will make similar proposals of 'give us your money, we'll manage it and we'll share the profits'. 4. This is not a good replacement for Project Catalyst which has transparency and empowers the community to decide. 5. This seems like a Hail Mary pass born out of desperation, not strategy. The community will probably vote yes but they'll regret it in 3-5 years when they realise that Draper Dragon make $20M+ in carried interest, the fund underperformed as most VC funds do, cardano is still playing catch-up to Ethereum, and the community lost both capital and control.
