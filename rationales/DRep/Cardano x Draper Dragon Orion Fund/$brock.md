I went back and forth on this proposal a lot. The primary factor that had me doubting it is that we previously gave this group 1.64M ADA and received absolutely no KPIs nor ROI data in return. The money seemingly went to a paid trip for some developers and no tangible benefit to the ecosystem nor treasury. Maybe their efforts require more time to come to fruition, but I cannot locate any sign of intent to provide this information to the community. And just as a reminder, I voted No on their last (Catalyst) proposal.

What is making me change my mind and vote Yes on this proposal is that it comes with a 6-year deployment horizon which is a much longer commitment than I've seen anyone else offer; and we also have no other VC efforts currently. This new proposal promises quarterly reporting and a public KPI dashboard, which is a significant improvement from their last proposal.

At the risk of "fool me twice...", I do not see any other signs of VC activity entering our ecosystem, so I would resent if we did not at least give this a try. I also appreciate the tranche formatting, so that I can still change my mind later on and vote No on future withdrawal actions.
