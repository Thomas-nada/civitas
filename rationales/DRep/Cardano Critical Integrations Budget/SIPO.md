SIPO supports this proposal because it provides the foundational infrastructure that Cardano has lacked for many years, and it addresses these gaps through an organized, neutral, and constitutionally compliant funding structure.

First, the proposal directly resolves Cardano’s most persistent structural limitation: the absence of core integrations that enable external liquidity, institutional participation, and large-scale economic activity. Tier-one stablecoin rails, global pricing oracles, cross-chain bridges, institutional custody solutions, advanced analytics, and scalable wallet infrastructures are essential public goods for any mature layer-one network. These components cannot be built piecemeal by individual teams. SIPO recognizes that without these integrations, Cardano’s DeFi activity, TVL, and ecosystem growth will continue to lag behind competing chains.

Second, the proposal is essential for Cardano’s long-term financial sustainability. Stablecoins, bridges, and institutional-grade infrastructure directly contribute to higher transaction volume and expanded fee revenue, enabling Cardano to operate as a financially sustainable blockchain. SIPO believes that economic viability is fundamental to the success of decentralized governance.

Third, the governance structure of the proposal maintains neutrality and transparency. A five-member Steering Committee representing IOG, the Cardano Foundation, EMURGO, the Midnight Foundation, and Intersect ensures balanced decision-making. Intersect administers funds under constitutional safeguards, with milestone-based disbursements, independent audits, and the return of unused funds. SIPO considers this a responsible and constitutionally aligned mechanism for deploying treasury resources.

Fourth, negotiations with integration partners are already at an advanced stage, allowing the budget to be deployed immediately. Given the speed at which competing ecosystems continue to expand their stablecoin and interoperability infrastructure, SIPO views timely execution as critical. Cardano must close the gap, especially in stablecoin readiness, where it is significantly behind other major networks.

Finally, SIPO regards this proposal as a strategic, long-horizon investment into Cardano’s future. By delivering the infrastructure required for institutional adoption and cross-ecosystem liquidity, this initiative will strengthen Cardano’s competitiveness, expand its economic surface area, and generate long-term value for all participants.

SIPO also fully recognizes the strategic value of this proposal and therefore supports it. At the same time, SIPO believes that ensuring transparency and auditability is essential for the success of this budget. SIPO will continue to monitor whether the proposal is executed appropriately and in alignment with the Constitution and community governance.

For these reasons, SIPO votes Yes.



SIPO は、本提案が Cardano の長期的な成長と競争力にとって極めて重要であると判断し、賛成票を投じる。

第一に、本提案は Cardano が長年抱えてきた核心的課題である「外部流動性とユースケースを引き込むための基盤インフラ不足」を体系的に解決する点で極めて意義深い。ティア1安定通貨、クロスチェーン、グローバルオラクル、カストディ、ウォレット基盤、分析プラットフォームは、成熟したレイヤー1にとって必須の公共財であり、個別チームでは構築できない性質のものである。SIPO は、この不足が TVL、DeFi 活動量、機関投資家の参入に長期的な制約を生み続けてきたと認識している。

第二に、提案は Cardano の財務的持続性に直結する。ステーブルコインやクロスチェーンが整備されなければ、取引手数料収入の拡大も困難であり、Cardano を自律的に維持するための経済基盤を構築することができない。SIPO は、レイヤー1としての持続的な収益性確保は、分散型ガバナンスの成立にも不可欠と考える。
