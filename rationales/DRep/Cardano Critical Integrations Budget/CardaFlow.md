I have consistently voted against gov actions (GA) and treasury withdrawals aiming at providing directly USD liquidity in cardano ecosystem because I think that 1:1 pegged stablecoin liquidities should come from external contributors whereas treasury funds should be used to create the right environment to attract them... 
The way I understand the current GA is very different from what I saw in previous tentative to generate liquidities. Although 70M is a significant investment, this GA is an attempt to tackle the lack of critical infrastructure with the objective to successfully attract external players. Expected amount of investment to achieve what is targeted may be well higher than what is proposed suggesting further contributions from steering committee members. Without being decisive, the convergence of funding entities + intersect to jointly propose it is a positive sign for its future execution.
I would convene that details on fund allocation, steering committee governance and KPIs may be missing, however, I would equally concede that and time and timing matter.
I am ready to trust the good will of steering committee members to bring us where cardano ecosytem deserves to be in near future...
