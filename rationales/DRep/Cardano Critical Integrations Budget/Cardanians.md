We support the goal of bringing critical integrations such as stablecoins, custody, bridges, and analytics to Cardano, but the current proposal lacks the transparency needed for a ₳70M treasury withdrawal. There is no itemized cost breakdown, no clear prioritization of integrations, and limited visibility into partner selection or execution strategy. This level of opacity is not acceptable for the largest budget request in Cardano’s history. We encourage a revised version with clear costs, justification, and accountability so the community can responsibly support this important initiative.
