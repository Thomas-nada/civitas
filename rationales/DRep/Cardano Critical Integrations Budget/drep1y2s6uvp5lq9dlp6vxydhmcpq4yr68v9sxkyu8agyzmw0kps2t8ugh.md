In its current form, I have voted no on this proposal. Athough I am delighted to see the 3 Founding entities working together and I do not have an issue with any of the things listed, there are some key concerns that the proposal should address in black and white. 
 
This proposal is as vague in terms of responsibility and clear deliverables as the original Roadmap which led to years of arguing and fall out between the 3FEs with no clear documented list of deliverables and responsibilities for people to fall back on.

The ecosystem won’t survive another fall out if this fails and we don’t have these in this proposal. Already many are saying these things have already been paid for and not delivered on. 

Can we get some minimum deliverables? For example, at least one of USDC or USDT native token on Cardano/Programmable token on Cardano, who much Liquidity will be provided and by whom and how long for… (as an example for each pillar) 
 
I also feel that the entities already have a lot of treasury funded deliverables for 2026, as well as recent promises and commitments. I would like them to confirm that none of these other things will be hindered in terms of quality and delivery by this new joint proposal. 

More information on what I am hearing from the community as concerns are found in the tweet thread here: https://x.com/invalid_eutxo/status/1994823474427355332?s=20
 
I have also broken down the recent Roadmap from CF, (https://x.com/invalid_eutxo/status/1994820752324133322?s=20) which includes some linked areas that could do with clarification.
