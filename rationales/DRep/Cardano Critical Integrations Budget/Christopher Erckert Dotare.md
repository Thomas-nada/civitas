We are voting YES on this proposal, as the integrations outlined are strategically important for Cardano’s ecosystem growth. However, we would have preferred clearer definitions or example groups of what constitutes tier-one partners—particularly for stablecoins, analytics platforms, bridges, and oracles.

For instance, explicitly listing which stablecoins qualify as tier-one (e.g., USDC, USDT, PYUSD, etc.) would strengthen the success criteria by clarifying that achieving deployment from any member of that predefined group meets the intended objective. Clearer categorization would improve accountability and alignment for all stakeholders while still allowing flexibility in partner selection.
