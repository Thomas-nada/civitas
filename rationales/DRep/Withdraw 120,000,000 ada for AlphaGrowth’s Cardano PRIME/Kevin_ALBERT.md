I vote NO on "Withdraw 120M ADA for AlphaGrowth's Cardano PRIME". I support making Cardano DeFi more visible, liquid and competitive. After reviewing the proposal, debate and transcripts, I think AlphaGrowth has relevant experience and a serious growth thesis. However, NCL is dominant: NCL is Cardano's treasury spending cap. Protecting Cardano's long-term financial freedom means not expanding or consuming that cap for a very large program before the budget room and the biggest Phase 3 spending plan are concrete. The Operating Group adds oversight, but it is not a second on-chain DRep vote. So DReps would not approve the actual Phase 3 recipients, KPIs, attribution method and risk limits after the audit/gap analysis. I would support a cleaner phased version: fund the diagnostic work first, publish results, then submit Phase 3 separately with evidence, measurable deliverables and fresh NCL impact.
