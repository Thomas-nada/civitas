# Ha-Nguyen

**Vote:** Abstain
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2z8gktqj27kwmmxesd0y484f7p4z9tjf5xu8m4sucstm3gvtywlp`

---

I acknowledge the technical expertise and long-term contributions of Tweag to Cardano’s core infrastructure. The initiatives proposed here have the potential to create substantial long-term value for the network.

However, I have chosen to abstain from voting on this proposal for the following reasons:

* The proposal consolidates multiple projects into a single large treasury withdrawal, making it difficult for the community to assess each component individually.
* The lack of an open process for public review or competitive bidding raises concerns about transparency and fairness in the selection of vendors.
* Information about how milestone completion will be independently verified remains unclear and difficult to access externally.

Given these ambiguities and the significant amount requested, I am unable to fully support the proposal at this time—though I do not deny its potential strategic value in the future.
