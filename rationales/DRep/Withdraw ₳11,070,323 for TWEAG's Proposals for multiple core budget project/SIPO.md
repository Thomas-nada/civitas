SIPO supports this proposal because it strategically advances the technical foundation of the Cardano blockchain through a well-structured suite of core infrastructure projects. Tweag’s approach addresses both current operational bottlenecks and long-term system scalability. The projects funded by this proposal include high-impact deliverables such as:
	•	Ledger and consensus conformance testing to improve correctness and protocol resilience.
	•	Plutus script re-executor and block cost investigation to empower developers with better tooling and benchmarking capabilities.
	•	Genesis sync acceleration and hoarding node to improve node bootstrapping and reliability.
	•	Canonical codecs and emulator tooling to ensure consistency and enhance testability across the ecosystem.

SIPO acknowledges Tweag’s long-standing technical leadership within Cardano, including their work on Ouroboros Genesis, consensus, and ledger protocols. Their partnership with Intersect and adherence to transparent contract management practices—monitored by independent third-party assurers and enforced via audited smart contracts—provides confidence in both accountability and delivery.

This proposal aligns with Cardano’s mission to build a resilient, scalable, and verifiable blockchain ecosystem. For these reasons, SIPO casts a Yes vote in support of this funding initiative.
