Although GovTool has laid the foundation for DRep registration and delegation, and play a crucial role in the implementation of CIP-1694, I am unable to support this proposal due to the amount being requested.

Requesting 1.5m ada for 12 months of maintenance and continued development in my view is excessive. While I acknowledge the importance of improving GovTool’s UI and UX, **the size of the budget and the duration of funding are the primary reasons for my decision.**

As a DRep who has consistently advocated for smaller NCL this year and has already approved a budget of approximately 280m ada within an NCL of 300m, I will **abstain **from this vote and leave the decision to my fellow DReps.
