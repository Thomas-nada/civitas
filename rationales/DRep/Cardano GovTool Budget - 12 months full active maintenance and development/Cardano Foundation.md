**Rationale Statement**

This decision follows a detailed review of the governance action and consideration of diverse viewpoints within our governance advisory team. Our support is based on a pragmatic assessment of GovTool’s role in the ecosystem, balanced against its acknowledged areas for improvement.

**Reasons for a YES Vote:**

1. **Strategic Importance for Governance Accessibility:** A core principle in "Our Cardano" is Accessibility, ensuring participation in governance is open to all. GovTool is the primary, non-commercial tool that currently fulfills this mission, providing a neutral and open-source public good for every ada holder to engage in governance. Maintaining its viability at this time, while governance is in a critical stage, is important for Cardano’s governance model, as it prevents reliance on proprietary, closed-source alternatives.  
2. **Commitment to Open Source and Ecosystem Growth:** The proposal reinforces a commitment to open-source principles, which prevents vendor lock-in and fosters a competitive environment. Furthermore, the budget’s Level 2 funding earmarks 250,000 ada for direct community contributions and to support other open-source governance tools, an approach that should benefit the entire ecosystem.  
3. **Pragmatic Support for a Proven and Improving Team:** While the tool has known user experience and technical shortcomings, the consortium has a proven track record of consistently delivering and maintaining this infrastructure. They have demonstrated responsiveness to feedback and a commitment to improvement. For lack of better alternatives, supporting this established team represents the most viable path to retaining and enhancing the value GovTool provides.
