I do not support the GovTool budget proposal in its current form for the following reasons:
- I think the budget is too high for the complexity of the project
- I think some of the things that are being proposed in the budget do not justify their cost
- I believe the GovTool team's past performance and that of their competition (namely Tempo.vote) demonstrates that there are more competitive teams out there capable of building more stable and usable alternatives at a lower cost for Cardano.
