On behalf of CASIA team, I’m voting no on this proposal — not because I don’t support GovTool (I do), but because the ₳1.15M ask feels too high right now (~ 100k ada/ month)

I get that a lot of work has gone into it, and the team clearly cares, but jumping straight to the top funding tier without trying a more phased or leaner approach first just doesn’t sit right. Especially with Treasury funds, I think we need to be a bit more cautious.

Would love to see a trimmed-down version, maybe start with just maintenance + community incentives, then build up from there based on progress. That would make a yes vote much easier.

Plus cost benchmarking to prove value for money for community is a plus point
