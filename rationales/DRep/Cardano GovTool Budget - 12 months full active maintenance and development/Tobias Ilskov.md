Gov tools is an open source backend used by many other governance interfaces, both open and proprietary. Improving Gov tools seems like a rising tide for all the governance boats in the race.

Some of the features to be added include:
- CC vote history and rationale presentation 
- Drep voting history interfaces
-Support for Group / Multi person DReps
- Group / Multi person proposers for on-chain actions 
- Introduce CoSponsors to Crowdfund ADA for governance action deposits.
- Pre-governance polling, to gauge DRep sentiment before on-chain actions.
- Integration of AI tools to aid in translation, etc to make multi-national involvement in discussion more feasible.

I like what I see here, and while the proposed budget is larger than I would prefer, I think it will be worth it if the teams involved deliver on all these features well. 

This is all new; and teams need chances to prove themselves. I am comfortable giving them a year of funding. I will make sure to confirm all these features are delivered before extending their funding next year.
