We cast a YES vote on this proposal.

This proposal supports 12 months of maintenance and development for GovTool, a key public infrastructure enabling participation in Cardano’s on-chain governance. It has been jointly submitted by developers including LidoNation, ByronNetwork, WeDeliver, and DQuadrant, and takes into account interoperability with other governance tools.

Its tiered budget structure, provision for unused fund returns, and smart contract–based fund management and audit mechanisms coordinated by Intersect contribute to decentralization and transparency.

We believe this proposal is a vital step toward strengthening Cardano’s decentralized governance culture, while also encouraging wider interoperability and open contribution to governance tooling. Therefore, we support it.
