[English]
We chose to vote "YES" on this governance action "Se7en Labs: Daedalus Wallet Maintenance and Improvements 2026-2027" (gov_action1mr0qdz2jmagvsch6r08fhqvq6vu8jakt4c8m9s7ea7z0p740vntqq4yjd6j), because we believe the proposal ensures the continued maintenance and evolution of Daedalus, Cardano’s only desktop full-node wallet. Maintaining this software is essential to preserving user security, self-custody, and compatibility with future protocol upgrades, while also expanding support for decentralized applications and new hardware wallet integrations. The requested ₳1,785,333 appears proportionate to the proposed scope of work, which includes ongoing maintenance, cross-platform support, and the delivery of new features. In our view, the proposal offers good value considering Daedalus’ strategic importance to the Cardano ecosystem and to users who prefer a fully decentralized wallet experience. We also view the proposal’s governance, oversight, and transparency mechanisms positively. These include monthly disbursements tied to verified work, financial audits, independent oversight by Intersect, smart contract-based payment management, and public on-chain monitoring of project execution. Together, these measures strengthen accountability and provide the community with clear visibility into how Treasury resources are being used.
