I support this Treasury Withdrawal.

Daedalus is an important full-node desktop wallet in the Cardano ecosystem. It provides a self-sovereign access layer where users can verify the Cardano chain themselves, without relying on third-party APIs or trusted backends. While Daedalus does not directly increase SPO decentralization, it reduces dependence on wallet backends and API providers, supporting Cardano’s access decentralization, resilience, and client/access diversity.

This proposal includes support for future hard forks, Leios, Peras, Nested Transactions, Keystone/Flex hardware wallets, a CIP-30 dApp connector, Japanese localization, and user support. These are important for Cardano’s long-term security, compatibility, and continuity of use.

I also value the treasury management design, including Intersect administration, smart contract disbursement, monthly disbursements, oversight, dashboard transparency, and return of unused funds.

At the same time, Daedalus has limited opt-in MAU, so the cost-effectiveness of spending 1,785,333 ADA should continue to be clearly explained. Going forward, I expect public reporting on pre-hard-fork releases, CIP-30 interoperability, support activity, Japanese localization, usage KPIs, and any unused funds returned to the Treasury.

Overall, I support this proposal as public-good maintenance that preserves an important self-verifying full-node wallet access point for Cardano, rather than as an attempt to turn Daedalus into a mass-market light wallet.
