I support this proposal.

I believe Daedalus is one of the pieces of infrastructure that Cardano cannot afford to lose.

At present, there is effectively no alternative full-node wallet for Cardano. Daedalus remains indispensable for users who value self-verification and self-sovereignty. It has also earned a strong reputation over many years—particularly within the Japanese community—and that trust is itself a valuable asset to the ecosystem.

Furthermore, recent security incidents surrounding wallet infrastructure have reinforced the importance of solutions that minimize reliance on third parties. I believe demand for trustworthy full-node wallets will continue to grow, making it even more important to preserve the reputation and confidence that Daedalus has built over the years.

For these reasons, I believe continued maintenance and improvement of Daedalus is a worthwhile investment in Cardano's long-term decentralization, resilience, and user trust, and I support this proposal.
