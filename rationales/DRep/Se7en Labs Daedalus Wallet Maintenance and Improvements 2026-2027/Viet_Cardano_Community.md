We recommend voting YES to this proposal as it is one of the clearest and most logical cases for public infrastructure funding.

First, Daedalus is Cardano's only full-node wallet, playing a vital role in the network's decentralization; each installation is a full node. If Daedalus falls behind in protocol compatibility, a group of users who value self-sovereignty will have no alternative.

Second, it is a transparent time-and-materials model, not a risky fixed-price one, with budgets tied to verified actual work, unused funds returned to the Treasury, and an independent financial audit.

Third, the Se7en Labs team has a concrete and verifiable track record: since January 2026, they have shipped Daedalus 8.0 and 11.0, integrated Mithril, supported Apple Silicon, and modernized their build system, with all builds publicly available on GitHub, eliminating the need for self-reporting.

Fourth, this is pure Apache 2.0 open-source software; no private business model benefits exclusively, and the asset permanently belongs to the community, truly embodying the "public good" that the Treasury should fund.

Finally, the cost (~1.78M ADA/year) is reasonable considering the scale of ongoing maintenance work across upcoming hard forks (Leios, Peras).
