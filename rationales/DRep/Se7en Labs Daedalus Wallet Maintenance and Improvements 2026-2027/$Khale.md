I am voting YES on “Se7en Labs Daedalus Wallet Maintenance and Improvements 2026–2027.” Daedalus is Cardano’s only full-node desktop wallet, and every installation also runs a full Cardano node, contributing directly to client diversity, P2P resilience, and a strong self-sovereign security model. This proposal funds a bounded time-and-materials engagement for protocol maintenance (including Leios, Peras, and nested-transaction hard fork readiness), ecosystem improvements such as Keystone and Flex hardware-wallet support and CIP‑30 dApp connector integration, and dedicated user support with continued focus on the Japanese community and full Japanese localisation.

The scope is focused on maintenance and targeted enhancements rather than speculative new product work, and it aligns well with the 2030 strategy’s emphasis on security, client diversity, developer experience, and localized engagement. IOG’s continued binary signing mitigates release integrity risk, while Se7en Labs takes on the ongoing engineering and support workload. Even though Daedalus serves a relatively small but privacy-conscious user base, maintaining at least one high-quality full-node wallet is important for Cardano’s decentralization and for users who explicitly require that trust model. Under current NCL pressure, this 1.78M ADA request is a modest but defensible use of remaining headroom, so I will be voting YES.
