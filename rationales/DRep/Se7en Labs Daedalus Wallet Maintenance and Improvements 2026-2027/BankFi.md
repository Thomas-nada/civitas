It's easy to lose focus of the core blockchain principles in today's climate with socials full of distractions, but having a self-hosted node is a necessity. Will the majority of users use a self-hosted node? No, of course not. But as with any industry, the "power users" are the ones that drive it forward while the majority of users follow their path. Shutting down maintenance of a self-hosted node would isolate power users and likely remove them from the ecosystem, which is a huge net negative.
