I have tried and used many wallets and Eternl is by far the most full featured and well designed.  Many in the Cardano community have become dependent upon its superior functionality, so much so that it would be a grave blow if Eternl was to cease being available.  However, it would be unfair to fund one private company over its competitors without requiring open-source code or community ownership and governance rights.  Doing so would set a precendent and seem to violate Cardano's decentralised ethos by handing a private company an unfair advantage.  For these stated reasons, I furthermore believe that approving such a request would contravene Cardano's constitution.
