I vote Abstain on this proposal.

First, I would like to acknowledge and appreciate the efforts made to address concerns raised during the previous proposal, particularly regarding transparency and audit oversight. I also respect the team's intention to move toward a sustainable business model rather than relying indefinitely on Treasury funding.

In particular, I view the proposed repayment mechanism and the commitment to return a portion of future surplus revenue to the Treasury as a constructive and responsible approach that aligns well with the long-term sustainability goals of the Cardano ecosystem.

I also fully recognize that Eternl is an important piece of Cardano infrastructure and provides significant value to users, developers, governance participants, and the broader ecosystem.

However, before supporting the proposal, I would like stronger confidence regarding the practical viability of the proposed Pro plan model.

The proposal states that approximately 4.2% of the user base would need to subscribe in order for Eternl to become financially sustainable. While the reasoning is understandable, I do not believe sufficient evidence has yet been provided to demonstrate the likelihood of achieving that conversion rate under current market conditions.

Additional information such as user surveys, market research, demonstrated demand for Pro features, previous monetization results, or benchmarking against comparable products would help provide greater confidence in the sustainability assumptions underlying the proposal.

I appreciate the improvements made since the previous submission and view the overall direction positively. However, because I remain uncertain about the viability of the sustainability model that forms the foundation of this proposal, I am not yet comfortable voting Yes.

For these reasons, I vote Abstain.
