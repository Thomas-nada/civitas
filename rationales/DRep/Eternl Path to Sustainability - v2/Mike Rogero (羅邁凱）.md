I really like Eternl, it is my first choice wallet. I would also like to fund this as a signal to other projects that treating the treasury as a loan-provider as opposed to a grant provider is the way to go. 

However, as a CFE and CFA credential holder, I can not consider a Yes vote on a proposal where the recipient is also the auditor.  Basic accounting concepts such as Separation of Duties, Conflict of Interest, Auditor Independence,  and Self-Review Threat among others precludes accepting a proposal written this way irrespective of its other merits.  This is a simple principles based decision.
