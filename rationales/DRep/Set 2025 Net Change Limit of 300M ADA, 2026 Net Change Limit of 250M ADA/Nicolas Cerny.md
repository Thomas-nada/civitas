All governance action metadata files should be hosted on immutable storage solutions such as IPFS, Filecoin, or Cardano-specific platforms like IAGON. Immutability ensures the integrity and trustworthiness of the information. Allowing metadata to be altered post-submission undermines the democratic process, opening the door to manipulation or misinformation. This is not only highly problematic but also violates the core principles of transparency and accountability that are fundamental to decentralized governance.
