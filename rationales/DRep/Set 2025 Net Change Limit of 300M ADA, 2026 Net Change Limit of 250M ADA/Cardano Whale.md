# Cardano Whale

**Proposal:** Set 2025 Net Change Limit of 300M ADA, 2026 Net Change Limit of 250M ADA
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1yfnuetvunwfl83qaq4e7vlg5w8dwwjmtt4j6tr9fegqvgmgtq5scg`

---

I want to firstly thank the proposers for getting the ball rolling on this one. I am cognisant of the fact we need a NCL, and failing to implement one risks stalling the progress of Cardano altogether. I also hope that, if this vote were to fail to pass, we can have a new vote with a less ambitious NCL target in quick succession.

I will vote against this proposal because, as I have elaborated in many posts on X these days, I think 300m ADA is too ambitious, especially given current market conditions. Other coins are burning a quarter of supply to stay in the game, Cardano cannot risk going too radically in the other direction. Especially not right now.

My ideal figure for now would be closer to 100m ADA, but unless radically new info comes out, I’ll commit to voting in favour of anything in the 100-150m range, and strongly likely to vote for anything 200m or less.

I want to emphasise that this is not a call on whether 300m ADA is enough to build what needs to be built, or whether having such a large treasury is appropriate. It’s taking a realistic monetary policy stance; for an outsider the treasury has been a temporary burn mechanism and we are reversing that.

We should use it as much as the market will permit us (as we have done with Catalyst), but like real world monetary policy, the economic conditions dictate how much freedom we have in spending. Right now the conditions are fairly dire, plus this is a new process and we have incomplete info about ADA selling by founding entities in the past years. 

For now I’d like to err on the side of caution. In future years I may become more flexible on the topic. Thank you.
