300m ADA may be fitting for a 12 month budget, however we're almost halfway through the year, so I would expect the net change limit to appropriately reflect the timeline that is being funded. If treasury income was approximately 168m for a 6 month period in 2024, then a net change limit closer to that figure would better suit the timeline. I'm aware that NCL does not determine the budget, but it does give the budget a guardrail, and with the excessive budget figures being proposed lately, it seems we could use a safe guardrail to prevent budget abuse. As for 2026 net change limit, it's still far too early to determine a realistic figure. In current volatile market conditions, the future fiat value of ADA is too difficult to predict. I would prefer to vote on 2026 NCL at the end of 2025.
