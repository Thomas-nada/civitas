**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2z8gktqj27kwmmxesd0y484f7p4z9tjf5xu8m4sucstm3gvtywlp`

---

I vote YES because:

✅ The proposed Net Change Limits align with forecasted treasury inflows (≈300M ADA for 2025, ≈265M ADA for 2026), helping to keep expenditures within a safe range and ensuring responsible, sustainable treasury management.
✅ It does not hinder Intersect’s operating budget (~260M ADA) while leaving room for strategic investments that support ecosystem growth.
✅ Setting NCLs for both 2025 and 2026 helps prevent front-loading of spending into one year, promoting long-term, transparent budget planning.
✅ Approving a baseline NCL now avoids the worst-case scenario where no limit is set—something that could invalidate budget proposals under current governance rules.
✅ This is a community-led proposal, not initiated by founding entities, and demonstrates the growing maturity and self-governance of the Cardano community.

💡 In the long term, I agree that the NCL should be defined as a percentage of treasury revenue to improve flexibility and predictability. However, at this stage, the proposed fixed amounts are a practical and necessary step to ensure budget continuity for 2025 and 2026.
