Why I Support the Community Proposal (300M ADA in 2025 / 250M ADA in 2026)

Among the Net Change Limit (NCL) proposals currently under review in Cardano’s on-chain governance, I hereby clearly express my intention to vote Yes on the community-submitted proposal (Gov_action10ueqgzwenxr39le68n0se9peu92r7gm2846xwehh3u0ahc0qd0uqqyljxu5).

This proposal sets the NCL for 2025 at 300 million ADA, and for 2026 at 250 million ADA. I believe this proposal is worthy of strong support not only because the numbers are reasonable, but because of its thoughtful structure, its emphasis on long-term sustainability, and its democratic, community-driven origins. It embodies the kind of governance culture we hope to foster in Cardano.

⸻

1. A Responsible Limit Aligned with Treasury Realities

The defining feature of this proposal is that the proposed NCLs closely match or modestly fall below the anticipated annual inflows to the treasury:
	•	Estimated inflow for 2025: ~300M ADA → NCL: 300M ADA
	•	Estimated inflow for 2026: ~265M ADA → NCL: 250M ADA

This demonstrates a prudent approach, one that prioritizes optimal use of available resources rather than maximizing the spending ceiling. By aiming to operate within known income levels—possibly with some margin for reserve—this proposal upholds a core tenet of sustainable treasury governance: spend only what you can responsibly afford.

In this regard, the proposal reflects a deep understanding of the NCL’s role as a constitutional safeguard—not a target, but a ceiling. The intent is not to spend to the limit, but to ensure spending does not exceed what the ecosystem can sustainably support.

⸻

2. Multi-Year Design That Enables Predictability and Strategic Planning

Another strength of this proposal is that it simultaneously defines NCLs for two consecutive years: 2025 and 2026. This multi-year scope enhances predictability for all stakeholders—DReps, vendors, community project teams—and enables strategic, forward-looking budgeting and development planning.

In a global, decentralized ecosystem like Cardano, which aspires to long-term viability, year-by-year uncertainty in budgetary policy can inhibit both innovation and execution. By establishing NCLs for multiple years at once, this proposal sets a precedent for better foresight and strategic alignment, potentially serving as a model for other blockchain ecosystems to follow.

⸻

3. A Flexible, Stepwise Governance Approach

At first glance, the community proposal might appear conservative. However, it is crucial to recognize the explicit flexibility built into the proposal. The authors state clearly that, should the need arise, a higher NCL can be proposed and approved through the appropriate governance process.

This represents an excellent example of adaptive governance—setting a safe starting point, with the openness to scale up when supported by evidence and community consensus. Rather than locking the network into a rigid or maximalist path, this proposal provides the flexibility needed to respond to emerging needs, while maintaining discipline and transparency.

This approach aligns perfectly with the Cardano Constitution’s emphasis on sustainability, adaptability, and community-led governance.

⸻

4. Grassroots Origins and Community-Centered Process

One of the most compelling aspects of this proposal is its origin. It was submitted by anonymous members of the Cardano community, unaffiliated with IOG, CF, EMURGO, or Intersect. This independence reflects a grassroots commitment to decentralized governance.

The proposers also explicitly ask the community to judge their proposal based on its merits and logic, not organizational affiliation. This demonstrates humility and a clear respect for the community’s intelligence and autonomy. In many ways, this proposal exemplifies Cardano’s ideals: decentralized, inclusive, evidence-based decision-making by and for the community.

Trust in governance doesn’t come from institutional authority, but from clarity, transparency, and thoughtful participation—and this proposal checks all those boxes.

⸻

5. A Realistic, Strategic Entry Point for Treasury Policy

By setting the 2025 and 2026 NCLs at 300M and 250M ADA respectively, the proposal strikes a balance between enabling ecosystem funding and avoiding excessive market impact. These levels are unlikely to trigger large-scale inflationary pressure or significant ADA selloffs, which could depress market confidence and undermine treasury sustainability.

In fact, this modest cap could foster greater trust in the treasury, by signaling that resources will be used with discipline and discretion. Moreover, starting with a conservative limit leaves room for further treasury innovation in the future, such as:
	•	Guarded staking (self-staking treasury ADA to earn rewards)
	•	Active reserve fund management
	•	Diversification into stablecoins or native tokens (subject to governance)

By leaving “headroom” for more advanced treasury strategies, this proposal lays the groundwork for the Cardano treasury to evolve from a passive store of funds into an active, resilient, and responsibly managed pool of shared resources.

⸻

Conclusion: A Balanced NCL Proposal that Embodies Responsibility and Vision

This community proposal presents a balanced, responsible approach to Cardano treasury governance. It is neither overly restrictive nor blindly optimistic. Rather, it reflects the maturity of a network that takes its public goods seriously, and is willing to tread carefully but confidently toward a sustainable financial future.

As a DRep, I will vote Yes on this proposal not just because the numbers make sense, but because the spirit of the proposal—its prudence, its flexibility, its transparency, and its community-driven nature—aligns fully with my vision for Cardano.

This is not simply about approving a budget cap. This is about affirming a governance culture: one that treats the treasury not as a spending account, but as a collective reserve for shared growth, responsibility, and legacy.

As a member of this community, I’m proud to support such a thoughtful and principled proposal, and I will do everything in my power to ensure that its values are reflected and reinforced on-chain.
