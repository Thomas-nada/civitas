Similar to my stance on Intersect, I fully recognize the undeniable value that Cardano’s research has brought to the ecosystem. However, moving forward, research efforts must be aligned with the network’s current needs and long-term strategic goals.

At present, there is no detailed breakdown of the proposed research initiatives, and not all components appear to be critical. While I support continued research, I believe it should be pursued in a more focused format, with clearly defined objectives that are aligned with Cardano’s evolving priorities—a conversation that has yet to take place within the governance process.

Furthermore, if we are voting to approve a 5-year research initiative, key questions remain unanswered:
– When are concrete results expected?
– How will costs be tracked and evaluated over time?
– Will a new 26M ADA research budget be introduced annually?

The absence of transparent budgeting, strategic planning, and open discussion around these long-term commitments is concerning. For these reasons, I have chosen to vote NO on this proposal.
