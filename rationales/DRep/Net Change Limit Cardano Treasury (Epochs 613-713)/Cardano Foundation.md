We recognize the practical problem this action addresses. The Net Change Limit agreed for Epochs 613–713 no longer leaves meaningful room for the treasury withdrawals now before DReps. Our reservations concerning the increase are the following:

* **No basis is given for the amount.** The motivation explains why more room would be useful but does not show how 500,000,000 ada was derived. There is no projection of expected demand over the remaining period, no assessment of treasury sustainability under the higher cap, and no comparison against other values within the range.   
* **A limit without a strategy is a weaker guardrail.** We would support an increase framed strategically: room allocated across defined spending categories with designated allocations and accountable recipients, anchored to a shared long-term direction such as the Cardano 2030\. The ecosystem should set those categories deliberately, weighing trade-offs and understanding what it costs to build and maintain Cardano.  
* **Raising the cap to accommodate pending requests inverts its purpose.** A limit that moves whenever demand approaches it functions as a spending target rather than a fiscal constraint. We would rather see the ceiling derived from agreed priorities than from the size of the requests in front of us.
