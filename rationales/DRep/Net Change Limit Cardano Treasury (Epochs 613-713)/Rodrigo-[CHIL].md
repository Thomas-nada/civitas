I vote NO.

I do not think this is the right moment to expand the Treasury spending margin from 350M ADA to 500M ADA.

Cardano should be more conservative right now. Market conditions are not strong, and before increasing the Net Change Limit, I believe we should first measure the impact of the current round of withdrawals and understand how Treasury spending affects the ecosystem and the market.

This action does not authorize spending directly, but it does create more room for spending. In my view, the more responsible approach is to remain austere for now and reconsider a higher limit later, once we have better data and clearer results.
