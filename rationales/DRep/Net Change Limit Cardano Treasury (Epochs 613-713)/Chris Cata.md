# Chris Cata

I am voting No on this proposal.

The problem is not that I oppose revisiting the Net Change Limit. The problem is that this proposal makes almost no meaningful effort to justify why the limit should be increased from 350M ADA to 500M ADA.

Its primary argument is that the current limit is becoming constrained because much of it has already been allocated. That is not, by itself, a compelling reason to increase the spending ceiling. The purpose of establishing an NCL is to create a fiscal guardrail in advance—not to simply raise it whenever we approach it.

If the ecosystem believes Cardano should spend more during this period, then present the case. Show the modeling. Explain why 500M is the appropriate figure. Demonstrate what has materially changed since the original NCL was agreed, what additional objectives require funding, and why those objectives justify increasing the spending cap by approximately 43%.

Instead, this proposal largely reiterates how an NCL functions while providing almost no substantive rationale for why this specific increase is warranted. It asks DReps to approve an additional 150M ADA of potential treasury spending with little more than “we’re running out of room.”

The fact that the current NCL is nearly exhausted is not evidence that it was set incorrectly. It simply reflects the funding decisions the ecosystem collectively made for this budget cycle. If additional treasury capacity is genuinely needed, that discussion deserves a thoughtful, evidence-based proposal—not an arbitrary increase supported by minimal justification.

For that reason, I cannot support this proposal.
