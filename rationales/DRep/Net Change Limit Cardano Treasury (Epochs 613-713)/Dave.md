I am voting NO on the Info Action to increase the Net Change Limit to 500 million ADA for Epochs 613 through 713. This decision ultimately rests on a fundamental question of fiscal reality: can we truly justify expanding our spending ceiling during a period of sustained market weakness? Cardano has slipped from the top 10 and continues to struggle within the top 20, reflecting some of our weakest relative market performance to date.

As a DRep, I cannot in good conscience signal approval for an expanded spending cap. We have already exhausted the majority of our original 350 million ADA limit, and simply raising the ceiling to accommodate further drawdown is an unsustainable path forward. At current market rates of roughly $0.17 per ADA, a 500 million ADA limit represents approximately $85 million USD. We are consistently spending far more than the treasury accumulates, maintaining a pattern of deficit spending that cannot continue while broader market conditions remain challenging.

Furthermore, treasury deployment to date has suffered from a lack of broad ecosystem distribution. Capital remains tightly concentrated within a small, isolated circle of familiar entities. While certain voices continue to cheer for unchecked treasury deployment, everyday ADA holders are the ones who bear the tangible cost through persistent sell pressure and suppressed price action.

Voting NO sends a clear, necessary message to both DReps and the broader community that our spending power requires firm boundaries and strategic discipline. Future budget frameworks must be built around a far more thoughtful, equitable allocation process rather than repeatedly lifting guardrails to satisfy the same recipient groups. Ultimately, enforcing a strict cap instills vital investor confidence by proving that Cardano governance is grounded in fiscal responsibility and genuine treasury stewardship.
