The Net Change Limit is a fiscal guardrail, not a budget and not an instruction to spend. Raising it does not approve AlphaGrowth or any other treasury proposal. Every withdrawal must still receive its own DRep support and satisfy the constitutional requirements for administration, oversight, custody, and reporting.
The existing limit is becoming a barrier to considering serious commercial investments through July 2027. An NCL should protect the treasury from uncontrolled spending, but it should not reject future proposals before DReps can examine their merits.
The risk is that additional capacity may encourage weaker proposals or create political pressure to use the full allowance. I accept that risk because DReps retain full authority to reject every withdrawal. The 500 million ADA limit remains a fixed ceiling, and withdrawals already made during the period count toward it.
The treasury exists to protect and advance Cardano. A full treasury attached to a commercially irrelevant network would not be a successful outcome. Cardano must retain the ability to fund credible growth while there is still time for that funding to matter.
I vote Yes.
