This Info Action sets a Net Change Limit of 500,000,000 ADA on total treasury withdrawals for the period from epoch 613 (February 13, 2026) through epoch 713 (on or about July 3, 2027), with withdrawals already debited during the period counting toward the cap. A hard aggregate ceiling is what makes individual withdrawal votes meaningful: it forces proposals to compete within a known budget instead of each being judged against an unbounded treasury, it gives ADA holders a predictable worst-case outflow, and it is the fiscal guardrail the Cardano Constitution's treasury provisions contemplate for withdrawals. Whatever one's view of any single spending proposal, agreeing a defined, binding envelope for the period is basic fiscal discipline, and Socious supports it.
