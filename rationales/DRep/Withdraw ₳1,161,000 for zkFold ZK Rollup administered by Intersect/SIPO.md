SIPO supports the treasury withdrawal of ₳1,161,000 to fund the zkFold ZK Rollup project administered by Intersect. zkFold presents a strategically significant solution to one of Cardano’s most pressing challenges: scalability.

Key Reasons for Support:
	1.	Addresses Scalability Bottlenecks
zkFold leverages ZK rollup technology to batch hundreds of transactions into one. This dramatically reduces L1 congestion and allows higher throughput, lowering transaction costs and wait times.
	2.	Enhances User and Developer Experience
With cheaper and faster transactions, dApps, DeFi protocols, and NFT platforms on Cardano become more user-friendly, boosting adoption.
	3.	Maintains Cardano’s Security Principles
ZK rollups process data off-chain, but verify validity on-chain using zero-knowledge proofs. This upholds decentralization and trustless computation — essential Cardano values.
	4.	Technically Capable Vendor
The zkFold team has a strong background in Haskell, Rust, and cryptography, and has already built a working prototype that fits within Cardano’s execution units.
	5.	Transparent Delivery & Governance
The project will be executed under a legally binding contract via Intersect and CDH, with milestones, audits, and payments clearly defined. Oversight is provided by independent entities like Sundae Labs and Cardano Foundation, and all fund flows are tracked on-chain.
	6.	Long-term Ecosystem Benefits
By enabling efficient Layer 2 scaling on Cardano, zkFold contributes to the long-term competitiveness of the network against L2 solutions on other chains.

SIPO believes that this proposal is a technically mature, governance-aligned, and strategically necessary investment in Cardano’s infrastructure.
