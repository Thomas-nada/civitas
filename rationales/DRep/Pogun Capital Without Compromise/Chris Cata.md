I am voting No.

I understand the potential upside of enabling Bitcoin-related DeFi activity on Cardano, and the appeal of creating new revenue streams for the treasury. However, this proposal still relies on demand that has not yet clearly materialized at scale.

Across the ecosystem, the primary constraint today is not access to new financial primitives, but users, activity, and sustained engagement. Introducing additional infrastructure and complexity ahead of that demand feels premature.

This is not a rejection of the idea itself, but a question of timing and prioritization. I would rather see stronger evidence of user pull and market fit before allocating treasury funds to expand into this direction.
