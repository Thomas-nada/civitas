The inclusion of a structured repayment mechanism and a perpetual 5% protocol dividend fundamentally changes the economic profile of this governance action. As a DRep representing the Latam Cardano community, one of our fundamental pillars is ensuring the long-term financial viability and resilience of the network. Transitioning the treasury into a productive asset allocator that builds external revenue streams directly mitigates the risk of treasury depletion. While the off-chain accounting and governance of EBITDA distributions present a distinct challenge that the community must rigorously audit, the strategic macroeconomic value of anchoring Bitcoin liquidity within Cardano via a self-repaying structure warrants an affirmative vote.
