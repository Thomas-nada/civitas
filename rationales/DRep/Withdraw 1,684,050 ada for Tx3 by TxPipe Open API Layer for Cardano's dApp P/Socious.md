Socious votes Yes. Tx3 is TxPipe's open API layer for dApp protocols: a standard way for applications to describe and construct the transactions a protocol expects, so integrating with a Cardano dApp stops meaning reverse-engineering its datum formats by hand. Cardano's eUTxO model gives strong guarantees but a steep integration curve, and shared transaction-level standards are one of the few levers that genuinely flatten it for every team at once. This is the largest of the TxPipe asks (1,684,050 ADA), reflecting new build work rather than pure maintenance, but it was vetted and approved through the Intersect community budget process alongside the rest of TxPipe's portfolio and is disbursed under milestone-based administration. Consistent with Socious's policy of supporting budget-process withdrawals, we vote Yes.
