I previously voted NO on NCL proposals targeting 300–350 million ADA, as I felt those limits were too high for where Cardano governance is today. Ideally, I'd prefer a lower NCL around 100–150 million, and I hope we can look at that properly for 2026.

This proposal sets the NCL at 200 million, which is more reasonable given the circumstances. With the time pressure and the need to have an NCL in place to allow Treasury spending, I am voting YES on this proposal. It's not perfect, but it's a sensible step for now to keep Cardano moving forwards. 

I'd also like to thank the proposer(s) for bringing this together at short notice.
