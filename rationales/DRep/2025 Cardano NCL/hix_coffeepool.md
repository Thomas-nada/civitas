Each approved budget item will still be subject to further discussion and scrutiny regarding necessity and scale. To preserve the value of ADA, budget distribution must remain minimal or be justified as a cost shared by all holders. I strongly encourage all projects to reconsider and revise their requested amounts to stay within the new NCL. Additionally, I will continue to advocate for reducing the treasury cut in order to support small-scale, community-led governance.
