The proposals currently submitted by IOG — covering Core Development, Catalyst, and Research — already request a total of 193M ADA. Setting the NCL at 200M ADA would nearly exhaust the limit, leaving little room for funding other critical infrastructure proposals. This situation risks hindering Cardano’s growth. Fundamentally, the NCL is not a budget intended to be fully spent, but rather a guardrail that defines the maximum amount that may be withdrawn within a given period. As we are still in the early stages of transitioning to on-chain governance, imposing a restrictive cap at this time could undermine the flexibility needed for effective decision-making and timely response. Moreover, the NCL is not fixed permanently once established. It should be subject to review and adjustment as budget execution progresses and as market conditions evolve. In this transitional period, it is prudent to design with sufficient margin to accommodate uncertainty. Doing so will support sustainable and strategic ecosystem governance.
