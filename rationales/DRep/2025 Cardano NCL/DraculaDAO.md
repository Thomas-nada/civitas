Dracula DAO strongly supports a conservative approach to budgeting and the lower NCL is better for the ecosystem long-term.  ADA not spent today is ADA available tomorrow when priorities and conditions change.  In general we also believe the treasury should provide funding in the form of incentive grants awarded upon project completion, and not a project's entire operating budget.
