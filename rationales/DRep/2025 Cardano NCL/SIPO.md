As the DRep representing SIPO, we vote NO on this governance action proposing to lower the 2025 Net Change Limit (NCL) to 200 million ADA.

Our position is grounded in the strategic necessity of enabling the 275M ADA unified ecosystem budget submitted by Intersect, which is already supported by a majority of DReps and designed to fund 39 critical proposals for infrastructure, community growth, technical innovation, and global expansion.

2025 is a foundational year for Cardano's Voltaire era. Attempting to reduce the already-ratified NCL of 350M ADA at this stage would undermine governance predictability and delay execution of ecosystem-wide development initiatives that have undergone robust community signaling.

It’s crucial to note that:
- The 350M ADA limit is an upper ceiling, not a mandatory spending requirement.
- Emergency or milestone-based Treasury Withdrawals can still be proposed and approved with full transparency.
- Real-world use cases—including Bitcoin DeFi integrations, Midnight privacy architecture, Hydra L2 scaling, and Leios throughput enhancements—require early, coordinated investment that can yield measurable returns within 2–3 years.
- Strategic spending now accelerates Cardano’s evolution into a sovereign, decentralized public infrastructure for digital societies.

We also support the view that NCL should not be interpreted as a fixed cap on spending alone, but rather as a dynamic net limit that can accommodate returns to the treasury via revenue-generating proposals (e.g., DeFi liquidity fund returning 15% monthly yield to the treasury).

Reducing the NCL to 200M ADA at this point would disrupt both the spirit and function of decentralized fiscal governance. Therefore, we reject this proposal and reaffirm our support for the previously approved 350M ADA limit, which reflects both financial prudence and readiness to support Cardano's ambitious roadmap.
