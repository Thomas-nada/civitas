This budget would, if passed, run from “the close of its voting period (13JUN2025) and continue for 73 epochs (52 weeks).” There is currently no in-force or proposed Net Change Limit Info Action covering 2026.

Our governance system incorporates three checks on treasury withdrawals: The in-force Net Change Limit set via Info Action, in-force budget(s) set by Info Action(s), and specific Treasury Withdrawal Governance Actions. The decision to vote for an NCL — whether 350M ada or 200M ada — does not authorize or signal support for spending these full amounts in the given time period. To withdraw ada from the treasury, each Treasury Withdrawal Governance Action must achieve sufficient voting consensus, correspond to an in-force budget, and not exceed the in-force Net Change Limit. My rationale for the in-force Net Change Limit provides my reasons for supporting it, and I’m not aware of convincing reasons to lower the 2025 NCL at this time.
