Without a confirmed Net Change Limit, we cannot proceed to the next steps, and I believe it is important to avoid governance stagnation. Although I can understand why a different NCL proposal is being submitted at this timing, I believe we should respect the already approved limit and move forward. Given these circumstances, I will vote against this "2025 Cardano NCL."
