I vote NO on this proposal for the following reasons:

1) 200M ADA is too low to cover the essential budget needs already submitted, including Core Development (IOG), Catalyst, research, and infrastructure projects such as Hydra, ZK, Midgard, Lucid, and Chainlink. The total for these essential items already exceeds 200M ADA.

2) A low NCL like this would block the approval of critical proposals, even those that are strategic for the growth and security of the Cardano ecosystem.

3) Reducing the NCL abruptly from the previously approved 350M ADA to 200M ADA creates instability and contradicts the governance direction, especially since there was no prior temperature check or broad community consensus.

4) Cardano needs to invest meaningfully to stay competitive in the global top 10. Without sufficient funding to support product development, community builders, and user adoption, Cardano may fall behind—even if the tech is strong.

💬 While I agree that spending must be responsible, budget discipline should come from reviewing each proposal carefully, not from imposing an excessively low, rigid NCL that limits flexibility.

*I would support a moderate NCL in the 250–300M ADA range, which balances sustainability with real ecosystem needs. Let’s not restrict innovation with an artificial spending cap too early in the governance process.
