# 2025 Cardano NCL - Vote NO on 200M ADA Limit

After careful consideration, Socious Drep have decided to vote **NO** on the proposed Net Change Limit (NCL) of 200 million ADA for the remainder of 2025.

## Key Points Against 200M ADA NCL

### 1. Inconsistent with Previous Positions
- Already supported 350M ADA NCL
- 200M ADA is too restrictive for Cardano's current development stage

### 2. Strategic Investment Needs
- Critical ecosystem growth phase
- Essential development initiatives require funding:
  - Scalability upgrades (e.g., Leios)
  - Programmability enhancements
  - Interoperability and security improvements

### 3. Treasury Alignment
- Annual treasury inflow: ~350M ADA
- 200M limit risks underfunding critical initiatives
- Current growth phase requires maintained investment


## Summary
While the cautious approach is appreciated, a 200M ADA NCL is too restrictive for Cardano's current needs. A limit closer to the annual inflow (350M ADA) better balances sustainability with necessary ecosystem investment.
