I have downvoted the preceding two NCL proposals that were targeting 300-350m ADA. I am approving this one. My reasoning for being cautious on NCL has been:

1) Spending is inflationary and leads to ADA sell pressure. 2) Other chains burned a quarter of their tokens to remain top 10, we’re going the other way here, against traffic. 3) Cardano is largely here because of its now ~5 years of unchanged monetary policy and 7-8 years fixed supply. 4) Bitcoin inflation <1% is forcing everyone to be tight on this. 2025 is not 2020. Cardano is low at ~2% but we can’t pump it up by more than a few bps. 5) We need to develop, but inflate yourself away and it’s game over. It’s mostly a monetary not a fiscal game.

We might have more space than 200m. Or even less. It is hard to say without insight into ADA selling by founding entities over the last years. Moreover, market conditions are unpredictable. For all these reasons, we need to start cautiously. My personal preference is 100-150m, but to be a pragmatic governance participant and keep things moving, I will support this proposal.
