---

Although the vision of supporting live dApps via a Builder DAO is promising, I vote NO for the following reasons:

Undefined governance structure: The proposal lacks clear information on DAO membership criteria, decision-making, and accountability. Without a well-defined structure, it risks centralization and inefficiency.

Redundancy & ecosystem overlap: There's insufficient explanation on how this DAO complements – rather than duplicates – existing funding mechanisms like Project Catalyst.

High risk, large budget: Requesting ₳12M without a smaller-scale pilot or proven track record is premature. It is unclear how builder applications, evaluations, and fund disbursement will be handled.

Not yet community-led: Despite aiming to empower builders, the DAO appears top-down and admin-driven at this stage.

I would support a revised, smaller-scope pilot with transparent milestones and decentralized governance design.
