I am voting Yes on reducing the Constitutional Committee minimum size from 7 to 5. In simple terms, this doesn’t change what the Constitutional Committee does—it simply makes it easier to keep the committee functional when members step down, terms expire, or replacements take time to appoint. A minimum of 5 members still provides multiple independent voices and safeguards while reducing the risk of the committee becoming unable to operate due to vacancies. To me, this is a practical governance adjustment that improves continuity and resilience without meaningfully reducing oversight or decentralization.
