I support this proposal.

In my view, the root issue is not the committeeMinSize parameter itself, but the lack of sustainable incentives and compensation for Constitutional Committee (CC) members. Creating an environment where qualified individuals are willing to serve on the CC should be a priority for Cardano governance.

At present, however, the pool of available CC candidates remains limited. With committeeMinSize set equal to the current committee size of seven, even a single resignation or expired term could disrupt governance operations.

While a smaller committee may increase concerns around centralization, I believe this proposal is a pragmatic and necessary measure to reduce the risk of governance paralysis under current conditions.

That said, I view this as a temporary solution. If the number of qualified CC candidates increases and appropriate incentive structures are established in the future, the community should revisit committeeMinSize and reassess what is appropriate for a healthy governance system.

Given the current circumstances, I believe this is a reasonable adjustment and therefore vote Yes.
