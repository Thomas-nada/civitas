# Nimuë Lady of the Lake Pool

**Proposal:** Reduce the committeeMinSize parameter from 7 to 5
**Vote:** Abstain
**Voter ID:** `drep1ygj02llvuusnhphvhlxf7d2cxhs53twjnwwl5at3zwht4xc6xdgl4`

---

I would prefer a CC composed of more individual members rather than consortiums as a solution to the problem this parameter change is trying to address. However, I recognize that this is not what the majority of the community appears to want, and we cannot risk CC fatigue leading to a governance shutdown.

I also believe that lowering this minimum will eventually result in the effective number of CC members falling to this level. In that scenario, assuming there are no abstentions, the effective approval threshold would become 80%, which I consider too high.

Given these conflicting considerations, I will vote ABSTAIN.
