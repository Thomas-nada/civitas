**Proposal:** Reduce the committeeMinSize parameter from 7 to 5
**Vote:** Yes

私は本提案に賛成します。現在のcommitteeMinSize 7は、7人のConstitutional Committeeに対して全く余裕がなく、1名の辞任・任期切れ・活動不能だけで、Parameter Change、Treasury Withdrawal、Hard Forkなど重要なガバナンス行為が停止し得る点に制度的な脆弱性があります。
committeeMinSizeを5に下げることは、CCを5人に縮小することを支持するものではなく、7席体制を維持したまま運用上の安全弁を設ける措置として妥当だと考えます。
ただし、5人CCが常態化すると少数拒否権が強まり、ガバナンスの正統性に影響し得ます。そのため、7席維持、欠員補充プロセスの透明化、CC活動状況の継続的な公開を求めた上で、私はYESを投じます。

---

I support this proposal. The current committeeMinSize of 7 leaves no operational buffer for a 7-member Constitutional Committee. A single resignation, term expiration, or inactive member could make key governance actions such as Parameter Changes, Treasury Withdrawals, and Hard Forks unable to progress.

Reducing committeeMinSize to 5 should not be interpreted as support for reducing the Constitutional Committee to five members. Rather, I see it as a reasonable resilience measure while maintaining the intended 7-seat Committee.

However, a 5-member Committee should not become the normal operating model, as it would increase the relative power of a small number of members to block governance actions. I support this proposal on the condition that the 7-seat structure is maintained, vacancy replacement remains transparent, and CC activity is continuously reported. Therefore, I vote YES.
