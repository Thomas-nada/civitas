# Dan

**Proposal:** Reduce the committeeMinSize parameter from 7 to 5
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1yfsgx4dqfcxxs0ms533twzs7w0cvw8vq8qwzmjyrp280jfsl8gusv`

---

Reducing the minimum CC size from 7 to 5 prevents governance from shutting down if a member resigns, as the process to replace them could take weeks or months.

The CC’s role in governance is a guard rail with well-defined responsibilities, so while it is helpful to maintain 7 members to provide a buffer, the job can be done by a smaller committee if needed (with 4/5 being required to pass a proposal, this change only reduces the required votes by one).
