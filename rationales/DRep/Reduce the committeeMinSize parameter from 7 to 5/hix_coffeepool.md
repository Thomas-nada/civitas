I am voting No on this proposal. Reducing the minimum Constitutional Committee size from 7 to 5 moves governance in the wrong direction: the goal should be to grow participation in this core function, not to shrink it. I would readily support a budget to attract and retain more qualified CC candidates — strengthening the Constitutional Committee is far more valuable to the ecosystem than much of what current treasury proposals fund. This year's approved budget already totals roughly 240M ADA; if we can spend tens of millions on "nodes" and "VC" but cannot spare even 1M ADA to strengthen the Committee, our priorities are broken. A substantively identical proposal was rejected last year, yet nothing was done since then to secure spare seats or otherwise mitigate the underlying risk, nor was a discussion opened on compensation for members — lowering the minimum size does not address that root cause, it only lowers the bar. Serving as a CC member is a genuine honour, but for volunteers it is a heavy personal burden; speaking from my own experience as a former interim orchestrator for the Japan team, I do not believe the candidate pool will grow until the role is made more sustainable and attractive.
