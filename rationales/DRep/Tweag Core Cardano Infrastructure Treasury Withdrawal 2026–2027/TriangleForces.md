Finality time and node storage costs are critical bottlenecks in Cardano's infrastructure. Peras’ promise of slashing finality by 84% is a game-changer if delivered securely and efficiently. 

The budget request of ₳18M though, pegged to a $176/hour senior engineer rate and a conservative ADA/USD ratio, demands scrutiny. While senior talent commands premium rates, the sheer scale here risks inflating operational costs without clear modularity or contingency. The proposal’s insistence on bundling interdependent packages (something I'm NOT a fan of as stated many times) raises delivery risk concentration. 

I remain skeptical about whether this approach is the most cost-effective or if alternative, more modular, or competitive bids could achieve similar results with less treasury exposure. Cardano deserves innovation but not at a price that risks treasury depletion without phased, accountable delivery. I'd like to see sharper modularity, competitive benchmarks, and explicit risk mitigation before committing ₳18M+ on a single, intertwined pipeline.
