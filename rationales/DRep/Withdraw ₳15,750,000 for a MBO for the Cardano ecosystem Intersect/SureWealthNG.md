This proposal funds Intersect, a neutral, nonprofit member-based organization, to provide essential governance, infrastructure, and coordination services to the Cardano ecosystem from June 2025 to June 2026. Intersect plays a critical role in stewarding Cardano’s core codebases, executing the Administrator role under the Constitution, and facilitating participation, budgeting, and roadmap development across the network.

The requested ₳15.75M aligns with the already approved ₳275M ecosystem budget and will enable:

Maintenance and coordination of Cardano node upgrades

Support and stipends for working groups and committees

Facilitation of the Constitutional Committee election

Execution of a transparent budget and RFP process for 2026

On-chain budget disbursement using smart contracts with strong multi-party oversight

Voting YES supports the continuity of decentralized governance, ensures safe network upgrades, and enables a scalable future for Cardano's governance infrastructure.
