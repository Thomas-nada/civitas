# Socious

**Proposal:** Cardano at TOKEN2049 Singapore 2026 Top-Up ‘Title’ Sponsorship Upgrade

**Voter ID:** `drep1ytcv4ax77s0enqef56qjflf4d8zjgxulukme9uf5p8cfaagysjppn`

Socious abstains. This proposal is an incremental 1,768,167 ADA (about $424,360) top-up that would upgrade Cardano's TOKEN2049 sponsorship from "Platinum" to "Title" — a larger booth, a 15-minute mainstage keynote, and additional branding and media access. It is explicitly dependent on the baseline "Platinum" proposal, which Socious supports.

Socious's abstention is a considered position, not indifference. The baseline proposal secures Cardano's presence; this top-up buys purely discretionary premium exposure on top of it. Socious does not hold an evidenced view that the marginal return on the "Title" tier justifies the additional spend over the already-substantial "Platinum" presence — and equally, it does not consider the upgrade unreasonable. This is a judgement call about discretionary marketing, and EMURGO deliberately split it out so DReps could decide it on its own merits.

On a question of that nature, Socious prefers to defer to broader DRep consensus rather than cast a decisive vote in either direction. Abstaining records that the baseline matters and the upgrade is genuinely optional — which is precisely how the proposal was designed to be read.
