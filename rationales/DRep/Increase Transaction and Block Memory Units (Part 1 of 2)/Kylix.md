I very much agree with Pi Lanningham's take on this protocol parameter change. Increasing block mem units is a great way to increase Plutus smart contract transaction throughput for the Cardano ledger without risking an eventual later change (back to a lower value) breaking dApps and protocols that depend on higher tx limits. This will also give us an idea of whether tx limits can and should be changed at a later stage.
