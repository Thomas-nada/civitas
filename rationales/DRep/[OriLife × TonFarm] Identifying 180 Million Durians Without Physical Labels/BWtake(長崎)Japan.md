---

I vote Abstain on this proposal. OriLife × TonFarm has meaningful potential as a real-world adoption case for Cardano, using agricultural traceability, DIDs, CIP-68 NFTs, and eUTXO batching. I also value the idea of using Bio-ID instead of detachable QR labels. However, OriLife is also essentially a customer using Cardano infrastructure for its own business. If the Treasury funds its initial deployment, there must be a clear return mechanism to the Cardano Treasury. The proposal mentions a 20% Treasury fee mechanism, but the scope, duration, enforceability, auditability, and applicability to future crop expansion are not sufficiently clear. I recognize the adoption value, but because the financial return design remains ambiguous, I abstain.
