I voted NO on this proposal.

While I appreciate the real-world adoption effort, I am not convinced blockchain is the optimal solution for agricultural traceability.

The core issue remains the oracle problem: blockchain can preserve submitted data, but it cannot guarantee the data is true.

In practice, food safety and compliance still rely on accountable real-world entities, audits, enforcement, and reputation systems.

Given the current environment, I do not believe this is the best use of Treasury funds.
