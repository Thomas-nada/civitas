I voted YES on the ₳889,500 community exhibition proposal.

My understanding is that the focus of this proposal is not so much on setting up a booth with a large Cardano logo, but rather on supporting Cardano projects to exhibit at a booth.

The funding is designed to be relatively cheaper than the 6 million ADA proposal, and the KPI is that companies that exhibit at the exhibition will be self-sustaining without needing to raise funds from the treasury from the next time onwards.

A close reading of the proposal reveals that financial efficiency has been carefully considered. One clear piece of evidence is that out of the 39 proposals, this is the only one that significantly reduces the ADA requested, based on factors such as the rate.

It is also aligned with the 6 million ADA proposal, so there is no overlap.
