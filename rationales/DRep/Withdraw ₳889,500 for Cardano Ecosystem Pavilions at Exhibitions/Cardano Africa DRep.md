# Cardano Africa DRep

**Proposal:** Withdraw ₳889,500 for Cardano Ecosystem Pavilions at Exhibitions

We vote YES on the treasury withdrawal of ₳889,500 for Cardano Ecosystem Pavilions at Exhibitions based on the rationale below:
Cardano has a brand awareness problem. People know the name, but too few understand what’s actually being built here, dApps, infrastructure, developer tooling, DeFi, and beyond. Exhibitions are one of the few global platforms where ecosystems can put their work in front of investors, enterprises, developers, and builders who are making long-term decisions on where to deploy capital and talent.

This proposal provides a cost-sharing model, not a free ride: projects pay part of their way in, while the treasury lowers the barrier to entry. That balance ensures skin in the game while still enabling small and mid-sized teams to access opportunities otherwise out of reach. Crucially, the vendor also requires pre-event prep and post-event follow-up, which means this isn’t just “show up, look pretty, and leave.” It’s designed for measurable ROI.

The requested amount (889,500 ADA) is significant but has already been reduced from the initial 1.1M ADA to avoid overlap with other community funding. Compared to the potential upside new partnerships, adoption pipelines, and direct visibility of Cardano projects in front of global decision-makers.
