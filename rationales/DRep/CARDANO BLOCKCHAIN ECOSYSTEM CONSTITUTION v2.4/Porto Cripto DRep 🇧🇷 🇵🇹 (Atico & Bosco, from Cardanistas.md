[English]
We chose to vote "YES" on this governance action "CARDANO BLOCKCHAIN ECOSYSTEM CONSTITUTION v2.4" (gov_action1jxne7hynfd7frcczwumd2eggps4kvy0msjztz9t0mutpy870ksgqqp6vp3p), because this new version of the Cardano Constitution introduces fundamental improvements to the maturity of governance. Among the most relevant enhancements is the removal of the Budget Information Action, whose requirements have been properly incorporated into the Treasury Withdrawal Governance Action, resulting in greater clarity and procedural efficiency. The update also introduces the requirement that documents hosted via URLs must be immutable, strengthening transparency, traceability, and legal certainty in governance processes. In addition, the revision includes corrections of typographical errors, simplified wording, and terminology unification, contributing to a clearer, more consistent text aligned with best institutional practices for Cardano’s on-chain governance.
