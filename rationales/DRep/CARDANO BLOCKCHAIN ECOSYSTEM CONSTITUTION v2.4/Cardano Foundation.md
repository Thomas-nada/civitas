Our support for this governance action is based on three key factors:

1. **The Compromise: "Ada Owner" vs. "Ada Holder"** We view the reversion of the term "ada Holder" to "ada owner" as less precise, as it blurs the distinction between protocol-level access (holding keys) and legal ownership rights. However, we accept this regression to accommodate the concerns of other major stakeholders and unblock the constitutional amendment process.  

2. **The Improvement: Streamlined Treasury & Governance Efficiency** We endorse the removal of the **Budget Info Action** requirement. This change aligns with our previous assessments (for v2.0 and v2.3), where we advocated for reducing bureaucratic friction. By consolidating the information requirements directly into the **Treasury Withdrawal** action, we eliminate a redundant administrative step without sacrificing accountability. This streamlining is essential to ensure the treasury can be deployed efficiently to support ecosystem growth and maintenance. Additionally, the new requirement for **immutable proposal documents** is an important security upgrade that protects the integrity of the governance process.  

3. **The Continuity: A Maturing Framework** This version continues the work of maturing the Constitution from an aspirational document to an enforceable rulebook. The removal of non-binding "expectations" (such as Codes of Conduct) clarifies the document’s purpose: it is a set of hard rules for the ledger, not a social manifesto. This focus on enforceability is a necessary evolution for long-term stability.
