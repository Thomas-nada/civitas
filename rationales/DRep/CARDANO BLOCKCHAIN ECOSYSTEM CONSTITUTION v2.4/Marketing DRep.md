I’m abstaining since appears that this GA is destined to pass, but my thoughts/objections are illustrated below.

While I agree the Cardano Constitution needs to be treated as a living document that can be improved over time, and some of the proposed changes in this GA draft have a view toward that very concept, it’s this Drep’s opinion that approaching changing this critical document a year after its implementation may be premature. I also feel that this is a substantial list of amendments. It may be better to take what the proposer feels are the most critically needed items and submit them as their own governance actions instead of taking this “over haul” approach. I also disagree with the removal of budgetary safeguards. We risk re-creating the same legacy system of financial abuse that this ecosystem was designed to move away from.
