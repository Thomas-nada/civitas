Cardano's TypeScript ecosystem runs on HLabs infrastructure whether most people realize it or not. Mesh, Lucid Evolution, and Midgard all depend on it. Keeping that foundation maintained through a hard fork is not optional. Pebble on top of that is a genuine onboarding lever for the millions of TypeScript and Solidity developers who would otherwise face an unnecessary learning curve just to write their first contract. Both parts of this proposal are worth funding.
