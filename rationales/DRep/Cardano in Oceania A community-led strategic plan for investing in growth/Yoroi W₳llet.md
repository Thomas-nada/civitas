Summary

Yoroi DRep votes to ABSTAIN on the governance action to fund Cardano in Oceania: A community-led strategic plan for investing in growth.

Rationale

We appreciate the proposer’s transparency and the good-faith action to reimburse the ₱ 100,000 Ikigai deposit, demonstrating accountability to the community.

While regional engagement is important, the proposal’s focus on events, hackathons, and community activities seems better suited for Project Catalyst. Catalyst provides a collaborative environment to test, refine, and scale these types of initiatives, giving them the best chance to thrive and deliver meaningful impact.
For these reasons, Yoroi DRep chooses to abstain from this governance action.
