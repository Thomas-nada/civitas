I want to credit the team for this excellent proposal, which has made this a difficult decision. However, I will be voting a reluctant **NO** out of fiscal prudence.

Having already supported a number of proposals, I am cautious about the total budget being approved. While I support regional **growth**, the high cost of this single-region pilot makes it a less efficient use of funds than a global framework. Given the need to be selective with the remaining budget, I cannot justify this particular expenditure at this time, despite its merits.
