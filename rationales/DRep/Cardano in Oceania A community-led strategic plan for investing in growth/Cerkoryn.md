Cardano is a global network, and treasury spending should be guided by neutral, impact-first criteria.  Not geography. This proposal targets one region without a convincing strategic rationale (e.g., binding partnerships, regulatory advantages, or meaningful co-funding) that justifies prioritizing Oceania over any other region.  I worry that approving it would set a precedent for generic regional asks from everywhere.  For these reasons, I am voting no on this proposal.
