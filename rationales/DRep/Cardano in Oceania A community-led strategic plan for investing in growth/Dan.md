I am voting Yes on this proposal because I see strong potential in Oceania as a testbed for Cardano adoption. The region combines diverse economies, cultures, and industries, and has historically been used as a proving ground for new technologies. The team has put forward a structured plan that invests in people, projects, and outreach, and the funding ask is reasonable given the scope and ambition. I believe this initiative can demonstrate how decentralised, community-led growth models work in practice and create a repeatable template for other regions.

That said, given that this Info Action appears unlikely to pass at this stage, it may be best for the team to pursue Catalyst funding in the short term. This would allow them to deliver visible, high-impact results, build measurable traction, and strengthen confidence before seeking a treasury withdrawal. Nonetheless, I support this initiative because at this stage in Cardano’s growth we need more pathways for real-world adoption. Cardano is already a technical achievement, but without tangible use cases and compelling success stories, adoption risks falling behind other chains. Oceania is well-positioned to help close that gap, and this proposal is a step in the right direction.
