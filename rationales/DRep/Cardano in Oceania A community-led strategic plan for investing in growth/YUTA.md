"Cardano in Oceania" is an inspiring, community-led initiative, and I deeply respect the team’s passion. However, I voted No, as I believe it would be more appropriate to start with a smaller budget through Catalyst.
While the vision is compelling, its success heavily depends on the team’s execution. Factors such as content quality, DRep development, business partnerships, and local transaction growth are all difficult to forecast and require significant trust.
Many similar initiatives have received funding in the past, yet few have delivered clear, measurable outcomes. Therefore, I recommend starting small and building up success incrementally.

https://cardano-in-oceania.github.io/how/

KPI-related Concerns:
1. Tracking wallets and transactions specific to Oceania seems difficult and potentially impractical.
2. The goal of onboarding three new Catalyst participants feels modest compared to the ecosystem’s scale.
3. Government or partner collaborations should be proposed with specific scopes and budget allocations.
4. BD training plans should identify candidates, their backgrounds, and expected results up front.
5. Since Oceania is largely English-speaking, global outreach in English may be more effective than region-specific campaigns.

KPIに関する懸念点：
1. オセアニア地域に限定したウォレット数やトランザクション数の追跡は、現実的に困難と思われます。

3. 政府や団体との連携は、具体的な内容と予算を明記した形で提示すべきです。
4. BD人材の育成については、候補者のプロフィールや期待される成果を事前に示すことが望ましいです。
