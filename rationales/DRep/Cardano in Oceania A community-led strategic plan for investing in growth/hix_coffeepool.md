I am voting "No" on this Governance Action. While the proposal outlines objectives, it does not provide clear and verifiable information about who will ultimately receive and manage the funds. Without explicit identification of the responsible entity, the transfer of treasury resources cannot be evaluated with confidence. Transparency and accountability are essential for any funding decision, and the lack of clarity in this case poses an unacceptable risk. For this reason, I cannot support the proposal in its current form.
