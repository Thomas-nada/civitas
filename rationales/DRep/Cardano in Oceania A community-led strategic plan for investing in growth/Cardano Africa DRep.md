# Cardano Africa DRep

**Proposal:** Cardano in Oceania A community-led strategic plan for investing in growth
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1ytj9kz0n5yamacgcj8jr6apn36hx5suzqpefxvhy4cvfqmsqangtx`

---

We have voted No for the proposal Cardano in Oceania: A community-led strategic plan for investing in growth. While the initiative outlines an ambitious vision for strengthening Cardano’s presence in the region, we believe the current design raises more concerns than it resolves.

The requested budget of 778,000 ADA feels disproportionate when weighed against the activities described. Community-building, hackathons, and awareness campaigns are valuable, but their impact is often difficult to measure, and the return on investment is not clearly demonstrated. Without stronger guarantees of tangible adoption or measurable on-chain activity, this level of funding is hard to justify.

We are also cautious about the heavy reliance on a single administrator, selfdriven Services. Although the proposal describes checks and dispute resolution mechanisms, entrusting so much control to one entity introduces risks of centralisation and inefficiency. A broader coalition of administrators would have provided greater confidence.

Finally, while Oceania is important, other regions currently show stronger demand and higher growth potential. Given limited treasury resources, we believe Cardano’s focus should remain on areas where adoption barriers are higher and impact is clearer. For these reasons, we cannot support this proposal in its current form.
