The Cardano in Oceania governance proposal merits serious consideration and clearly would provide value.  However, it aligns closely with many very similar Catalyst proposals in form, scope, and scale.  Evaluating similar proposals concurrently allows for direct comparison, better prioritization, and more efficient use of funds. Letting each proposal go through its own direct vote weakens the cumulative knowledge and institutional continuity within the Catalyst framework, and will overload the direct voting process.

Because this proposal lies squarely in the domain that Catalyst was created for, I can not approve it directly as an end-run around that system and its checks and balances where it properly belongs.
