# Chris Cata

Voting yes on this proposal for a few key reasons.  Instead of a broad or sweeping growth initiative it's focused on a region, Oceania. From there this team has put forth a clear proposal on the who, what, how, and why. The funding ask, 778K ADA, to me seems to be about as lean as possible. I reached out to the team for feedback on this and it's intentional - minimum required to get the tasks completed. Ultimately, this proposal should help to establish putting down roots in the region and overall awareness for Cardano. We should be able to support this localized initiative for Cardano for these costs and look forward to 2026-2027 budgeting to determine if it's something we want to continue.

One line item that would have seen this proposal come down another 100K, which did raise an eyebrow, was the 100K reimbursement for the Ikigai action. I personally had thought that issue was resolved but if not, it should be reimbursed - just not sure it needed to happen bundled with this proposal.  However, given the team involved I don't have any concerns around this line item - it's the right thing to do - and up to this point it has not been addressed by anyone else.

Over time, I've come to not want to vote no anymore on any project that could have gone another avenue for funding (ie. Project Catalyst). The reason being is that it pigeon holes it to a specific process that not everyone may be using or want to. The treasury is more open and available to all in my opinion, so I will always review a proposal as is.
