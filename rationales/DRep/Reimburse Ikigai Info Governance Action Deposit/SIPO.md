SIPO DRep Statement on “Reimburse Ikigai Info Governance Action Deposit”

As SIPO, I voted Yes on the “Reimburse Ikigai Info Governance Action Deposit” proposal.
I believe this proposal represents an important step toward strengthening the integrity and fairness of Cardano’s on-chain governance system.

The original “Ikigai” Info Action, submitted shortly after the Chang hard fork in 2024, was a symbolic governance proposal.
It didn’t request funding or technical changes, but simply expressed gratitude to everyone who helped bring Cardano this far and hope for the future.
It was a gesture that captured the community’s spirit — the idea that Cardano is not only a technology, but a shared journey built on human trust and collaboration.

Unfortunately, due to a node bug that allowed an unregistered stake key to be used, the proposer of the Ikigai Action lost their 100,000 ADA deposit permanently.
At the time, many community members agreed that this deposit should be reimbursed, as it was lost not through user error, but through a software fault during the earliest stage of Cardano’s governance.
However, the treasury reimbursement capability did not yet exist then.
Now that this capability has been introduced through the Plomin hard fork, it is both fair and responsible for the community to finally resolve this unfinished issue.

This reimbursement proposal is not about rewriting history, nor about reversing the blockchain.
It does not attempt to roll back any transaction or modify the immutable ledger.
Instead, it uses the legitimate constitutional process — an on-chain treasury withdrawal — to correct a past injustice in a transparent and auditable manner.
It is a clear example of how Cardano’s governance can act with compassion and fairness while preserving immutability and rule of law.

From a DRep perspective, this proposal is constitutionally sound and technically safe.
It has clear parameters (a fixed reimbursement amount of 103,000 ADA), complies with the current Net Change Limit, and specifies DReps as administrators for execution oversight.
Funds are distributed directly to the affected individual, allowing full on-chain transparency and community auditability.

Supporting this proposal also sets an important precedent for the future.
It demonstrates that our governance framework can respond to legitimate errors in good faith — not by changing the past, but by taking responsible action within the rules of the system.
In this way, Cardano shows that governance on a blockchain can be both human and principled.

As SIPO, I support this proposal not only as an act of fairness, but also as a symbolic gesture that honors the early pioneers of Cardano governance.
The “Ikigai” proposal expressed the soul of our ecosystem — gratitude, hope, and shared purpose.
Approving this reimbursement is a way of keeping that spirit alive, showing that Cardano’s governance is built on empathy as much as it is on code.

Therefore, I, SIPO, have voted Yes — to uphold the integrity of our governance, to close a chapter that began with goodwill, and to remind ourselves that Cardano’s true strength lies in its community’s sense of justice and care.

これはユーザーのミスではなく、カルダノ・ガバナンスの初期段階で発生したソフトウェア上の不具合によるものでした。
多くのコミュニティメンバーが「このデポジットは返還すべきだ」と賛同したものの、当時はトレジャリーからの払い戻し機能がまだ存在していませんでした。
そしてPlominハードフォークでその機能が実装された今、未解決のまま残してきたこの問題を制度として正しく処理することが、私たちコミュニティの責任だと感じます。

むしろこれは、憲法に基づいた正当な手続き──すなわちオンチェーンのトレジャリー支払い──を通じて、
透明かつ監査可能な形で過去の不正を是正する行為です。
カルダノが「不変性」と「人間的な公正さ」を両立できることを示す好例だと思います。

請求額（103,000 ADA）は明確に定義され、ネットチェンジリミットを超えず、DRepが実行監視の管理者として指定されています。
資金は直接受取人に支払われ、オンチェーン上で完全に透明に監査可能です。

さらに、この提案を支持することは今後への重要な前例になります。
カルダノのガバナンス制度が、誤りや不具合に対して「過去を書き換える」のではなく、
「制度の中で誠実に修復する」ことができるという証明になるからです。
それはつまり、ブロックチェーン・ガバナンスが“人間的でありながら原則的”であることを示す実例です。
