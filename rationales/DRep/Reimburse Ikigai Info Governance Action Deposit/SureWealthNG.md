I vote YES to reimburse the 103,000 ADA to the submitter of the Ikigai Info Governance Action.
This was one of the first symbolic actions submitted after the Chang hard fork intended to thank Cardano’s builders and express hope for the future. Due to a verified bug in the Cardano node (allowing an unregistered stake key), the submitter permanently lost their 100,000 ADA deposit through no fault of their own.
The community strongly supported reimbursement at the time. Despite inclusion in the Cardano in Oceania budget proposal, it was not enacted due to the package’s overall rejection—not opposition to this item.

The requested 103,000 ADA covers:

100,000 ADA (original deposit)
3,000 ADA (conservative compensation for lost staking rewards, Midnight airdrop eligibility, and opportunity cost)

This action is:

i. Constitutionally compliant (Articles III.5, IV.2–4)
ii. Fully on-chain auditable
iii. Negligible treasury impact (<0.0003%)
iv. A one-time fix, not a precedent for misuse

Voting YES honors early governance pioneers, corrects a launch-era software flaw, and fulfills a clear community commitment.
Early participants should not bear the cost of ecosystem bugs. This is about fairness, trust, and closing a small but symbolic debt from Cardano’s on-chain governance dawn.
