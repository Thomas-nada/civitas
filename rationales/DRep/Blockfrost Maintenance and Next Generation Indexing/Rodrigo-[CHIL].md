Blockfrost is important infrastructure for Cardano, and I recognize the value it has provided to builders over the years.

I also like the direction of Project Cayley. If Cardano scales with Leios, indexing costs will become a real issue, and we need more decentralized and affordable ways to serve blockchain data.

My concern is the operational subsidy. I understand why it may be needed now, but I don’t want the treasury to become a recurring support mechanism for one dominant provider.

I would like to see a clearer path showing how this funding reduces dependency over time, brings more operators into the system, and makes the free-tier model sustainable without repeated treasury requests.

For me, the key question is whether this proposal moves us toward decentralization, or just helps maintain the current dependency for longer.
