I am a big supporter of Blockfrost and have used their API many times myself, but this proposal is difficult to justify spending treasury funds on at this current time.  Project Cayley is an interesting idea and the prospect of SPOs being able to serve data slices as an additional form of revenue is enticing, but I struggle to see them earning enough through this.  Especially when there are several other competing API services already such as Koiois and Maestro, as well as many other locally-hosted tools that can connect to the Cardano network and serve data.  I'd like to see Blockfrost succeed, but at this current point in time this proposal is a NO for me.
