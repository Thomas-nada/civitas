# Goofycrisp

**Proposal:** Blockfrost Maintenance and Next Generation Indexing
**Vote:** Abstain
**Voter ID:** `drep1ygxasnzmrevqra9vscpsy4xf4rqthzkp6rr6zzft2lc7nscahj9r9`

Blockfrost has clearly provided useful infrastructure and value to the Cardano ecosystem. However, there are fair concerns about using treasury funds to subsidize infrastructure that also has commercial users and paid services, especially when alternatives exist. I recognize the importance of reliable indexing and developer infrastructure, but I am not fully convinced this is the right treasury allocation at this stage.
