I voted NO because Blockfrost has already received significant Treasury funding and operates as a private business with paid services. The proposal lacks transparency on financial sustainability and does not justify why additional subsidies are needed. Cardano already has multiple infrastructure providers, including Maestro, Cardanoscan, and decentralized options like Koios. Funding one provider risks centralization and unfair competitive advantages. I also disagree with bundling Project Cayley development and Blockfrost operational funding into a single vote instead of evaluating them separately so we can better utilize and optimize our limited treasury.
