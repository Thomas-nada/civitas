I vote NO to "Blockfrost: Maintenance and Next Generation Indexing."

1. Rather than positioning Blockfrost as a public service, I believe it's better overall to position it as a service competing with other API providers, designing it as a pathway to guide users from the free tier to the paid tier.

If it's positioned as a public service and eliminates other providers, there will be no competition within the ecosystem, and although a commercial tier is suggested in the proposal, its design priority will likely not increase. This includes not only price competition but also competition in terms of features, etc.

I myself started using Blockfrost in the free tier last month, moved to the paid tier, and have now moved back to the free tier because I have my own server. I am one of the users for whom the free tier acted as a pathway to the paid tier.

2. When using Treasury, I think it's beneficial to present a monetization and repayment plan, as seen in the current Eternl proposal. This would alleviate the concerns mentioned in point 1.
