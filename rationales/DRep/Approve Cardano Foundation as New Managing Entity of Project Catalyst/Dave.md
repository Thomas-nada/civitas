I am voting **YES** on this Info Action to appoint the Cardano Foundation (CF) as the new managing entity for Project Catalyst. 

Before addressing the administrative transition, I want to express my profound appreciation for Project Catalyst, its hard-working team, and its massive impact on our ecosystem. The innovation and Catalyst-funded tooling were exactly what inspired me to personally learn and build on Cardano. It has been a foundational pillar of our community's growth and onboarding.

As we enter this new phase, I also want to state my sincere appreciation for the Cardano Foundation stepping up to take on this massive responsibility. Ensuring operational continuity so that builders from Funds 10-14 receive their milestone payouts without disruption is critical for maintaining trust with the teams doing the hard work. 

From a fiscally conservative perspective, this transition is the most responsible path forward. Not only does it protect active builders, but it also secures CF's explicit commitment to return the previously approved allocations for Funds 15 and 16 directly back to the Cardano Treasury. This aligns perfectly with my mandate to enforce strict fiscal discipline and protect our treasury by recovering these unspent funds. A clean handover to CF honors Catalyst's legacy, protects our builders, replenishes our treasury, and provides a much-needed reset.
