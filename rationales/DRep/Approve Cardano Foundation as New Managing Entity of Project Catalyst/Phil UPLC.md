I do not believe CF has the relevant experience to manage an ecosystem grant system. Their perspective on the primary markets for Blockchain is incompatible with years of demonstrated history and the current market reality. CF has always prioritized supply-chain tracking, decentralized identity, and web2 partnerships (FIFA, FC Barcelona, Dubai missile tracking, and so on) and indicated essentially zero interest in the DeFi market, or in marketing / assisting any of the DeFi projects building on Cardano. This is incredibly dangerous, as it ignores the current reality which is that practical all onchain activity in the blockchain space is in the DeFi market, that DeFi is the only market in the blockchain domain to achieve product market fit. CF will likely guide catalyst towards funding dead markets like web3 supply-chain tracking solutions, decentralized identity, and other non-DeFi focused funds, as opposed to focusing primarily on the existing market (DeFi) and having smaller allocations for experimental markets that have yet to achieve any success (blockchain supply-chain tracking, DIDs, and so on)
