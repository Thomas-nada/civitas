**Proposal:** Approve Cardano Foundation as New Managing Entity of Project Catalyst

---

Project Catalyst is one of the largest decentralized grant programs in the blockchain industry, having funded over 2,200 projects with more than 500 still active. Continuity of operations for these active grantees is paramount. The transition of the Catalyst team from IOG to the Cardano Foundation ensures that existing commitments are honored, milestone payments continue, and grantees are not left without administrative support.
The Cardano Foundation is a non-profit entity whose mission is explicitly focused on the long-term stewardship and adoption of the Cardano ecosystem. Moving Catalyst management from a commercial entity (IOG) to the Foundation aligns the program’s governance structure with its public-goods purpose. This separation also strengthens the argument for Cardano’s decentralization from a regulatory perspective, as the funding mechanism is no longer directed by a single private company.
The decision to pause Fund15 and Fund16 and return unallocated ada to the treasury demonstrates responsible capital management. Rather than continuing to deploy funds through a system undergoing structural transition, the Foundation is choosing to stabilize operations first and redesign the process before committing new capital. This is a sign of governance maturity.
The transition also creates an opportunity to address long-standing community criticisms of Catalyst, including concerns about accountability, the impact of funded projects, and the need for independent audits. The Foundation has signaled a more rigorous, high-assurance approach to grant administration, which should improve the quality of funded projects and strengthen community trust in the program.
Both IOG and the Cardano Foundation support this transition, and community forums show active discussion and broad acknowledgment that change was needed. Voting YES enables a smooth handover that protects existing grantees, strengthens governance, and positions Catalyst for a more transparent and accountable future under Foundation stewardship.
