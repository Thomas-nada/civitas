# Cerkoryn

**Proposal:** Approve Cardano Foundation as New Managing Entity of Project Catalyst
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1yglrf4el8gghum239fggvfrau25k2576y4dvcz65r2ukj8sqpsc2k`

I am voting YES on this proposal because I believe the existence of an Innovation fund is important to the growth and success of Cardano.  Historically I have been frustrated with the lack of much-needed improvements to the Catalyst program and I am optimistic that improvements will be made under new management.  In particular I would like to see the ability to vote entirely via web/desktop without the use of a mobile app.  I'd like to drop the requirement for registration at all, though it could optionally be used to link wallets together so that you can vote only a single time with more than one wallet.  I'd like to fix proposal/text spam and quality, especially now in the age of AI.  I'd also like to see more specific categories that are laser-targeted at exactly the products and use cases Cardano most desperately needs.
