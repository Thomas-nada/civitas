SIPO DRep votes YES on Approve Cardano Foundation as New Managing Entity of Project Catalyst.

This Info Action is a necessary governance step to ensure continuity of Project Catalyst operations. With IOG stepping back from its administrator role, the Cardano Foundation is the only viable entity positioned to assume management of the Catalyst Foundation Company (CFC) and protect the 500+ active projects currently awaiting milestone reviews and fund disbursements.

Why SIPO votes YES

1. Protecting 500+ active projects is the immediate priority
Project Catalyst has distributed over $150 million across 2,200+ projects since inception. More than 500 projects from Funds 10-14 are currently active with outstanding milestones and pending disbursements. Without a legitimate administrator for the CFC, there is no legal entity authorized to approve milestones and release funds. Delaying this transition directly harms builders who are relying on committed funding.

2. There is no alternative candidate
IOG has signaled its withdrawal from the Catalyst administrator role. This is not a choice between CF and another entity — it is a choice between CF and a governance vacuum. If not approved by end of May 2026, Catalyst operations face an undetermined pause with no clear path to resumption.

3. CFC statutes legally require community approval
The Catalyst Foundation Company was designed with a governance safeguard: any change of administrator must be validated by the Cardano community. This Info Action fulfills that requirement. It is a procedural necessity, not a policy endorsement of any particular vision for Catalyst's future.

4. CF is institutionally appropriate for this role
The transition from a product organization (IOG) to a mission-driven Swiss nonprofit foundation (CF) reflects the maturation of Cardano's governance. CF has already begun onboarding Catalyst team members to ensure operational continuity.

5. Fund 15/16 Treasury return is accelerated by approval
CF has committed to working with Intersect to return the Fund 15/16 allocations (18.5M ADA + 250K USDM) to the Cardano Treasury as quickly as possible following approval.

6. Future Catalyst direction remains under DRep governance
Approving CF as administrator does not lock in any model for future Catalyst rounds. Under the 2026 Budget Process Framework, future Catalyst funding will need to be proposed through Intersect's budget process and approved by DReps.

Expectations

- Expeditious milestone administration: Clear the backlog of milestone reviews and disbursements for Funds 10-14 without further delay.
- Transparent reporting on transition progress: Regular updates on timeline, team onboarding, and operational readiness.
- Fund 15/16 Treasury return timeline: A concrete timeline, not an open-ended commitment.
- Catalyst redesign with community input: Meaningful consultation on future direction, addressing regionalization risks (governance capture, fragmentation, cultural bias).
- Avoiding excessive role concentration: CF assumes multiple governance roles (Catalyst, Orion Fund, ecosystem steward). Clear separation of responsibilities and transparent accountability for each.
- CFC governance accountability: The same transparency, reporting, and oversight standards demanded of all treasury-funded operations.

This is not a vote about the future design of Project Catalyst. It is a vote about ensuring 500+ active projects receive their promised funding and the transition proceeds without a governance vacuum.

For these reasons, SIPO DRep votes YES.
