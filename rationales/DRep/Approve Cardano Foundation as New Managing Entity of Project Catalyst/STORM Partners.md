# STORM Partners

**Proposal:** Approve Cardano Foundation as New Managing Entity of Project Catalyst
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1yf0s5guxklwlqn7ttdpfwmmkaucwaqkkm6hzncy8nful5fgemurcj`

---

We vote YES. The CFC's governing statutes require a community decision to transfer management to a new Administrator, and without one by end of May, fund disbursements to teams delivering on Funds 10-14 face an uncertain pause. Supporting continuity for those builders is the right call.

We do want to note that the communication surrounding the Fund 15 and 16 pause fell short. Proposers invested significant time preparing submissions in good faith, received little explanation mid-process, and were left without clarity for an extended period. We acknowledge that, and we expect Cardano Foundation to carry that lesson into how it manages the programme going forward.

Catalyst has produced real value for the ecosystem and has also accumulated real lessons about process design, accountability, and what a public funding mechanism should optimize for. We see this transition as an opportunity to act on those lessons rather than carry forward the patterns that didn't work.
