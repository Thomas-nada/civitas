I vote NO on this proposal.

While I acknowledge Snek’s contribution to Cardano's visibility, using ₳5 million from the public treasury to fund CEX listings for a single community token raises significant concerns about precedent, risk, and fairness. Exchange listings are traditionally private investments with high cost and uncertain returns — not guaranteed public goods.

This proposal’s benefits to the broader Cardano ecosystem are largely indirect and speculative, and the execution — while backed by experience — remains centered around a single token’s success.

I believe the treasury should focus on neutral infrastructure, ecosystem-wide tools, and long-term decentralization — not funding centralized listings or liquidity for individual tokens.
