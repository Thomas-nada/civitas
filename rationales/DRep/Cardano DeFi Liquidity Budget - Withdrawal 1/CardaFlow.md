By contrast with recent Pentad investment for infrastructure which could contribute to attract liquidity, the Cardano DeFi Liquidity Budget governance action consists in a direct spending of ADA for USD-based stablecoins.

As I do not trust in US debt management, trajectory, and do not believe in related fiat appreciation Vs $ADA, I did not support Gov Action and thus does not support corresponding treasury withdrawal.
