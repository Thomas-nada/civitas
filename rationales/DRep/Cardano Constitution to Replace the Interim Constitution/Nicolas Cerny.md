I am voting Yes as a DRep on governance action ID `gov_action133jnaewfsq8x6v08ndd87l2yqryp63r30t2dkceacxx5cply5n7sqzlcyqf`, which seeks to replace the Interim Constitution with the delegate-approved Constitution, approved at the Constitutional Convention in Buenos Aires and Nairobi in December 2024. My vote is based not on my involvement in co-drafting the document, but on the clear improvements it offers over the Interim Constitution, which lacked extensive community consultation and feedback.

**Key Improvements:**

- **Cardano Blockchain Tenets:** A set of ten guiding principles that will serve as the north star for all future governance actions.
- **Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget:** A new article that enables the management of multiple budgets and administrators, mitigating potential single points of failure. Although it mandates that administrators can only auto-abstain with the voting power derived from treasury withdrawals (instead of delegating it to SPOs for yield), this restriction is acceptable given that administrators hold funds only for a limited period.

**Areas for Acceptable Refinement:**

While I would have preferred additional measures—such as including definitions in the main body of the Constitution to guide the Constitutional Committee (CC), enhancing the accessibility and clarity of the text, focusing solely on ada holders (thus avoiding the complexities of traditional legal frameworks for ada owners), and providing a more detailed CC election process—these issues do not outweigh the overall benefits of the new Constitution.

**Future Necessary Amendments:**

- **Info Actions Consistency:** Simplify the section so that the Constitutional Committee clearly assesses the constitutionality of all governance action types, including info actions. (Article 3(4))
- **TENET 5 and Staking Rewards:** Clarify that withholding accrued staking rewards until a stake credential is delegated to a DRep does not constitute locked value, potentially by redefining TENET 5 to only apply to UTxOs, special thanks to @HephyPool for sharing this idea. (Article 1(1))
- **Treasury Funds Delegation:** Although the Constitution restricts treasury funds held by a budget administrator from being delegated to an SPO—which may appear to conflict with staking as a security mechanism—the limited holding period and the relatively low amounts involved mitigate this risk. (Article 4(5))
- **Time Measurement:** Replace epoch-based measurements with conventional timeframes, as epoch length is a mutable protocol constant. (Article 4(1))
- **Code of Conducts:** Rather than enforcing rigid codes of conduct for governance roles, a framework that encourages ethical and transparent behavior may suffice. (Article 5(3), Article 6(3), Article 7(6))

In conclusion, the delegate-approved Constitution is a clear improvement over the Interim Constitution. Not adopting it would introduce greater risks, especially given the ongoing discussions around budgets. I remain committed to actively contributing to future amendments and the continued evolution of Cardano’s governance framework.
