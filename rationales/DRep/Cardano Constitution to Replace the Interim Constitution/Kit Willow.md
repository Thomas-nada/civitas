# Kit Willow

**Proposal:** Cardano Constitution to Replace the Interim Constitution
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1ygk7qax0ztrv7a7unzgs4apj5mhk5a0058ne3fgfftj4tmgvuk5a7`

---

As a community member and a Cardano Constitutional Alternate, I have been actively involved in the constitution development process and governance-related matters since around the time of its inception. 

The constitutional workshops and convention captured as much input from the community as it was structured to capture. 
As someone who participated, I believe some of the processes involving those workshops and the convention were done well enough, but I have to admit that some of the processes could have been done quite better than they were done. 

Amongst the issues that I have with the constitutional process, is the fact that the delegates and alternates were not allowed to touch the guardrails which directly hindered any form of appeal in reducing the hard capital requirements of 100k ADA to initiate any governance action. This especially disturbs me as someone who believes in financial inclusivity for all. 

The issue of exchanges being able to vote with deposited ADA was also one part of the constitution that left a bad taste in my mouth, but for now, I see a temporary fix in compelling every ADA holder to remove their assets from exchanges and utilize native wallets. 

From behind the lens of a DRep, these are real issues that I think should be tackled and it’d have been nicer if we had focused more on them at the convention, at least in my opinion. 

I am voting yes, however, because of the following reasons; 

1. While the constitution as it stands is not 100% to my liking, I do think that the inputs from the delegates, alternates and the community have helped improve it a bit better from the original draft and as such it stands quite improved and more progressive in contrast to the Interim Constitution. 

2. While I believe the delegates and alternates were restricted in their inputs by factors like time constraints and verbal restrictions on the guardrails, the fact of the matter is that there is currently no other draft of a Cardano constitution that has a wider and more public scope of inputs than the current one. 

3. The issues I have with this current draft and its process are numerous — some of the more serious ones I have listed above. But time and time again, the ecosystem has surprised me with its capabilities to rally around a cause in solidarity. It was done for Phil Chang and then for Mathew Plomin. This is why I am confident that should any of those issues with the constitution become immediately disastrous, the community can socialize and come together to push a change.
