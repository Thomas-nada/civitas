I'm concerned about the 1 token 1 vote voting model. 

It is not representative of the will of the Community or even representative of the involvement and interest of the individual in the success of Cardano. 

It is a flawed model as it was shown in the last round of Project Catalyst, a model that makes it very exclusive for newcomers. 

Would prefer a better model, that considers other things such as contribution to Cardano,  time spent in Cardano, knowledge on a certain topic, or even the percentage of wealth Cardano represents in the overall wealth of the voter. An algorithm that takes different to make some kind of weight voting.

We need to explore better and fairer models, and the first governance system should already include such fairer models. It shouldn't be biased from the start, as we risk not being able to change it later on.

Additionally and considering this is the voting model we will use from now onwards, I'm extremely concerned about the real possibility, that when we find a better, fairer, and more representative business model, we won't be able to upgrade it as large whales and token holders will likely not be receptive to losing voting power and influence regarding the future of Cardano, especially considering that there are several billion dollars at stake. 

From the Content of the Constitution, would like to see clearer boundaries and principles regarding the administration of funds from the budget.

While there are other minor concerns with this Constitution, especially within the lack of a clearer more straightforward mention relative to decentralization and peer review research, it is the flawed voting model that is the major concern.
