I vote in favor of the Cardano Constitution because it establishes a structured, decentralized governance system that empowers the community, ensuring transparency and long-term stability. The extensive consultation process and the principles embedded in CIP-1694 provide a solid foundation for decision-making that aligns with the core values of decentralization, representation, and fairness.

However, my support comes with reservations regarding areas that require further refinement, such as enhancing oversight of the Constitutional Committee, preventing power concentration among DReps, improving the efficiency of constitutional amendments, and ensuring full transparency in treasury allocations.
