**Vote:** No

I believe that undue pressure was put upon those working on drafting the Constitutional text to have a Ratified Hash of a Final Draft for Proposal On-Chain before 31 Jan 2025, which resulted in an incomplete document.

I believe that the definitions should form a part of the main Constitutional text, in order to make them actually binding, instead of just being 'optional guidance' for the CC. The definitions forming a part of the text would also ensure they have a high Threshold to be changed.
