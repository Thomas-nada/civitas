**Voter ID:** `drep1yfnuetvunwfl83qaq4e7vlg5w8dwwjmtt4j6tr9fegqvgmgtq5scg`

---

I find the constitution overly long, and would have preferred it to be a one page document. The long form potentially facilitates the creation of a Cardano bureaucracy, which I think is something we should avoid or at least minimise at all costs. 

I am not a fan of some specific aspects like the support for off-chain governance, the prominent role of the constitutional committee or the overly precise limits and guardrails, which I think would ideally be decided by individual votes.

Nevertheless, I agree strongly with the 10 tenets and with the overall tone and scope of the document, while I observe no items that clash directly with my principles. Hence I will vote in favour.
