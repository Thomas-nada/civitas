I vote YES for this proposal because:

1) It aligns with the 2025 roadmap approved by DReps, where node diversity is a strategic priority to improve decentralization and resilience.

2) Amaru reduces reliance on the Haskell node maintained by IOG, strengthening network security and fault tolerance—similar to how Ethereum benefits from multiple independent clients.

3) The development team is highly reputable, with members from dcSpark, TxPipe, Cardano Foundation, and others. They are also actively coordinating with IOG’s Haskell team to ensure compatibility.

4) The requested budget of 1.5M ADA (for 6 months) is reasonable, with clear smart contract-based fund management and oversight by PRAGMA.

5) As an open-source public good, Amaru strengthens Cardano’s infrastructure and invites broader participation from Rust developers.

* Amaru is not just an alternative node—it’s a strategic investment in a more decentralized, robust, and future-ready Cardano.
