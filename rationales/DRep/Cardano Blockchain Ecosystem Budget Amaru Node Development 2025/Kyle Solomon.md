I have voted YES on the current proposal to fund Amaru.

I firmly believe our absolute top priority must be establishing ecosystem sustainability. While introducing new alternative block-producing nodes certainly entails potential to introduce delays in releasing mission-critical scalability components due to the necessity of maintaining compatibility across nodes, I believe the Amaru initiative is critical to solidifying the censorship-resistance of the Cardano mission and introduces a potential path for more cost-effective node development in the long-term. I also believe learnings from building out an alternative node inherently lend to improve the overall integrity of the existing Haskell node.

The Amaru team is solid, reputable and well-deserving of the immense responsibility.
