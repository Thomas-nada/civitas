Krypto Labs as DRep votes NO on the Cardano Treasury DeFi Liquidity Budget. A robust stablecoin ecosystem is organically built through community adoption and developer innovation, not by deploying treasury funds. The inherent yield generated from fiat-backed stablecoin reserves represents a perpetual income stream. Diverting this significant, long-term benefit to commercial entities without a substantial, independent stake in the Cardano ecosystem is fiscally irresponsible. This proposal risks subsidizing external ventures at the community's expense, rather than fostering genuine, self-sustaining growth within our decentralized finance landscape.
