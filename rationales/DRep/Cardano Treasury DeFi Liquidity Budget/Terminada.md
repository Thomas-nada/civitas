Cardano treasury funds should not be used to speculatively provide extra funding liquidity to chosen lending apps and DEXs, nor should treasury funds be used to purchase fiat backed stable coins.  Selling treasury Ada for fiat backed stable coins will negatively impact the Ada price as well as negatively impact Cardano decentralisation through dependence upon the stable coin issuer.
