# Cardano Whale

**Proposal:** Cardano Treasury DeFi Liquidity Budget

As a major holder of stable coin liquidity on Cardano personally, I recognise the need for it to grow. This is particularly pertinent given the boycott of Cardano by the likes of Tether, Circle and the VC complex. Currently, dollar linked stable coin liquidity on Cardano is approximately $33m, and a 1k and 100k ADA sell into USDM currently loses you 1.7% in slippage on our largest DEX. The issues are crystal clear and I recognise this as one of the key areas where our DeFi must improve. Although I’d caveat that it will be a function of price, and liquidity will organically become multiples higher at higher ADA prices.

My primary concern with the proposal is firstly that it doesn't fit my own budget/preferred NCL. I have a 150m ADA NCL target, which is a personal threshold that I feel comfortable approving as I believe it won't negatively impact ADA price. This fits all the forthcoming budget proposals I felt fit to approve, but leaves little space for anything else. I would like to support this proposal as a starting position, but see myself forced to stay within the budget or at least not move 25% away from it.

Another point to make is that this proposal calls for passively managed liquidity (essentially deploying the funds into LP and lending and let it sit there), but the suggested fee profile is on the very high side. Assuming the salaries are part of the total cost budget, this is looking at 1.74% costs based on current ADA price. If we compare this to tradfi vehicles like ETFs - which I think we should, given the amount - particularly those with an active element, we should rather be looking at maybe 50-75bps costs at most.

Finally, it’s great to see community members stepping up and taking these type of initiatives. It would be my wish that we start with a much smaller pilot program with the ability to ramp it up in due time. I would revisit my stance for a smaller ask.
