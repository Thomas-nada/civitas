I first met Max in Nairobi. He was full of joy and life. Just one of those people with pure, pushful energy who pulled everyone along. At some point he was walking around with a tiny piece of paper asking people to sign their names for something. I honestly don’t even remember what it was for. I just remember thinking, “yeah, sure, whatever this guy is doing, I’m in,” and signing it. That was his spirit. He cared. And he moved things forward.. or at least he tried to.
Cardano has a long-standing tradition of naming upgrades after people who made meaningful contributions to the ecosystem. I believe Max was one of those people.
May his legacy live on.
