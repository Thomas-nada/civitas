SIPO DRep – Voting Rationale (Yes)
Governance Action: Protocol Version 11 hard fork – van Rossem
Vote: Yes

As SIPO DRep, we vote Yes on this proposal.

This Governance Action is an Info Action that does not introduce technical changes, economic parameter updates, or protocol risk. Instead, it proposes naming Protocol Version 11 the “van Rossem Hard Fork”, in memory of Max van Rossem, a respected contributor to the Cardano community.

From my perspective as a DRep, this proposal should be evaluated not on emotional grounds, but on its institutional and governance significance.

Cardano has a long-standing tradition of naming hard forks in ways that reflect its history, values, and contributors. This practice is not cosmetic. It plays an important role in shaping Cardano as a long-lived public infrastructure with memory, accountability, and continuity. We see this proposal as a continuation of that tradition.

Max van Rossem made substantial and lasting contributions to Cardano’s governance foundations. His work on the Cardano Constitution, his role in the Constitutional Committee election process, and his participation as a DRep represent the kind of long-term, community-oriented engagement that Cardano’s governance model depends on. Recognizing such contributions at the protocol-history level sends a clear and healthy signal to current and future participants.

As DReps, one of our responsibilities is to help cultivate a governance culture where participation matters, contributions are remembered, and responsibility is shared across time. Supporting this proposal reinforces that principle without introducing technical or economic trade-offs.

We also believe that abstaining or opposing an Info Action of this nature would not meaningfully protect the network, while potentially weakening the message that Cardano values sustained, constructive governance work. In contrast, supporting this proposal strengthens the social and institutional layer of Cardano without imposing costs or risks.

For these reasons, SIPO DRep supports this proposal and votes Yes.

We do so not as an act of sentiment, but as an expression of support for Cardano’s maturing governance framework and its ability to preserve the legacy of those who helped build it.
