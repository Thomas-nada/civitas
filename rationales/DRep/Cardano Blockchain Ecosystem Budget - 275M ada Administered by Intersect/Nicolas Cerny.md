I view this budget as a pragmatic and necessary step to ensure the Cardano ecosystem receives crucial funding for 2025 and to demonstrate that our on-chain governance model can deliver. 

While I did not vote for all 39 proposals in Ekklesia during off-chain signaling and I have significant reservations about the "less than ideal" 2025 budget process that led us here, I believe the damage of further delay now outweighs the imperfections of this specific budget. 

However, this vote comes with the strong expectation that the process must fundamentally change for 2026. I want to see a shift from the current vendor-led model to a more effective **purpose-led** approach, where strategic work packages are prioritized first, and teams then propose competitive solutions to deliver on them. 

My 'YES' vote is a decision to move forward, while clearly stating the need for a more mature and transparent process next year. From a procedural perspective the buduget proposal ticks all the boxes, it includes sufficient information about how the funds will be administered, where all the details need to be included are the actual treasury withdrawals.
