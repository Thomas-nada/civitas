I am voting “No” on the Ecosystem Budget 275M proposed by Intersect.
While I recognize that some of the proposals may bring value to our ecosystem, this does not imply that I support all of them — particularly not the amounts being requested.
I do believe that certain projects are important for Cardano’s growth, but we must seriously question whether the requested amounts are truly necessary, especially considering the impact on the overall ADA supply in such a short timeframe.
For any currency, supply control is far more critical than technological advancement.
A currency must preserve value and maintain trust in transactions.
Large-scale ADA withdrawals from the treasury could significantly harm the value of ADA, potentially undermining the entire Cardano ecosystem.
To safeguard the resilience of ADA’s value, we must keep budget allocations minimal and ensure that treasury spending is balanced and benefits all holders.
I urge all projects to revise their funding requests, aiming to minimize withdrawals and adopt a more conservative, long-term approach to treasury utilization.
