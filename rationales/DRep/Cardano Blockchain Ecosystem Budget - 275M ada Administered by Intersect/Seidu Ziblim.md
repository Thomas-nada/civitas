As a DRep and active contributor to the governance process, I have consistently advocated for fiscal discipline and prudent treasury management, originally supporting a 200M ada annual cap to ensure long-term sustainability. However, my support for this 275M ada budget for 2025 is informed by a broader strategic perspective and current realities, including:

**Alignment with Governance Signals**

I voted in favor of the 300M ada NCL, recognizing that a slightly higher ceiling may be necessary in the short term to accommodate evolving ecosystem needs. The 275M ada allocation falls below this approved cap and is therefore within the community-approved threshold, representing a moderate and responsible use of available resources.

**Balancing Growth and Sustainability**

While I favor a lean budget in principle, the Cardano ecosystem is currently undergoing critical infrastructural, governance, and adoption phases. A 275M ada budget enables:
* Continued development of core infrastructure (like governance tooling and smart contract scaling).

* Capacity-building initiatives across governance, developer outreach, and community hubs.

* Strategic ecosystem investments that is essential for bootstrapping decentralized decision-making.

I believe that strategic spending today will yield compounding benefits in the future.

**Responsiveness as a DRep**

As a Delegated Representative (DRep), my role is not only to uphold my values but also to listen to community feedback, assess treasury health, and adapt to shifting priorities. This 275M ada proposal reflects community input, DReps reviews, and a broad-based understanding of what’s required to maintain Cardano's momentum in 2025.

**Commitment to Accountability and Evaluation**

My support for this budget is not unconditional. I expect robust oversight mechanisms, clear milestones, and impact assessments for funded projects. I will continue to advocate for transparent treasury reporting and outcome-based funding models that ensure ADA is spent wisely and in the ecosystem's best interest.
