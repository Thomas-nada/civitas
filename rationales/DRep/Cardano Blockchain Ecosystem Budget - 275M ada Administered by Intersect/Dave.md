I am voting NO on the proposed "Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 275M ada Administered by Intersect" because I believe the total amount requested is too high. More importantly, I believe this process has failed to meet the standards of decentralized governance.

I’m disappointed that the vote was not conducted fully on-chain. As a DRep, I cannot support off-chain processes that lack transparency, auditability, and decentralization.

Bundling 39 proposals into a single vote is misleading, non-transparent, and enables manipulation. It forces DReps into a binary, all-or-nothing choice despite significant differences among the proposals.

The proposal also relies on unverifiable off-chain metrics, such as the claim that "More than 3.8B ada in voting stake participated, representing over 70% of actively delegated stake (excluding pre-defined voting options)." Without on-chain data, such figures cannot be independently verified or trusted and as a result I believe does not support this proposals accuracy.
