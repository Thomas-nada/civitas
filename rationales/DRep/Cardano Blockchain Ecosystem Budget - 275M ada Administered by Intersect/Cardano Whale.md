I vote against. In prior votes I have at length explained why we cannot be too excessive with treasury disbursements. 

High level: We’re tinkering with monetary policy, it is a mistake to view the treasury through a fiscal or even budgeting lens. Crypto is about disinflation, Bitcoin is sub 1% now (putting everyone under pressure, 2025 is not 2020) while Cardano is sub 2%. Treasury spending introduces new inflation at a time other protocols like BNB and Tron have manually had to burn double digits of supply to stay around the top 10. If this comes along with adoption or big improvements to Cardano it may be worth it, but it can’t be haphazard and it makes sense to start cautiously.

I consider this Intersect budget proposal to be haphazard, and want to flag my discontent with the entire process. Voting should have been done on-chain from day 1, scrutinised by the full DRep set and with appropriate spam filters applied. 

But also, even if we were to see this exercise as a sort of fiscal budgeting, budgeting is not about finding out how many items have a critical mass of people who would - leaving all other considerations aside - like to spend on them, it’s about putting those considerations in a wider context of how much we CAN spend first, and then being SELECTIVE.

The worry I and many others have had, that the NCL became the target instead of a constraint, is thus turning out to be valid. I don’t think the market in 2025 has the appetite to stomach 350m ADA in emissions, or even 275. 

I have lodged these complaints here before and have signalled multiple times I would not be able to support such a budget bucketing things I consider potential scams with critical tech contributions. I will once again reiterate that this spending path we are on is the single biggest risk to Cardano currently.
