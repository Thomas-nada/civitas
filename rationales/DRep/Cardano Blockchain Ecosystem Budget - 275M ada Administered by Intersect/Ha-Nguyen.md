I vote YES on this proposal for the following reasons, while also setting clear expectations for any future Treasury Withdrawals:

1) Strong community signaling
All 39 proposals in this budget surpassed the 50% support threshold from participating DReps on the Ekklesia platform, with over 3.8 billion ADA cast (~70% of active staking). This ensures legitimacy and transparency in the decision-making process.

2) Aligned with the Cardano 2025 Vision & Roadmap
This budget includes critical components such as core infrastructure (IOG, TWEAG, OSC), governance infrastructure (Intersect, Catalyst), scalability (zkFold, Midgard), and ecosystem tools (Lucid, Blockfrost). All are in line with the strategic direction approved by the community.

3) Transparent fund management via smart contracts
Intersect will manage fund disbursement using decentralized smart contracts and multisig oversight, with auditability, milestone-based releases, and refund mechanisms. This increases transparency, reduces risk, and improves fund efficiency.

4) Complies with the 2025 Net Change Limit (NCL)
The total budget (275M ₳) remains within the approved 2025 NCL of 350M ₳, allowing room for flexibility while maintaining financial discipline.

⚠️ Conditions before approving any Treasury Withdrawals
I do not commit to voting YES on Treasury Withdrawals by default. I expect:

Withdrawals in manageable and reasonable chunks – Proposals bundling too many components into one large request will be rejected.

Committee-level evaluations – Each proposal should be reviewed for milestones, KPIs, and feasibility by relevant committees, with results made public (even if negative).

Clear segmentation of large proposals – Proposals covering multiple initiatives should be broken down and subject to DRep voting (off-chain voting is acceptable) before withdrawal actions.

* These conditions ensure better governance, prevent wasteful spending, and hold proposers accountable.

This is the first major budget under the Constitutional governance model. If implemented with proper oversight, it can strike a healthy balance between strategic investment and financial discipline.
