While I appreciate the ambition behind this budget, the 275M ADA allocation feels large for a first cycle under a still-maturing governance process. Including 39 different proposals in a single budget adds significant administrative overhead, especially when several projects may struggle to reach the 67% withdrawal threshold later on. A more modest budget with fewer proposals would allow us to refine our procedures and iteratively improve for the next cycle.  For these reasons, I will be voting NO on this proposal.
