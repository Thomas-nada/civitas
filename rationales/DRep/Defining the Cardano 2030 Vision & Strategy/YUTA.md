I voted YES for "Defining the Cardano 2030 Vision & Strategy" because I believe it can be considered a provisional Vision & Strategy and is appropriate for the ecosystem.

The Vision and Strategy should naturally be constantly evolving, so if the Product Committee or other community members submit a better one, I will vote YES for it and overwrite the Vision & Strategy.

I could go on and on about the endless shortcomings of this Vision & Strategy, such as it being lacking in X or Y. Many DRep members have pointed these out, and I agree with many of them.

However, it seems to me that moving forward with Catalyst, Builder DAO, and other proposed governance actions with a blank Vision & Strategy poses a deeper risk.

As discussed in Las Vegas, we need a North Star, but the biggest problem is that neither Catalyst, Builder DAO, nor any other Treasury Withdrawal will wait for the perfect North Star to appear.

It's like an intern constitution. It may not be perfect, but it's better than nothing. At the very least, I haven't found any fatally harmful elements in the Vision & Strategy.

So I'm voting YES for the intern Vision & Strategy. If something better is proposed, I'll vote YES for it and override it.
