I vote **YES**. Capturing the 2030 Vision on-chain is of high public value, ensuring alignment and clarity for Cardano’s long-term development trajectory.
In addition, it would be advisable for the strategy to explicitly consider **post-quantum resilience**. This may include:

* Securing crypto-agility to allow future replacement of cryptographic schemes
* Establishing a phased migration plan combining current and post-quantum methods
* Exploring the use of PQ signatures with Mithril to preserve historical integrity

Incorporating these perspectives would strengthen Cardano’s capacity to remain robust and trustworthy well beyond Q-day.
