# Chris Cata

I'm voting yes to signal my support for this vision and strategy.  I don't intend to get hung up on some of the details in terms of how we measure the success (KPIs), whether it's achievable, or other nuances of it.  While it's important, this is an info action, not an ask for treasury withdrawals which for me gives some room for flexibility in terms of what this vote is asking for in terms of signaling support.

A 2030 vision/strategy that includes high or low-level KPIs isn't going to really fit into the compact document provided to us. Getting into the measurement specifics (technical numbers and how to achieve them) should be handled in a different format/document altogether. 

There will be many different KPIs we could use at lower-levels, but at the highest organizational level the goal of the vision/strategy is clear - and that is what I'm voting for.  As time passes, I expect how we measure and what we measure to change and as we apply specifics to achieving those things and start to look to delegate those action items to different groups within the community. Those KPIs will then start to become clearer through insight and more fleshed out which I do believe some are looking for.  I would like to see these as well, but I don't see that as the intent of this Info Action. It's clear based on some feedback out there in the community that some intend to leverage KPIs more as a rigid target that has a guaranteed metric for success - instead of an effective and informative tool.  It can be counterproductive, short-sighted, manipulated, and kill opportunities for growth and innovation.

This info action is looking for community feedback and signaled support on the overall scope of what has been put forth, and I don't wish to see getting hung up over whether that's $100M or $200M in growth in L1 revenues. It matters, but not here. The goals of Cardano is set clearly here, and that's what is most important - Grow revenues and daily transactions, drive enterprise integration and adoption, continued focus on decentralization.
