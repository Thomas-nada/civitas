I have chosen Abstain for this Governance Action. The strategic direction itself is valuable, since improving user experience, sustaining decentralization, strengthening treasury management, and setting adoption goals are all meaningful priorities. However, the document goes beyond a simple slogan by presenting specific KPIs and targets without providing a clear execution roadmap or accountability framework. Without defined responsibility, these goals risk remaining aspirational declarations rather than actionable commitments. I support the vision as a guiding aspiration, but I cannot endorse it as an accountable and executable plan in its current form. Abstain therefore best reflects both support for the intent and concern over the absence of a credible framework for delivery.
