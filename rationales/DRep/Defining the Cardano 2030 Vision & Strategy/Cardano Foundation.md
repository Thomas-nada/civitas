A clear, community-ratified vision and strategy is crucial for Cardano's long-term success. The Cardano Foundation is an active and supportive participant in the Product Committee’s work to develop this vision, a mandate they received via a funded treasury withdrawal (gov_action13tfag48nf94rtjcdq7c06vhkslmxxw9h6c88sl7q5g5nnewcsvlr24hsvpk). Our team has been collaborating with the Committee, attending workshops, and providing detailed feedback through the Committee’s established channels to help refine the draft document.
We have decided to vote **ABSTAIN** on this governance action as we believe it has been submitted prematurely and its content is not reflective of the Product Committee’s final draft. This action was submitted by a community member, not by the Product Committee itself. While we encourage community initiative, voting 'Yes' or 'No' on this submission would undermine the Committee's own structured process for gathering and incorporating feedback into a finalised draft. 
To ensure our feedback continues to be constructive and delivered effectively, we will continue to provide input directly to the Product Committee via their official channels.
