I am voting NO on this proposal.

This vision provides an inspiring direction for Cardano, but the current proposal feels incomplete. KPI targets, such as the $200M L1 revenue goal, lack clear calculation methodology or ADA price assumptions, making them difficult to evaluate as concrete objectives.

Additionally, the strategy reads more like a comprehensive wishlist of everything we hope to achieve, rather than a focused plan that prioritizes initiatives and allocates resources in a way that is realistically actionable.

Most importantly, the concept of an “active, managed treasury” represents a significant strategic shift. Before the community can align around such a change, a detailed governance and risk management framework is needed to ensure decentralization, operational security, and protection of community assets.

Therefore, I am voting NO as a constructive signal, not as a rejection of the vision itself, but as a request for a more detailed, measurable, and debate-ready version of this plan. My vote may change if a more complete and transparent proposal is presented in the future.
