We agree that, generally, improvements to Cardano governance and treasury withdrawals can be made as the ecosystem matures. This Info Action identifies many issues which can be improved, however the “how” of these improvements, the operational element, requires further discussion. 
Specifically, we have identified the following issues as particularly salient to governance growth, and are actively discussing these with other community members:
Treasury budget, coordination and agreed use: we would caution that implementation of this should avoid centralization or bottlenecks to a single actor
DRep support: we agree that expert insights into Treasury Withdrawals could be amplified to support DReps in their evaluations.
We encourage discussion of the points raised in this Info Action across existing channels, e.g. Intersect or Cardano Forum, to obtain feedback on each point raised. 
We also note that once a concrete proposal is available, a CIP may be the optimal forum for technical refinement and enforceable implementation.
