I am voting NO. While I agree the current treasury process is adversarial, creating an "Expert Commission" and a "Strategic Entity" centralizes too much power. This distances the community from actual funding decisions and risks reducing DReps to mere rubber stamps for top-down omnibus budgets. We must solve DRep cognitive overload through better tooling and voluntary coalitions, not by creating centralized bureaucracy that removes our granular oversight.
