I am voting No on this information action because I disagree with its underlying characterization of Cardano’s treasury governance.

While our governance processes are still maturing and there are certainly opportunities for improvement, I do not believe they are fundamentally dysfunctional. Robust debate, differing priorities, and difficult funding decisions are expected in a decentralized governance system—not evidence that it is failing.

Many of the ideas presented here may be worth discussing in the future, but they begin from a premise that I do not accept. Cardano governance is evolving, proposals are being evaluated, funding decisions are being made, and the system is functioning as designed. Incremental improvements should be based on observed experience rather than an overstated narrative of crisis.
