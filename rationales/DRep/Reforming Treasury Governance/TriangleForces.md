# TriangleForces

**Proposal:** Reforming Treasury Governance
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2y3euudfnw48xeulsfupsttlx8c4xphhaw90d66dvcrazgjdm5uc`

---

I've been saying from the beginning of this governance journey that being careful and responsible is super important, and right now, the way we're managing the treasury feels stuck in a confusing mix of budget signals and scattered withdrawals. 

So yes, pushing to move from a vague NCL to a more organized, themed budget framework is definitely overdue and makes sense strategically. But the clarity of this proposal isn’t matched by practical details as there are no milestones, no risk analysis, and no real ways to ensure accountability or stop powerful interests from taking over. The idea of passing project vetting to an “expert commission” could end up making things less clear, which might leave the community feeling sidelined and hurt decentralization. I’m not on board with that! 

Plus, the proposal skips over a major issue: how to keep things flexible while also maintaining strategic discipline without getting treasury decisions stuck in red tape. Although the themed areas do cover everything well, the proposal seems to assume we can agree and work together, which hasn't really been the case in decentralized settings. It’s a decent starting point for discussion, but it feels more like a rough draft than a plan we can just dive into. Cardano’s treasury governance really needs a solid, clear, and accountable overhaul, not just a vague outline full of committees and budget categories.
