私は本Info Actionに賛成します。これはTreasury改革の議論開始に賛成するものです。
現在のCardano Treasury governanceは、個別提案が並列に競合し、DRepに過大な認知負荷を与え、コミュニティ内の対立も生みやすい構造になっています。
NCL、予算、個別Treasury Withdrawalを明確に分け、戦略的な予算構造、専門的評価、監査・説明責任を組み込む方向性は妥当です。
ただし、将来の実装では、戦略主体や専門委員会が新たな中央集権にならないよう、選出方法、任期、利益相反管理、議事録、評価基準、DRepによる最終統制を制度的に担保する必要があります。

I support this Info Action. My support is for starting a discussion on Treasury reform.

The current Cardano Treasury governance process puts many individual proposals in direct competition, creates a heavy cognitive burden for DReps, and can increase conflict within the community.

I agree with the direction of clearly separating the NCL, the budget process, and individual Treasury Withdrawals, while introducing strategic budget categories, expert evaluation, audits, and accountability.

However, any future implementation must prevent a strategic entity or expert commission from becoming a new centralized gatekeeper. Selection methods, terms, conflict-of-interest rules, meeting records, evaluation criteria, and final DRep oversight must be institutionally secured.
