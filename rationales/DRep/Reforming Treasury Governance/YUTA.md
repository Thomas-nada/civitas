Abstain. I do not oppose the overall idea. However, the most difficult question is ultimately how this strategic entity and expert commission would be selected and organized, and as long as that remains unresolved, it is hard for me to vote YES. The proposal does state that implementation is out of scope, but unless at least the selection and organizational process is defined—even in rough outline—I find it difficult to say YES even at the level of an idea.

As we all know, even Intersect—into which enormous sums were invested, with substantial support from the treasury as well—was unable to fulfill this role. The Cardano Foundation, of course, has not been able to do so either, and frankly, it does not even appear to be interested in doing so.
