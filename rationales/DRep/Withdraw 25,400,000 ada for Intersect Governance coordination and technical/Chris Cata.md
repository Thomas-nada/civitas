I am voting Yes on this proposal.

I have consistently supported Intersect because I believe Cardano benefits from having a neutral coordination layer that helps turn governance decisions into execution. As governance has matured, so too has Intersect. Rather than expanding its remit indefinitely, this proposal demonstrates a more focused approach—reducing operating costs while concentrating on the responsibilities that are most critical to the ecosystem.

The technical stewardship component is particularly important. Coordinating network upgrades, maintaining core repositories, supporting incident response, and providing operational continuity are functions that are often invisible when they work well, but become immediately apparent when they do not. As Cardano continues moving toward decentralized governance and a more distributed development model, these responsibilities do not become less important—they become more important.

I also view this proposal as an investment in continuous improvement. Over the past year, Intersect has refined both its operating model and budget process, and this request reflects lessons learned rather than simply asking for more. That willingness to evolve, reduce scope where appropriate, and focus on core responsibilities is exactly what I expect from organizations receiving treasury support.

No organization should be beyond scrutiny, and continued accountability through milestone-based funding, independent assurance, and transparent reporting remains essential. However, based on Intersect’s demonstrated progression and the operational role it now fills within the ecosystem, I believe continued support is warranted. A resilient governance system requires not only good ideas, but also capable coordination and execution, and Intersect has become an important part of that foundation.
