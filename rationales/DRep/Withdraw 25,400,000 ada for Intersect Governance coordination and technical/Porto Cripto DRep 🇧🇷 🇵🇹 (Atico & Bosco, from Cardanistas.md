[English]
We chose to vote "YES" on this governance action "Withdraw 25,400,000 ADA for Intersect: Governance Coordination and Technical Stewardship for the Cardano Ecosystem" (gov_action1k02990lhw6wh74t7c6ufw3mqaek9ujtvyan99dj5qv5kvcs7pn8sgx6wlxf), because we believe the proposal funds essential activities that support the continuity, stability, and security of the Cardano ecosystem. These include governance coordination, maintenance of core code repositories, incident response, and coordination of protocol upgrades—functions that benefit the entire network and are fundamental to its long-term development. Although the requested ₳25,400,000 is substantial, we believe it is proportionate to the breadth of responsibilities involved, the number of teams delivering the work, and the critical nature of the services provided. In our view, the proposal offers an appropriate cost-benefit balance considering the ecosystem-wide importance of these activities and the potential impact that interruptions could have on Cardano. We also view the proposal’s governance, oversight, and transparency mechanisms positively. These include milestone-based disbursements, independent audits, regular reports documenting deliverables, smart contract-based payment management, and external oversight of execution. Together, these measures improve accountability, enhance transparency, and strengthen community confidence in the responsible use of Treasury funds.
