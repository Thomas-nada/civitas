Intersect has been quietly doing excellent work behind the scenes for some time now.  In the past I have been skeptical about the many different shoes they tried to fill in the Cardano ecosystem, but they have exceeded my expectations in almost every domain.  I regularly see Intersect members in committee meetings holding vendors accountable.  And looking at the treasury withdrawal contracts still holding sizeable amounts of undisbursed funds, it's clear that they are doing their due diligence in their duties as the budget administrator to ensure that milestones are met.  In many ways, Intersect has grown to be the organization that many of us felt were lacking with some of Cardano's founding entities.  I am happy to continue subsidizing the many successes of Intersect with treasury funds by voting YES on this proposal.
