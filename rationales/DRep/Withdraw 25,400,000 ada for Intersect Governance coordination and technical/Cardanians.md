The main concern is that this is a very large withdrawal for Intersect’s core coordination and technical stewardship functions, but the proposal does not make the marginal value clear enough. A significant part of the scope appears to overlap with Intersect’s normal role as the ecosystem coordination body.

If these functions are essential to Intersect’s mandate, they should be presented with much clearer operational detail, measurable deliverables, spending breakdowns, and boundaries against other Intersect-related withdrawals.

We are also concerned about continued fragmentation of Intersect funding across multiple proposals. Intersect and its committees have already received significant treasury funding, and the ecosystem needs a clearer picture of total Intersect cost, recurring obligations, and what each additional withdrawal specifically adds.

This is not a vote against governance coordination or technical stewardship. It is a vote against approving another large operational withdrawal without stronger budget transparency, clearer accountability, and better separation from Intersect’s core budget.
