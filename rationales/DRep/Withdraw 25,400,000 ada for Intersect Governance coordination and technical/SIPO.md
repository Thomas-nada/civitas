SIPO DRep votes YES, with expectations, on the treasury withdrawal of 25,400,000 ada for Intersect: Governance coordination and technical stewardship.

Why SIPO votes Yes, and how SIPO reads the size. This is the batch's largest ask and SIPO treats it plainly. Of the 25,400,000, some 18,800,000 — 74% — funds technical stewardship, incident response and release coordination, including the core Cardano repositories; 6,000,000 funds Intersect's operations and ecosystem coordination; 600,000 covers management of critical processes. The bulk is therefore not organizational overhead but the technical care of the core repositories. The overall ask has been reduced year over year from $7.875M to $6.35M, and the prior treasury funding received by Intersect and its committees — 22,385,000 ada — is disclosed in the proposal. Intersect has coordinated two network upgrades, is preparing a third, and led ecosystem coordination during the November 2025 chain partition incident. SIPO is voting Yes on Intersect Technical Steering Committee Support in this same batch; rejecting the operating budget that sustains that committee while funding the committee itself would be incoherent.

SIPO attaches two additional expectations to this action specifically: a granular breakdown of Work Package 2 (18,800,000) — repositories covered, headcount and contractors, and the incident-response record; and, before the next annual request, a long-term sustainability and cost-sharing model, so that this function does not become a permanent and unexamined treasury dependency. SIPO also expects articulation, at ecosystem level, of how Haskell repository stewardship relates to the multi-client future (Amaru, Dingo and others), so that scope does not silently duplicate.

SIPO DRep votes on this action as part of the 2026 Intersect Budget Process infrastructure batch — eleven treasury withdrawals submitted in a single transaction by Intersect on behalf of vendors, each having achieved the required 67% support threshold in the 2026 Intersect Budget Process Hydra Voting phase.

Shared basis. All eleven withdrawals share a single on-chain recipient: the 2026 Intersect Treasury Reserve Smart Contract at the script credential stake1784sdxt6jjennmstphgdu7l7c2scf5d02a6cve2dgn5s2kq5u3j9v, built on the Sundae Labs treasury-contracts framework. Disbursement is milestone-gated through a Project-Specific Smart Contract; authorization requires a quorum spanning Intersect admins, Intersect leadership and independent trusted entities; an Oversight Committee of six external parties — Sundae Labs, Cardano Foundation, Dquadrant, NMKR, Sundial and Eternl — independently verifies key administrative actions using on-chain logic; the contracts cannot be staked with an SPO and are delegated to the auto-abstain DRep; and a public dashboard lets the community audit the flow. This is the strongest fund-control class SIPO recognizes, and the same structure SIPO has already supported on Cardano Critical Integrations V2, Cardano Vision 2026, IO Hydra and Tweag Core Cardano Infrastructure. SIPO voted Yes on the Cardano Budget Process Framework and holds that treasury allocation should converge and mature through that route; having supported the route, SIPO does not reject its outputs absent a specific defect. This withdrawal complies with the 350M Net Change Limit covering Epochs 613 to 713 and with guardrail TREASURY-02a.

Expectations attached to this Yes. (1) Milestone-level reporting through the TRSC and PSSC of what was disbursed, to whom, and against which deliverable, surfaced on the public dashboard. (2) Continued open-source release of the funded work, and a clear statement of which work is baseline maintenance versus new development. (3) Continued independence of the Oversight Committee, and transparent tracking of cumulative treasury receipts by the recipient and by Intersect. (4) Where scope could overlap with other funded efforts, articulation at ecosystem level of the boundary, so that scope does not silently duplicate.

This vote is SIPO DRep's recorded position.

SIPO が賛成する理由と、規模の読み方。本バッチ最大の要求であり、率直に扱います。25,400,000 のうち約 18,800,000（74%）は、コア Cardano リポジトリを含む技術スチュワードシップ・インシデント対応・リリース調整に充てられます。6,000,000 が Intersect の運営とエコシステム調整、600,000 が重要プロセスの管理です。したがって大半は組織の間接費ではなく、コアリポジトリの技術的な世話そのものです。要求総額は前年の $7.875M から $6.35M へ減額され、Intersect と各委員会がこれまでに受領したトレジャリー資金 22,385,000 ada も提案書で開示されています。Intersect はネットワークアップグレードを 2 回調整し、3 回目を準備中で、2025 年 11 月のチェーン分断インシデントではエコシステム調整を主導しました。SIPO は同じバッチ内で Intersect Technical Steering Committee Support に賛成しています。その委員会を支えている運営予算を拒みながら委員会だけに資金を出すのは、論理的に一貫しません。

SIPO は本アクションに固有の期待事項を 2 点追加します。Work Package 2（18,800,000）の粒度ある内訳 — 対象リポジトリ、人員と contractor、インシデント対応の実績 — を示すこと。および次年度要求の前に、長期的な自立・費用分担モデルを提示し、本機能が恒久的で検証されないトレジャリー依存にならないようにすること。加えて、Haskell リポジトリのスチュワードシップが multi-client の将来（Amaru・Dingo 等）とどう関係するかをエコシステムレベルで明確化し、スコープが暗黙裡に重複しないようにすることを期待します。
