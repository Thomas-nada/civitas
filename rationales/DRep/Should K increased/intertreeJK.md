I am FOR the increasing of the K Parameter as there are already over 1,000 SPO Pools that contribute Blocks to the Chain on a semi regular basis today, and being able to help guide Stake to be more evenly spread among them will, I believe, aid with the sustainability of those 1,000+ Pools. Potentially helping to increase the Cardano MAV Score.

I also think we need to test higher K On-Chain to have real world insight into latency issues it may cause. K can be reduced if it turns out not to suit.
