Positive:
Delegators to many currently saturated pools may be awakened when they hear they are earning fewer rewards than they otherwise would. Negative:
The main negative is that some single SPOs (who are committed to only operating 1 pool) may no longer be able to run their pools profitably. 

I see the benefit as more impactful
