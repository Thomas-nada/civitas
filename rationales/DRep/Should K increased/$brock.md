Increasing K: 
- Increases the number of effective stake pools on the network, increasing decentralization. 
- Causes multipools to go over maximum saturation, forcing their delegated ADA to spread out to already existing, smaller, single pools. 
- Multipools are hosted on centralized cloud services, so they won't double their number of pools as it would also double their operational costs. 
- Allows a0 to become more effective for smaller pools who can't afford millions of ADA in pledge.
