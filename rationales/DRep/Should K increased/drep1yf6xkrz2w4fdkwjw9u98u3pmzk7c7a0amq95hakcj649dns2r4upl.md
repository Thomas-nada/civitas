**Proposal:** Should K increased
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1yf6xkrz2w4fdkwjw9u98u3pmzk7c7a0amq95hakcj649dns2r4upl`

---

Raising k damages the stability of the ecosystem in many ways, but the primary reason is operational resilience of block producing nodes. With a larger k, the profitability of running a pool goes down, as the total epoch rewards remain the same, but are distributed to a larger set of block producers. At the same time, running a node doesnt get cheaper, meaning the majority of pools have even tighter margins than they already have, leading to a decrease in quality of node setups (cheaper).
