**Proposal:** Should K increased

**Voter ID:** `drep1yglrf4el8gghum239fggvfrau25k2576y4dvcz65r2ukj8sqpsc2k`

---

In the short term, I believe raising k will shake up stake and hopefully increase decentralization, so will vote yes.  However, I also think that k is currently ineffective. K represents the target number of stake pools and is set to 500, but there are over 3k stake pools.  Additionally, it measures POOLS, not OPERATORS.  The latter is more important for decentralization.  Raising it will punish saturated single SPOs, but will hopefully add more new SPOs.  Long term I think a new RSS is needed.
