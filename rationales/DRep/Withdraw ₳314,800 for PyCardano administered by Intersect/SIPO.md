SIPO supports this proposal and votes YES for the following reasons:

1. Expanding Cardano’s Developer Ecosystem

SIPO recognizes the importance of making Cardano accessible to a broader range of developers. While Haskell and JavaScript are dominant in Cardano tooling, Python is one of the most widely used programming languages globally. PyCardano offers a native, lightweight Python library that eliminates the need for external serialization tools, lowering the barrier for Python developers to contribute to and build on Cardano.

2. Long-Term Infrastructure Investment

With over 170 dependent projects on GitHub and 9,000+ monthly downloads, PyCardano has already proven its utility and adoption within the ecosystem. This funding ensures that the library stays up to date with anticipated protocol changes such as Ouroboros Leios and Midnight integration. Continued maintenance of core tools like PyCardano is essential to developer trust and long-term sustainability.

3. Reliable Vendor and Proven Track Record

The proposal is led by Jerry, the original creator of PyCardano, who has demonstrated sustained commitment and technical expertise. Past successful adaptations to Cardano protocol upgrades prove the team’s capacity for reliable execution.

4. Transparent Execution with Safeguards

The project is executed under the oversight of Intersect, including legal contracts, milestone-based disbursements, and third-party assurance from independent entities such as Sundae Labs and Cardano Foundation. This structure ensures accountability, transparent budget management, and public traceability through the smart contract tooling.

5. Strategic Alignment with Cardano 2025 Roadmap

Given the upcoming advancements in Cardano (e.g., Leios, Midnight), the continued functionality of developer tools is critical. This funding contributes to ecosystem readiness, resilience, and innovation.

Conclusion:
SIPO supports this proposal because it contributes directly to the robustness, inclusivity, and developer-friendliness of the Cardano ecosystem. It is a modest, well-structured request with a clear and high-impact outcome.
