# TriangleForces

**Proposal:** Withdraw 3,120,000 ada for Republic Integration Wallet, & Token Management
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2y3euudfnw48xeulsfupsttlx8c4xphhaw90d66dvcrazgjdm5uc`

---

Republic wants 3M ADA (about $312k as of today's price) to connect its already licensed private markets stack to Cardano. **Use floss to pull that through your teeth: A company that already holds the licenses, the transfer agent tooling, and the cap table software is asking the treasury to pay for adding one more chain to its menu. Nice vendor subsidy**, isn't it?

As many before, **Republic asks for the handout first and offers an unmeasured promise of "real, non-speculative activity" later**. Yeah, right. With **no committed issuance volume, no identified issuers ready to launch, and no investor demand figures** at all. Then again, these are constantly missing from proposals, so it's nothing unusual. And, much in line with others, there's no statement of what Republic puts in from its own balance sheet. 

And why would they take that risk by putting up a share of their own hard-earned dough? **Republic is a commercial platform. Adding a chain is a business development decision, and the usual reason to make it is that issuers and investors are asking for it. If that demand exists, Republic can fund the integration from the revenue it expects. If it does not exist, the treasury is paying to create a market (yet again!) that Republic itself has not even tested.** Neither case justifies a withdrawal. In the first, the money is unnecessary. In the second, it is a bet by the treasury on someone else's pipeline.

Republic's investors are largely retail users who have no reason to hold ADA or even touch a Cardano wallet. **Didn't we learn from past costly experiences that listing a token on a platform does not make that platform's users Cardano users?** Neither does having a booth at some fair, getting some dude to register a test wallet while offering him a free cocktail! Cardano has paid for integrations before, and I have seen what follows. Funding arrives, a launch announcement goes out, the vendor's commercial priorities shift, and the integration goes unmaintained. Bear in mind that nothing in this proposal binds Republic to maintenance or volume after the money is spent!

**At this point in the game, at least in my book, Cardano's brand and technology should be enough of a draw for a platform with Republic's reach.** If they are not, a payment will not make them so, and it will not keep Republic committed once the money is gone.

Here's what I'd like to see from Republic (and most other proposals): 1) Payment tied to outcomes and tranches released only against verified on-chain issuance volume and assets held in Cardano-based instruments, as reported by an independent party; 2) a matching contribution from Republic (in cash or in committed engineering and marketing spend) large enough to show real intent, i.e. 40%+; 3) a maintenance commitment with a clawback if the integration lapses within a defined period; and lastly 4) start with a much smaller pilot, so demand is proven before the treasury commits to anything near seven figures.

No from me. **Cardano's treasury is not a marketing budget for other people's products!**
