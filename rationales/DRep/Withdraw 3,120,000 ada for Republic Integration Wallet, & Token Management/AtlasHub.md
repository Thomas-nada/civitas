This proposes a focused integration of Cardano into an existing regulated private-markets infrastructure stack at a comparatively moderate dollar-cost rather than requesting Treasury funding to establish a new commercial platform. While the proposal would be stronger with measurable adoption commitments such as actual Cardano issuances or issuer onboarding and a precise definition of the open-source deliverables, the relatively modest cost and potential strategic value of establishing a regulated RWA pathway in our view justify support.
