I am voting YES on this proposal, as I fully agree with the requested amount and the overall approach to providing fair remuneration to the Constitution Committee (CC) members. Recognizing and compensating their time, responsibility, and contribution to Cardano’s governance structure is crucial for maintaining accountability and sustaining long-term participation.

However, I do have an open question that I’d like the community and my delegators to reflect on: the current structure excludes individual CC members—such as KtorZ and Phil_UPLC—from receiving payment simply because they serve independently rather than as part of a group. This could unintentionally discourage future candidates from running solo, incentivizing people to form artificial groups or organizational entities just to qualify for funding.

While I understand that multi-member or script-based CCs may spend more time coordinating and reaching consensus, both individual and collective CCs fulfill the same constitutional responsibilities. The mechanism for balancing these payments between individuals and groups deserves deeper discussion within the community to ensure fairness and maintain motivation for all future CC candidates.
