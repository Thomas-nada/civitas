While I'm in favor of CC compensation, the amount asked (200k ada per CC member for 1 year) is just too much, imho, especially without any cost breakdown whatsoever included in the proposal. I acknowledge most CC members are groups consisting of multiple individual members, but this should not be at the cost of the treasury, imho. Those groups are free to have multiple members, but this is at their own discretion.
Because this is the first time CC members will be compensated, we should be more conservative, and I'll approve a budget of up to 100k ada per CC member for 1 year. For the next year, we could revisit that amount if necessary (if members show their actual cost breakdown).
If this budget gets approved, I might reconsider my decision for the actual treasury withdrawal, but only if it contains a more detailed cost breakdown.
