I am voting **NO**. While I greatly value our CC members and the critical role they perform, this ask is way too high.

My concern is that it creates an unfair compensation gap between the CC and other vital contributors, additionally it sets a standard which is difficult to reverse. More importantly, this committee **stepped in and committed to this role without any promise of compensation** for this term.

For the sake of our long-term **sustainability** and **decentralization**, I believe we need more governance maturity before we can commit to a spend like this.
