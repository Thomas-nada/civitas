Im not categorically against compensating CC members, but the amount is so outrageously high that it warps incentives for future CC candidates.
Honestly? Id even consider 10k per CC seat (yes. thats 70k total, not per individual CC member) a lot.

Compensation also massively disincentivises CC consortiums as it would benefit individuals the most. In which case, we would literally need to rely on the individual opinions and moodswings of just 7 people.

I think a reasonable amount would be between 1000 and 5000 ada per year per seat. Thats low enough to not be a reason to apply and high enough to cover material costs, and/or a group dinner - considerable as "thanks" from the community.

## Looked at from a different angle:
The whole blockchain currently earns about 3.6m ada every year in fees.
If 1m or 27% of the total revenue of the chain would be the cost to securing constitutionality in a programmable environment, then the trifecta governance model should be deemed at a failure.
