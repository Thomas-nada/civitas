I'm voting NO on this CC compensation proposal despite supporting compensation in principle. The inconsistency of some members accepting funds while others decline creates an inequitable and confusing precedent. Either all CC members should receive compensation for their governance work, or none should—this mixed approach undermines the fairness principle and makes it unclear whether CC service is voluntary or compensated labor. We should pass a compensation framework where all members are treated equally, even if individuals choose to donate their compensation back to the treasury.
