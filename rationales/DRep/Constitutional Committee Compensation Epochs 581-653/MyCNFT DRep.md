I support the idea of compensating Constitutional Committee (CC) members as an important step toward sustainable and fair governance in Cardano. The CC’s work requires time, expertise, and independence, and establishing a transparent, smart contract–based compensation mechanism aligns well with the Constitution.

However, the requested budget of 1,000,000 ADA — up to 200,000 ADA per member — seems relatively high for an initial funding round. At this stage, a phased or partial approach might be more appropriate, allowing the community to evaluate the model’s effectiveness before scaling further.

Therefore, I generally support the concept but abstain from endorsing this specific budget to encourage more detailed justification of the compensation level and ensure a stronger balance between cost and outcome.
