There are no public reports on the actual workload and working hours of CC members.
Payments should be proportional to contribution and transparently justified.

Reduce compensation to ₳30,000–₳60,000 per member per year.
Introduce CC activity metrics — number of proposals reviewed, time spent participating in discussions, actual reports.
Maintain the principle of rewarding contribution, but with a reasonable balance and strict auditing of payments.
