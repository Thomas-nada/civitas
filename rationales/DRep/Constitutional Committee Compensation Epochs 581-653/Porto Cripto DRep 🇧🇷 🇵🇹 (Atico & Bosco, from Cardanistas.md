[English]
We chose "ABSTENTION" on this governance action (gov_action16f69yf2f35w9ds8sr05ewyr55jg5f439mu8882rv295fvf8m4kcqql7y6nc), because we believe that financial compensation for DReps should also be discussed together with that of the Constitutional Committee. It is important to emphasize that we are not against compensation — we simply believe the discussion should take place in an integrated manner, ensuring coherence among the different governance roles. We disagree with the opposing argument that the Committee has a fixed number of members, since it is possible to limit DRep compensation to those with greater delegation power, as reflected in the various workshops held during the pre-constitutional period worldwide — including one we organized for the Brazilian community. We suggest resubmitting this governance action to also include the option of DRep compensation in a model that achieves broader community consensus.
