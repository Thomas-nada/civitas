I am reluctantly voting against this proposal. I am very much in favor of compensation for the activities of the constitutional committees however the amount of compensation matters and needs to be better examined. This proposal does not explain what the community is paying for in essence. Why was the amount of 200,000 Ada chosen? Why would the amount not be dependent on the number of actions examined by the committees? At this stage in Cardano's development I don't see how this function could be considered full time employment so why a set rate? Perhaps a set rate per proposal examined and a max cap of 200,000 Ada would work. I believe more work is needed before I can vote in favor of this proposal.
