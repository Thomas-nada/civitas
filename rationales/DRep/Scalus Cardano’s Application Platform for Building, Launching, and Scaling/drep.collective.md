Scalus: Cardano’s Application Platform for Building, Launching, and Scaling

Rationale

We are voting against this proposal because, while we recognize the quality of the team and the work already delivered, we are not convinced that Cardano currently needs another large-scale application platform. The ecosystem already offers a wide range of developer tools, SDKs, frameworks, and infrastructure solutions, and we believe the priority should be driving adoption and usage of existing solutions rather than funding yet another platform.

With a treasury request of over 8.5 million ADA, we do not see a sufficiently clear path to ecosystem-wide impact that justifies the cost. Given limited treasury resources, we would prefer funding initiatives that directly drive users, transactions, liquidity, and adoption rather than expanding an already crowded tooling landscape.
