The timeline for this NCL is more clearly described than the other NCL votes. However, I want to clarify that it had always been my intention that an NCL gets consumed pro rata. E.g. if the NCL is 200m and commences in July, no more than give or take 100m should be spent in that half a year, and no more than 200m over the full 72 epochs (1y). In this sense, I recognise a 300m ADA as too high an ask and hence I will reject it like the previously proposed 300m and 350m NCL votes. Rationales for my NCL target (personal preference 150m) can be found in previous votes.
