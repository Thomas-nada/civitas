# Ha-Nguyen

**Proposal:** Set a 300 million ADA Net Change Limit for Epochs 563–635
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2z8gktqj27kwmmxesd0y484f7p4z9tjf5xu8m4sucstm3gvtywlp`

---

I vote YES on this proposal because:

1) This is a necessary next step to ensure continuity for 12-month budget cycles. Many current proposals are structured to begin in Q3 2025 and span one year. Without a Net Change Limit (NCL) that covers this full fiscal period, project implementation could be disrupted.

2) The 300M ADA limit aligns with the spending direction already approved by the community. DReps have previously approved a budget of approximately 281M ADA, but the corresponding withdrawal governance action has not yet been submitted. Setting the NCL at 300M ADA provides a buffer to adjust if needed.

3) Epochs 563–635 correspond to Q3 2025 through Q2 2026, making this a practical reference frame for future annual budget cycles. Having a fixed time window also helps stakeholders plan more effectively.

*Setting a 300M ADA NCL for this period is a reasonable step that balances fiscal prudence with the need for strategic ecosystem investment during a critical phase of the Voltaire era.
