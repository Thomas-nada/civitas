I am voting NO on the governance action "Set a 300 million ADA Net Change Limit for Epochs 563-635" (gov_action1vdlrcftd48qn2zz8egepr6xyfcmxpfz8r69k769gc5mhg662adesq9yy8pl). While the constitution allows for the Net Change Limit (NCL) to be modified, making frequent adjustments is reckless and undermines trust in the stability of Cardano's fiscal policy.  

A predictable and stable NCL is essential for teams and builders to confidently create long-term budgets. This proposal disrupts the current 2025 NCL period, introducing uncertainty that could interrupt development on the platform.  

While some proposed budgets may extend beyond the current NCL timeframe, this does not necessitate a hasty and reactive change to the limit itself. The proposed solution overcomplicates the matter when existing constitutional mechanics already accommodate for it.  

Although the proposed 300M ada figure is logically sound for the specified period, the process is flawed. I advocate for a more disciplined and predictable approach to setting the NCL. We should establish a tradition of setting the NCL on an annual basis (1 January – 31 December). This would involve a thorough discussion in Q3 and a vote in Q4, providing the ecosystem with the foresight it needs. This process should also include a more comprehensive selection of choices for DReps, rather than a simple binary vote.
