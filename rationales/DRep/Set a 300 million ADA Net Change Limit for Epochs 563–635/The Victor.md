The last time I voted Yes for the 350M ADA NCL, noting that I find it too high and that I will support a subsequent proposal for a reduction. I could not however support the 200M ADA NCL, as I found that to be dangerously restrictive, given we cannot be sure where the ADA valuation ends up throughout the year. This 300M NCL however, is something I can get behind. Although valuation risks still cannot be eliminated, I firmly believe a $2 value is the bare minimum that ADA can and will reach, and hold its value above that for a considerable amount of time. Bearing this in mind, +$600M should be plenty for the next 12 months to come.
