As the DRep representing SIPO, we vote YES on the proposal to set a Net Change Limit (NCL) of 300 million ADA for the period covering Epochs 563 to 635.

This proposal is not a new request for funds but a necessary infrastructure mechanism to support the continuity of approved treasury disbursements—specifically, the unified 275M ADA budget submitted by Intersect and endorsed by a majority of DReps. Many of the proposals included in the Intersect budget follow a 12-month funding cycle, meaning that part of their execution extends into the first half of 2026 (Epoch 563 onward). Without a valid NCL covering that period, those proposals—even though previously approved—would become technically ineligible for treasury withdrawals.

According to Article IV of the Cardano Constitution, all Treasury Withdrawals must occur within an active and approved NCL. This proposal ensures that the second half of the Intersect budget, including mission-critical initiatives in infrastructure, marketing, research, and governance, can be executed legally and smoothly without delay.

We also believe this proposal strikes a reasonable balance between fiscal prudence and ecosystem stability. The proposed 300M ADA is close to the expected treasury inflows for the relevant period (approx. 265M ADA in 2026), representing a conservative but realistic upper limit. Moreover, it offers a natural transition from the 350M ADA limit ratified for 2025, forming a sustainable year-over-year curve that supports planning and confidence across the ecosystem.

In summary, we support this proposal as a technical and constitutional necessity to uphold the integrity of the Intersect budget and ensure continuous delivery of approved programs beyond 2025.

This is a conditional but clear YES vote—anchored in the principle that fiscal continuity is essential for decentralized execution.
