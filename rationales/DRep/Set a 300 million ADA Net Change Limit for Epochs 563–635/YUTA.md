I will vote YES on both of the next two GAs.

200 M ADA NCL for 2025
300 M ADA NCL for 2025 3Q- 2026 2Q

Because I think the following order is good, in which case it would be reasonable to vote YES for both:

1. 300 M ADA NCL for 2025 3Q- 2026 2Q
2. 200 M ADA NCL for 2025
3. 350 M ADA NCL for 2025（now）

The approach of determining spending based on inflows has been well-received by the community.

The 350M ADA NCL is too large for the second half of 2025 alone.

The 200M ADA NCL may be appropriate for the second half of 2025 alone, but many of the current Cardano budget proposals are expected to cover a 12-month period, i.e., up to mid-2026. From that perspective, it seems insufficient without a separate NCL for 2026.

Therefore, I would vote YES on both and recommend setting the 2026 NCL in the case where the 200M ADA NCL is approved and the 300M ADA NCL is rejected.

But personally, I think it would be better to set it as 3Q2025-2Q2026, rather than setting separate NCL 2025 and NCL 2026. This is easier to manage, since most of the Cardano budget work will probably be set in 3Q2025-2Q2026.
