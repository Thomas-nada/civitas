I have chosen to vote no on the proposal to set the NCL at 300 million ADA. While this figure is not inherently unreasonable, I believe it is insufficient to ensure the robustness and flexibility needed for effective treasury management.

A higher NCL would not imply immediate or full expenditure of those funds. Rather, it would provide contingency capacity, enabling the ecosystem to respond to unforeseen circumstances, critical opportunities, or systemic risks that may arise.

By setting the threshold too low, I believe we risk introducing constraints that could hinder our ability to act decisively in moments that demand it. This could result in delays or missed opportunities at key junctures in Cardano’s growth and governance evolution.

In my view, a more prudent approach would be to maintain a higher NCL, offering necessary headroom without obligating use, thereby strengthening our preparedness for an uncertain future.
