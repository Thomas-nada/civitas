We are voting against this proposal. While this proposal covers a slightly longer period of 13 months and may appear flexible on the surface, we have already supported an NCL proposal for 350M ADA covering an equivalent period (epochs 532-604), and this proposal is inconsistent with that position. We have determined that it lacks fiscal policy consistency, as the cap has been lowered despite the extended period. Furthermore, Cardano is currently in a critical development phase involving the implementation of decentralized governance and scalability enhancements, requiring agility in strategic investments. Even with an extended spending period, maintaining strict caps could limit our ability to deploy necessary funds when needed, potentially reducing flexibility. Additionally, the proposal takes a conservative approach by setting the cap equal to expected treasury income, which raises concerns about constraints on contingency spending and emergency responses. For these reasons, Socious prioritizes sustainable and strategic fund management and takes a position against this proposal.
