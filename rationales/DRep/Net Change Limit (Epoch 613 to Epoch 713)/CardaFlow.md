I differentiate NCL and budget. NCL is a maximum limit, whereas budget implies a definition of what is going to be spent and for which purpose. Under my understanding, NCL is not the most critical aspect of our yearly budget as related Gov Action does not cover sufficient granularity in spendings and purposes as a budget would... What matters to me is the budget, not the NCL...

On the NCL itself, 350M over 18Mo is too much complacent as of today. We have even not seen, checked and evaluated any significant outcomes from previous budget exercise. I would rather recommend a shortened timeframe e.g. 6Mo and a smaller limit <100M to ensure that a proper reporting and evaluation of 2025 budget spendings and outcomes is shared with the community, given that a new NCL for 12Mo could be voted around end of Q2 2026.
