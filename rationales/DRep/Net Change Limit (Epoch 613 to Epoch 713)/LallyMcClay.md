Under "application and compliance" the proposal states the NCL shall be applied to ensure compliance with Article IV of the constitution,  and therefore references an outdated constittutional structure and requires amendment to remain accurate.  My vote is a highlight of an ongoing risk where proposals rely on on constitutional references without proper coordination after constitutional changes have taken effect.
