This proposal finalizes Cardano’s transition from a temporary, interim Constitutional Committee (ICC) to the fully elected, community-backed Constitutional Committee (CC).

The ICC was always intended as a stopgap measure. An election process has taken place to  select the seven members to carry this responsibility with staggered terms for long-term stability.

Approving this update ensures: Continuity, Legitimacy & Functionality and maintains the checks and balances needed for treasury withdrawals, gov actions and other key decisions to proceed without disruption.
