## Full Rationale

Since registering as a DRep, I chose to remain inactive in onchain voting to protest how Minimum Viable Governance (MVG) was established on Cardano. I believe this process has concluded with the enactment of the 39 treasury withdrawal governance actions.

### My Core Concerns

* **Rushed Implementation**: the process was rushed and dominated by actors seeking outsized near-term benefits rather than serving the ecosystem. The focus appeared to be accessing the Cardano treasury rather than implementing comprehensive governance capabilities.
* **Misplaced Priorities** I believe we should have prioritized Cardano adoption and reducing barriers to it, especially addressing scalability capabilities by completing the Basho roadmap era.

### Moving Forward

However, we must build from where we are. A key step forward is replacing the Interim Constitutional Committee (ICC) with a Constitutional Committee (CC).
While I would have preferred immediate onchain elections, I understand the MVG compromise through Intersect. I hope:
* Future elections occur onchain independently of a centralised actor
* The elected CC neither calcifies nor becomes a chaotic risk to governance

#### My Vote

By voting YES for this New Committee governance action, I vote to prevent interruptions to Cardano's governance and support its maturation.
