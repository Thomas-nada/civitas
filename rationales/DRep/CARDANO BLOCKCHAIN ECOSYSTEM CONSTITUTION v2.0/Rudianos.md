After careful review of this governance action, I have decided to vote no on this proposal. 
I will start by saying that I like almost the entirety of this proposal, and there are many excellent changes. I would like to commend the work that was done to clean up the constitution, add definitions etc. While most issues are positive, there remains some issues around enforceability of some clauses. Requirements to disclose compensation and SPO/DREP overlap are simply not enforceable and should not be in the constitution. It's one thing if people would like to make it part of the social contract and simply advise against delegation to people who fail to provide these things but enshrining this in the constitution is fundamentally foolish without enforcement and against principles of freedom and inclusivity that I would hope are core to any blockchain community. That said I recognize that this is already an issue of the constitution in it's current form and if it was only this then I could look past it.
The main red line for me as to why I cannot vote for this proposal is the removal of the budget info actions. I believe the community made a fundamental error in how we handled our very first budget, which caused us to make the budget info action redundant. The budget info action was meant to separate funding into tranches, and then those tranches would be administered by groups chosen by the voters through that same budget info action. We failed to utilize it as intended and instead crammed proposals into the budget info action then voted again for the same proposals. We also voted to increase granular control of DReps in approving individual proposals which was ill-advised. The fact that we failed to utilize the budget info action properly does not mean it should be removed. Without a budget info action we will simply be conducting a "catalyst version" project run on funds as soon as a net change limit is approved. For various reasons, this is not an outcome that I feel will improve the ecosystem at all. 
I would very much recommend that this proposal be resubmitted with the important but less contentious improvements only and that more contentious elements like removing the budget info action done individually for a full and fair discussion on each.
