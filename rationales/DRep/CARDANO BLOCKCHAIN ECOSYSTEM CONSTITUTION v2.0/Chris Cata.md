While I am for continuous improvement, I have a couple of key issues here where this goes a bit beyond what I think of as iterative. While Yuta has gone through some lengths to collect feedback from the community this is absent, in my opinion, of broader community involved problem solving methods outside certain areas of social media feedback.  It attempts to make a significant change to the budget process with insufficient explanation, reasoning, or guidance – as well as removes CC “gates” put in place when it comes to the original BIA – and perhaps its intent. **The result here is a NO vote from me.**
Where would I vote yes? I want to see a narrowed approach to the submitted changes (more than one proposal effectively) – with comprehensive and focused changes to key areas with expanded upon reasonings - which I would prefer to see for a change to the constitution at this level.  The brief set of information in the proposal in my opinion lacks sufficient supporting links/evidence of the pros and cons of those larger non-insignificant changes (NCL, BIA, CC scope/impact). Even the link to the Google doc used to collect initial information from community members is missing here, which could have been a supporting link (although still not sufficient for me). Below is perhaps an iterative roadmap on constitutional changes that will be easier to move forward with.

**Proposal 1 – Constitution Update v1.1 ** - What I see as fixes to typos and additional definitions, clearer or narrower scopes defined amongst the roles, and ultimately some tidiness around guardrails and their groupings (network, economic, governance, etc.) – this should have been 1 proposal in and of itself. Easy win.
**Proposal 2 – Constitution Update v1.5 Suggestion** - I would have considered a second constitutional update proposal when it came to the NCL.  Shoring up the definition and aligning the budget process to fit around those needs. There is still debates ongoing about how to handle this, so we should not look beyond this because of the downstream impacts this definition may have in the process. 
**Proposal – Constitution Update v2.0 Suggestion**  - Finally – if v1.5 did not resolve key process issues around the BIA and TWA - create another more narrowed/focused constitutional update to address budget/treasury related process changes specifically as it will make fundamental changes to the budget process, change the CC scope/”gated” process how it is today, and more things I’m forgetting to mention here.
