I’m voting NO on this proposal. While I recognize the effort and intent behind Constitution 2.0, I believe that any update to the Cardano Constitution should emerge through a broad, inclusive process with deep community input and not be authored and advanced by a small group alone.

The Constitution is foundational to Cardano’s governance future. Even if the proposed changes are well-meaning and appear minor, this process must set a precedent for legitimacy, transparency, and participation. Rushing this step could undermine trust in the very framework we're trying to strengthen.

I fully respect the time and care put into this draft, and I’m confident a more participatory path will be taken moving forward, but for now, I believe we must pause and develop a process that truly reflects the decentralized values of the ecosystem.
