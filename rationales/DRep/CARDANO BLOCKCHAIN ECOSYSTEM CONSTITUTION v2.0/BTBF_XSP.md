As a DRep and developer, I support this proposal. This revision does not introduce major changes; rather, it makes the current Constitution smarter. I believe this will enable the Cardano ecosystem to move forward more quickly.
Furthermore, regarding the removal of the recommended statement to include a DRep/SPO Code of Conduct, I believe there is no need to include in the Constitution matters that lack enforceability. It should be sufficient for individuals who consider it necessary to specify a code of conduct according to their own needs.
