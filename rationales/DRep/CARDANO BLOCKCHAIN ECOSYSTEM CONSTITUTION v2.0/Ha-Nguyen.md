I vote NO on this proposal.

While the proposal meets formal procedural requirements, the process of drafting and presenting this constitutional update lacked broad, transparent community participation. There is no clear evidence of structured forums or feedback mechanisms involving constitutional delegates from different regions, as was done when transitioning from the Interim to the official Constitution.

Approving this proposal may set a risky precedent of frequent constitutional updates without a well-defined process, undermining transparency, representation, and governance stability. Cardano needs a more rigorous and inclusive constitutional update process to ensure long-term legitimacy and community trust.
