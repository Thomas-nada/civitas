As an Info Action that does not withdraw treasury funds or alter protocol parameters, the procedurally correct vote is to ABSTAIN. However, I want to use this rationale to actively signal my full support for how this was executed. Gathering critical, empirical data on our developer ecosystem is exactly the kind of evidence we desperately need to rigorously evaluate future funding asks. Because the Cardano Foundation is covering the costs, this is a zero-risk, high-value exercise in network intelligence that does not request a single ADA from the community treasury.

I also really appreciate the personal call-out in this on-chain governance action; it was a pleasure to contribute my feedback to the draft, and I have already completed the survey myself. I strongly urge all developers and technical SPOs to take 15 minutes to fill this out so we can continue making strict, data-driven governance choices.
