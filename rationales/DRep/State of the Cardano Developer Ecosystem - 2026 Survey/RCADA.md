# RCADA

**Proposal:** State of the Cardano Developer Ecosystem - 2026 Survey
**Vote:** Abstain
**Voter ID:** `drep1yf7kjx5rlyuyzxkr4dzyccdfs8tesh29amrtcpyplu3asnqjgmdz3`

---

RCADA welcomes the 2026 State of the Cardano Developer Ecosystem Survey as a constructive initiative to better understand the experiences, challenges and priorities of developers and technical stake pool operators across Cardano.

I believe that gaining a clearer understanding of the people building and maintaining our ecosystem is essential to its long-term development. Reliable feedback can help identify where improvements are needed in developer tooling, documentation, infrastructure and community support, while informing more effective governance and Treasury decisions.

RCADA particularly welcomes the survey's multilingual accessibility, open-source implementation and use of CIP-179 to make the collected information independently verifiable.

I also appreciate the effort to make participation straightforward and accessible without requiring a wallet or ADA.

One important consideration is that survey responses, including free-text submissions, are published permanently on-chain. Participants should be fully aware of these implications and take appropriate care when sharing personal, professional or potentially sensitive information.

While survey results can provide valuable insights, I would also encourage their interpretation in the context of participation levels and potential response bias. A survey is most useful when its findings are presented transparently, including any limitations in how representative the responses may be.

As this is an informational initiative rather than a request for Treasury funding, a protocol change or a substantive governance decision, RCADA records an ABSTAIN position, consistent with the proposer's stated intention.

This abstention does not indicate opposition. On the contrary, RCADA supports initiatives that improve ecosystem understanding, promote informed decision-making and encourage constructive participation.

RCADA encourages developers, technical SPOs and other eligible contributors to participate and share their experiences.

This approach aligns with RCADA's commitment to responsible governance, decentralisation and the generation and regeneration of environmental, social and economic sustainability.
