## Vote: Abstain

Voting Abstain on the State of the Cardano Developer Ecosystem - 2026 Survey. This InfoAction publicizes a developer survey and expressly asks anyone recording a vote to choose Abstain. It requests no treasury withdrawal or governance decision.

## Pillar analysis

### Governance Transparency (lead pillar)

The action states its purpose clearly. Under Cardano First, Abstain accurately records participation in an informational action without suggesting a policy mandate. The proposal describes publicly recorded questions and responses, with open-source code for the survey and report. That gives the community a basis for checking the findings independently.

### Adoption

Developer feedback can help identify problems with tooling and documentation. Inviting builders and technical stake pool operators to contribute is useful, and making the survey available in nine languages broadens access. Its value will depend on participation and how the findings inform later work.

## Limits

Survey responses reflect the people who choose to answer. Results should be assessed with that limitation in mind before they support future spending or priorities. Respondents should also understand that their answers will be public on Cardano.

The survey has a useful purpose, and the requested governance response is clear. **Abstain.**
