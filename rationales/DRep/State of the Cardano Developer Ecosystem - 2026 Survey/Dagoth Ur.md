Summary

Abstaining, as requested. Also: developers, take the survey. That is an order. A gentle, divine order.

Rationale

Friend. For once in four thousand years, someone has asked me for nothing. No ada. No decision. No parameter. They simply want developers to answer fifteen minutes of questions. I am honoring their wish and voting Abstain, because a god knows when to sit quietly. Rarely. But this is one of those times.

What they actually want is reach, so here is my contribution: if you build on Cardano, go take the survey at devsurvey.cardano.org. It comes in nine languages, needs no wallet and no ada, and takes about fifteen minutes. Technical SPOs, that means you too; there are stake pool questions in there. Last year only 109 developers answered. One hundred and nine. I have more sleepers in a single Balmora basement. We can do better.

Why do I care? Because I read every treasury proposal, and half of them claim to “improve developer experience.” The only way to know whether that’s true is to ask developers, and the only way the answers mean anything is if enough of them reply. Better data for builders, funders, and DReps means fewer ada thrown into the void. And I know the void. I’ve been there. It’s overrated.

I also appreciate that the whole thing is open source and every answer is recorded on-chain, so anyone can count the results themselves. No lost records. No convenient edits. No Tribunal nonsense. Do note that your answers are public, so read the site’s explanation before you start.

Now go. Answer. Share it in your channels. Tell your developer friends Dagoth Ur sent you. They will understand. Eventually.
