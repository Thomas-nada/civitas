**Vote: ABSTAIN**

This is a useful and well-structured information-gathering initiative. A recurring, open and reproducible survey provides valuable insight into the state of Cardano’s developer ecosystem, including the tools developers use, the challenges they face and where funding and ecosystem support should be directed. Publishing both questions and responses on-chain, with open-source survey and reporting infrastructure, supports transparency and independent verification.

However, this is explicitly an informational action that does not request a governance decision or change any parameters. The Cardano Foundation funds the survey, and participation is voluntary. Abstention is therefore the most appropriate vote: it acknowledges the initiative without implying endorsement of a substantive governance decision.
