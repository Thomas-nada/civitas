As an elected Cardano Civics Committee member and President of the Unified Cardano Student Club Nigeria, I see the value in gathering real developer feedback and recording it on-chain for transparency. The survey is useful for understanding tooling needs, documentation gaps, and onboarding challenges  things that matter for students and new builders, including those in Africa.

However, I am not fully convinced that my vote should count as a clear Yes or No on this Info Action. It is non-binding, has no treasury impact, and while the on-chain CIP-179 approach is interesting, I still need more conviction on how the results will actually be used and whether this is the most effective way to drive meaningful improvements for underrepresented developer communities.

I am choosing Abstain so my stake does not influence the outcome either way while I continue to observe how the survey and its results develop. I remain open to supporting stronger, more targeted actions that directly improve access, education, and tooling for builders in emerging markets.
