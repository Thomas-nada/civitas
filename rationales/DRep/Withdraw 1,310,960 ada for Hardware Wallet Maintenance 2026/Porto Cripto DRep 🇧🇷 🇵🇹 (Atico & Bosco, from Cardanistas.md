[English]
We chose to vote "YES" on this governance action "Withdraw 1,310,960 ADA for Hardware Wallet Maintenance 2026" (gov_action1k02990lhw6wh74t7c6ufw3mqaek9ujtvyan99dj5qv5kvcs7pn8syhyg4hw), because we believe that maintaining ongoing compatibility with Ledger and Trezor is essential to preserving user security, ensuring continued access to funds, and preventing integration issues as the Cardano protocol, wallet software, and hardware devices continue to evolve. The requested ₳1,310,960 appears proportionate to the proposed 12-month scope of work and offers strong value by reducing risks that could otherwise affect users, developers, wallets, and decentralized applications across the ecosystem. We also view the proposal’s governance, oversight, and transparency mechanisms positively. These include milestone-based disbursements, detailed reporting of hours worked and associated costs, independent oversight, multisignature authorization, and public monitoring through smart contracts and an on-chain dashboard. Together, these measures make execution more transparent and verifiable, reinforcing confidence that Treasury funds will be managed responsibly and in the best interests of the Cardano community.
