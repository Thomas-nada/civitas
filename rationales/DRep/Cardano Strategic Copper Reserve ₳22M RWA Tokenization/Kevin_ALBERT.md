I vote NO on "Cardano Strategic Copper Reserve | ₳22M RWA Tokenization." I support real-world assets and recognise that copper may be a strategically valuable scarce asset, but Cardano’s Treasury should fund ecosystem growth and public goods, not operate as an investment firm taking commodity, FX, custody, counterparty and bridge risk. Only 66.93% of ₳22M buys copper while the rest funds execution, operations, technology and oversight; meanwhile key independent protections are not yet in place and no return of the original ADA is guaranteed. It is particularly hard to justify funding a project whose canonical CuTT token is an ERC-20 on Ethereum while Cardano receives only a bridged representation. I would reconsider a smaller Cardano-native, milestone-gated pilot with independent escrow, completed audits and enforceable Treasury safeguards that proves demand before substantial public funds are committed.
