## Vote: No

I am voting No on the 22,000,000 ADA Cardano Strategic Copper Reserve withdrawal. This commits treasury capital to a copper position and a tokenization program whose costs materially reduce the capital buying metal. The proposed return does not protect the original ADA amount, and the independent enforcement and oversight arrangements are still to be established.

Under Cardano First, the economic case and the protection of treasury funds do not justify this allocation.

## Pillar analysis

### Economic Sustainability (lead pillar)

The [proposal metadata](https://totofinance.com/cardano-treasury-proposal-strategic-tokenized-copper-reserve/data.jsonld) allocates 14,724,409 ADA, or 66.93% of the request, to copper. The rest funds execution, operations and assurance, Toto Finance technology and project management, and independent oversight. The 2,640,000 ADA technology/project-management allocation and 660,000 ADA oversight allocation are part of the 22 million ADA total.

Returning all realized net sale proceeds is useful, but it is not a promise to return 22 million ADA. Copper prices, ADA/USD movements and the costs of holding and exiting the reserve determine the eventual ADA return. Unused and unearned budgets are refundable; costs that have been earned or consumed are not automatically recovered from the metal.

The proposal acknowledges the hurdle. Its own illustration requires about 10.06 USD per pound to reach 22M-ADA break-even from a 6.73 USD entry, assuming unchanged ADA/USD and full fee ceilings, before additional basis costs. That is a stated scenario rather than a forecast. It shows how much recovery depends on market outcomes even before accounting for the other risks.

An 18-to-24-month intended hold and a final return deadline at month 36 also tie up capital that could fund other Cardano work. I do not consider this cost and risk profile a sound treasury reserve allocation.

### Governance Transparency

The disclosure is candid about important limitations. A preferred US PwC member firm is not engaged, the independent oversight provider is still to be appointed, and a Toto-controlled receiving wallet is not independent escrow. The proposal says third-party enforcement arrangements must exist before funds are placed at risk.

Those conditions improve the proposed process, but I would want the independent provider and enforceable custody, title and release arrangements settled before approving a withdrawal of this size. Toto Finance is both the recipient and accountable administrator. Future contracts need to protect the treasury if that company becomes insolvent, disputes an instruction or fails to deliver.

The operating ceiling also permits a disclosed margin of up to 30% on verified third-party warehouse and insurance invoices. A conditional ADA-to-TOTO-to-USD settlement route adds another dependency, although direct fiat settlement is allowed if the token route is unsuitable. These provisions are disclosed and fall inside the budget. I still do not see sufficient value for the treasury in accepting them alongside the wider exposure.

The submitted metadata and full proposal are hosted at publisher-controlled URLs. The metadata bytes match the recorded hash today. I would require a fixed, directly retrievable version of the agreed funding and return terms for any resubmission.

### Adoption (mixed)

Auditable commodity assets could give Cardano useful applications. This design, however, first mints canonical CuTT as an Ethereum ERC-20, locks the Treasury-funded tranche in Ethereum escrow and issues a matching active Cardano representation. Physical ownership and warehouse controls must work alongside Ethereum contracts, bridge custody and token-supply reconciliation.

That can be engineered, but it creates several failure points whose cost the treasury bears. The proposal does not guarantee token adoption, liquidity, a listing or DeFi yield. I would need stronger evidence that the Cardano benefits justify funding both the commodity exposure and this technical structure at the proposed scale.

## Risks I'm accepting with this No

### Missing a useful RWA demonstration

The project could deliver a working commodity-backed asset and reusable reporting tools. A No may delay those benefits. I would consider a smaller, staged infrastructure proposal with contracted independent safeguards and clear Cardano deliverables.

### Forgone market gains

Copper could appreciate enough to cover costs and produce a positive return. I accept missing that opportunity. A favorable market outcome would not remove the funding structure's risks or make it an appropriate treasury allocation in advance.

## Decision

The promised return of net proceeds does not offset the cost burden, capital exposure and unfinished independent protections. I do not support withdrawing 22 million ADA on these terms. **No.**
