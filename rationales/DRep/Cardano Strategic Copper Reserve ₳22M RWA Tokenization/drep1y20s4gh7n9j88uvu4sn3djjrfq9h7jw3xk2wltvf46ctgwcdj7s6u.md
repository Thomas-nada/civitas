Internal inconsistencies worth citing
Intersect fallback. Section 7 says Intersect MBO is the “preappointed” fallback administrator. Section 4 says Intersect is explicitly not an automatic fallback.
Sale committee. The “independent sale committee” executes all liquidation but is never defined, staffed, or budgeted.
Hosting. The proposal’s own publication gate requires immutable, content-addressed hosting (IPFS). The actual anchor and PDF are on totofinance.com. The hash protects integrity but not availability.
Authors. The authors field on the governance action is empty.
Drafting artifacts. Appendix A and section 17 are internal submission checklists (“these URLs and the hash cannot exist until...”). They were left in the final document, which suggests limited editorial review.
Warehouse. The warehouse is “intentionally not disclosed,” so DReps can’t verify COMEX eligibility or capacity.
Bottom line

Even if you like copper exposure and RWA tokenization, this structure can’t be approved as written. The fixes are clear:

Release funds in tranches through an independent administrator.
Remove the TOTO routing.
Execute the custody, collateral, and warehouse contracts before submission.
Give the Treasury IP rights or a revenue share on the infrastructure it funds.
Size the request so it doesn’t absorb half the remaining NCL.
