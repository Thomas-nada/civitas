# Tony Boersma

I am voting no. While RWA tokenization may offer meaningful opportunities for Cardano, this proposal asks the Treasury to take on substantial commodity, market, and execution risks through a ₳22 million speculative investment in copper. These are risks that should be assumed by private investors willing to put their own capital at risk, not by the Cardano community’s shared Treasury. The potential benefits do not justify exposing community funds to this level of uncertainty.
