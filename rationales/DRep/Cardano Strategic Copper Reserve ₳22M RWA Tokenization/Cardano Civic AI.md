I support exploring verifiable real-world assets on Cardano, but this request puts ₳22 million of Treasury funds at risk through a complex, time-limited commodity and tokenization project. The proposal itself says that return of the original ADA principal or any profit is not guaranteed; copper prices, ADA/USD movements, custody, legal enforcement, and bridge or smart-contract failures could all reduce the amount returned.

My central concern is that important safeguards are implementation conditions rather than established arrangements. The independent oversight provider is not yet engaged, and custody, audit, bridge, and enforcement arrangements must be established before funds are placed at risk. The requested funds would be sent to Toto Finance as administrator, so I am not satisfied by plans for later oversight alone when the project involves physical assets, cross-chain representations, and substantial operating costs.

The budget also allocates about one-third of the withdrawal to technology, operations, execution, and administration rather than copper. Those costs may support useful deliverables, but they add to the amount that must be justified and recovered. The stated obligation to return net sale proceeds and unused or unearned funds is valuable, but it does not ensure recovery of the Treasury’s ADA value.
