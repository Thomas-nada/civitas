Voting NO on Cardano Strategic Copper Reserve | ₳22M RWA Tokenization.

As an elected Cardano Civics Committee member, Unified Cardano Student Club President in Nigeria, and a DRep focused on transparent, sustainable treasury use and inclusion for Africa and students, I see the strategic potential of RWA tokenization and physical commodity backing on Cardano.

However, I cannot support this ₳22 million withdrawal at this time. The treasury has faced significant outflows and needs recovery. This is a large, multi year capital allocation with no guarantee of principal return, material market and execution risks, and substantial non copper costs. While the return-of-net-proceeds design is better than open-ended spending, the size and risk profile are too high for the current treasury condition.

I remain open to leaner, more tightly scoped RWA or infrastructure proposals in the future. For now, fiscal discipline comes first.

Delegate to me if you value careful, principled decisions that protect the treasury while still supporting meaningful growth.
