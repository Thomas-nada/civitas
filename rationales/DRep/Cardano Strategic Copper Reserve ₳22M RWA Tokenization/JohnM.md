# JohnM

**Proposal:** Cardano Strategic Copper Reserve ₳22M RWA Tokenization
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2hky8fgx58luuuz5at5dr4xw0jzlre87wwyw3agra9f8js5eu9hp`

---

Tokenised real-world assets on Cardano are a reasonable thing to want, and this proposal is more open about its own risks than most. It prints its own break-even.

I am voting No because the money arrives before any of the protections do. The full 22M ada goes to an address one key controls, and the proposal says plainly that until independent controls exist, Toto Finance is the fund controller. The oversight provider is preferred and not engaged. The warehouse is not named. The collateral agent, the legal opinions, the audits and the bridge are all things to arrange after enactment. Every safeguard in the 29 pages rests on contracts that do not exist yet, and once the ada has left the treasury there is nothing on chain to hold anyone to them.

Then there is the position itself. Only 66.93% of the request buys copper. On the proposer's own figures, with every fee cap spent and ADA/USD unchanged, copper has to rise about 49%, from $6.73 to $10.06 a pound, just to return 22M ada. The most bullish forecast it cites is $10 by the second quarter of 2027, which is short of that and a year or more before the planned sale. The treasury carries both the copper price and the ada price. Toto Finance can earn up to about 3.36M ada from its three fee lines (my sum, not the proposal's) and keeps any DeFi revenue CuTT generates.

The token's home would also be Ethereum. CuTT is minted there as an ERC-20 and locked in an Ethereum escrow, with a bridged copy on Cardano. That adds bridge risk to everything else.

A Yes would put 22M ada into one company's wallet on the strength of paperwork still to be written. My No has a cost. If copper runs and the controls would have been built, the treasury misses the gain and Cardano loses an early commodity reference. I would look at it again if the money waited for the controls.

**Risks**

I could be wrong about copper. At $12 a pound the proposal's own table returns about 119% of the ada, and a No gives that up. I am not voting on a price view, and none of the conditions below depend on one.

Commodity and RWA projects may read a No as the treasury being closed to them. That is a fair worry. My objection here is to sequence and control, which a revised proposal can fix.

This vote does not settle whether the treasury should ever hold assets other than ada. There is no agreed framework for that, and one proposal is a thin basis for deciding it.

**Uncertainty**

Whether PwC or any other independent provider will take the oversight role, and on what terms. Not stated.

Which warehouse would hold the copper. Deliberately not disclosed.

How a security interest held "for Treasury economic benefit" would be enforced, when the treasury is not a legal person. Independent counsel's sign-off is a condition before purchase, not before the vote.

The Net Change Limit headroom. The proposal cites a DRepTalk figure of 42,604,371 ada remaining on 14 October. I have not recomputed that.

The record of Toto Finance's existing marketplace: more than 45,000 tokens issued to over 5,000 holders since 2023, by its own account. Apart from the Foundation's accelerator and case study, I found nothing independent.

The proposal names no team. The leadership appears on Toto Finance's website only.

The PDF sits on Toto Finance's own site with no hash on chain, although the proposal's own checklist calls for immutable hosting. The copy I read is dated 14 October.

I obtained no expert or peer input before the deadline. The questions are recorded; the answers are not.

**Conflicts**

I worked at the Cardano Foundation until mid-2023 and have had no role, payment or equity there since. The Foundation selected Toto Finance for its Spring 2026 accelerator, has published a case study on it, and is described on Toto Finance's website as an official partner. The proposal also plans to use Reeve, the Foundation's reporting product, though nothing is agreed. All of this came after my time there, and the proposal itself does not mention the accelerator. I treat it as a past association, disclosed so delegators can judge for themselves.

Intersect appears only as a possible fallback administrator, subject to its own agreement.

I do not run a stake pool. My delegation to a small pool has no bearing on a treasury withdrawal.

**What could change this position**

- The withdrawal paid to an independent smart contract or administrator, and released only against executed warehouse, collateral and assurance contracts.
- A signed engagement with the independent oversight provider before the vote.
- A named warehouse and an independent legal opinion that the treasury's security interest can be enforced.
- A smaller first tranche that tests the structure before the full amount.
- The treasury tranche of CuTT issued natively on Cardano, or a clear account of why it has to start on Ethereum.
- A treasury share of CuTT's DeFi revenue, or a floor on principal, in return for carrying the price risk.
