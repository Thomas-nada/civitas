Given the market conditions and NCL, I believe voting no makes the most sense.

Copper is extremely important more than ever for technology, although I'm concerned about the desirability, feasibility, and viability of such endeavors, given the plethora of options already available for RWAs with competing products on other chains, and the importance of supercharging Cardano's DeFi and liquidity, driving stablecoins and more readily usable products for users on Cardano instead of 22M ADA on copper.

Happy to evaluate another look at this proposal if refined, as I do think RWAs, copper, have an important role on Cardano, although I think the timing, the size of the ask, and the focus are not optimal for a Yes vote at this current moment. Taking a look at others who tried Copper, Granite, Diamonds, the flow of users and demands just isn't there.

The immediate urgency should be about the rails and top-notch dApps to ensure such an ask can indeed be taken advantage of, which in their absence, would create large operational risk, with a smaller idealistic benefit to Cardano.
