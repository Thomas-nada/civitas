Tokenized real-world assets represent a meaningful opportunity for Cardano, and I support expanding our ecosystem beyond crypto-native markets. **Conclusion: Conditional Pass. Vote: NO, pending material restructuring.** Toto Finance proposes a ₳22M treasury-funded copper reserve over 36 months, introducing physically backed commodity assets and new RWA infrastructure. My concern is the economics: only ₳14.72M (66.93%) purchases copper, while ₳7.28M funds development, operations, execution, and administration. Assuming full expenditure and a constant ADA price, copper would need to appreciate approximately 49.4% just to recover the original treasury commitment. That is a substantial market assumption, not a principal-preservation strategy.

Treasury stewardship is where this proposal falls short. Toto Finance would receive the funds, administer the project, and hold legal title to the copper, while independent oversight remains unconfirmed. Enforceable treasury recovery rights, custody independence, and insolvency protections need to be established before deployment. Stablecoin utilization is also weak, with no defined ADA conversion or hedging strategy to protect purchasing power across the 36-month period. Hoping commodity and ADA prices remain favorable is not financial management. I would favor a smaller, milestone-gated pilot with independent custody, verified tokenization, enforceable recovery rights, and demonstrated commercial demand. Cardano needs real-world assets that create sustainable economic activity, but protecting treasury capital must remain a priority.
