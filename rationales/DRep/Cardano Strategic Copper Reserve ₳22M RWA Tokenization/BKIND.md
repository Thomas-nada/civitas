What I support. Tokenized real-world assets can bring real economic activity to Cardano. My No is to this way of paying for it, not to that aim.

A bet, by the proposer's own numbers. Only 14,724,409 ada, 66.93 percent, buys copper. If every fee budget is spent and ADA/USD does not change, the proposal itself computes that copper must rise "approximately 49.41%, to about $10.06/lb, to recover ₳22M of value in ADA". It adds that even the bullish Deutsche Bank scenario of $10/lb "would still be marginally below full original ADA recovery", and that this call is for the second quarter of 2027 while the sale may fall in 2028 or 2029, a "temporal mismatch" it names itself. On the same assumptions an unchanged copper price returns 66.9 percent, and $5/lb returns 49.7 percent. In its own words: "No original ADA principal, copper appreciation, token adoption or profit is guaranteed". The treasury is not a fund for commodity positions.

One company, one key. The withdrawal goes to a reward address controlled by a single key, not to a contract. The proposal states that "no independent administrator currently controls the receiving wallet" and that "Until independent controls are actually established, Toto Finance is the on-chain fund controller". The independent oversight provider is preferred, not engaged. The warehouse is not named, and "No warehouse agreement or stock ownership is represented as existing at submission." Independent legal sign-off is a condition before the purchase, not before the vote, and on insolvency "Warehouse metal does not automatically transfer to a blockchain Treasury solely by reason of insolvency."

What Cardano gets. The canonical token is "one canonical ERC-20 CuTT per verified pound on Ethereum". Cardano holds a bridged copy, and Toto Finance "is free to expand separately funded CuTT supply on Ethereum and other supported networks". The treasury of Cardano pays to issue a token whose home is another chain, and the proposal guarantees no adoption, listing or liquidity on Cardano.

Treasury ada through the proposer's own token. Warehouse and insurance fees are to be paid by exchanging treasury ada for TOTO, Toto Finance's own token, and then into USD, although "Warehouse operators remain paid in USD under their commercial contracts." The treasury does not need that route. The proposal itself calls it a "related-party token conversion".

The cost. 7,275,591 ada, 33.07 percent, is budgeted for costs whatever happens to copper, of which 2,640,000 ada for Toto Finance itself for development and project management. What is not spent is to be returned.
