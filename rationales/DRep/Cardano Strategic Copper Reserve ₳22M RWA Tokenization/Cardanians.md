# Cardanians

**Proposal:** Cardano Strategic Copper Reserve ₳22M RWA Tokenization
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1yt4lyf0fwlrz8k6j5evd3rxw0sqp67qkh36su98a97q3qsc0h03y4`

---

We support RWA tokenization and initiatives that bring useful infrastructure and sustainable adoption to Cardano. However, this proposal does not sufficiently justify the ₳22M Treasury exposure relative to its demonstrated ecosystem benefits.

Approximately 67% of the withdrawal would purchase copper, with the remainder allocated to technology, operations, execution and oversight. Under the proposal’s full-budget assumptions and an unchanged ADA/USD exchange rate, copper would need to appreciate approximately 49% to recover the original ADA amount. This creates substantial market risk and opportunity costs for the Treasury.

The proposal includes commitments to independent oversight, collateral protections and milestone acceptance. However, key counterparties and enforcement arrangements remain unconfirmed when funds would leave the Treasury. For a withdrawal of this size, independent control and legally enforceable protections should be established before funds reach the operator.

The adoption case also needs stronger evidence. The ability to sell physical copper does not demonstrate demand or liquidity for CuTT. We would expect the proposal to examine earlier Cardano commodity-tokenization initiatives, explain lessons learned and demonstrate how its approach addresses their commercial and distribution challenges.

The Treasury would finance both the reserve and supporting infrastructure, while Toto retains commercial revenues from its broader CuTT business. The public benefits, access and reuse rights for funded infrastructure must be sufficiently clear to justify this allocation of risk.

A smaller, staged pilot with independent custody, benchmarked costs, clear public deliverables and measurable adoption targets would provide a more proportionate starting point.

Our objective is to support builders and meaningful Cardano adoption while preserving the Treasury’s capacity to fund sustainable ecosystem growth.
