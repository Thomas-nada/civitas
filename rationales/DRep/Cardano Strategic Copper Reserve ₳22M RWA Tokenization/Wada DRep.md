# Wada DRep

**Proposal:** Cardano Strategic Copper Reserve ₳22M RWA Tokenization
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2jnqfamg4tq5aepxhggzhqg7pq5gunph4wypkyh9q3qt2cru5m2j`

---

We recognize the strategic importance of real-world asset (RWA) tokenization and its potential to connect Cardano's blockchain infrastructure with the physical economy. The Cardano Strategic Copper Reserve presents an interesting opportunity to demonstrate commodity-backed tokenization, cross-chain infrastructure and transparent reserve management. However, we do not believe the proposal sufficiently justifies the requested ₳22 million Treasury withdrawal relative to its demonstrated ecosystem benefits.

Approximately 66.93% of the requested funding would be allocated to copper acquisition, with the remainder covering technology development, operations, execution and independent oversight. Under the proposal's full-budget assumptions and an unchanged ADA/USD exchange rate, copper would need to appreciate approximately 49.4% to recover the original Treasury contribution. This exposes the community to considerable commodity-price and exchange-rate risks without guaranteeing the recovery of its original ADA principal.

We are also concerned about the readiness of the proposed accountability and custody arrangements. Although the proposal commits to independent oversight, collateral protection and public reporting, key arrangements, including the independent oversight provider, custody controls and legally enforceable security mechanisms, remain to be established. For a Treasury allocation of this magnitude, we believe these safeguards should be independently established before funds are exposed to operational or market risks.

Furthermore, the proposal does not sufficiently demonstrate actual market demand for CuTT, sustainable adoption opportunities or the broader economic benefits that Cardano will retain from the publicly funded infrastructure. The recipient also retains commercial rights and income from its wider CuTT business, making it important to establish clearer public-benefit commitments, infrastructure reuse rights and safeguards against conflicts of interest.

We believe a smaller, staged pilot with independently secured custody, clearly defined milestones, competitive cost benchmarking, measurable adoption targets and transparent public deliverables would provide a more proportionate approach to validating this initiative.

Our objective is to support meaningful RWA innovation and sustainable Cardano ecosystem development while preserving the Treasury's capacity to fund initiatives that deliver demonstrable public value.

For these reasons, we vote NO on this governance action in its current form.
