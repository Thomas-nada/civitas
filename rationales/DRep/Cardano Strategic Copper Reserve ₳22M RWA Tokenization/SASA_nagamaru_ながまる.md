I vote No on this proposal. I recognize the value of demonstrating real-world asset tokenization on Cardano. However, the proposal provides insufficient evidence of prospective CuTT users, substantiated demand, or credible plans for token liquidity to assess whether the benefits justify a 22 million ADA allocation. The ability to liquidate physical copper must be evaluated separately from demand and liquidity for CuTT.

Up to 33.07% of the budget is allocated to non-copper expenditure. Meanwhile, commercial CuTT business revenues accrue to Toto Finance, while public access and reuse rights for Treasury-funded deliverables remain unclear. Before committing this amount, I would require demand validation, a smaller pilot, independent controls established before fund transfer, and clearly defined public benefits. The proposal’s scale and terms should be reconsidered on that basis.
