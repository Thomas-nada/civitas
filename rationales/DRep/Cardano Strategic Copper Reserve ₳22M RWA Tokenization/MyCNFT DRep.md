I vote NO.

I support RWA development on Cardano, but this proposal effectively turns the Treasury into a commodity investor with high risk and asymmetric upside:

1. ~49% break-even hurdle on copper, bought near all-time highs, with additional ADA/USD exposure. Principal recovery is not guaranteed.
2. Legal title to the copper sits with Toto Finance Inc. No independent escrow, collateral agent, custodian or oversight provider is named or contracted.
3. Conflicts of interest: 12% development fee, up to 30% markup on warehouse/insurance invoices, and settlement via the proposer’s own TOTO token, while the proposer keeps all commercial rights to CuTT.
4. Limited ecosystem value: the canonical token is issued on Ethereum; Cardano receives a bridged copy with added bridge risk.
5.Scale: ₳22M is a large share of the remaining Treasury withdrawal allowance for an unproven team.
