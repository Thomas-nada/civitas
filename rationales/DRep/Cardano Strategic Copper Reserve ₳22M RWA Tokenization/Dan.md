This is a bad proposal. Essentially, the Treasury is being asked to take the risk on a copper trade while paying hefty overhead and management fees (roughly a third of the total funding).

The final nail in the coffin is the decision to issue the token as an ERC-20 token rather than natively on Cardano. The Cardano version would simply be a bridged representation of the Ethereum token. Not only does this introduce unnecessary bridge risk, but it’s also an insult to the Cardano community to request such a large sum of ADA for a project that places its core token infrastructure on Ethereum rather than Cardano.

An easy no vote.
