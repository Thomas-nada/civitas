I'm voting No on the Cardano Strategic Copper Reserve proposal.

First, despite being framed as Cardano RWA, the canonical token is issued on Ethereum. The copper tokens are minted as ERC-20s on Ethereum and locked in escrow, and only a bridged representation circulates on Cardano. There is no reason this couldn't be issued natively on Cardano, yet Treasury funds would pay for Ethereum issuance and bridge infrastructure, with the Treasury also bearing the bridge risk the proposer itself acknowledges.

And this is effectively the Treasury taking a bet on commodity prices. The proposal itself states that, if the full fee ceilings are used, copper would need to rise roughly 50% just to recover the principal, and it explicitly says the principal is not guaranteed. Attaching a repayment condition doesn't make this a risk the Treasury should carry.

RWA is an important direction for Cardano, but this proposal is not the right way to pursue it, so I'm voting No.
