No for a lot of reasons:

I believe that the treasury should be used for basic infrastructure that cannot be financed in any other way, not for venture capital for businesses that could find other investors, let alone speculative businesses like this one.

But if we are funding businesses, they should be Cardano-native businesses, not merely bridged Ethereum businesses.

But even if you disagree on those two, copper seems to be a very questionable choice since it is still very close to its all-time high (closer than a lot of other industrial and precious metals).
