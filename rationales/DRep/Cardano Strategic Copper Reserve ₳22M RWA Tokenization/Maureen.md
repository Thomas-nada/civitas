A copper reserve for the Treasury. I read the whole thing, PDF included.
Here's the maths that stopped me. Of the ₳22M, ₳14.7M buys copper. The rest goes to fees, storage, insurance and oversight. Toto's own numbers say copper has to climb from about $6.73 to $10.06 a pound before we get our money back, and the principal isn't guaranteed.
The token is minted on Ethereum. Cardano gets the bridged copy. Toto would administer its own grant, the receiving address is delegated to a stake pool (§7.6 rules that out), and the anchor is a link on Toto's own website.
It's also more than half of what's left under the Net Change Limit.
If I wanted a copper position, I'd call a broker, not the Treasury.
A small pilot, minted natively on Cardano, with an independent administrator and protected principal is one I'd read with interest.
