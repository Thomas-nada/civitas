# Chris [STR8] - Straightpool

**Proposal:** Cardano Strategic Copper Reserve ₳22M RWA Tokenization
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y27qjjnz9a7q6zmvpajzqpqylnka6lpkvcm9sgjrdheqt0qq6pchw`

---

I recognize the potential value of real-world asset (RWA) tokenization for Cardano and support exploring initiatives that connect blockchain technology with the physical economy.

However, I cannot support allocating ₳22 million in Treasury funds to a project with this level of financial, operational, and counterparty risk. Critical safeguards, including independent oversight, enforceable custody arrangements, and audit mechanisms, have not yet been established.

Furthermore, only approximately 67% of the requested funds would actually be invested in copper, while the remaining 33% would cover fees and operational expenses. There is no guarantee of recovering the original Treasury allocation, and the proposed liquidation timeline introduces additional market risk.

While the proposal acknowledges these risks transparently, disclosure alone is not sufficient mitigation. I would expect stronger independent oversight, legally enforceable protections, and a substantially more favorable risk-reward profile before supporting such a significant Treasury allocation.

**I therefore vote NO.**
