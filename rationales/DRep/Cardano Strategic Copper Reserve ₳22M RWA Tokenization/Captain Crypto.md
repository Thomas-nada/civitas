# Captain Crypto

**Proposal:** Cardano Strategic Copper Reserve ₳22M RWA Tokenization
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2x0d3jgae3xs7kdagq2w74kmzlgktw3l5th2lusrgn7m6gg52x78`

---

I am voting No because I do not believe this is an appropriate use of Cardano’s community treasury. These funds should prioritize strengthening the network, supporting developers, improving infrastructure, and driving meaningful adoption.

Using ₳22 million to purchase and tokenize copper exposes the treasury to commodity speculation, substantial operating costs, and third-party execution risks. The potential tokenization benefits do not justify the size of this request.

I support real-world asset development on Cardano, but projects like this should demonstrate demand through private investment or a smaller, milestone-funded pilot before seeking substantial community funding.
