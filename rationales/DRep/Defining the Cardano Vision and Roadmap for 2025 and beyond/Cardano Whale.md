The imminent area of focus for Cardano in my opinion needs to be scaling, which is the focus of >50% of this roadmap, emphasising all the key technical initiatives in this area (Hydra, Leios, Midgard, Peras). Strongly support.

That said - The wording around Leios seems somewhat vague and doesn't explicitly commit to full delivery, which I know we're unlikely to see in 2025. But I think it would be worthwhile to make things more specific in terms of tangible outcomes and a timeline for Cardano users, and feel free to be conservative in it. 

E.g. "in 2026 we aim to have a very first rough iteration of a Leios testnet ready, in 2027 we will deliver it on mainnet" and further explain why the intermediate steps in 2025 are critical to get there. Transparency is key here.

A final note is that in my view the text around multiple node implementations reasons from a perspective of centralisation, e.g. "Ensuring node conformity (...) concrete, actionable instructions and guidelines for building and configuring nodes (...) This combination of precise specifications is essential for maintaining the integrity and stability of the Cardano network, guaranteeing that all participating nodes operate according to the agreed-upon rules and protocols." 

Totally understood on the concerns in this area, but at the same time Cardano is decentralised and I think patronising language like this is best avoided.
