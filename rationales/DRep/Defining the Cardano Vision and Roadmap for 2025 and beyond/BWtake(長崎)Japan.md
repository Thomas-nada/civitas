I believe that the provable security enhancements made to resist adaptive attackers should be explicitly expressed as "quantum resistance" in a way that is clear and understandable to the general public and the media. Currently, Cardano is working on improving its resistance to adaptive attackers through the Ouroboros Praos protocol, but it is important to clarify how this work relates to quantum resistance. I hope that this topic will be more clearly articulated moving forward, and I am maintaining a neutral position at this time.
