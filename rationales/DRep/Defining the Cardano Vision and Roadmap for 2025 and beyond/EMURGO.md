Rationale

Regarding the Governance Action “Defining the Cardano Vision and Roadmap for 2025 and beyond” with the Action ID (gov_action12meeq4r43udremwpm6fzt4nt7fctvt0ah7798x036m2r4nhlccmqqhmr9wx), EMURGO has decided to vote “Yes.”

We believe that a few core tenets in this roadmap for 2025 and beyond align with EMURGO’s near-term objectives in select key focus areas, which can facilitate greater commercial adoption of Cardano while being flexible enough to accommodate long-term variables that may necessitate further discussion.

While this may leave the door open to later changes that can cause greater debate, especially about Treasury management and funding, EMURGO expresses support for the following mentioned key areas that we believe will help to accelerate the commercial and user adoption of Cardano:

Enhancing Cardano’s Technology

The Actively Validated Services (AVS) Layer for Partner Chains enhances Cardano’s ability to broaden its DeFi accessibility and scalability. This can unlock much-needed cross-chain asset management and facilitate greater DeFi activity in Cardano by supporting cross-chain DeFi applications and asset bridges to integrate with other widely adopted applications on other networks. Users can enjoy increased access to liquidity and financial services, helping to grow Cardano’s TVL and drive more assets into the Cardano ecosystem.

Layer-1 Improvements

Leios can enhance Cardano’s security and fairness, particularly for dApps and DeFi platforms. The introduction of anti-grinding mechanisms can prevent users from exploiting vulnerabilities in consensus protocols, ensuring that the network remains more resilient to malicious activities and grows its dApps and TVL sustainably.

Expanding the Developer Community

Cardano’s long-term expansion is mutually aligned with the continued growth of its developer community. The roadmap includes various measures to reduce technical barriers and enable greater developer activity in Cardano. These include multiple node implementations, support for popular programming languages (Rust, JavaScript, and Python), and wider integration with Web2 and Web3 infrastructures. The roadmap also focuses on improving developer documentation, tooling, and SDKs.
