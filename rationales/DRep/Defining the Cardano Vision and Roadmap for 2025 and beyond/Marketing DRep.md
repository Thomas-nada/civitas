# Marketing DRep

**Proposal:** Defining the Cardano Vision and Roadmap for 2025 and beyond
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1y23kumvpqgal3t6v4tamwrprjya453p3djam0eqg63usm9su77hjl`

---

As a marketing-focused DRep I had initial reservations about supporting this governance action and planned to abstain. Because it was released alone without complimentary roadmaps from other Intersect committees I first assumed that this roadmap was to function as the sole illustration of how Cardano will move forward with expansion and growth, therefore the clear lack of marketing support for the product was problematic. 

After chatting briefly with one of the authors of this document, the purpose of the road map was made clearer. In the future it would be appreciated to see the committees work more closely together, to release materials like this in unison to avoid confusion and to foster greater inter-committee cooperation. 

As it stands, this roadmap is well delineated, is clearly orientated toward supporting expansion of Cardano’s adoption and further enabling its existing use case through improved programmability, better focus on user experiences, enhanced scalability and speed, and further stable coin implementation. For this reason I’m voting “Yes” on this governance action and I suggest other committees study this roadmap and release similar documents to clearly inform the community about what goals they wish to achieve, and how they will achieve them.
