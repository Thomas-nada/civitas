I decided to vote in favor of the proposed definition of the Cardano vision and roadmap beyond 2025 because I believe it contains many important elements that will facilitate Cardano's technological evolution and ecosystem growth.

In particular, we are very excited about the increased transaction processing capacity offered by Leios. The introduction of parallel processing will enhance network scalability and allow us to process more transactions faster and more efficiently. We also believe that the application of the Active Validation Service (AVS) layer is very important because it has the potential to increase the diversity of the ecosystem by promoting collaboration with privacy-enhancing blockchains such as Midnight.

Expanding liquidity through zero-knowledge proofs is another attempt to make Cardano function as a DeFi layer for Bitcoin, creating new market opportunities. Furthermore, the introduction of Babel Fees will lower the barrier to entry and make Cardano more accessible to more users, especially if users who do not have ADA are able to trade. The improved Ouroboros Praos protocol is also expected to enhance use cases that require immediacy, such as DeFi, by improving the settlement speed.

The implementation of distributed identity with soulbound tokens will contribute to the development of distributed identity (DID) solutions and will be an important element in supporting the foundation of Web3. This is an essential measure to support sustainable network operations.

This roadmap incorporates a balance of scalability, interoperability, and user experience improvements that will both advance Cardano's technical evolution and expand its ecosystem. Against this background, I vote in favor of this proposal.
