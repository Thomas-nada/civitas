Reasons for SIPO’s Yes Vote

As a Cardano DRep, SIPO has decided to vote “Yes” on this governance action. The reasons are as follows:

Clear Vision and Strategy
	•	The 2025 roadmap serves as a crucial guideline for Cardano’s long-term development and technological innovation.
	•	It includes comprehensive strategies to enhance Cardano’s evolution, covering L1 and L2 scaling, developer support, and governance improvements.

Enhancing Scalability and Performance
	•	Strengthening the Leios protocol, Hydra, and Midgard will significantly improve transaction processing capacity and enhance the user experience.
	•	Decentralization of Mithril and the introduction of LSM trees will reduce node load.

Supporting Developers and the Ecosystem
	•	Strengthening APIs, enriching development tools, and providing developer programs will invigorate the entire ecosystem.
	•	The promotion of decentralized application (dApp) development is expected.

Improving Interoperability and Liquidity
	•	Integration with Bitcoin and the introduction of Babel fees will lower entry barriers for new users.
	•	Strengthening interoperability with other blockchains will enhance competitiveness in the DeFi market.

Advancing Governance and Decentralization
	•	Transparency in governance and the stabilization of the voting process are progressing.
	•	Improvements to SPO incentives and the implementation of multiple node versions will establish a healthier decentralized network.

Establishing a Long-term Vision and Sustainability
	•	A framework for defining the roadmap beyond 2026 has been presented, ensuring sustainable development.
	•	Strengthening collaboration between research and the community will maintain Cardano’s technological superiority.

For these reasons, SIPO supports this governance action with a “Yes” vote, contributing to the future growth of Cardano.
