I am voting YES on the proposed 2025 Vision and Roadmap for Cardano due to its comprehensive and strategically robust approach toward enhancing scalability, security, and usability, which are crucial for Cardano's long-term success. The roadmap clearly addresses key community priorities, including Layer 1 performance optimization through Leios, expansion of Layer 2 solutions such as Hydra and Midgard, and enhancements in developer and user experience, which is essential to Cardano's adoption and, I personally believe, has been overlooked in the past.

Specifically, the introduction of Leios is particularly promising as it targets significant scalability improvements through parallel block creation, potentially positioning Cardano as a leading blockchain for high-throughput applications.

There are certain items within the roadmap that appear subjective, in my opinion, but I welcome further research, community feedback, and refinement on these topics as indicated by the iterative nature of the roadmap.
