These two quotes from this Action’s Text concern me greatly:

“… the Product Committee will iterate upon the roadmap based upon DRep feedback, adjusting the referenced roadmap.”

“While the referenced roadmap develops, DReps should consider submitting multiple votes to signal approval across versions.”

The idea that a Yes OR a No Vote could end up being recorded as a DRep's Official Position on an On-Chain Voting History when a Governance Action *itself* clearly states that the content of what is being Voted on can change *midway through* the On-Chain Voting Process gives me the ick (to put it mildly). 

It puts me in the position of not being able to place a *definitive* Vote on my *actual* position as a DRep on this Action. 

As my Yes, or indeed my No, Vote by the end date of this On-Chain Action could have been intended for an older version of the, since updated, ‘referenced roadmap’.

I appreciate the *idea* that an Action could have multiple Voting Rounds following version revisions based on DRep feedback but, in order for this to work *properly* mechanically, each new version of the Action should *necessarily* start from a blank slate of DRep Voting Data, while still capturing the DReps’ Voting Record prior to the version change.

It should most definitely NOT carry forward previous the Voting Positions of the DReps *as if* they have stayed the same. 

As this could end up being *grossly* misrepresentative of the *actual* DRep Consensus once the Action has reached its final version within its Lifespan.

Due to this issue with not being able to actually interpret the *reality* of this Governance Action’s final Voting Outcome, I cannot in good conscience Vote *either* Yes OR No on this Action. 

So I have chosen to Abstain this time. Unfortunately.
