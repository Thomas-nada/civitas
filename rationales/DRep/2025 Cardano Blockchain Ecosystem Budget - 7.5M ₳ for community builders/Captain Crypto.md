Why I’m Voting YES
This budget is Cardano’s chance to live up to its Voltaire vision—decentralized, community-driven governance. With 3.92 billion ada cast in Ekklesia (72% of active voting power), the community showed up in force. Each project cleared a tough 2.69B ada threshold, proving they’ve got broad support. That gives me confidence I’m backing what the ecosystem wants.

Kicking out founding entities’ proposals ensures fairness—everyone plays by the same rules. Plus, Intersect’s role as administrator, with standardized submissions and public Ekklesia data, adds transparency, and the Constitutional Committee’s oversight (needing 5/7 votes) keeps it legit.
