I vote no on the proposal requesting 7.5 million ADA for Community Builders initiative. While I strongly support efforts to grow and strengthen Cardano’s community, I find this particular proposal flawed on several critical fronts.

First, it lacks transparency and professionalism. It references a number of projects including at least one that has not requested to be administered by Intersect yet here  citing Intersect as administrator.  I do not see evidence of coordination or consent from those initiatives. This raises concerns about how accurately the proposal reflects the intentions and needs of the named contributors.

Second, the grouping of disparate initiatives into a single budget request without clear governance, delivery mechanisms, or accountability structures diminishes confidence in the proposal’s ability to deliver on its goals effectively and responsibly.

In its current form, I do not believe this is a credible or well-structured funding request. I encourage future proposals in this space to prioritize consultation, coordination, and clarity in their design, our community deserves nothing less.
