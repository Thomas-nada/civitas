# SIPO

**Proposal:** 2025 Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 7.5M ₳ for community builders

SIPO votes ABSTAIN on the “2025 Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget – 7.5M ₳ for community builders” proposal.

SIPO fully recognizes the community-driven nature of this budget and supports the underlying principles of fairness, inclusion, and bottom-up governance. However, at this stage, SIPO believes that further clarification and safeguards are needed to ensure effective execution and to avoid unintentional overlap with the already-approved 275M ADA Intersect budget.

In particular, SIPO identifies the following concerns:
	•	Some proposals appear in both budget packages, raising concerns over potential double allocation.
	•	The execution frameworks across the bundled proposals vary significantly and may lack the standardization of oversight and smart contract-based disbursement mechanisms established by Intersect.
	•	There is currently no unified process for selecting which version of a proposal should proceed to Treasury Withdrawal.

Therefore, SIPO abstains not out of opposition, but as a call for procedural refinement. SIPO would strongly support a revised version of this budget that aligns with a unified execution framework or is integrated under Intersect’s smart contract governance model. SIPO believes this will strengthen transparency, prevent redundancy, and build a sustainable funding precedent for community-led innovation.
