Krypto Labs acknowledges the vital importance of transparent, constitutionally aligned treasury allocation for community builders. While we support the spirit of empowering grassroots initiatives, we will be voting 'No' on this Info Action. Our decision stems from the understanding that the projects listed within this proposal are already slated to receive funding through the established IntersectMBO 2025 budget process. Approving this separate action would create redundancy and potential for double-funding, undermining the integrity of our budgetary framework. We advocate for a singular, clear funding path to ensure fiscal responsibility
