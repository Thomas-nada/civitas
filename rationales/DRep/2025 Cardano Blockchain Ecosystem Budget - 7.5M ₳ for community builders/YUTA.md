I voted YES for the 7.5M and 275M budgets, including requirements that I would like met before treasury withdrawals could be made.

*Even if all of these conditions are met, I would not necessarily automatically vote YES on the Treasury Withdrawal governance action.

1) Treasury withdrawal governance actions must be performed in reasonably granular chunks. This is a requirement, and any unreasonably aggregated Treasury withdrawal governance actions will be immediately voted NO.

2) Please have it reviewed by the relevant committee. (In particular, milestone setting, KPIs and target values, and their feasibility (including technical reviews if necessary) ) This does not necessarily mean getting "approval" from the committee. Please attach the review results from the committee (even if they are negative).

3) For each proposal, I outlined the requirements I wanted them to meet before the treasury drawdown could take place. In particular, I would like the following to be implemented for proposals that include large budgets with multiple contents:"Please rationally break down the contents of the proposal, hold a DRep vote on each one (off-chain voting is also acceptable), and reflect the voting results in the treasury withdrawal governance action."
