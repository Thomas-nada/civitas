I do not like surprise requests for money for budgets after the fact.  

However, I look at this request as:
1. Maintenence for a set of software which is a public-good and widely used throughout the ecosystem.
2. I see the ask at economically rational
3. I will accept by giving the benefit of the doubt that the team needed to be able to evaluate the actual real costs before being able to quote on them.  If this was the actual need, then I would have expected the quote to be after 3-4 months max of evaluation, not 12, and that is a yellow-flag for me.  However, I'm willing to accept this as a one-off as the ecosystem is better off approving this correction and keeping the team motivated and feeling supported rather than taking all risk on costs on themselves upfront, and then finding that the ecosystem will not support them in the end.

If knowing what we knew then, or what we know now, I  would have approved supporting this project with a maintenance budget at the beginning of the year, and thus it doesn't seem rational to reject it now just because the process and timing  of the funding request itself is irksome.
