Already used, already done, paid after. I vote Yes.

Mesh asks 600,000 ada, about 1.4% of the 42.6M left under the current spending limit, to maintain the Mesh SDK through 2026. My test for treasury requests is simple: who uses it, and does the money move before or after the work.

1\. Who uses it. Core package downloads were 311,234 in 2026, up from 127,746 the year before (2.4x), and 889 GitHub projects depend on it (the team notes this includes dev and indirect use).

2\. Why it matters. Every hard fork, an SDK has to keep up, or the apps built on it break. Maintenance is the boring part nobody wants to fund, and if it stops, every builder downstream pays for it.

3\. When the money moves. **This pays for 2026, a year that is almost over.** It is close to paying after delivery, not before.

4\. Track record. Two Catalyst maintenance grants (F12, F13) with every milestone completed, and the team kept maintaining after that funding ended in mid 2025.

5\. Cost. 50,000 ada a month, close to their Catalyst maintenance rate, fixed in ada with no price assumption.

6\. Conflict of interest. Mesh's own DRep abstains on this action. Good practice.

7\. In the same epochs, another action asks 25M ada upfront for next year's events. This one asks 600k for this year's work. The order of the money matters to me.

8\. One concern. The 3-of-4 multisig signers are all Mesh team members, so there is no outside check on the funds.

9\. For the 2027 proposal, I ask for **one independent signer outside Mesh**, submission inside the 2027 budget process (developer support domain), and how many of the 889 projects run on mainnet.

10\. These are not conditions for this vote, but I will look for them next year.

11\. Quarterly reports before quarterly payments from 2027 is the right model for yearly maintenance funding. I hope others copy it.

I vote Yes.
