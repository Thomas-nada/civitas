This proposal has several things I like as an ADA holder:

**1. "If the ada price changes: the budget stays the same in ada, whatever happens to the price. 1 ada = 1 ada."**
By doing useful work and valuing their contribution in ada, the Mesh team helps strengthen the Cardano ecosystem and its currency. 

**2. "Contributors are paid for their 2026 work once we receive the funds from the Treasury."**
Already performed useful work is a high form of stake. I find it entirely fair to convert contributions into financial stake if the work has demonstrated value. The numbers provided by Mesh team indicate the work is useful.   Next time, I would like to see an even more systematic and in-depth assessment of what would fail or become unreliable without the proposed maintenance. We might agree that some activities are essential, while others are desirable but not necessary.

**3. "Conflict of interest. Mesh is a registered DRep. The Mesh DRep will abstain on this governance action."**
Abstaining is the right decision. 

**4. The proposal reads well and supported by evidence.**
When a proposal is well written, it often indicates that the team behind it has a sufficiently clear vision of what it wants to achieve.

**5. Low delivery risk.**

A big chunk of the work has been performed already. 

**Issues I would like to see addressed more clearly in future proposals asking for retrospective funding:**

**1. A clearer justification of individual compensation.** The proposed ₳600,000 budget is distributed primarily among five team members, but there is little information about their respective workloads or how the compensation amounts were determined.

**2. Full transparency about other funding sources.** Mesh maintainers have reportedly also received support through Intersect's Maintainer Retainer Program. Future proposals should disclose such funding if ti exists and explain whether it compensates any of the same work / overlap.

**3. A deeper assessment of Mesh's importance to Cardano.** Future proposals should go beyond download counts and GitHub dependencies to show how major Cardano projects actually use Mesh. I would particularly welcome a narrative assessment, starting with leading projects, of what would fail or become unreliable if Mesh were no longer maintained and upgraded, when those problems might emerge, and what alternatives developers would have.
