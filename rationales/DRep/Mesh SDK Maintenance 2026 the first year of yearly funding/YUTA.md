I vote Yes on "Mesh SDK Maintenance 2026" (600,000 ada, paid after the fact for the 2026 maintenance work).

Use and work: worth the amount.
Mesh completed all 15 Catalyst projects it was funded for (all 76 milestones approved). Transactions built with Mesh are a small share of all Cardano transactions, about 0.2–0.4% by my count, but Mesh is widely used as the component that connects websites to wallets. Among the production Cardano websites I checked, 35% of the 26 whose wallet library I could identify use Mesh, and of the 243 public GitHub repositories active in 2026 that use one of the five main TypeScript libraries, 44% use Mesh. 600,000 ada pays five people for one year and is about 0.04% of the treasury. The work is already done, so there is no delivery risk.

However, two of the five people to be paid are also the maintainers of Mesh's main repository under Intersect's Maintainer Retainer Program, which is funded from the treasury budget (neither of them holds any other slot in the program). I traced the program's payouts on-chain and estimate that the two received about 21,000 ada in total from August to November 2026 (for one of them, the keys of the receiving wallet are the same as signing keys of this proposal's receiving multisig). The proposal does not mention this retainer and says Mesh kept going in 2026 "without dedicated funding". I would like the team to explain how that statement relates to the retainer.
