Payment after delivery. The proposal asks payment for work that was done first: "we did the work, without dedicated funding, and measured what a year of Mesh maintenance really takes". I am strongly in favor of this order. When the treasury pays afterward, ada holders judge what was delivered and what it added, not what is promised. The risk of the work sat with the team, not with the treasury. The proposal states: "Contributors are paid for their 2026 work once we receive the funds from the Treasury."

Work that can be checked. I measured the figures the proposal gives on 17 November 2026. The npm registry reports 314,034 downloads of the main package from 1 January to 7 November 2026 and 127,746 for the same dates in 2025. The proposal gives 311,234 and 127,746. GitHub shows 58 pull requests merged from 1 January to 14 October 2026, 41 in the Mesh repository and 17 in the providers repository, by 14 authors, as the proposal states. GitHub lists 893 repositories that depend on Mesh, where the proposal gives 889. The code is public under the Apache 2.0 license.

Custody that can be read on chain. The receiving credential is a native script that requires 3 of 4 key signatures, as the proposal describes. The reward account is registered, has no stake pool delegation and is delegated to the always abstain DRep. My DRep registration says that I verify "against the chain myself rather than take claims on faith". Here that was possible: the controls are in the script.

A fixed and small amount. The request is 50,000 ada a month for 12 months, fixed in ada: "the budget stays the same in ada, whatever happens to the price". The proposal adds: "There is no contingency line". By my count on chain 42,604,371 ada remained under the Net Change Limit for epochs 613 to 713 on 17 November 2026, and the proposal gives the same figure rounded. This action takes 600,000 ada of that.

A public good by an independent team. Mesh is a toolkit other builders depend on, maintained by a small team that is not a founding entity. Builders need tools like this to keep working through every protocol change. My DRep registration asks for "value-adding solutions" for Cardano's stakeholders, and this is one that others build on.

No earlier treasury funding, and no double claim. The proposal states: "Mesh has never received Treasury funding through on-chain governance". I found no earlier treasury withdrawal to this credential on chain and no payment to Mesh in the metadata of the treasury contract administered by Intersect. A wallet toolkit the team built in 2026 was paid by Project Catalyst. The proposal keeps it out of this request: "it was funded separately by Project Catalyst". The proposal lists eleven Catalyst proposals as completed. On 17 November 2026 the Project Catalyst site showed all eleven with the status Complete.

What I note. The anchor names no author and carries no signature. The budget gives an amount per person and no hours, so the price is judged against the output and not against time spent. The year 2026 is not complete: November and December are still ahead, and the pool for community contributors is still to be assigned. The pull requests I counted run to 14 October 2026 and the downloads to 7 November 2026. None of these points outweighs work that is largely delivered and public.
