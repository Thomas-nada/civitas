I support this ₳600,000 proposal because dependable developer tooling is essential infrastructure for Cardano. Mesh makes transaction building, wallet integration, and blockchain services accessible through a shared, open-source SDK. Its place in the Cardano Developer Portal—including a Mesh application starter—demonstrates its practical role in helping developers build. Cardano Developer Portal

The benefit extends across projects. Maintaining shared tooling reduces duplicated engineering effort, helps applications stay compatible with protocol changes, and lowers the barriers facing new builders. Those benefits justify collective funding: the ecosystem depends on work whose costs otherwise fall on a small group of maintainers.

The proposal provides concrete evidence of that work. The reported transaction failures ahead of PV11, and the subsequent cost-model update, show why maintenance matters. Keeping applications working through network changes protects the value of improvements funded elsewhere in the ecosystem. The underlying issue and merged fix are publicly inspectable. Issue #824, fix #827

I consider the request proportionate to its stated scope: a year of maintenance, named contributor allocations, a community contribution pool, and 84% of the budget directed toward engineering. The proposal connects its funding request to identifiable work and public records, giving voters a substantive basis for assessing value.

I also support moving toward annual maintenance proposals. Predictable funding can help retain expertise and sustain essential tools, while a fresh governance decision each year preserves accountability. This vote funds the 2026 reference year; future requests must earn approval on their own merits.

My yes recognises the value already delivered and the importance of sustaining the people who keep Cardano usable for its builders.
