I’m voting YES on the 600,000 ADA withdrawal for Mesh SDK Maintenance 2026. What stood out to me was what happened ahead of Cardano’s protocol upgrade. Developers reported that some transactions built with Mesh were failing on the test networks, and the team fixed the issue before the upgrade reached mainnet. That’s a concrete example of what Cardano builders got out of this work.

I think it’s fair for the treasury to help pay the people who keep these tools working throughout the year. Anyone can look at the issue developers reported, the fix Mesh delivered, and the releases that followed. Being able to see what was done gives me confidence in supporting the 2026 request.

I’d still like Mesh to explain who will independently review the payouts, how that review will be funded, and what work Intersect’s maintainer payments already covered. Those details matter to me, especially if Mesh plans to come back each year. For this vote, the work the team has shown earns my support. I’ll judge the 2027 request on its own merits.
