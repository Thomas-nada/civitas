# TriangleForces

**Proposal:** Mesh SDK Maintenance 2026 the first year of yearly funding
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2y3euudfnw48xeulsfupsttlx8c4xphhaw90d66dvcrazgjdm5uc`

**Mesh is solid infrastructure that other funded projects already rely on**, and the **cost is reasonable for a five-person team** (given current exchange rates) for keeping an SDK up to date.

That said, **I'm not comfortable with the proposal being retroactive and with fund control being self-referential**. I also have a nagging feeling the usage numbers are somewhat inflated. Yet, I vote **'Yes', but on the condition that 2027 is submitted as a forward-looking plan with real quarterly reporting and an independent signer.**
