I vote YES on "Reduce minPoolCost to 75 ada". The fixed fee takes a much larger share of rewards from small pools, making them less attractive to delegators, while no operator is forced to lower its fee. The 2023 cut did not cause a market-wide race to the bottom, so 75 ada is a reasonable interim step that also reduces the reward for splitting stake across multiple pools. This is not a complete solution, and I still expect a proportional minimum margin and broader reform.
