I vote ABSTAIN on "Reduce minPoolCost to 75 ada".
1.My own honest view is YES. Lowering minPoolCost only lowers a floor; it forces no pool to change its setting and widens the options for small pools. This is a direction I have long supported, and I voted YES on the previous bundled action (Part 2) for the same reason.
2.What changed my judgment is the SPO reaction to that previous action. Of the 247 pools that voted, 59 (20% by stake) voted an explicit No, consistently citing "introduce minMargin first" and "75 ADA does not cover operating costs". The non-votes weigh even more. When the same Plutus limit change was proposed on its own (Part 1), 316 pools voted Yes and it passed; with minPoolCost bundled in (Part 2), Yes fell to 147 pools, and default No from non-voting pools made up 56% of the tally. In the same epochs (646–653), 302 pools voted Yes on the committee update, so this was not forgetfulness. I read it as broad, silent opposition from operators on the ground.
3.This time the change is a standalone economic parameter, so SPOs have no vote and that opposition has no path into the tally. Taking this broad, silent pushback from SPOs into account, I abstain. I will consider YES on a proposal that comes with a mitigation SPOs can accept, such as pairing it with the introduction of minMargin.
