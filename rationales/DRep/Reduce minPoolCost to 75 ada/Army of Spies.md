Allowing a free market with respect to minPoolCost is more consistent with the ethos of crypto. It's unclear whether this will actually allow smaller pools to compete more effectively with established pools in a real on-the-ground measurable way. But, history seems to indicate that allowing the free market to do its thing (barring any tragedy of the commons externalities) is generally the more fruitful path.  While a minPoolCost of zero would be a true free market, this seems right directionally.
