Lowering the minPoolCost to 75 ADA might help address a rewards distortion between operator/delegator but does not more broadly promote the ideal of stake pool diversity and health. Based on the last fee lowering, I think the change will do very little to drive stake flow to smaller pools. The fee level is also way out of line with the actually costs necessary to run a pool. I'd like to see a diverse array of somewhere between 500-1000 healthy stake pools. Pools that are sustainable to run and an attractive source of yield for delegators. This will require other changes that address both min margin and value of pledge.
