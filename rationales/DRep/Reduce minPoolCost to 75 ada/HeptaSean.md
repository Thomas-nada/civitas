minPoolCost has to go. PoolCost was a bad idea from the start. It makes pools that produce few blocks very unattractive (because it deduces a fixed amount before distributing to the delegators). On the other hand, it does _not_ guarantee a fixed income for the SPOs. If they do not produce any blocks (because delegators fled their bad offer), they also don't get the PoolCost. It's name is a lie.
