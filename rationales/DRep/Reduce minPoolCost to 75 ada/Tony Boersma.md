I'm voting Yes. The current fixed minimum really hurts smaller stake pools, especially as block rewards drop, because it takes a bigger chunk of the rewards from pools that only make a few blocks each epoch. Lowering the floor gives smaller pools a better shot at competing for delegation and boosts returns for delegators without forcing anyone to actually lower their fees. I think this is a solid temporary fix while Cardano works on a fairer, more proportional setup like minPoolMargin.
