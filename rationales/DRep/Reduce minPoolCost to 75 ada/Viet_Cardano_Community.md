# Viet_Cardano_Community

**Proposal:** Reduce minPoolCost to 75 ada
**Vote:** No

While reducing the minimum pool cost (minPoolCost) from 170 ADA to 75 ADA might appear beneficial to small stake pools at first glance, this move actually poses significant risks to the security and decentralization of the Cardano network.

First, the minimum fee was established to prevent a "race to the bottom" and guard against Sybil attacks. Drastically lowering this fee reduces financial barriers, making it easy for large entities such as exchanges to spin up numerous "clone" pools with rock-bottom fees to attract delegations. Paradoxically, this could lead to even greater centralization of power.

Second, operating a stable, secure, and professional stake pool entails real infrastructure maintenance costs (servers, electricity, network connectivity, etc.). Excessively low fees might force operators to cut corners by using substandard systems, thereby undermining the reliability of the entire network.

This is why, during the vote held in early September, the majority of SPOs (approximately 65.5%) rejected a similar proposal. Rather than altering a single parameter in isolation, the network requires a more comprehensive overhaul (such as simultaneously adjusting the K parameter and the a0 pledge influence factor). Consequently, voting "No" at this juncture is a prudent decision to safeguard Cardano's core economic structure.
