I'm voting Yes on the Reduce minPoolCost to 75 ada proposal.

The decisive argument for me is that IO Research reversed its own position. minPoolCost existed as a Sybil defense, but the latest incentives report assesses that a high fixed-fee floor now encourages Sybil behavior rather than deterring it, since well-capitalized operators can split stake across many single-block pools and harvest the fixed fee repeatedly. When a defense mechanism becomes an arbitrage tool, the justification for it no longer holds.

And this is a matter of timing, not preference. As reserve depletion keeps shrinking block rewards, the delegator penalty on single-block pools has climbed back to 52.8%, and left alone it reaches 100% around 2028.
