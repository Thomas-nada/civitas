A minPoolMargin would make more sense in the long term. minPoolCost causes issues for smaller pools, potentially weakening decentralisation by encouraging delegators to choose larger pools. A margin-based floor would help create a more level playing field.

In the meantime, I support reducing minPoolCost. The previous lowering in 2023 provides reassurance that this will not trigger a race to the bottom.
