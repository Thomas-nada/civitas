I am voting YES. I operate a small stake pool and state that interest openly.

A fixed fee falls hardest on operators who earn least. At ~300 ada per block, the 170 ada floor takes ~57% of a single-block pool's reward, against ~3% for a twenty-block pool. At 75 ada that drops to ~25%.

It is also worsening: the single-block penalty fell to ~28% after the 2023 cut, is now above 50%, and reaches 100% around epoch 758 — at which point those delegators receive nothing.

The race-to-the-bottom concern was tested by the 2023 reduction and did not materialise. Input Output Research has since reversed its own position, finding a high fixed fee now favours Sybil fragmentation by large operators rather than deterring it. No operator is compelled to lower their fee.

TSC ratified, notice period satisfied, guardrails addressed, staged rather than straight to zero, with a reversion path. Viable small-pool economics is a precondition for a decentralised operator base.
