# Wada DRep

**Proposal:** Governance Incentives Framework 2026
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2jnqfamg4tq5aepxhggzhqg7pq5gunph4wypkyh9q3qt2cru5m2j`

---

We recognize that governance incentives are an important issue for Cardano and agree that any future incentive mechanism should be based on research, data, modeling, testing, and careful evaluation rather than being implemented prematurely. The proposal's theory of change and its intention to study the potential benefits and risks of governance incentives are therefore valuable.

However, we are unable to support the withdrawal of ₳4,207,967 from the Treasury due to significant concerns regarding value for money and financial transparency.

The proposal presents high-level budget categories but does not provide sufficient detail regarding personnel costs, compensation, contractor rates, and specific operational expenditure. For a multi-million ADA treasury request, we believe DReps and the wider Cardano community should have enough information to independently assess exactly how funds will be allocated and whether the proposed expenditure represents reasonable value for the ecosystem.

We are also concerned about the substantial allocations to conceptualization, administration and coordination, and contingency, particularly given the significant amount of existing governance research and ecosystem work that the proposal intends to build upon. With 49 relevant initiatives, proposals, research papers, and tools reportedly already identified, it remains unclear why the proposed scope requires ₳4.2 million in new Treasury funding.

While the problem being addressed is legitimate and the research may be valuable, we do not believe the current proposal provides sufficient justification for the scale of the requested budget. Governance incentives are too important to approach without proper research, but Treasury funds must equally be allocated with strong financial discipline, transparency, and demonstrable value for money.
