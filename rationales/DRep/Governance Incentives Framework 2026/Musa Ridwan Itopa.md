Voting NO on Governance Incentives Framework 2026 (₳4,207,967).

As an elected Cardano Civics Committee member, Unified Cardano Student Club President in Nigeria, and a DRep focused on inclusive, transparent, and sustainable governance, I genuinely appreciate the intention behind this proposal. Researching proper incentives for DReps and other governance actors is important work participation currently costs time and money, and we do need better data on how to encourage broader, more diverse involvement (especially from underrepresented regions like Africa and student communities).

That said, I cannot support the current budget. ₳4.21 million for a 12-month research and pilot framework feels too high relative to the scope and relative to other priorities the treasury must carefully balance. While the goals are good, the cost does not yet feel justified enough for me to vote Yes.

I remain open to a more tightly scoped, lower-cost version of this work in the future. For now, fiscal discipline and sustainable use of treasury funds lead me to vote No.

Delegate to me if you value careful, principled decisions that protect the treasury while still supporting meaningful governance improvements.
