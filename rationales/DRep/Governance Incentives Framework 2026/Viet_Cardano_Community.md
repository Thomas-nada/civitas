# Viet_Cardano_Community

**Proposal:** Governance Incentives Framework 2026

**Voter ID:** `drep1ygpzpm4q38rfueu2use5te4ylykn4smvs7cxj2ggvktcjkqpxhvf4`

Regarding the proposal "Governance Incentives Framework 2026", we recognize that the overall goal of the project to build a research framework for incentive mechanisms for governance activities on Cardano is a necessary direction. However, after careful consideration, we decided to vote NO on this specific proposal.

The main reason for our decision comes from concerns about an unreasonable budget structure. Furthermore, this proposal completely leaves open the most core bottleneck in the network's current governance system: "consensus formation". While we fully agree that Cardano needs incentive mechanisms to promote community participation, the model proposed this time has not proven to be cost-effective commensurate with the funding required from the Treasury.

Therefore, we choose to vote "No" to send a clear message: we expect and are ready to support a new proposal that optimizes the budget and focuses on thoroughly resolving the consensus issue in the future.
