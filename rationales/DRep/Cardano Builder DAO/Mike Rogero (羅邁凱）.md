# Mike Rogero (羅邁凱）

---

I do not believe BuilderDAO is the best way of supporting projects and it is ripe for collusion and fraud with its small size, and overlap among organizations and relationships.

However, management of ecosystem members by guilds has a long and storied past as a way to make sure that promising members survive and thrive.  Cardano must offer a path to surviving for those who build on it, especially when the economics are hard.  The guild model is much more fit for purpose to support builders than the DRep vote on direct treasury withdrawals by non-negotiated vendor priced single-bid proposals.

Thus, between our two functioning models - BuilderDAO is a better choice to deal with funds than the current DRep process.  Thus, I support it and what I consider its modest and justifiable ask.
