The inclusion of Cardano Yoda and Marco Grendel Moshi as dReps overseeing the treasury withdrawal is reassuring. Both have demonstrated commitment to the ecosystem and a solid grasp of Cardano’s technical foundations. 

That said, the proposal’s KPI framework remains underdeveloped. Leaning on self-reported data compromises the integrity and objectivity essential for accountable treasury spending. Without rigorous, independent verification of KPIs tied directly to on-chain metrics, funding decisions risk being driven by narratives rather than measurable impact. The DAO’s evolution toward on-chain KPI tracking is promising but still immature; it demands sharper mechanisms and stronger safeguards before full confidence can be placed in this model. 

Given the potential upside of a builder-focused treasury allocation aligned with Cardano’s 2030 vision, I offer a cautious Yes, contingent on urgent improvements in KPI transparency and third-party validation.
