I narrowly support this Treasury Withdrawal.

Cardano Builder DAO is a specialized DAO that allocates funding to proven builders aligned with Cardano’s Vision 2030 KPIs, including MAU, monthly transactions, and TVL. In 2025, 12,000,000 ADA was approved for Cardano Builder DAO through the Cardano Treasury funding process. Using that funding, the DAO operated two rounds, allocating about 11.1M ADA to 34 proposals and returning 354,790 ADA of unused funds to the Treasury. I also value its direction of using smart contract governance and KPI dashboards to make Treasury spending more measurable and transparent.

There are risks of conflicts of interest and insider bias because builders evaluate other builders. However, the past two rounds show that this was not simply broad distribution to everyone. In Round 1, 7 of 27 proposals were not funded, and in Round 2, only 14 of around 29 proposals were funded. This suggests that meaningful competition and selection took place. A process where active Cardano builders evaluate proposals through peer review, debate, temperature checks, and final voting can be seen as a specialized funding model with domain expertise.

At the same time, 20M ADA is a very large expenditure. This is not just support for individual projects; it is approval of a major funding allocation mechanism within Cardano. Therefore, I strongly expect clear KPI baselines and post-funding KPI results for each funded project, transparent allocation records, conflict-of-interest management, milestone-based release conditions, return of unused funds to the Treasury, and continuous public dashboard reporting.
