While acknowledging the project's efforts, we recommend voting NO on the Cardano Builder DAO proposal based on the following core reasons:

* Incomplete Measurement Mechanism: The proposal emphasizes linking funding to measurable KPIs (MAU, on-chain transactions, TVL), however, the monitoring mechanism is still in "Version 2" and not fully on-chain. This means transparency and verifiability are not up to par, forcing us to rely on an incomplete measurement system.
* Signs of Conflict of Interest: We note that the Clarity team, which develops the operational tools for DAO initiatives, was funded by CB DAO in 2025 and continues to play a central role. The fact that the same group is involved in both building the infrastructure and directly benefiting from the funding raises serious questions about the independence of the monitoring process.
* Lack of data on actual impact: "Success" metrics such as participation rates of 83-88% or total ADA allocated do not directly reflect the actual impact on Cardano's MAU, transactions, or TVL. The proposal still lacks specific data demonstrating how previously funded projects have actually improved these core metrics.

Due to these shortcomings, we believe the proposal currently lacks sufficient transparency and accountability to warrant further expansion of funding at this time.
