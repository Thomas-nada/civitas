While I fully recognise the necessity of this Governance Action and the value it aims to deliver to the ecosystem, I vote NO on this governance action. I dont vote no to block this GA, that train has left, I vote no to not compromise the decentralized principles that secure the Cardano network and have a unilateral celebration of that compromise go on the record. I do not condone the excuses of NDA requirements, as I can understand that the nature of the business demands privacy in numbers. I do not condone the aims of the Governance Action or want to delegitimise the effort that already went, and will still need to go in it. Also do I recognise that 70m likely wont be enough to cover all the bills. I want to respect and thank the proposers for that. 
However, to me, decentralized governance relies on rigorous process standards, which the proposers simply don't follow. The "Pentad", a term I despise, spits in the face of all the effort that went into forming decentralized Governance in the past year. It appears to establish the same founding entity power structure as before Voltaire. In my mind, approving this action in its current form would validate a precedent of unilateral authority that sidesteps the inclusive oversight decentralized Governance tried to establish and our Constitution strives to uphold.
