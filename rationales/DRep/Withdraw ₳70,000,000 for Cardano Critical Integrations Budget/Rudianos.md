I am in favor of this treasury withdrawal action. This is a needed step to address some deficiencies in the ecosystem, and as I have stated in the info action vote, I believe that the Cardano community is getting very good value for money given the intended goals of the withdrawal. There is one important aspect of this withdrawal that will likely shape how the ecosystem moves forward with similar withdrawals. This is the fact that the details of the proposed various actions are not explicitly stated in the withdrawal. Instead, the proposal gives high-level goals and asks that we put the individual decisions in the hands of Intersect and the steering committee as the administrator. Intersect and the steering committee would serve as an executive function within the existing governance structure. DReps approve the budget and the main scope, while Intersect approves the details of individual allocations. I think this is a perfectly acceptable way of moving forward. In this way, DReps remain in charge of the big picture while also allowing for a more agile group to take care of the day-to-day decisions. At the very least I believe that we could not ask for a better group of proposers or a better administrator to try this way of doing things. As such, I approve of this action, and if approved, I will keep a close eye on how the administration of the budget unfolds over time.
