Por esses motivos, entendemos que o voto "SIM" contribui para uma evolução técnica segura, eficiente e coerente da infraestrutura Plutus e do ecossistema Cardano como um todo.
[English]
We chose to vote "YES" on this governance action "Update Plutus Cost Models" (gov_action1eqhnsdyf3exhp5mqt7sdjtl7xy69wqg8tvg854psns2jt72cra3qqrcnr8r), because we understand that updating the Plutus cost models is a necessary step to prepare the Cardano network for the new capabilities that will become available following the van Rossem hard fork and the adoption of Protocol Version 11. We view positively that the proposal not only enables new primitives across Plutus V1, V2, and V3, but also improves consistency between the different language versions. These enhancements expand the capabilities of Plutus scripts in areas such as cryptography, list processing, array operations, and value and data manipulation, contributing to a more efficient and flexible smart contract platform. We also support the proposal because the changes are based on benchmarking results, have undergone technical review, and were tested in environments such as SanchoNet, Preview, and Preprod before being deployed to mainnet. We further consider it important that the proposal aligns with the constitutional guardrails applicable to cost models, particularly regarding the need to update them when introducing new primitives and the requirement for validation through benchmarking on reference architectures. For these reasons, we believe a "YES" vote supports the safe, efficient, and coherent technical evolution of the Plutus infrastructure and the broader Cardano ecosystem.
