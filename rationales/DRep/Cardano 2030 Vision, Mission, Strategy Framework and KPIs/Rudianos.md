I am voting in favor of this info action. The overall goal of the info action is a good one. In general a vision document is valuable to have to focus efforts towards a common goal, and I believe this should be supported. The vision outlined is also well thought through and aligns with my view of where Cardano should move towards in the future. The pillars identified are all important but are not assigned a particular order of priority. This does make the vision slightly less focused but is reasonable since there would be significant disagreement on that front. I do appreciate, however, that the main focus is on branding Cardano as a mission-critical, high-assurance protocol, and this focuses on its legitimate strengths. This is a good decision in my view, as we need to stop comparing all blockchains using the same metrics. Cardano's focus should be on decentralized stability and security. All the pillars in the action support that goal. The KPIs are an interesting component. I don't actually think that KPIs are required for this type of vision document, and I can't say I fully agree with the KPIs listed. I only say this in the context that this space moves so quickly that I don't think KPIs can stay relevant for a 5 year period. That said, I don't see anything that is incorrect in terms of focus and the KPIs can always change in the future if they become irrelevant and the community agrees to change the focus. Overall a solid proposal that I am happy to support.
