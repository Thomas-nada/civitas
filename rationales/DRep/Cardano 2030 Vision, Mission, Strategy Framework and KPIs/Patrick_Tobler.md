The proposal has multiple good points & same bad ones too. First of all, I appreciate the effort of trying to "unify" the community behind one vision. The reality is that Cardano lacks this kind of vision. Realistically speaking there was a time when Cardanos traffic was mostly due to NFTs but focus was not shared by the founding entities & supported by most of the community. I believe if we would've a more joint vision back then, it would've helped Cardano a lot.

I also appreciate all the interviews that have been undertaking by the Product Committee, I went through the Gitbook to look at the companies and was pleasantly surprised to see names such as Maerki Baumann on there. 

A point I definitely support is: "Actively manage the treasury & network economics actively: evolve from a passive pool to a yield-generating, multi-asset treasury, ensure L2s return value to L1, and SPOs are economically incentivised to secure the network over the long term."

Now what I don't like about this proposal is the relative lack of clarity. While yes, there are some focus points, it's still quite a wide range which almost makes it pointless.

In my opinion to really have a strong vision, we need a very simple and quick statement of a trend that Cardano is actually following that everyone can rally behind. Something like "Bitcoin L2" or "RWA Chain" or "Serious AI Chain".

That leads me two my second main critique point. Quote: "2. Create robust on-chain demand: focus on DeFi, RWA, supply chain, and payments, while making Cardano usage “invisible” for users and enterprises."

Focusing Cardano on RWA's does not make any sense. It's a lost battle. Yes, there can be RWA projects on Cardano but as of today there is almost none. All the big RWA players are on other chains. Why run after a trend we can not win?!

The same with Supply Chain. What Supply Chain projects does Cardano have? Almost none. 

Another point: I see no mention of NFTs or AI in this proposal, rendering both of my main projects as outside the scope of the official Cardano Vision if the proposal gets passed. Yes, the time for NFTs might be over but still many of the biggest projects on Cardano that lead most of the transactions are/were NFT projects. Additionally, the only large Enterprise deploying Millions of Capital on Cardano right now does so for an AI project, which also is out of scope for this vision.

Now to the KPIs. The KPIs themselves are good, onyl 3 main KPIs, very clear & very focused. But the KPI goals are a bit strange. No idea where these numbers are coming from but I think the proposal would've benefitted more from focusing on tracking these KPIs, not on setitng random goals. 

KPI targets without an owner are useless in my opinion.

Everything else is fine. But because I personally don't think this vision supports my own projects & what I'm focused on as a builder, I can not vote yes.
