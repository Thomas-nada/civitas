I am voting YES for "Cardano 2030: Vision, Mission, Strategy Framework and KPIs".

I have read these and did not identify any major issues.

There are some concerns, such as the fact that TVL KPIs are listed first and that their definitions seem somewhat vague. However, even taking these into account, the risks of moving forward with Catalyst, CBDAO, and Treasury Withdrawal without a Vision or KPIs are extremely high. Even if we read this many times and identify 100 small improvements, I believe that the first version should be approved as soon as possible.
If there are no major issues, I personally think it would be best to approve this first and make small improvements after approval.

The formulation of KPIs will not only have medium- to long-term effects, but is also expected to have the following short-term effects, which will greatly help the ecosystem from January 2026:

- It would be great if this were approved before the start of Catalyst F15 on January 12th, and an efficient F15 proposal that is deeply compatible with this roadmap would be listed.
- The weekly DM inquiries about Treasury Withdrawal could be discussed mainly based on compatibility with this Vision and KPIs.
