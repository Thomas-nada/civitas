I am voting **NO**. I commend the significant work regarding this strategy and vision, and I understand its importance for Cardano. I specifically appreciate the **open source focus** and the inclusion of **node diversity**.

However, I cannot overlook the recommendation to:
> "Fund DReps, SPOs and the Constitutional Committee based on observable activity (votes, rationales, attendance)..."

**Who decides** the value of this attendance or what observable activity really means? This metric invites centralized judgment, possible exclusion and gate-keeping.

Additionally, the proposal states this document gives DReps a "community-vetted filter to rigorously evaluate funding requests." This concerns me; DReps must act **independently**, voting based on their own judgment and principles, not bound by a static document.

I believe this needs further work, and would welcome further changes.
