# Army of Spies

**Proposal:** Cardano 2030 Vision, Mission, Strategy Framework and KPIs
**Vote:** No

---

This is a very mixed bag of a very large number of items. There are general ideas (e.g. let's ensure very high uptime for the blockchain) to which most people are very likely to agree.  But, there are also many sentiments or ideas that are more controversial (e.g. DRep incentives, SPO incentives for support of L2s, multi-asset Treasury, managed treasury, "refine the ecosystem's tokenomics").  There are items in that latter category that may or may not be even directionally desirable.  But, in any case , I think it would be far premature to commit to all these ideas at once for a timeline going all the way to 2030.  Dealing with each of these items individually would be vastly superior from my perspective.   Agreeing to all these things in one fell swoop without more detail or discussion doesn't seem prudent or desirable.   I agree with other DReps that have noted they can see scenarios in 3 or 4 years when people are trying to reject or enforce ideas based on concepts being or not being "a part of the 2026 plan".
