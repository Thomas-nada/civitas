We support the Vision 2030 Info Action as a non-binding strategic framework that provides a shared long-term direction and measurable outcomes for Cardano. It does not mandate implementation, authorize spending, or constrain future governance decisions, but offers useful reference KPIs and priorities to evaluate proposals more consistently. We view this as a guiding compass for DReps and the ecosystem, not an execution plan, and future actions must still be assessed on their own merits.
