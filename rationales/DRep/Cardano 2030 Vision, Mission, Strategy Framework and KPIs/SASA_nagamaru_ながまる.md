I understand this proposal as the first long-term vision and strategic framework that organizes where Cardano aims to go toward 2030 and the value axes along which it seeks to evolve, through a community-led and formally recognized governance process. The involvement of more than 700 participants, multiple rounds of input collection and review, and the fact that it was developed under authority and funding delegated by DReps all support the assessment that this initiative carries a meaningful degree of legitimacy and process integrity.

The proposed Vision clearly positions Cardano around its core strengths—security, reliability, and censorship resistance—by defining its role as the foundational infrastructure for mission-critical applications. I consider this a reasonable and coherent differentiation strategy in the competitive Layer 1 landscape. Furthermore, the inclusion of a Mission statement, five strategic pillars, and associated KPIs provides a shared vocabulary and reference framework that can support DReps in evaluating future proposals and treasury allocations, thereby contributing to more consistent and accountable governance decision-making.

It is also important that this proposal is explicitly positioned as a strategic document rather than a constitutional one, and that it does not bind or compel DReps to vote in any particular manner. While there is a need to remain cautious about the potential for Vision statements or KPIs to become overly rigid or mechanically applied over time, the proposal is designed to be iterative and subject to future revision through subsequent Info Actions. On this basis, I consider the risk of undue constraint at this stage to be limited.

Overall, in the context of Cardano’s transition to full on-chain governance, I view this proposal as a meaningful foundation for aligning community efforts and enabling DReps to make more strategic and explainable decisions. For these reasons, I support this proposal as an initial step toward sharing a long-term strategic direction for Cardano.
