# Wistkey Lab

**Proposal:** Cardano 2030 Vision, Mission, Strategy Framework and KPIs
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1yt2g95nkd7q3kase2jdc92fjrhuyh7zgnj27mu8fu7n57yclhp5ts`

---

Wistkey Lab appreciates the significant effort and community input invested in developing the Cardano 2030 Vision, Mission, Strategy Framework, and KPIs. We recognize the value of attempting to articulate a long-term direction for the ecosystem. However, we are voting NO at this stage due to several substantive concerns.
First, DReps must retain independent judgment when evaluating governance actions and funding decisions. Framing this document as a “community-vetted filter” for future proposals risks turning a strategic document into a soft policy or gatekeeping tool. Strategy should inform decision-making, not constrain it or condition votes against a static framework.
Second, the proposed KPIs are largely aspirational and difficult to measure or hold accountable in practice. Many outcomes described are influenced by external factors beyond direct governance control. We believe performance-based measures and clearly defined measures of effectiveness would provide a more practical and accountable way to assess progress over time.
Third, the Sustainability & Resilience pillar introduces a major strategic shift toward a managed, multi-asset treasury with yield generation and ROI targets. While this direction may be worth exploring, the proposal does not sufficiently address critical governance requirements such as risk management frameworks, accountability structures, conflict-of-interest protections, custody models, failure modes, or rollback mechanisms. Without these safeguards, the treasury strategy appears premature.
Finally, the framing of Cardano as “The World’s Operating System” is overly expansive and lacks focus. Such broad positioning risks diluting strategic clarity rather than reinforcing Cardano’s core strengths and differentiators.
