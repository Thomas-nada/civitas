We support this Info Action.

The proposal provides a clearly articulated Vision, Strategy Framework, and concrete KPIs toward 2030. These give the entire Cardano ecosystem a shared roadmap and a common set of measurable reference points that DReps, builders, and treasury proposals can align with over time.

The work originates from Intersect’s Product Committee, following a broad, community-led process and drawing on the expertise of many experienced contributors across the ecosystem. This lends both legitimacy and practical relevance to the outcome.

Our main concern is that Cardano currently lacks a clear executive function. If ecosystem-wide KPIs are not met, accountability is inherently diffuse. However, this is a structural challenge of decentralized governance rather than a flaw of this proposal itself.

Despite this limitation, we believe adopting a shared vision and KPI framework is an important step toward more disciplined, coherent, and outcome-driven governance. For these reasons, we vote Yes.
