Without clear goals and a defined strategy to reach them, any effort is likely to fail. A strong Vision and Mission strategy establishes measurable KPIs, which in turn create clear direction and accountability around what we are trying to achieve. It shows how to align effectively with the broader community rather than working in silos. We are now moving toward an organized, well managed governance structure, supported by a coordinated and professional group focused on connecting commercial entities and delivering enterprise grade solutions that generate real revenue in the real world.
