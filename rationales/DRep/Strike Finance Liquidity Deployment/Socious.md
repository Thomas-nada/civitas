Socious abstains. This proposal is not a grant but a 12-month "productive treasury" deployment: 9,000,000 ADA would be sold for USDM and deployed as liquidity into Strike Finance V2's perpetual-futures infrastructure, with yield accruing to the Treasury. Strike's traction figures (over $1.13B cumulative volume per the proposal) are notable, but converting treasury ADA into a stablecoin position concentrated in a single protocol raises market, smart-contract, and accountability questions — who bears drawdown risk, how the position is unwound, how performance is verified — that we have not independently assessed.
