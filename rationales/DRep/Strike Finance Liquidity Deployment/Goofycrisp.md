I am voting YES because this proposal is structured as a productive treasury deployment rather than a traditional grant or expense. While there are risks, I believe they should be compared fairly against the execution risks of other treasury proposals, many of which are 100% expenses with no direct return mechanism.

In this case, the Treasury retains ownership of the deployed capital, the structure includes potential yield, reporting, oversight, and a defined return of funds. Strike also has a proven track record of delivering high-quality products and has already demonstrated strong organic traction.

I believe deeper liquidity on Strike can directly benefit the Cardano ecosystem by attracting more users and increasing on-chain activity.
