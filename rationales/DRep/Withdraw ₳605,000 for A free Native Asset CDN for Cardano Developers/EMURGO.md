Summary
EMURGO as DRep votes YES on the governance action to fund A free Native Asset CDN for Cardano Developers.

Rationale
Looking back, NFTCDN has proven itself as a highly reliable infrastructure provider, serving hundreds of million API requests with zero downtime. Its tools are already embedded in key Cardano products like Pool.pm, Eternl, and NMKR, showing clear real-world utility.
By offering this service free for 18 months, the proposal lowers technical and financial barriers for native asset adoption, an area central to Cardano’s growth in NFTs and RWAs. While the scope remains narrow, its infrastructure value is well-aligned with improving developer experience and end-user content delivery. EMURGO decides to support this proposal based on this rationale.
