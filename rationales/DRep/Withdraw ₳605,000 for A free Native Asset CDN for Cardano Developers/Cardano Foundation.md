Our initial 'no' vote was based on financial and funding concerns, which have been addressed by additional information from the NFTCDN team, found in the reference materials.

This proposal funds a free Native Asset Content Delivery Network (CDN) for all Cardano developers for 18 months. This infrastructure solves a complex and expensive problem for builders, and the service is already trusted and utilized by key ecosystem projects like the Eternl and Vespr wallets. Making it free removes high-cost barriers, especially for low-revenue and non-profit projects.

Key points that justify this change:

* **Addressed Financial Concerns:** The NFTCDN team provided a clear budget breakdown for salaries and infrastructure costs based on current usage patterns.  
* **Clear Long-Term Strategy:** The 18-month funding period is strategic. It will be used to gather meaningful data to allow DReps to vote on one of three sustainable future paths for the service: making it open-source, decentralizing it, or transferring ownership to a non-profit.  
* **Appropriate Funding Venue:** Our initial assessment suggested Project Catalyst as a more suitable venue. However, the NFTCDN team clarified that no appropriate funding category exists in Catalyst for this type of infrastructure project, which led them to the Treasury Withdrawal process.

We now consider this proposal to be a suitably justified, strategic investment in public infrastructure that offers a clear path to long-term sustainability and provides immediate value to the entire Cardano developer community.
