# SIPO

**Proposal:** Withdraw ₳605,000 for A free Native Asset CDN for Cardano Developers
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh`

---

SIPO supports the proposal “A free Native Asset CDN for Cardano Developers” and votes YES.

SIPO believes that this proposal directly addresses one of the most critical technical and economic challenges in the Cardano ecosystem: the display and delivery of native asset media (NFTs & FTs) in decentralized applications.

1. Strategic Value for Developer Experience

Cardano’s roadmap emphasizes improving developer usability and reducing time-to-market. NFTCDN, by making its infrastructure freely available to all Cardano developers for 18 months, significantly lowers both the financial (OPEX/CAPEX) and technical barriers associated with native asset integration. This enables startups, open-source teams, and smaller builders to experiment and innovate without worrying about costly infrastructure burdens.

2. Cost Savings and High ROI for the Ecosystem

The proposal outlines a clear, data-backed ROI of over 17x, with $5.5M in estimated ecosystem-wide cost savings and 39,000 developer hours conserved. These savings translate into:
	•	Avoiding ~$100K per project in infra cost
	•	Speeding up product launches by 9+ months
	•	Avoiding $3M+ in ongoing operational costs for 25 projects

The ₳605,000 (~$320K) ask is thus highly justified against these tangible benefits.

3. Proven Track Record and Reliability

NFTCDN has been operating since 2022, serving over 800 million API requests with 100% uptime. It already supports flagship Cardano dApps such as Eternl, NMKR, Pool.pm, and NEWM. Making this infrastructure available for free will accelerate adoption across dozens more projects.

4. Technical Maturity and Unique Value Proposition

Unlike generic CDN providers like Cloudflare, NFTCDN is purpose-built for Cardano and supports:
	•	Direct access via asset fingerprint
	•	Automatic handling of metadata formats (CIP-25, CIP-68)
	•	Real-time chain indexing
	•	Integration with IPFS, Arweave, and on-chain content
	•	Cross-app caching and rendering safety

These features make it not just a CDN, but a high-level backend abstraction for any native asset-based Cardano app.

5. Alignment with Cardano 2025 Goals

This proposal directly supports Cardano’s 2025 ecosystem goals such as:
	•	“Get More Usage”: By removing barriers to DApp development
	•	“Developer/User Experience”: By abstracting complexity and increasing accessibility
	•	Fostering innovation across RWA, SSI, media, and educational use cases

6. Robust Governance and Oversight

The proposal is administered via Intersect under the approved ₳275M budget process, and backed by a legal contract, milestone tracking, and third-party assurance. These measures ensure delivery, accountability, and transparency.

3. 実績と信頼性の高さ

NFTCDNはすでにVerspr、Eternl、NMKR、Pool.pmなど複数の主要dAppに導入済みであり、2022年から100%稼働を継続し、累計8億回以上のAPIリクエストに対応しています。この安定した実績は、提案の信頼性を裏付けています。

4. Cardano特化型の高機能CDNであること

一般的なCDNと異なり、NFTCDNは以下の機能を標準装備しています：
	•	指紋IDによる直接アクセス
	•	CIP-25、CIP-68対応のメタデータ処理
	•	IPFS/Arweave/オンチェーンの自動フェッチ
	•	ネイティブアセットのリアルタイム監視
	•	マルチdApp間キャッシュ共有
	•	コンテンツの安全性管理（CSP制御）

これにより、各dAppが個別に複雑なミドルウェアやバックエンドを構築する必要がなくなり、開発が大幅に効率化されます。

5. Cardano 2025ロードマップとの整合性

6. ガバナンスの透明性と契約管理体制の整備

Conclusion｜結論
