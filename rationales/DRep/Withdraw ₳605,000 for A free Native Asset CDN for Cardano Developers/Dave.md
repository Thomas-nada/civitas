While improving native asset delivery is a worthy goal for ecosystem **growth**, I am voting **NO**.

I believe the proposal overstates the problem, as services like Blockfrost already solve the core challenge of querying the chain. Consequently, the 9-month build estimate seems highly inflated. A top-tier CDN like Cloudflare also offers superior global reach than a bespoke service. Given that developers can already leverage these powerful tools, I cannot justify this level of treasury spend for the value offered.
