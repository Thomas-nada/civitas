Although I love Eternl and use it almost daily, I find this proposal highly problematic. 

The justification for voting on their own proposal is insufficient. While Eternl’s delegators (like all Cardano users) would love to see the wallet succeed, this should not come through repeated treasury funding, especially at the proposed price tag.

Furthermore, the Pro plan should have been introduced years ago, before the Catalyst, Intersect, and last year’s funding rounds. Even now, we still lack clarity on what specific Pro features will be offered. Projecting that 4% of users will subscribe monthly without knowing the details does not make sense.

I suggest the team return to the drawing board and develop a truly sustainable business model, rather than relying on their voting power (which is particularly questionable for wallets) to continually draw funds from the Cardano treasury.
