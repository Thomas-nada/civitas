I really love the work Eternl has done. However, the attempt at having people pay for a "Pro" plan was already somewhat tried with the NFT collection that the Eternl team put out (I know it's not exactly the same, but it's close) and that didn't really work out. I don't generally see this kind of "pro" plan being a popular business model for wallets in general. Given Eternl has received money from the treasury before, I would prefer if the proposal just accepted the reality that until things like built-in swaps can generate more revenue (the most common wallet revenue generation model) that Eternl acts somewhat as a public good and therefore should be open sourced.
