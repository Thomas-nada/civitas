As a user of Eternl from the beginning, I see Eternl as critical infrastructure for the entire ecosystem and I find its maintenance costs to be very reasonable. The extra features that Eternl provides over other Cardano wallets are actually crucial for many such as myself. That is justification enough for my Yes vote. I would like to see Eternl move towards profit generation, however as a product manager myself I cannot see a realistic path to this. I do believe that someone will usually come around to offer a free version of any paywalled service, though I acknowledge that the lifespan of the free version is usually not long. All that is to say, I am interested to see where Eternl will take the paid features and how many users will pay for them.
