Rationale
Eternl handles 10-18% of Cardano's mainnet transactions, supports 66 languages across every major platform, and is the only wallet with a comprehensive in-app governance UI. Five years of near-zero downtime speaks for itself.
This is bridge funding while the team transitions to a paid Pro model. The repayment mechanism — returning 100% of surplus to the treasury until the full $420K is repaid, then donating 50% of excess income up to an additional $210K — is a structure I'd like to see more proposals adopt. The commitment to convert immediately to stablecoins and return any ADA surplus above $0.25 adds further discipline.
The ask feels high for a wallet, but if we don't fund the teams building core user-facing infrastructure, we lose them to other ecosystems — and replacing five years of institutional knowledge and user trust costs far more than ₳1.68M.
I support the transition to sustainability and expect the Pro model to reduce or eliminate treasury dependency by the next cycle.
