I will vote YES for "Add Constitutional Committee Member."

1) Not adding a CC would not only make treasury withdrawal impossible, but would also make parameter updates and hard forks more difficult, potentially putting the Cardano chain at risk.

2) The Cardano Constitution requires that an election be held to add a CC, and one member was elected. No other entity has the resources to hold a similar election. In other words, even if we were to reject the results of this election, it would likely be a waste of time, as I am not currently aware of any entity that would want to hold an alternative election.

3) The elected Cardano Curia members are experienced members. I had expressed the view that an individual CC would be better than a consortium CC, mainly from the perspective of CC compensation, but I have heard from Cardano Curia members that they will work without compensation. Considering this, my concerns do not apply to Cardano Curia.
