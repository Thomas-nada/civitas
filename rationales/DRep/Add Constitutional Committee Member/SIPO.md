# SIPO

**Proposal:** Add Constitutional Committee Member
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `drep1yffld2866p00cyg3ejjdewtvazgah7jjgk0s9m7m5ytmmdq33v3zh`

---

SIPO votes YES on this governance action to add a new Constitutional Committee (CC) member.

We decided to support this proposal because restoring the CC to its required minimum size is essential for Cardano’s on-chain governance to function normally. At the moment, the CC has been operating with only six members due to a recent resignation. This puts the committee below the committeeMinSize parameter and prevents it from ratifying governance actions that require CC approval. As a result, important items such as treasury budgets, key protocol parameter updates, and constitutional matters cannot move forward.

For us in SIPO, ensuring the continuity and stability of Cardano’s governance is one of the most important responsibilities as a DRep. A governance system that can recover quickly from unexpected events—such as the resignation of a committee member—is vital for maintaining trust in the ecosystem and supporting long-term growth.

This proposal is the final on-chain step to confirm the results of the snap election facilitated by Intersect. The election process was transparent, verifiable, and fully aligned with the Cardano Constitution. A total of 88 DReps participated, representing over 3.16 billion ADA. Cardano Curia—who received support from 33 DReps and 1.679 billion ADA—was selected through a legitimate and democratic process. We believe it is important to respect and uphold this community-driven decision.

By approving this proposal, the Constitutional Committee will return to a full seven-member structure, enabling Cardano to resume all governance actions without limitation. This is especially important as our ecosystem continues to evolve, requiring predictable and reliable decision-making mechanisms.

We also welcome Cardano Curia’s addition to the CC. Their expertise in governance, constitutional interpretation, and ecosystem education will contribute positively to the quality and resilience of Cardano’s decision-making processes. As Cardano moves toward greater institutional, civic, and global use cases, the presence of diverse and capable CC members becomes increasingly important.

In short, SIPO supports this proposal because:
	1.	It restores full governance functionality to the Cardano chain.
	2.	It respects the democratic outcome of a transparent snap election.
	3.	It strengthens Cardano’s institutional stability and long-term reliability.
	4.	It upholds the principles and guardrails defined in CIP-1694 and the Cardano Constitution.

For these reasons, SIPO casts a clear YES vote.

SIPOは、この「憲法委員会メンバー追加」提案に YES を投じます。

今回賛成する理由は、憲法委員会（CC）を最低必要人数の7名に戻し、オンチェーンガバナンスを正常に動かすことが、今のカルダノにとって最優先の課題だと考えているからです。現在、委員の辞任によってCCは6名体制となり、committeeMinSizeを下回ってしまいました。そのため、CCによるラチェットが必要な重要な提案──予算、ネットチェンジリミット、パラメータ変更、憲法関連など──が処理できない状態になっています。

SIPOとして、カルダノのガバナンスが安定して継続的に機能することは、DRepとして最も大切にしている責任のひとつです。委員辞任のような予期せぬ出来事から速やかに立ち直れるガバナンス体制は、エコシステム全体の信頼性や持続性にとって不可欠です。

	3.	ネットワークの制度的安定性と長期的な信頼性が高まること。
