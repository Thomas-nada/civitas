SIPO’s Rationale for Voting “Yes” on the Cardano Product Committee Treasury Withdrawal Proposal (₳750,000)

SIPO supports this proposal because it addresses a critical and timely need in the evolution of the Cardano ecosystem: the transition from a founder-led vision to a decentralized, community-driven long-term roadmap.

1. Strategic Importance

The proposal funds the creation of Cardano’s first community-led 2030 vision and a detailed 2026 roadmap, built through structured workshops, stakeholder focus groups, and product research. This marks a necessary step in empowering the Cardano community to guide its future direction—ensuring that the roadmap reflects real user needs and ecosystem priorities.

2. Fills a Major Gap in Product Research

Currently, Cardano lacks a systematic, research-driven product development framework. This initiative introduces rigorous and repeatable methods to:
	•	Identify and prioritize user pain points
	•	Guide builders and dApps with validated market insights
	•	Enable coordination between users, SPOs, developers, and governance actors

3. Community Participation and Transparency

This proposal ensures broad participation by SPOs, developers, businesses, and users through:
	•	Open workshops and focus groups
	•	Public research documentation
	•	A transparent, consultative process managed by Intersect and its elected Product Committee

4. Governance Alignment and Delivery Oversight

The project is governed under the Intersect-led budget action already approved by DReps. Funding will be transparently disbursed via audited smart contracts (by Sundae Labs, TxPipe, MLabs) and supervised by third-party auditors and an Oversight Committee (including Cardano Foundation and NMKR).

5. Concrete Deliverables

Expected outcomes include:
	•	A published 2030 Cardano Vision
	•	A 2026 roadmap co-designed by the community
	•	A centralized repository of product research and market insights
	•	A research infrastructure that supports future funding and direction-setting

6. Minimal Cost, Maximum Ecosystem Impact

At only ₳750,000, this proposal delivers ecosystem-wide benefits—strategic clarity, reduced fragmentation, and empowered builders. These are foundational for a decentralized ecosystem aiming for mainstream adoption and sustainability.

SIPO believes this proposal strengthens Cardano’s long-term resilience, maturity, and community ownership. Therefore, we cast a decisive “Yes” vote.
