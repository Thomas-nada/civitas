While global events like the Cardano Summit and regional tech gatherings can boost marketing and community engagement, as a DRep, I have significant concerns about the justification for treasury funding here. 
Firstly, based on the commitments and transparency expected from Core Entities like the Cardano Foundation regarding their Member Based Organization model, I feel those obligations have not been fully met, which reduces my trust in the governance and management of these events.
Secondly, from my personal experience attending the Cardano Summit 2024, I fail to see why treasury funds should support an event where ticket sales generate revenue. Such funding priorities seem questionable, especially when community members can independently purchase access.
Finally, many countries and community members cannot attend such events due to geopolitical or financial restrictions. Therefore, treasury resources should prioritize projects that represent and benefit the entire Cardano community inclusively, rather than costly events with limited accessibility.
For these reasons, I will vote NO on this proposal.
