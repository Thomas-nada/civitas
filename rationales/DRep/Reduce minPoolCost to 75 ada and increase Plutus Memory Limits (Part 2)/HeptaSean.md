MinPoolCost always was a bad idea. It gives small pools a relevant competitive disadvantage because it gives an overproportional share of the rewards to the pool operator when the pool only produces one or two blocks in an epoch leaving a very suboptimal return on stake for the delegators. This parameter should go down asap. A replacement by MinMargin in one of the upcoming protocol upgrades would be good, but letting MinPoolCost go down further is a good step independent of that.

For the increase of memory limits, I trust the judgement of the committee.
