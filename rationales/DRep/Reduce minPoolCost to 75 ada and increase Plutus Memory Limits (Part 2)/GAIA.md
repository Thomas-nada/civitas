This is a hard NO for me. Bundled parameter proposals should never be submitted.

These two parameters are unrelated, and it is an insult to DReps and SPOs for Intersect to be submitting this proposal as a bundled action. It also brings into question the validity and the credibility of the parameters committee.

This is essentially a way of forcing minPoolCost reduction through by bundling it with a useful and unrelated parameter change (increase Plutus Memory Limits).

By this point it has been factually proven that reducing minPoolCost will not solve the issue small pools have of attracting stake. The only way that problem will be solved is with K and a0 parameter changes, and a viable solution to solve for how a0 is broken and that both K and a0 do not stop pool splitting. This is the only way forward to solve for small pools attracting stake and surviving long term.

Reducing minPoolCost any further guarantees further monopolization of our stake pool ecosystem by multi pool operators and large pool operations. It also increases the risk that pools will be run on substandard hardware and will lack required resources for secure operation. You cannot run a secure network like Cardano for free. 

The only way a further reduction of minPoolCost should even be considered is if the fiat price of ADA increases dramatically and is continuously stable (~at the 3 USD mark), or if minVaribaleFee is introduced to balance out this overall loss of required revenue for proper pool operation.
