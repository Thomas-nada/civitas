I vote YES, with conditions.

Blockfrost is critical infrastructure for Cardano builders, wallets, and dApps. Keeping a reliable free public API available can reduce friction for developers and protect the applications that already depend on it.

My support is based on the transformation being real: the source code, trademarks, domains, and related assets must be transferred into genuine community-governed not-for-profit stewardship, with clear public reporting, usage dashboards, and legal accountability.

If this becomes a true public good for Cardano, I support it. If it remains effectively a private provider funded by the Treasury, then it would not meet the standard I expect.
