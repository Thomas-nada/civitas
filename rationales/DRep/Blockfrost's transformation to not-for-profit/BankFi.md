**Voter ID:** `drep1ytdj0mu4kfhzekjx8yragvc64fcvkxp435fcxytv2kg7l2qpyccd5`

BlockFrost has been pivotal to our team's infrastructure for 6 years without a single lapse in service. They are one of the most, if not THE most, reliable data providers on Cardano. We are not voting YES out of a bias to avoid changing our tech stack -- but our history with BlockFrost does give us confidence in our vote to support them. There may be competitors, but high-throughput data infrastructure is not a commodity; not all companies have the same offerings and capabilities. We do not have enough confidence in other data providers to readily sunset BlockFrost, and that does not even factor in all of the existing companies that utilize BlockFrost and would need to either migrate to another provider or shut down their Cardano integrations.
