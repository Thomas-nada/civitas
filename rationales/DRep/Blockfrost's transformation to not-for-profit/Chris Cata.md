# Chris Cata

**Proposal:** Blockfrost's transformation to not-for-profit
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1yfdfs28uwafjgmrkatdektlzrvha2cmvqjhuz700e04mawq23rmrg`

---

I recognize Blockfrost’s importance within the Cardano ecosystem and appreciate the effort to transition it into a community-governed public good. However, at nearly ₳10M, I cannot justify this request against other competing priorities for treasury funding. This is not a judgment on Blockfrost’s utility or the team’s intentions; rather, it reflects my view that limited treasury resources require difficult prioritization, and this proposal does not rise high enough on that list to warrant support.

Furthermore, while the proposal outlines potential paths toward long-term sustainability, the operating model beyond the 18-month transition remains largely to be determined by a future board. I would have preferred to see a more clearly defined path toward self-sufficiency before committing treasury funds.
