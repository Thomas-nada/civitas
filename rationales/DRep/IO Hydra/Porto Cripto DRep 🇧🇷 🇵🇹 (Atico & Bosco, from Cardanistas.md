[English]
We chose to vote "YES" on this governance action "IO: Hydra" (gov_action1fah9m7dxu99af8jqdc4mkrgs3va790nyh9tfhycq2wsvrm47p4rsqtcm6ry), because we believe scalability is a strategic necessity for Cardano’s future. We consider Hydra to have evolved beyond an experimental initiative, demonstrating practical value across multiple real-world use cases, including DeFi, payments, autonomous agents, gaming, voting infrastructure, and commercial applications. By providing transaction finality in under one second, near-zero fees, and settlement secured by the Layer 1 blockchain, Hydra strengthens Cardano’s ability to support high-performance applications and compete for new users, developers, and ecosystem projects. We also support the proposal because it presents clearly defined deliverables organized around performance optimization, operational excellence, ecosystem support, and improvements to the developer experience. We view positively that funding is tied to milestone-based disbursements, with administration by Intersect and independent verification, increasing transparency and reducing execution risk. We believe that investing in Hydra’s continued development protects previous infrastructure investments, expands real network usage, contributes to higher TVL, transaction volume, and protocol revenue, while strengthening Cardano’s adoption and reducing the likelihood that applications migrate to competing blockchain ecosystems due to scalability limitations.
