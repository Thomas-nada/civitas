I conditionally support this proposal. Hydra is a production-stage Layer 2 infrastructure for Cardano, and I see it as important for keeping use cases such as DeFi, AI agent commerce, micropayments, gaming, and point-of-sale applications within the Cardano ecosystem, where L1 alone is not sufficient.

This proposal is focused on Hydra v2 performance, operational quality, ecosystem support, and developer experience. It is not a marketing or abstract strategy proposal, but a largely implementation-oriented expenditure. I consider the ₳5.1M ask reasonable as an investment in Cardano’s scaling infrastructure.

At the same time, real adoption, L1 fee generation, and economic return to the Treasury are not automatic. Therefore, I ask for clear public reporting on deliverables, payment amounts, third-party assurance, real usage KPIs, and unused fund return conditions. Based on this accountability, I vote YES.
