The Cardano Foundation abstained from the previous version of the governance action due to bundling of two distinct Layer 2 solutions, contested IP risks, and insufficient budget granularity. We commend the proposers for submitting a revised proposal that extracts Midgard, effectively halving the requested budget to focus on a single, coherent Hydra workstream.

Our decision to support this governance action is driven by the following factors:

* **Constructive Rescoping:** The removal of Midgard addresses our primary concerns regarding structural bundling and contested IP. The proposal now presents a focused 5,100,781 ADA workstream dedicated to maturing Hydra.  
* **Enterprise Utility and Developer Experience:** Layer 2 scaling solutions are valuable for expanding Cardano's enterprise adoption and high-volume use cases. We value the team's stated commitment to addressing technical debt, improving tooling, and delivering features requested by production users and pipeline integrators.  
* **Administrative Safeguards:** The deployment of funds is based on  milestone-gated disbursements, an independent third-party assurer, Intersect oversight, and a proportional refund mechanism for undisbursed funds. This helps mitigate execution risks and improves transparency of delivery. 

There are elements of the revised proposal which warrant further clarity, and which we expect clear reporting on:

* **Financial Granularity and Proposal Overlap:** The proposal lacks granularity on milestone delivery and associated cost. . Clarity on the work already funded and delivered and the new work to be performed under this proposal could be improved.   
* **Roadmap Clarity:** The development roadmap, including precise deliverables and their associated timelines, remains opaque and requires further definition to ensure effective milestone assessment.
