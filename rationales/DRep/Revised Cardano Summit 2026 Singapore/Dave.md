Singapore is a great place to do this, and I absolutely back the decision of location. The strategic value of aligning with TOKEN2049 in a major financial hub is undeniable.

I am formally registering a NO vote on the Cardano Summit 2026 in Singapore Treasury Withdrawal. This was an incredibly difficult decision, and I want to begin by explicitly acknowledging how much I value the Cardano Foundation's continued hard work and unwavering commitment to expanding our global footprint.

To be completely transparent, I was massively on the fence regarding this proposal. Out of all the marketing and event funding requests presented to the community, this Summit was the absolute only event spend I was genuinely prepared to consider at this time. I also deeply appreciate the feedback they have taken on board since the original proposal, which demonstrates a commendable willingness to listen to the community.

However, as a DRep entrusted with delegation from the Cardano Foundation, I have an absolute duty to stay true to my core principles and beliefs. Given the current market position of ADA, I simply cannot support this magnitude of spend for the proposal in question. An ask of 7.8 million ADA for a single event is exceptionally high, particularly when paired with a milestone structure that unlocks eighty percent of the capital immediately upon enactment. I have to prioritize this finite treasury spend toward other critical areas of the ecosystem right now.

Additionally, I want to highlight a broader reality for the community. Voting is incredibly difficult for DReps right now. We are entirely unpaid, doing our absolute best to diligently review and work through a massive volume of significant proposals currently in flight. While I deeply respect the Foundation's strategic vision for Singapore, strict fiscal discipline must take precedence under these specific financial parameters.
