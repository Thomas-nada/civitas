Honestly, I am strongly against this kind of spending on summits, parties, guest artists, and what feels like excessive use of treasury funds, especially under current market conditions. On top of that, it feels like we are reinforcing the same echo chamber the same Cardano community gathering at a Cardano-focused event. If the goal is growth and adoption, then we should be prioritizing initiatives that expand beyond the existing community and deliver measurable results.
We are not in a bull market with ADA at all-time highs to justify this kind of spending on events and celebrations. Additionally, there have already been several summits, and from my perspective, I don’t see the value this event brings especially when it is being funded by the treasury.
