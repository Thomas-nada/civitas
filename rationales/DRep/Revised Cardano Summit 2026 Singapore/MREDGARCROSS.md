Revised Cardano Summit 2026 Singapore — I’m voting NO.

This proposal asks for **7,800,000 ADA** from the Cardano treasury to fund Cardano Summit 2026 in Singapore.

And honestly, I see no real value in spending that kind of money on stages, coffee, flights, hotels, networking, panels, and presentations.

Yes, they call it:

• institutional exposure
• strategic meetings
• ecosystem growth
• enterprise adoption
• global visibility

But the simple reality is this:

millions of ADA from the treasury are being spent on a short event that will come and go.

Meanwhile, real governance work happens every single day.

In my opinion, it would make far more sense to allocate treasury funds toward supporting DReps for actual governance work.

Because DReps are the ones who:

• review proposals
• analyze budgets
• vote on governance actions
• explain governance to the community
• protect ADA holders’ interests
• actively participate in the future of Cardano

A conference lasts two days.

Governance work never stops.

That’s why my vote is — NO.
