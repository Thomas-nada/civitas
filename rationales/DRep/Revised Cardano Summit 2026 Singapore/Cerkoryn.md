I would very much love to see a vibrant Cardano Summit event this year, but this proposal is unfortunately difficult to justify spending treasury funds on in this point in time IMHO.  While it would be great to see many industry giants exposed to Cardano, I feel like we are in a position where we don't really have much to offer that other blockchains can't also do just as well or better.  In addition, a Cardano-only event is less likely to give us the visibility we desire compared to a joint event with the larger cryptocurrency/blockchain industry.  As much as it pains me to say it, I think Cardano needs to spend more time focusing on key infrastructure such as alt. nodes and Leios, as well as unique commercial use cases that truly leverage the UTxO model that is our golden goose.  I think that voting to spend treasury funds on in-person events right now is putting the cart before the horse.  For that reason I have to unfortunately vote NO on this proposal.
