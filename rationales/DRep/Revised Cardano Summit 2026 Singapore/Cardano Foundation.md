The Cardano Foundation has submitted this revised Cardano Summit 2026 proposal in direct response to significant community feedback and input. It is clear that we did not sufficiently engage with the Community on this topic prior to the first proposal. We continue to believe in the strategic value and the leaner, optimized budget of this revised Cardano Summit.

However, in light of the significant community feedback and as a signal that we continually strive to improve our engagement with the community, we feel that it is appropriate to ABSTAIN on this proposal, so that the community can independently direct its course.

This abstention is a reflection of the unique history of this specific proposal. It is not a precedent for how the Cardano Foundation will vote on any future proposals. We will continue to vote on any proposal based on the principles set out in “Our Cardano” and what we believe is in the best interests of Cardano and its ecosystem.
