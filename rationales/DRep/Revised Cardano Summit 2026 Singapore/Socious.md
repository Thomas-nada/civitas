Socious votes Yes. This is the Cardano Foundation's revised proposal for the 2026 Summit in Singapore (5–6 October): 7,800,000 ADA (about $1.95M), a budget cut by 22% — $550,000 — after community feedback, decoupled from EMURGO's separate TOKEN2049 request, with the stage count reduced from three to two and the Foundation's own in-kind contribution increased.

The flagship Summit is a primary venue for reaching institutions, enterprises, and media at a moment when Cardano's interoperability and institutional readiness are advancing. Socious sees clear value in that.

What converts the Yes from "reasonable" to "earned" is the process. The Foundation took explicit DRep concern about bear-market spending, returned with a materially smaller and restructured ask, and tied this one to measurable targets — enterprise leads, post-event strategic meetings, developer participation, and hackathon submissions captured in a KPI framework. Rewarding proposers who listen and add accountability is good governance in itself. Socious supports this proposal.
