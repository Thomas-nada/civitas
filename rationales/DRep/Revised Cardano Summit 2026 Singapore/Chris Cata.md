I am voting Yes.
The proposal being separated from TOKEN2049 addresses one of my main concerns from the prior version. The more focused scope, along with a modest reduction in total cost and a leaner event structure, moves this in a better direction.
I still believe there is opportunity cost here. My original concerns around pipeline, conversion, and post-event outcomes are not fully satisfied, but I’m also not sure they realistically can be at this stage. This is still partly an awareness and engagement effort, and the real test will come after the event.
I don’t know that the Cardano Foundation can fully predict what this converts into, but I do think they understand where Cardano needs to be represented and who the target audience is. Given the location, timing, strategic opportunity, and Cardano’s position as an L1 that needs to show up in these conversations, I view this as an acceptable proposal and cost.
That said, support here should not be interpreted as a blank check for future Summits. We need to take a hard look at the results in the months after the event and evaluate what actually came from it. Future Summit proposals should be benchmarked against the outcomes of this one.
