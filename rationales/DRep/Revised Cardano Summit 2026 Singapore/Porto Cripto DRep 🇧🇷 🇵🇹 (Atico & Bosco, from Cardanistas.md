[English]
We chose to vote "YES" on this governance action "Revised Cardano Summit 2026 Singapore" (gov_action10dp9wzmgt2nqshyrghufff4sfhcxedhmzluly5k0azguatnsthwqqs84cjf), because we understand that, although the requested amount is significant and must be assessed responsibly, the revised version demonstrates a concrete effort to reduce costs, including a 22% budget reduction, a greater internal contribution from the Cardano Foundation, and a leaner scope without abandoning the event’s strategic ambition. The Cardano Summit 2026 in Singapore represents an important opportunity to position Cardano beyond the crypto retail audience. The choice of location and timing seeks to place the ecosystem in front of financial institutions, companies, regulators, investors, builders, and strategic partners capable of accelerating real adoption, commercial integrations, and the development of new use cases. Rather than being merely a community event, the proposal targets an audience with the potential to generate qualified relationships, institutional opportunities, and long-term impact for the ecosystem. For these reasons, we believe that the investment, despite being substantial, can be justified by the combination of global visibility, focus on institutional adoption, support for builders, and accountability mechanisms included in the proposal. Cardano’s success in the coming years will depend not only on the quality of its technology, but also on the ability to present that technology to the right stakeholders, in the right places, and at the right time.
