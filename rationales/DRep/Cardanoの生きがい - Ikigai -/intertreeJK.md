Under the definition of 'Ikigai', as put forward by Héctor Garcia, co-author of the book 'Ikigai: The Japanese Secret to a Long and Happy Life', it is stated that having an Ikigai is made of having a combination of:

1. What you are good at
2. What you love
3. What the world needs
&
4. What you can be paid for

As I am currently not completely sure that the Role of DRep should be a paid position in the future, I am choosing to Vote Abstain on this Proposal to reflect that unsureness.
