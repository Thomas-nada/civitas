Summary
EMURGO as DRep votes YES on the governance action to fund the Eternl Maintenance proposal.

Rationale
Eternl is one of the most widely used wallets in the Cardano ecosystem, playing a key role in user accessibility and developer testing. While the wallet remains a closed-source product, we recognize the operational importance of keeping its services stable and responsive.

This proposal provides transparent, cost-based justification for maintenance over the next year. Given its current role and community reach, we support this proposal with the understanding that further openness and long-term sustainability planning will be needed going forward.
