This might be interesting to build, but I'm not convinced it addresses the hardest problems in real-world lending.

Lending in the real world is fraught with risks, even when you lend money to a close friend or family member you personally know.

AURORA addresses one missing layer, but there are many other pieces that need to be in place for real credit markets to work on Cardano. Some of those missing pieces seem much more difficult and important than the infrastructure proposed here.

I understand what the team wants to build, but I haven't seen convincing evidence that the other essential pieces are falling into place, or that there is sufficient demand for this particular infrastructure.

It seems like one part of a much bigger picture, but a very large and critical part of that picture is still missing. I'm not convinced that funding this part now is justified.
