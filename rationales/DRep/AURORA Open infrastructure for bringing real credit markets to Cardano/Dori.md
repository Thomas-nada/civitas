I'm voting No on AURORA.

Fairway has already received Treasury funding through Catalyst, yet I can't find a track record of that work turning into real usage on Cardano mainnet. The lending model this proposal builds on has no recorded mainnet use either, and no lender or capital provider has committed to using this infrastructure.

With no track record and no demand, I don't understand why the team is now asking for roughly ten times its previous grant. The right order is to prove real usage first, then ask for support. So I'm voting No.
