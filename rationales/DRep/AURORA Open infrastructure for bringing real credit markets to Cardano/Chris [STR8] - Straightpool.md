# Chris [STR8] - Straightpool

**Proposal:** AURORA Open infrastructure for bringing real credit markets to Cardano
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y27qjjnz9a7q6zmvpajzqpqylnka6lpkvcm9sgjrdheqt0qq6pchw`

---

I recognize the potential value of shared, open-source infrastructure for Cardano credit markets.

However, I cannot justify the requested ₳940,000 given the limited evidence of committed adoption, demonstrable technical progress, and the lead team's relevant delivery track record. The proposal also does not sufficiently establish how its deliverables complement existing Treasury-funded ecosystem initiatives, including AlphaGrowth, rather than creating overlapping efforts.

While I appreciate the milestone-based funding and open-source commitments, the cost and execution risks remain too high relative to the demonstrated demand and expected ecosystem impact.

**I therefore vote NO.**
