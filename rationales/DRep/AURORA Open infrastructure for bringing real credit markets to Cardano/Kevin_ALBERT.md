I vote NO on "AURORA: Open infrastructure for bringing real credit markets to Cardano." I support RWA credit where there is credible collateral, underwriting, enforceable default processes and a real path to use, but AURORA funds a discovery and metadata layer before any mainnet lending market, named adopter, capital provider or pilot has been demonstrated. The difficult parts of real credit, jurisdictional compliance, underwriting, collateral, defaults and recovery, are outside this scope, so open APIs and a testnet demonstration do not yet prove public value. The Treasury would take early product and adoption risk without a repayment, revenue-sharing or credible recovery mechanism if adjacent commercial layers later succeed. I would reconsider a smaller, independently adopted, mainnet-proven version with immutable governance metadata and stronger payment protection.
