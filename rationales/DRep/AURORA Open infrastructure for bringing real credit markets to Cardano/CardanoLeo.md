# AURORA: Open Infrastructure for Bringing Real Credit Markets to Cardano

**My Position:** **NO**

This proposal requests **940,000 ADA** to build infrastructure for discovering and verifying credit opportunities across different lending implementations on Cardano, with an estimated delivery period of five months.

I support the development of credit markets on Cardano. However, I have several concerns about the market demand, adoption strategy, acceptance criteria, and the team’s experience.

---

### 1. The underlying market has yet to demonstrate real demand

* **Lack of Mainnet Activity:** According to the DBSync query results cited in the proposal review, `cardano-loans` currently has no recorded loan requests, loan offers, or active loans on mainnet. Testnet activity is also limited to a small number of examples created for the project’s documentation.
* **Absence of Commitments:** The proposal provides no named lenders, borrowers, or letters of intent demonstrating that market participants are ready to use this infrastructure.

Building a complete market discovery layer before real lending activity has been demonstrated seems **premature**.

### 2. Technical delivery does not guarantee adoption

AURORA plans to deliver metadata standards, a discovery engine, APIs, a verification framework, and developer tools.

* **The Adoption Hurdle:** These are measurable technical deliverables. However, the real value depends on whether lending protocols, applications, and capital providers actually adopt them.
* **Missing KPIs:** The proposal provides no concrete integration commitments and sets no targets for mainnet usage or adoption.

A shared standard only creates ecosystem value when multiple independent participants actually use it. Otherwise, AURORA could end up as a well-documented open-source project with little real-world usage.

### 3. The milestones do not measure actual utility

* **Output vs. Outcome:** The proposed milestones focus mainly on specifications, testnet functionality, documentation, and technical demonstrations. These deliverables can show that the software has been built, but they do *not* demonstrate that it has been integrated into real lending applications or adopted by capital providers.
* **Governance Standards:** Even smaller proposals under Catalyst are expected to demonstrate team capability, adoption potential, market strategy, and realistic milestones. For a treasury request of **940,000 ADA**, I believe stronger accountability and measurable adoption requirements should be expected.

### 4. The team’s experience does not match the scope of this request

* **Track Record Mismatch:** Fairway, the lead implementation team, has received Catalyst funding three times. However, its previous projects have mainly focused on recruitment and digital identity, with *no clear track record* in lending systems or market infrastructure.
* **Previous Project Reality:** One example is F12, which involved Ethiopia’s national identity system. According to the team’s own close-out report, **Cardano was not used** during the pilot.
* **Scale Discrepancy:** This proposal requests approximately **ten times** the team’s previous largest individual grant.

Based on the available record, I have not seen sufficient evidence of relevant delivery experience to give me confidence in a project of this scale.

---

## My Conclusion

I support the direction of building credit markets on Cardano, and I believe shared market discovery infrastructure could have long-term value.

However, this proposal lacks sufficient evidence of market demand, concrete integration commitments, and acceptance criteria that measure actual adoption. Combined with the team’s limited experience in this area, I do not believe the current proposal justifies a treasury allocation of **940,000 ADA**.

For these reasons, based on the publicly available information, **I will vote NO**.

If the team can provide concrete integration commitments, demonstrate interest from capital providers, and include measurable adoption requirements in its milestones, I would be willing to reconsider my position.
