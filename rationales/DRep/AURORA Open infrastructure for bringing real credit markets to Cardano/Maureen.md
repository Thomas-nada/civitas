AURORA wants to build a really nice market stall for on-chain loans. Open source, independent multisig, audits, and aimed at credit cooperatives in Africa. I love the stall.
But nobody has agreed to sell anything at it yet. No lender, no cooperative, no protocol. The build ends at a testnet demo, ₳140K goes out on day one, and the proposal sits on a GitHub branch that can be edited after we vote.
So it's No for now. Fairway says they're resubmitting. If they came back with the anchor on IPFS and one cooperative already standing behind the counter, then we'll talk.
