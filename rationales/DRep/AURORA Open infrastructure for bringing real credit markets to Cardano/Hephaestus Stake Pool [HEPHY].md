I vote NO on the Treasury Withdrawal action "AURORA: Open infrastructure for bringing real credit markets to Cardano." (14d6a48df8b9099a3e727e1bf6a9ac6c911341a9601e91c7d3ac799bfd89b01b#0).

Despite appearing aware of the immutable storage requirement (one of the supporting links is immutable), the [action metadata](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fairway-global/aurora-proposal/refs/heads/main/data.jsonld) itself is not immutable and uses a plain GitHub link instead of a GitHub permalink. As previously stated in the Copper Reserve proposal, generating a GitHub permalink is as simple as pressing the "y" key in the repository. The proposer clearly recognises on some level that immutable storage is a requirement, as some immutable links have been provided in the supporting links. However, Article II, Section 6 of the [Cardano Constitution](https://cardano.org/constitution/#section-6-governance-action-standards) mandates that the proposal "document hosted by such a URL shall be immutable and incapable of being altered after submission." As such, this should be deemed unconstitutional by the Constitutional Committee, and therefore I will not spend additional time evaluating the proposal at this time. The time of voters should be respected by proposers, and they should not be expected to provide feedback on a proposal that is not going to pass the most basic of constitutional requirements first. I will note that the [receiving address](https://cardanoscan.io/stakeKey/stake179uthrjzt0395mukqctux9u2zchytk5py4hr0xd9ka7jtdqa9jgyq?tab=voteDelegations) does meet the constitutional requirements of not being delegated to a stake pool and is set to the `always_abstain` DRep.
