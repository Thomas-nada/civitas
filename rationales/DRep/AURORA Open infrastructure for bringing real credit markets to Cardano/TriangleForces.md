**Another Treasury withdrawal of 1M ADA to fund a market layer before any evidence that a market exists to use it.** All for a "testnet demonstration." 

As other DReps have stated as well, **there's no established demand through observed usage, named lenders, borrowers, or partner commitments.** And without that, AURORA's added value to this ecosystem of ours rests on an assumption, and that is the kind I do not fund. Especially not at this size.

I've** no doubt that the software is probably buildable, but my gut tells me the technical scope is smaller than 1M ADA.** There is no mainnet KPI, no committed originator and no committed capital provider. **A standard with no ecosystem can pass every milestone and still deliver nothing of lasting value. **Speaking of which, after the funding ends, I've no clue who pays to operate and maintain this. 

**Good plumbing matters, but I'd like to see at least one thirsty customer before we fund the pipes.**
