What I support. Open standards and shared infrastructure can add real value to Cardano, and a working credit market on Cardano would. My No is to paying for that infrastructure before there is anything for it to serve.

No market to serve. AURORA indexes credit opportunities held as individual Loan Request UTxOs and made discoverable through beacon tokens. That is the model of cardano-loans, the protocol of the proposal's technical advisor, which the proposal cites as "directly relevant architectural experience". I checked my own db-sync. The loan validator of cardano-loans version 1 and version 2, b07bdac32910d4c1ea425edadf87ebbe1393394ee36623e97e1d14cc and 9ea2fdfeb892c9b62cdb42f004a606cd39dd5edd0b9833d8ad6dcf09, has never held an output on mainnet, and no minting policy on mainnet has minted an Ask, Offer or Active token in more than one transaction. The protocol's own guide states: "Registration has already been done for all scripts for the preproduction testnet." On mainnet there are no loan requests for AURORA to discover.

No demand shown. The proposal names no lending protocol that will adopt AURORA, and no lender, borrower, capital provider or letter of intent. It states the risk itself: "Open standards do not guarantee adoption by future lending protocols, applications or capital providers." And it leaves adoption out of what is measured: "Treasury delivery is measured by the completion and usability of the public infrastructure rather than by speculative adoption targets." The word mainnet does not appear in the full proposal. Delivery "culminates in an end-to-end testnet demonstration", and nothing after the five months is funded.

A fixed fee, paid in advance. 800,000 ada, 85 percent of the request, is "a fixed implementation fee payable to Fairway", paid "rather than reimbursed against Fairway's actual internal costs". The proposal shows no staffing, hours or rates. "Funding is released in advance for each implementation stage": 140,000 ada at commencement before anything is delivered, up to 460,000 ada after the first milestone, 750,000 ada after the second and the full 940,000 ada after the third. The money for the final independent review and release may leave custody before that work is done. Unused review, hosting and contingency funds may become "additional implementation payments to Fairway".

Limited protection for the treasury. The 3 of 5 multisignature holds only what is not yet paid out. "Implementation instalments leave this custody structure when transferred to Fairway", and on termination only the "ADA remaining under administrator custody" returns to the treasury. The administrators check that outputs "reasonably satisfy the approved completion criteria" and "are not expected to reproduce the engineering work or independently reimplement AURORA."
