I appreciate Fairway and Sundial's desire to unify Cardano's credit markets, but I am voting NO on the AURORA proposal. While the 940,000 ADA ask is smaller than recent requests, it remains disproportionately high for the actual value delivered. At a time when the ADA price is struggling, approving near-million ADA grants for non-essential API and indexing frameworks creates unnecessary sell pressure that is ultimately borne by everyday token holders.
