# Wada DRep

**Proposal:** AURORA Open infrastructure for bringing real credit markets to Cardano
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2jnqfamg4tq5aepxhggzhqg7pq5gunph4wypkyh9q3qt2cru5m2j`

---

We recognise the strategic importance of developing real-world credit markets on Cardano and acknowledge the potential value of AURORA's proposed shared infrastructure for standardising, discovering and verifying credit opportunities across independent lending implementations.

However, we do not consider the current proposal sufficiently justified for a 940,000 ADA treasury allocation.

Our decision is based on three principal concerns:

1. Insufficient evidence of ecosystem demand and adoption

Although the proposed infrastructure addresses a relevant ecosystem opportunity, the proposal does not demonstrate sufficient commitment from independent lending protocols, credit originators or capital providers who intend to use it. Without credible integration commitments or a practical pilot involving actual market participants, there is a significant risk of financing infrastructure without establishing meaningful adoption.

2. Insufficient budget transparency and value for money

The proposal allocates 800,000 ADA as a fixed implementation fee to Fairway without providing adequate detail on contributor compensation, workstream allocations, development resources or third-party costs. While we recognise the defined funding request and milestone structure, the available information does not allow DReps to properly assess whether the implementation fee is proportionate to the expected deliverables.

3. Accountability and treasury protection concerns

The proposed independent 3-of-5 multisignature arrangement and independent audits are positive safeguards. However, concerns remain regarding advance milestone payments, independent verification of deliverables, administrator relationships and the clarity of expenditure controls. Stronger safeguards are necessary to ensure that treasury funds are released against independently verified results and that potential conflicts of interest are appropriately managed.

We believe open credit-market infrastructure is worth pursuing on Cardano. Nevertheless, strategic importance alone is insufficient justification for treasury funding without demonstrated demand, transparent costs, robust delivery controls and a credible long-term sustainability model.

We encourage the proposers to address these shortcomings through a revised proposal that establishes measurable ecosystem adoption commitments, provides a detailed implementation budget, strengthens independent oversight and ensures that the infrastructure remains a sustainable public good.
