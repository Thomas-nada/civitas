I don't have confidence in this proposal, as I see many overlaps and existing funded endeavors pursuing both the infrastructure and liquidity flow and incentives for credit markets. I would suggest for the team to work in synergy with AlphaGrowth and other endeavors for unification.

With LayerZero coming to Cardano in December, and Midnight now launching v9 ledger for composability and cross-chain efforts, I just don't see the value for ADA holders in a 5 month infrastructural campaign when those efforts could likely be poured into existing funded teams in the form of grants or collaborative work, especially because our front-end dApps are still lacking strong UX UI to drive those credit markets, to bring users from other ecosystems, hence why I've been pointing to RealFi, AlphaGrowth, LayerZero, etc.

Ultimately, I'm not a developer, but as a certified UX Designer, I don't see the need from my perspective, on pouring more infrastructural funding when there's already been a colossal amount poured in.

Perhaps working with RealFi could provide some opportunities? RealFi is driving credit markets with yield-bearing assets, and I often see what seems to be fragmentation among proposals where there could be unification on aligned missions.

Tooling means little without the front-end focus with usability testing, competitive benchmarking, research on the demand within this ecosystem and others, human-centered design, and so on, otherwise it's just 'code' that's left without the real world implementation and driving the user journey.

I imagine there could be value to the other dApps to enhance their existing features, although I just don't have confidence at this time, that's my perspective as a UX Designer, I wish the team well on their endeavors.
