# Cardanians

**Proposal:** AURORA Open infrastructure for bringing real credit markets to Cardano
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1yt4lyf0fwlrz8k6j5evd3rxw0sqp67qkh36su98a97q3qsc0h03y4`

---

We support AURORA’s goal of reusable, open-source credit-market infrastructure. However, the proposal needs stronger evidence of likely adoption, including identified pilot participants and concrete integration plans. A testnet demonstration alone does not sufficiently establish the expected ecosystem value.

The payment schedule also permits the full allocation to leave independent custody before final reviews and acceptance. We would expect a retained final payment and clearer recovery provisions for advances associated with unfinished work.

We remain open to a revised proposal with stronger adoption evidence, funding protections and a clear maintenance plan. Our objective is to support builders while ensuring Treasury spending delivers lasting value for Cardano.
