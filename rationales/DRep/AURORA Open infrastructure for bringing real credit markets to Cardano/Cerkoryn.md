---

I'm voting Abstain. I'm a big fan of Fallen Icarus's work and the DeFi Kernel philosophy, and I want to see this kind of thing succeed. But it's not clear to me how much of this overlaps with existing work like CIP-89 and Cardano-Loans, which already cover beacon-based discovery, filtering, and credit histories, or with the attestation work in CIP-170. I'd like the proposal to spell out which parts are new and which extend what's already there.

I'd also like to see a stronger plan for bootstrapping liquidity and demand, such as committed lenders or a pilot with real capital, instead of more standards and tools that might go unused.  I believe this will need to be resubmitted anyway, and I hope these points help make the next version one I can fully support.
