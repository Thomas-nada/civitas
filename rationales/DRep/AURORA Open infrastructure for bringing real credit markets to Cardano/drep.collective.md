# drep.collective

**Proposal:** AURORA Open infrastructure for bringing real credit markets to Cardano
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1ydwtnlvdd8atlj5c5ylgw4kg5e640vyt8resattrjmwufyg4qclf7`

---

We vote NO on this proposal. We recognize the potential value of open infrastructure that enables different lending protocols to share standardized credit information, improve discoverability and reduce integration costs across Cardano.
However, we believe Treasury funding should prioritize infrastructure with demonstrated ecosystem demand and clear benefits for multiple independent projects. The proposal does not provide sufficient evidence of committed integrations or adoption, making its practical impact difficult to assess. We are also concerned about the relationship between Treasury-funded development and AURORA's longer-term commercial ambitions.
Finally, the constitutional concerns regarding the mutable governance metadata must be resolved. While we support the broader vision of strengthening Cardano's credit markets, we cannot support this proposal in its current form.
