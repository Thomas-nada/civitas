I recognize the public-good value of this proposal. Shared standards, discovery infrastructure, and verification frameworks can reduce fragmentation and improve the efficiency of Cardano’s lending ecosystem. The requested budget is modest relative to many treasury proposals, and the deliverables are open source. However, with the remaining Net Change Limit headroom now severely constrained, I believe treasury priorities must be applied more conservatively. My vote should not be interpreted as a rejection of the proposal’s merits. Under a future NCL period with greater budget capacity, I would be inclined to support it. Therefore, I vote No at this time.
