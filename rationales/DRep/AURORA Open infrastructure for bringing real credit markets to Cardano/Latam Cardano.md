While standardizing credit markets and building open-source public infrastructure are commendable goals for Cardano's long-term maturity, a critical analysis of ecosystem timing and capital prioritization dictates a more conservative stance. Cardano's treasury must currently preserve liquidity for foundational base-layer scaling (such as consensus upgrades and core node performance) and high-impact regional adoption catalysts. Consequently, favoring strict capital conservation leads to a recommendation to vote 'No' on this specific treasury allocation.
