# JohnM

**Proposal:** AURORA Open infrastructure for bringing real credit markets to Cardano
**Vote:** No
**Voter ID:** `drep1y2hky8fgx58luuuz5at5dr4xw0jzlre87wwyw3agra9f8js5eu9hp`

---

I'm voting No on AURORA. Shared discovery and verification tooling for loan-by-loan credit on Cardano is a reasonable thing for the treasury to fund, and 940,000 ada is modest for the scope. The custody design is better than most at this size. Fairway holds no signing key, and unspent ada sits with five other signers. My problem is with the terms, not the idea.

The payment terms were rewritten less than an hour before the action went on chain. Fairway's 800,000 ada is now a fixed fee, paid in advance and not reconciled against cost. Once M3 is accepted, the full 940,000 ada can leave custody before the security review, the legal review and the public release exist. The refund terms only cover ada the signers still hold, and nothing says how an advance for undelivered work comes back. The signers can also move review and contingency money to Fairway with no stated basis. The version the community reviewed kept funds in the multisig until spent. That was the better design, and it should not have changed at the last minute.

Signer independence is the safeguard this proposal leans on most. The proposal says all five signers are independent, yet Fairway's own site lists one of them, James Meidinger, as an adviser. That role may be unpaid, and I'm not suggesting bad faith. But the claim should hold up without a narrow reading.

The benefit is also hard to pin down. Delivery ends with a testnet demo. No lender, originator or capital provider is committed to using the standards, there's no mainnet target, and nobody is named to maintain the format after month five. Fairway presents this as the first stage of its own business. That's fine, but the credit format for Cardano shouldn't be left in the hands of a firm with products built around it and no stated change process.

This is a fixable No, not a verdict on the team. I would look again if the proposal came back with:

- part of the final instalment held until M4 is accepted, plus a recovery clause for advances
- a cap on extra payments from contingency
- every signer relationship disclosed, with any conflicted signer stepping back
- at least one lending implementation committed to mainnet use
- a named home and change process for the standards

I worked at the Cardano Foundation until mid-2023, but it has no part in this proposal. I don't run a stake pool. I had no expert or peer input before the deadline.
