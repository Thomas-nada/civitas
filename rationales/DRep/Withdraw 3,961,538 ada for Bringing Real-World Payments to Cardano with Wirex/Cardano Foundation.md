Our approach is designed to ensure both transparency and efficiency, applying a consistent evaluation framework (see the “[Cardano Foundation Voting Decisions for the 2026 Intersect Budget Process”](https://cardanofoundation.org/blog/voting-decisions-2026-intersect-budget-process)) to each proposal and presenting our reasoning in a single, comprehensive document.

No material changes have been made to the individual proposals since the budget proposals were approved in Hydra Voting, therefore our votes on these withdrawals reflect our original assessment and vote. Our voting decisions on the 11 treasury withdrawals are made available in the table below.

Fund administration clarity is an important consideration for our voting decisions. Our expectation is that expect Intersect, as administrator, will manage funds diligently, provide public transparency on contracts, and ensure robust oversight, holding recipients accountable for deliverables.

## **Conclusion**

The Cardano Foundation’s votes on these 11 treasury withdrawal governance actions are the direct execution of a single, comprehensive review process that began during the Hydra Voting phase. Although we present one aggregate rationale for efficiency, each proposal and governance action was individually assessed against a consistent framework anchored in our publicly stated principles.
