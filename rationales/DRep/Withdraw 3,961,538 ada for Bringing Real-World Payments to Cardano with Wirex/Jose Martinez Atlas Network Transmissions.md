The requested deployment of 3,961,538 ADA from the public treasury to fund the Wirex integration represents a systemic misallocation of capital. Ecosystem utility is strictly derived from the establishment of ground-zero, open-source primitives, not the subsidization of closed-loop, consumer-retail payment gateways for heavily capitalized incumbent vendors. They can already afford their own development for their aspired endeavours.

Developer-grade APIs capable of routing stablecoin liquidity and executing fiat settlement via smart contracts currently exist on the network. These tools are actively driving institutional adoption within automated escrow exchanges and architectures. There's better use case funding for better, more useful middleware API infrastructure.

Allocating treasury reserves to replicate existing infrastructure in the form of a legacy debit card yields negligible macroeconomic benefit. Retail point-of-sale applications inherently promote transient monetary velocity—extracting capital to traditional clearinghouses—rather than retaining structural Total Value Locked (TVL) on-chain. Network capital must be rigorously preserved to fund foundational B2B pipelines that secure sustained enterprise liquidity, rather than underwriting redundant consumer products.
