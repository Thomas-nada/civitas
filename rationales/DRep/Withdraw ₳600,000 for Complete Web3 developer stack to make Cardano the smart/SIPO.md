SIPO supports this proposal because it strategically positions Cardano as the smart contract layer for Bitcoin, unlocking Bitcoin’s liquidity while significantly expanding Cardano’s developer and user base.

Maestro’s proposal solves a long-standing limitation in the blockchain industry: Bitcoin’s lack of native smart contract capabilities. By leveraging Cardano’s robust EUTXO model and Maestro’s developer infrastructure, this initiative enables Bitcoin to access DeFi, while Cardano gains massive liquidity inflow and cross-chain utility.

The key reasons for our support are:
	1.	Cross-Chain DeFi Enablement
This proposal allows Cardano to act as Bitcoin’s smart contract execution layer, enabling advanced applications such as lending, staking, and governance for Bitcoin users. It transforms Cardano into a Bitcoin-integrated DeFi hub.
	2.	Robust Developer Tooling
Maestro’s infrastructure stack (indexers, wallet manager, mempool monitoring, etc.) lowers development barriers and accelerates time-to-market for dApps, wallets, and L2 solutions, including zkFold and BitcoinOS.
	3.	Proven Team with Cardano Track Record
Maestro has delivered infrastructure for existing Cardano products and wallets. Their continued involvement ensures stability, scalability, and reliable integration.
	4.	Bitcoin Liquidity = Cardano Growth
Access to Bitcoin’s capital and user base will dramatically improve Cardano’s TVL, ecosystem visibility, and global competitiveness.
	5.	Governance and Delivery Accountability
The contract is legally structured, milestones are clearly defined, and oversight is ensured through Intersect’s governance and a third-party assurer.

In summary, this proposal directly supports Cardano’s evolution as a Bitcoin-compatible DeFi platform and enhances its infrastructure to support large-scale growth. It’s a critical step toward making Cardano a foundational layer in the next era of Web3 finance.
