Wistkey Lab votes YES on the 300M ADA Net Change Limit.

The NCL defines the maximum potential treasury withdrawal for the period but does not authorize spending. Establishing this limit ensures governance continuity while allowing DReps to evaluate individual treasury withdrawal proposals on their merits.

We view the 300M ADA cap as a cautious and reasonable boundary, broadly aligned with the previous year’s treasury inflows and lower than the earlier 350M proposal. In the current market environment, fiscal discipline is important to protect ADA holders while still enabling necessary ecosystem investment.

Supporting this NCL does not imply support for increased spending. Wistkey Lab will continue to apply strict scrutiny to treasury withdrawal proposals and prioritize funding for neutral public infrastructure that delivers clear ecosystem-wide value.
