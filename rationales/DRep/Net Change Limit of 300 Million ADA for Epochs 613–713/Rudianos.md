I am in support of this NCL. It falls within a reasonable range for our treasury while still allowing for meaningful spending.
I also appreciate the 18-month timeframe. Getting everyone to think long-term and avoid overspending in the first six months will certainly be a challenge, but in my view, that's all part of the governance experiment. As I have said for some time now, I hope we eventually move toward a more formal budget process. Simply setting an NCL and vetting proposals as they come in until the money runs out lacks operational directionality. That said, for the time being, this proposal makes sense.
