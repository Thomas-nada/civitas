While sustaining Cardano's open-source infrastructure is critical, handing over 4 million ADA to a single individual administrator at launch creates an unacceptable centralization risk. This budget is far too expensive for an unproven pilot program in current market conditions, especially before the planned legal entity is even fully operational. I urge the proposer to return with a significantly reduced budget and a decentralized, multi-sig control structure from day one.
