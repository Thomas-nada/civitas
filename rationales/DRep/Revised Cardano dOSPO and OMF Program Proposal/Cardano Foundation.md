Our vote is based on the following factors: 

* **Accountability preconditions removed, not met.** Councils are now "advisory only" with "no veto authority," their formation "a program deliverable, not a precondition," and the administrator's designation "unconditional." Sole allocation authority over 4,094,000 ada rests with one individual at withdrawal, advised by two bodies that do not yet exist and whose members are unnamed. Article II.7.5 requires a named administrator, not an unreviewable one. The previous version called independent councils and a replaceable operator "not features — they are preconditions for the program's legitimacy." Further, governance of the legal entity to be established is not addressed.   
* **Safeguards cannot compel.** Administrator replacement, sunset, and return of uncommitted funds all route through Info Actions, which carry no enforceability.   
* **Capital precedes its evidence.** The full sum is withdrawn upfront and untranched, with 1,625,000 ada committed across twenty-five retainer slots before the week-twelve audit meant to determine them. Tier 1 rises from five or six projects to ten on a two-thirds smaller budget, without new data.  
* **Assurance is not independent.** The firm engaged to establish the charity is also its named independent auditor. No accounting credentials are detailed.  
* **Coordination undocumented.** The proposal will "replace and evolve" the Paid Open Source Model, but offers no documentation or evidence of Intersect's approval or coordination.  
* **Measurability declined.** WP3 to WP5, a third of the request, carry no milestones or acceptance criteria. Operations rose to 18.6 percent, the itemised staffing table was withdrawn, and the 255,500 ada WP3 stipend discrepancy we named previously is unchanged.
