EN — iFly (SWADA) votes YES. This program funds sustained maintenance of the open-source libraries, SDKs, indexers and tooling Cardano builders depend on — an under-funded layer that is otherwise a systemic single point of failure. It is a scaled-down 12-month pilot (4,094,000 ADA) with sunset clauses and an on-chain-replaceable administrator.
Disclosure of interest: in keeping with the transparency I promised in my DRep profile, my own project (Intellishares / Clastenium) is built directly on community tooling of exactly this kind — for example Ogmios, Kupo and Lucid. I judge the network-wide benefit to be real independently of that interest, but disclose it so my vote can be weighed accordingly.
Reservation: funds are released to a single-key stake address rather than escrow, with sole allocation authority for roughly the first six months. I would prefer escrowed, milestone-released custody, and I support the on-chain replaceability and sunset mechanisms if oversight lags. On balance the builder-foundation benefit and modest size outweigh this concern. I vote YES.
