I am voting a strong **YES**. This is a direct investment in the **decentralization** of Cardano's infrastructure layer.

The high cost of running a full node is a centralizing force. By providing a lightweight alternative, Dolos empowers more developers and teams to run their own backends, reducing reliance on a few large API providers. This enhances ecosystem resilience and **sustainability**. My continued confidence in the TxPipe team to deliver critical, open-source public goods makes this an easy decision.
