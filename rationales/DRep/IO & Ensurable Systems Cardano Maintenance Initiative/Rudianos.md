I am voting in favor of the Cardano Maintenance Initiative. While the funding request is substantial, this is a "keep the lights on" type of proposal, and I have little doubt that IO is the most qualified entity to do this work. The funding ask is hard for a non-expert to justify, as it seems very high, but without a competing bid, this is not really a negotiable initiative. If you need to maintain your roof and there's only one roofer, you pay the roofer.
I am voting in favor because core infrastructure maintenance is the highest-priority use of treasury funds. Protecting the integrity and uptime of the Cardano blockchain is essential for institutional trust and long-term ecosystem sustainability.
