I am voting NO on the dual event proposal for the Cardano Summit and TOKEN2049. Initially, I strongly considered abstaining, given that approximately thirty percent of my voting power comes directly from the Cardano Foundation. However, stepping aside would be a profound injustice to the rest of my loyal delegators. I possess a strong conviction on this matter, and my duty requires me to represent my entire constituency transparently.

Candidly, the requested expenditure of over fourteen million ADA is simply too high. We currently lack the luxury to sustain spending of this magnitude. With ADA having fallen out of the top ten and struggling in price action against competing blockchains, we must operate with ruthless fiscal responsibility. I do not believe the tangible value returned to our ecosystem justifies this massive capital outlay.

If our ambition is to host flagship global events, our primary focus must first be directed toward reclaiming our position in the top ten and pushing into the top five. Attempting to improve our relevance by purchasing presence at large industry events does not fundamentally drive the sustainable growth and fiscal discipline we require to compete right now. We must prioritize strict capital efficiency over expensive marketing, and therefore, I must firmly reject this proposal.
