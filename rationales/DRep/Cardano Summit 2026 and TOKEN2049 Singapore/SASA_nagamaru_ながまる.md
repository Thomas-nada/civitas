I will abstain on this proposal.

At the current stage of Cardano’s development, I believe that in addition to building products and infrastructure, it is essential to strengthen awareness and business engagement to connect those offerings with external users and partners. Expanding supply alone is not sufficient if it does not translate into real usage, partnerships, and sustainable ecosystem growth.

From this perspective, hosting the Cardano Summit in Singapore alongside TOKEN2049 is a strategically sound approach. Singapore serves as a major hub for finance and Web3, and this co-location can help expand Cardano’s visibility and create meaningful touchpoints with institutions, enterprises, and developers.

I also recognize that events of this scale cannot realistically be executed by the community alone, and that it is appropriate for entities such as the Cardano Foundation and EMURGO to take responsibility for organizing them, with treasury support as a reasonable funding mechanism.

However, I remain concerned about the overall scale of the budget. At this time, I do not have sufficient confidence in the return on investment, particularly regarding how event-driven exposure will translate into measurable on-chain activity and sustained ecosystem growth. More clarity is needed on the target segments, types of organizations being pursued, and the expected tangible outcomes such as partnerships and ecosystem impact.

In summary, while I acknowledge the strategic direction and intent of this proposal, I do not believe the current level of detail and cost justification is sufficient to support a definitive vote. For these reasons, I choose to abstain and look forward to a refined proposal with clearer cost optimization and outcome visibility.
