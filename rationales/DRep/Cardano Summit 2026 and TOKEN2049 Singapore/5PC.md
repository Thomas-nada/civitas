**Voter ID:** `drep1yfvql7r7ceeh24w8u5mu3kqvrpjk46scejvha6xpp23dqtqh5f9dn`

While I recognize the strategic value of hosting a Cardano presence in Singapore alongside TOKEN2049, I am voting NO on this proposal for the following reasons:
1. KPIs are decorative, not binding
The proposal lists ambitious targets — 1B digital reach, 700 earned media pieces, 1,200 attendees — yet Milestone 2 only requires "proof of delivery of the event." This means the full budget can be disbursed regardless of whether any KPI is met. A 14M ADA withdrawal should have enforceable performance conditions, not aspirational metrics.
2. Cost justification is insufficient
At USD 2,500,000 for a 1,200-person, two-day event, the per-attendee cost (~USD 2,083) is high. The largest budget line — Venues, On-site Management & Logistics at 47% (~USD 1.32M) — is presented without vendor quotes or contractual commitments. The community is being asked to approve significant spend based on estimates alone.
3. Bypassing Intersect sets a damaging precedent
Timing pressure is cited as justification for circumventing the standard budget process. This is not the first time this reasoning has been used. If the community routinely accepts such exceptions, the Intersect governance process loses its purpose as a meaningful oversight mechanism.
4. ADA price risk and refund commitment lacks enforcement
The proposal commits to returning any profit from ADA price appreciation — but this commitment is entirely trust-based. No on-chain mechanism enforces this return obligation within the smart contract framework described.
