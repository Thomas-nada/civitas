I am voting against this proposal. My primary objection is two-fold: fiscal responsibility and the lack of a measurable framework to assess impact. As this proposal is essentially a marketing initiative, I believe it should be evaluated within the context of a dedicated marketing budget rather than as a standalone treasury withdrawal.

To clarify my position, I have recently supported several proposals that presented clear, quantifiable outcomes or addressed critical infrastructure needs. For instance, I strongly support initiatives to increase node diversity, as this is a tangible security requirement. In such cases, I am prepared to vote in favor of credible teams working toward that specific goal until a sufficient threshold is met. These are strategic imperatives that do not require an overarching budget to justify.

Conversely, treating marketing initiatives as ad-hoc, standalone proposals is inefficient. We should have a comprehensive strategy that outlines our total marketing spend and community priorities before approving individual, fragmented requests. While I acknowledge the proposal’s rationale for bypassing the Intersect budget process, I cannot justify a 'Yes' vote based on a time constraint alone. We need a more structured approach to capital allocation for marketing efforts.
