I am voting No (regrettably).

I support investing in growth and ecosystem expansion, and I believe initiatives like this can play a role in increasing visibility. I also recognize that marketing ROI is not deterministic—KPIs here are signals, not guarantees, and I’m not expecting a direct “ADA spent = users onboarded” model.

What I am looking for is clearer evidence that this is intentionally building a pipeline—how exposure translates into sustained engagement—and that this progression will be tracked and reported over time. While the Summit has been running for several years and there is acknowledgment that it has operated somewhat within the existing ecosystem, I have not yet seen enough detail on what has changed structurally to produce different outcomes going forward.

I also think it’s important to distinguish between the two components of this proposal. External visibility at events like TOKEN2049 is highly valuable and aligns with the need to expand beyond our existing ecosystem. The Cardano Summit, while historically important, has largely served the internal community and may warrant reevaluation as a treasury-funded initiative at this scale. Bundling both together makes it difficult to assess their individual impact and necessity.

For a proposal of this size, I’m not yet convinced on that linkage, which is why my No is a regrettable one.
