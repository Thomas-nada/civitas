I am voting No on this proposal. As a DRep, I evaluate all treasury withdrawal governance actions against my published voting framework (see References below), with a focus on the healthy development and long-term sustainability of the ecosystem. Events and marketing activities do not fall within the scope of treasury funding under my framework. They are neither protocol development nor critical infrastructure. This is not a judgment on the quality or ambition of the events themselves, but a structural one: the treasury is not the appropriate funding source for this category of activity. If the Cardano ecosystem's value proposition is genuine, events of this scale should be sustainable through sponsorships, ticket sales, and private capital. Relying on the treasury for recurring event costs bypasses this market validation entirely. The organizers should explore models that allow these events to stand on their own commercial footing, rather than depending on treasury withdrawals as a default funding mechanism. Voting No to protect treasury discipline and reserve funds for protocol essentials.
