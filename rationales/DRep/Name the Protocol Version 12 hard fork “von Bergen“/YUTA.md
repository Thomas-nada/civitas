I'll be voting YES on the "von Bergen" hard-fork naming.

While looking into it, I gathered the other Cardano contributors we've lost recently into one place.

So far: Fabian von Bergen (Zyroxa), Prof. Steven Lupien, "Bone" (@bone_pool), Siu Mui Wong, and Sean Davies.

On voting: I'd rather not turn this into a contest — scoring one late contributor's record against another's to decide who is "worthy" of a name feels wrong to me. So unless a proposal is clearly unreasonable, I'll vote YES on naming actions as a rule, which means I may vote YES on several at the same time.

This list came from searching X and the community, so I've almost certainly missed people. If someone isn't here, let me know and I'll add them.

Rest in peace to all of them.
