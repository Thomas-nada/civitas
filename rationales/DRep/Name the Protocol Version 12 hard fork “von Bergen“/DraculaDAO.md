**Vote:** Abstain

Vote: ABSTAIN

This is a non-binding Info Action proposing that the Protocol Version 12 hard fork be named 'von Bergen', in honour of Fabian von Bergen, a departed member of the Cardano community. It requests no treasury funds, involves no ADA burning, and changes no protocol parameter. Intersect's Hard Fork Working Group has separately stated its intent to name the next hard fork after Alexander Esgen while adding an In Memoriam section to the governance action metadata honouring other recently departed contributors — and has explicitly acknowledged this Info Action, inviting community input on names for that section.

Dracula DAO holds the impulse behind this action in genuine respect. Cardano has, in the naming of the 'van Rossem' hard fork, established a practice of commemorating the people who built and defended this network, and that practice is worth keeping. The community members who sustain a protocol across years of unglamorous work deserve to be remembered in the permanent record of the chain, and an on-chain Info Action is a legitimate way for the community to raise a name that a working group might otherwise overlook.

Dracula DAO abstains because this is not a question our mandate equips us to arbitrate. Our voting philosophy governs the stewardship of treasury funds and the long-term technical direction of the protocol. It offers no principled basis for ranking one departed contributor's memorial against another's, and we will not manufacture one. The Hard Fork Working Group has proposed a resolution that honours both nominees — Esgen in the fork name, von Bergen among the names recorded in memoriam — and that process is already open to the community feedback this action is seeking. A DRep vote weighted by delegated stake is a poor instrument for settling a matter of remembrance; the working group's consultative process is a better one.

Dracula DAO records no objection to the name 'von Bergen' and would raise none were the Hard Fork Working Group to adopt it. We register this abstention as deference to that process, not as indifference to its subject. On the substance of Protocol Version 12 — its ledger changes, its readiness thresholds, and its activation — Dracula DAO will vote on the merits when the hard fork action itself comes before us.
