I'm voting YES to name Protocol Version 12 "von Bergen" as a small but meaningful way to honor Fabian's memory and everything he gave to this community. Naming this hard fork after him ensures his name lives on in the very fabric of Cardano he worked so hard to build. I hope by doing this it can serve as a simple gesture of respect for someone who truly cared about this ecosystem and the people in it. May he be with us forever on chain, and may we meet with him again some time in heaven.
