[English]
We chose to vote "YES" on this governance action "Name the Protocol Version 12 hard fork ‘von Bergen’" (gov_action1fzatpjn3e3r09mjzzfptznef9wxg8q4a5uraq04xvfjyhmfzhzfsqqgfc9h), because we believe it is important to recognize and preserve Fabian von Bergen’s contribution to Cardano’s history. As an active and respected community member since the ecosystem’s early years, this recognition continues Cardano’s tradition of associating important protocol milestones with individuals who have made meaningful contributions to the development and strengthening of its community. As this is an informational and symbolic governance action, no Treasury funds are requested, meaning there is no budgetary impact or financial cost-benefit consideration. Likewise, no additional mechanisms for financial oversight are required, while the public and verifiable nature of Cardano’s on-chain governance provides transparency over both the decision and its approval process.
