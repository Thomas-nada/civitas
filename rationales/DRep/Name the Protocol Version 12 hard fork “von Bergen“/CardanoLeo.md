I support naming the Protocol Version 12 hard fork “von Bergen” without hesitation.
The fact that Cardano chooses to honour long-standing community contributors in this way is one of the ecosystem’s quiet strengths, and something we should continue to uphold. Fabian von Bergen (Zyroxa) was part of the community from the ITN days. He contributed with quiet diligence, humility, and consistency. Thoughtful yet clear in his views, he never softened his words for convenience and never stepped back from difficult conversations, always meeting the community as his genuine self.
These are exactly the qualities a healthy and lasting ecosystem needs. Naming the hard fork after him is more than a personal tribute. It ensures that future participants reading the ledger and the history of Cardano will know that someone of real substance once stood among us.
May the name “von Bergen” remain a lasting reminder that this community remembers those who truly contributed.
