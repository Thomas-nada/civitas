# Governance Action Review Report [EN]

## 1. Introduction

This Info Action proposes naming the Protocol Version 12 hard fork **“von Bergen”** in recognition of Fabian von Bergen, also known as Zyroxa, who passed away in January 2026.

Fabian participated in the Cardano community since the Incentivized Testnet and was active on the Cardano Forum. He operated the Tempus Stake Pool and served as a Cardano Ambassador from the early years of the program. The action highlights his work introducing and guiding people through the Cardano ecosystem, as well as his critical, honest, and independent participation in the community.

Naming the hard fork “von Bergen” is intended to preserve Fabian’s name in Cardano’s history and recognize his lasting contribution to the ecosystem. As an Info Action, it establishes a community signal regarding the proposed name and does not directly initiate the hard fork or modify the protocol.

## 2. Governance Action Analysis

Although I did not knew Fabian personally, messages had already been exchanged with him, and his importance to the Cardano ecosystem is known, especially through his constant work providing support and moderation in the community’s official channels.

It is valuable for his contribution to be recognized through this initiative. Cardano has an important culture of honoring deceased contributors by preserving their names in the history of the protocol and the community.

## 3. Vote

**Vote: YES**

## 4. Conclusion

The initiative provides a way to recognize Fabian von Bergen’s importance to the Cardano ecosystem and preserve his contribution in the history of the protocol and the community. This form of recognition is consistent with Cardano’s culture of honoring contributors who have passed away.
