Bifrost addresses one of Cardano's most significant infrastructure gaps by creating an open, permissionless Bitcoin bridge designed to bring Bitcoin **liquidity** into the Cardano ecosystem. While the proposal is expensive and technically ambitious, it is focused on taking an **existing testnet** to an audited, controlled mainnet deployment rather than funding a speculative concept.
