Since the Pogun proposal (which included a direct return to the Cardano treasury) was rejected, I see no reason to support other initiatives that do not generate revenue for the treasury.
Although this proposal comes from one of the most respected teams in the ecosystem, it follows the usual pattern: it highlights the potential benefits of a large BTC liquidity injection, but fails to clearly explain how it will attract users in the competitive Bitcoin DeFi space. 
Cardano is not the only player in this area. Also many Cardano teams are currently competing for the same opportunities. Without a concrete and differentiated plan to stand out from the competition, I cannot justify supporting this proposal.
