# Cardanians

**Proposal:** Bifrost Unlocking Bitcoin DeFi on Cardano — Road to Mainnet (Phase 1 of 2)
**Vote:** Abstain
**Voter ID:** `drep1yt4lyf0fwlrz8k6j5evd3rxw0sqp67qkh36su98a97q3qsc0h03y4`

---

We see strong strategic value in bringing Bitcoin liquidity to Cardano. A secure BTC rail could improve Cardano DeFi, create new opportunities for SPOs, and make Cardano more attractive for BTC holders.

However, we are not fully comfortable voting YES in the current form.

The main concern is that this is only Phase 1. The proposal requests ₳12.3M before public launch, while the actual public rollout and 24 months of operations are planned for a separate Phase 2 proposal. If Phase 1 succeeds but Phase 2 is not approved, it is not fully clear what practical value the treasury receives.

We also have concerns around bridge risk, custody/security assumptions, final stewardship structure, operational accountability, and long-term maintenance costs. For a Bitcoin bridge, these details need to be exceptionally clear before a large treasury commitment.

We appreciate the testnet progress, security focus, and the intent to return surplus bridge fees to the treasury, but we would like stronger clarity on launch conditions, governance of the bridge, risk responsibility, and the full cost to reach public operation.
