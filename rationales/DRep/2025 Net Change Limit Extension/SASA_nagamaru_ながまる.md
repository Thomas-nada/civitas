This proposal does not alter the existing framework of the Net Change Limit; rather, it introduces an appropriate buffer to prevent a fiscal gap caused by the expiration of the current NCL.
Since the current NCL ends at Epoch 604, the available time for reviewing, approving, and enacting TWGAs and Budget GAs is extremely limited. Without an extension, the decision-making processes of DReps, the Constitutional Committee, and the Budget Committee would be unnecessarily compressed, potentially impacting the broader community’s governance operations.

The additional eight epochs do not relax the established upper limit. Instead, they provide the healthy operational margin needed for proper review and enactment. This buffer benefits not only governance bodies but also the community as a whole, aligning with the constitutional principles of fiscal soundness, transparency, and predictability.

For these reasons, I consider this proposal reasonable and will support it.
