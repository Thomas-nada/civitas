YES vote  on the 2025 NCL Extension. Rationale:  To protect continuity in the governance system and prevent a constitutional committee "no" vote.

This action doesn’t change the Net Change Limit,  raise the cap, or replace the existing NCL.
It just extends the time window by 8 epochs so that Treasury Withdrawal Actions can still be lawfully enacted under Article IV.

Without this extension, the current NCL expires before key actions, including the Critical Infrastructure action, can be enacted. Even if DReps overwhelmingly support those actions, they would almost certainly be deemed unconstitutional at enactment because the NCL window would have closed therefore i deem this action to be necessary.
