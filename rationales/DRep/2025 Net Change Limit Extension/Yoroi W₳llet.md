Rationale

Critical Integrations Budget timing

The Net Change Limit (NCL) sets a withdrawal limit for the Cardano treasury over a defined period, and the current NCL expires at the conclusion of Epoch 604. Extending it by eight epochs will help keep the current limit active long enough for the Critical Integrations Budget to be enacted within the budget that the community has approved, rather than being pushed into the next NCL period.

Maintaining the treasury safeguard

The extension does not raise or reset the ₳350M limit. It only extends the timeframe, with prior spending still counted inside the same ceiling. This keeps treasury discipline exactly as the community approved it.
