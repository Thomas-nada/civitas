I am in favor of what this proposal is trying to accomplish, and I consider tool maintenance and DevEx to be critical needs.  However, this proposal feels too heavy for something that still needs to be built largely from the ground up.  There are also several related proposals competing under the same NCL, including the Paid Open Source Model, Cardano Tooling DAO, and the already approved IO Developer Experience Initiative. I would much rather see these teams work together on a unified plan that reduces duplication of effort to solve the same problems.  I support the problem this proposal is trying to solve, but I think it is too much all at once. For these reasons, I am voting NO.
