I am voting No on this proposal. Open source software sustainability is a genuine challenge for the Cardano ecosystem. The concentration of critical maintenance in a small number of contributors is a real risk, and a structural response is warranted. However, the projects to be funded are undefined at proposal time, the selection criteria for the dOSPO remain unclear, and there is no direct accountability linkage between the treasury withdrawal and measurable outcomes. DReps would be delegating their oversight role to a newly created intermediary body without sufficient transparency into how decisions will be made. If specific open source software is genuinely critical to the Cardano protocol, it should be proposed directly to the Treasury with clearly scoped deliverables, milestones, and accountability mechanisms — the same standard applied to any other proposal. The establishment of such an intermediary body carries the responsibility to present clear intended uses and fund management capabilities. This proposal does not meet that standard. I hope to see individual projects brought forward in a form that allows DReps to evaluate each on its own merits.
