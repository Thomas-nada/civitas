I am voting Abstain.

I think this proposal identifies a very real problem around open source sustainability, maintainer burnout, dependency risk, and long-term ecosystem resilience. The overall structure is thoughtful, governance-aware, and significantly more mature than many proposals in this cycle.

My hesitation is less about the intent and more about prioritization and ecosystem timing. This introduces a fairly large operational and governance framework around OSS sustainment, and I’m not fully convinced Cardano is yet at the stage where this level of institutionalization should be a top treasury priority relative to current adoption and growth constraints.

That said, I do think the underlying problem being addressed here is legitimate and increasingly important long term.
