I am abstaining on "Tweag Core Cardano Infrastructure: Treasury Withdrawal 2026–2028". Peras v1/v2 mainnet delivery is core Tier 2a infrastructure: reducing settlement finality from approximately 12 minutes to ~2 minutes is a meaningful and user-visible protocol improvement. Tweag has a credible track record in formal verification and core Cardano work, making them a qualified deliverer. The bundling of 17 work packages across 9 areas is a structural concern — the governance community cannot selectively approve individual workstreams — but the proposer's interdependency argument is plausible given the integrated nature of the deliverables. ₳39.8M over 2 years is a large ask, but the roadmap is clear and the timeline is structured. Abstaining reflects the bundling risk and current market conditions rather than a judgment against the technical content or Tweag's capability.
