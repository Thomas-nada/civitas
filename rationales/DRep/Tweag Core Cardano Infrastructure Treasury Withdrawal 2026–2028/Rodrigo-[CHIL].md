That said, I am concerned about approving a two-year budget in a single withdrawal, especially given the current uncertainty in the broader crypto market.

I would prefer to see this negotiated into a staged proposal: one first phase to address the most urgent work now, with clear deliverables and public accountability, and a second phase next year based on progress, results, and the treasury context at that time.

For now, I am not fully comfortable supporting the proposal in its current structure, but I would be open to supporting a more phased and flexible version.
