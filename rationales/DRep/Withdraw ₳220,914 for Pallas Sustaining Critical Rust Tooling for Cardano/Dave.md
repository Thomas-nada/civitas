I am voting a strong **YES**. Funding foundational, open-source libraries like Pallas is one of the most effective uses of our treasury.

Pallas is the bedrock for our Rust developer ecosystem, with critical projects like Aiken, Lucid, and Mithril depending on it. Its maintenance is a direct investment in the **decentralization** of our tooling and the **sustainability** of many other projects. Supporting a trusted team like TxPipe to maintain this critical public good is a straightforward, high-impact decision.
