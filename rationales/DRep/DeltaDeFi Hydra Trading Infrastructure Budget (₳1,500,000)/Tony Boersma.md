I am voting NO on the DeltaDeFi: Hydra Trading Infrastructure Budget because projects funded by the Treasury should demonstrate clear, durable, ecosystem-wide benefits. While the proposal is ambitious and technically interesting, the benefits appear to accrue primarily to a specific team and product rather than to the broader Cardano ecosystem in a neutral, public-goods sense. The proposal does not yet make a sufficiently strong case that the resulting infrastructure would be widely reusable, permissionless, and adopted beyond this single implementation. At this funding level, I expect clearer guarantees of open access, shared utility, and long-term ecosystem value before committing Treasury resources.
