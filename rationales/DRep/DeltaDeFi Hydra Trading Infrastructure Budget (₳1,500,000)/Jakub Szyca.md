I vote NO on this info action. While DeltaDeFi is a welcome innovation in the crypto ecosystem and could showcase Hydra to more people, it doesn't justify this amount of ADA to be withdrawn yet. The project is in its early stages and frankly doesn't feel like a next-gen product at this moment. In my view projects that intend to feel like a CEX and enable fast trading have a hard time succeding as there isn't enough demand for that kind of product. Especially on Cardano which just doesn't have enough trading volume yet. One of the prime examples is the downfall of AXO which also promised exciting features but ultimately failed due to lack of adoption. DeltaDeFi has potential, but this should be done with help from Catalyst which was convieved to help projects exactly like this. It's a much more suitable place for that proposal. Additionally this kind of action shouldn't be decided on just before the proposed 2026 budget is formed.
