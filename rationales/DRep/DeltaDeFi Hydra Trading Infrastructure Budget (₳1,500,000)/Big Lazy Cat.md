# Big Lazy Cat

**Proposal:** DeltaDeFi Hydra Trading Infrastructure Budget (₳1,500,000)
**Vote:** Abstain
**Voter ID:** `drep1yffcjs0njfea09xhs6up429rhya5pxd7qz7hvmwq6vqy2nczwpj3g`

---

The project direction has merit, and the DeltaDeFi team has demonstrated strong execution through long-standing contributions to developer tooling and infrastructure in the Cardano ecosystem. This deserves recognition.
However, at this stage, order-book trading is not among Cardano’s most urgent priorities, and Hydra has yet to show broad, mature application-level adoption. Advancing complex Hydra-based applications too early risks limited liquidity and usage.
I would prefer to reconsider such initiatives once Hydra itself is more mature and supports a wider range of real-world applications. For this reason, I abstain on this Info Action.
