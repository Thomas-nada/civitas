While the DeltaDeFi team is competent and Hydra development is valuable for Cardano, the treasury should not subsidize operational costs or scaling for a single commercial DEX at this scale, especially amid ongoing treasury depletion, low inflows, and ADA price pressures. Direct funding risks picking winners in a competitive market (e.g., vs. Minswap) and lacks clear strategies for attracting liquidity/users/market makers, which are outside the team's control. Projects like this should pursue Project Catalyst, private loans or even treasury loans (as seen with SNEK). Reserve treasury resources for neutral public goods, protocol-level infrastructure, and critical ecosystem needs. Let's prioritize fiscal discipline now to protect long-term sustainability.
