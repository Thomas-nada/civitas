I am voting in favor of the Cardano High Assurance Technical Collaboration. This proposal ensures that the network continues to double down on the security guarantees and I believe this is the right focus for our community to maintain.
By funding this collaboration, we are protecting one of Cardano's most valuable asset—its reputation for being the top prospect of high-assurance engineering.
Also I believe that as the ecosystem expands into complex DeFi, identity systems, and enterprise solutions, the cost of a single major exploit would far outweigh the cost of this proposal. 
Security is an ongoing process and we should keep funding it and keep the experts in the community working on it. This proposal is a strategic investment in one of the main features that makes Cardano unique.
