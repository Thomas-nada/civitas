Socious votes Yes. This 1,162,746 ADA withdrawal funds MLabs' maintenance and enhancement of Plutarch and Ply. Plutarch is a typed eDSL for writing highly optimized Plutus scripts — many production Cardano protocols compile their on-chain code through it because hand-tuned script efficiency translates directly into lower fees and higher throughput for users — and Ply handles the safe serving and reuse of those compiled scripts across off-chain code. Tooling that production dApps already depend on is precisely where unfunded maintenance does the most silent damage at each hard fork. The proposal was vetted and approved through the Intersect community budget process and is disbursed under its milestone-based administration; consistent with Socious's policy of supporting budget-process withdrawals, we vote Yes.
