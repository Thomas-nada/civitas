The Cardano Foundation recognizes Rare Network’s experience and  multi-year record of executing events. Rare Evo 2025 reported 2,500 attendees and 40+ Cardano projects, and the inaugural Cardano Dev Gov Day drew roughly 500 participants, providing publicly accessible governance and developer onboarding. Our YES vote reflects the following:

* **A Public-Good Core We Value:** Dev Gov Day's free governance and developer programming, livestream, complimentary access, and contributor travel support align directly with the Accessibility, Education, and Governance principles set out in "Our Cardano." A dedicated Cardano-focused day is a venue we can use to amplify ecosystem coordination and the latest developments on Cardano, and to host governance-improvement workshops.  
* **Strategic Visibility at a Reasonable Price:** A title sponsorship of an established, multi-chain conference with strong Cardano roots positions the ecosystem prominently during a market contraction. Building on existing venue relationships, production capability, and sponsor networks reduces execution risk relative to a new initiative, and 2,750,000 ada for two events of this value to the community is, in our view, a defensible cost.  
* **Concerns We Hold But Do Not Block On:** Our YES is not an endorsement of every line item. The budget is pegged at a $0.24 ada valuation that may not reflect market conditions at execution; a meaningful share of the request covers overhead, a kickoff party, withheld taxes, and a deposit for an as-yet-unapproved 2027 event rather than 2026 Cardano programming; and the complimentary booth benefit may overlap with Catalyst-funded provisions. We would have preferred these tightened before submission.  
* **Why We Vote YES Regardless:** With the event scheduled for July 28–31, 2026, there is no longer a realistic window to revise and resubmit. We judge that the public-good value of Dev Gov Day and a Cardano title sponsorship, with the marketing exposure it brings, outweighs these shortcomings.
