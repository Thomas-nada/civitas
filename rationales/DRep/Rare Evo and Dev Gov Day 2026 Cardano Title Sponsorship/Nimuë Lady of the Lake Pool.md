I attended both Rare Evo and the Dev&Gov Day in 2025 and greatly enjoyed both events, so this was not an easy vote for me. After careful consideration, however, I have decided to vote NO.

If this proposal were solely for Dev&Gov Day, I would approve it without hesitation. I consider it a highly valuable event that directly benefits the Cardano ecosystem and is well worth funding.

As for Rare Evo, it positions itself as a multi-chain event, so I do not believe it should be funded as a Cardano event. At the same time, as a multi-chain event, I do not believe it is of sufficient scale to justify a standalone treasury proposal, unlike events such as TOKEN2049. Last year, we funded a Unified Events proposal, and I believe it would make more sense for Rare Evo to be considered alongside other events within such a proposal, with attendance decisions made according to the available budget and expected value.
I am also concerned that treasury funding is being used to compensate for the loss of sponsors that previously supported the event. While I understand the challenge, I do not believe the treasury should be expected to fill that gap.

Regarding the requested deposit for Dev&Gov Day 2027, I am not convinced it is necessary to commit funds more than a year in advance. The event attracts only a few hundred attendees, which does not seem to warrant such an early commitment, especially before the next edition has even taken place.
We already had a Dev&Gov Day in Las Vegas in 2025 and may have another one there in 2026. We also had a similar event, Day 0, before the Cardano Summit in Berlin in 2025. In the future, we may want to hold such an event in a different part of the world, giving other members of the community a better opportunity to attend.
