Vote: NO

This proposal requests treasury funds for Cardano to serve as title sponsor of Rare Evo 2026, a blockchain and AI conference held in Las Vegas, alongside a co-located Dev Gov Day event. The proposal is a direct marketing and event spend with no infrastructure deliverable, no treasury repayment, and no on-chain accountability mechanism tied to outcomes.

Vampyre Fund votes NO.

The Vampyre Fund DREP does not support treasury funds being used for short-term marketing efforts. A conference title sponsorship is precisely that — a brand spend whose value is measured in impressions and attendee counts over a single event cycle. It does not build infrastructure, does not return value to the treasury, and does not compound over time. The 6.89% DRep support this proposal has received as of June 2026 reflects broad community alignment with this assessment.

The inclusion of a Dev Gov Day component does not change this judgment. Governance education and developer outreach are worthwhile goals, but they do not require treasury-funded conference sponsorship to achieve. Community-led governance events have historically operated without top-tier sponsorship budgets. Bundling a developer day with a Las Vegas conference sponsorship does not transform the proposal into infrastructure spending.

Vampyre Fund's position is consistent: treasury funds should be reserved for long-term foundational work. Event sponsorships, regardless of the event's quality or the sincerity of the organizers, are not the appropriate use of ADA held in trust for the long-term benefit of the Cardano ecosystem.
