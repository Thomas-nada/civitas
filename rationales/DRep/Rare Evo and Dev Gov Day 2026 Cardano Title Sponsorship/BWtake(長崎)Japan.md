I conditionally support this proposal. Rare Evo is an independently operated multi-chain event, but it has long been closely connected to the Cardano community. I see it as an important ecosystem touchpoint that connects DReps, SPOs, developers, projects, enterprises, and media, and therefore has public-good value for Cardano.

This year is a critical moment for Cardano to turn development, governance, and external visibility into real growth. Cardano is a chain that must create and communicate its own narrative, connecting its technology and philosophy to the wider industry. In that sense, Rare Evo should not be treated as just another event, but as an international gateway to present Cardano’s next stage.

At the same time, the impact of Treasury support must go beyond internal community enthusiasm. It should be reported transparently and quantitatively through external reach, developer onboarding, enterprise and institutional connections, media exposure, transparent selection of supported participants, and the actual amount of the 20% of VIP ticket sales returned to the Treasury.

On these conditions, I vote YES.
