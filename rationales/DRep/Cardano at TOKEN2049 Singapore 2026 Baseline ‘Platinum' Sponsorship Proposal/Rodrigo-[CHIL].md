I vote **YES** on this proposal.

I do think the cost is high, and in the long term these kinds of major ecosystem events should move toward a more self-sustaining model, with stronger participation from sponsors, partners, and ecosystem entities rather than depending primarily on the treasury.

That said, after attending Consensus in Miami, I have a much clearer appreciation for the value of physical presence at major industry events. The visibility, networking, and access to institutional actors can be significant when the execution is professional and well coordinated.

For that reason, I see value in Cardano having a strong presence at TOKEN2049 Singapore, especially if the focus remains on showcasing real builders, live products, and concrete ecosystem opportunities.

My support comes with the expectation that EMURGO and the administrators provide clear post-event reporting, measurable outcomes, and a serious path toward making future activations more financially sustainable.
