Securing a sponsorship at TOKEN2049 is an important opportunity to promote Cardano’s visibility, extend its reach and provide a critical platform for ecosystem builders.  As with the Summit, this proposal represents a counter-cyclical investment which we believe will support Cardano’s momentum. This proposal has been evaluated together with the complementary proposal for the 'Title' sponsorship at TOKEN2049: 

**1\. The Baseline 'Platinum' Sponsorship:** Establishing a footprint at the largest industry event of 2026 ensures Cardano remains visible and highly competitive in the Web3 market. Covering 30 conference tickets and providing exhibition space for community projects empowers the teams building the network to represent Cardano directly to institutional audiences.

**2\. The Top-Up 'Title' Upgrade:** We maintain our strong conviction in the Title sponsorship’s strategic value. Upgrading to the Title tier unlocks a Mainstage Keynote and guaranteed premium media reach, giving Cardano access to a completely different level of exposure. We believe that going "all in" on this event is a net-positive that maximizes the treasury's return on investment.

**Budget and Execution:** Although the unbundled cost includes new administrative fees for Intersect, this decentralized setup supports transparency and accountability. We find this slightly higher overall cost acceptable, especially given the proportional reduction in other management fees.
