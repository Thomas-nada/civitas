I know this proposal has already passed, but I wanted to still formally register my NO vote and share my rationale for transparency.

Singapore is a great location, and I credit EMURGO for a zero profit, Platinum tier pass through structure that directly responds to community feedback. The proposal easily demonstrates clear benefits to Cardano by providing free tickets, dedicated stage time, and critical networking access directly to ecosystem builders.

However, a 3.3 million ADA ask paired with an 85% upfront drawdown conflicts too deeply with my core fiscal principles. This is an exceptionally high amount for an event where, historically, I am unable to easily tie the spend back to clear value on Cardano in the form of KPIs. Ideally, I would prefer we focus on treasury replenishment initiatives, growing our treasury and on chain usage, before we have the luxury of spending out at events to join other blockchains that do have utility generating further on chain usage. Even though the community heavily favors this presence, as a DRep I must prioritize strict treasury discipline, and I cannot in good conscience support these specific financial parameters.

As this has since passed, I would welcome EMURGO to demonstrate this proposal's value, and I look forward to seeing this during the event, especially across social media, which will in turn assist my position for future funding.
