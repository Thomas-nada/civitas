We are being asked to approve a base cost of nearly $800,000 USD (₳3.3 Million) for a two-day event.

1. Unsustainable Budgeting: Allocating $250,000 USD purely for "Booth Build and Logistics" (on top of the $400,000 sponsorship fee) is astronomically high, even for Singapore standards. Furthermore, charging the Treasury a $97,000 (15%) "Management Fee" to coordinate an event that fundamentally serves EMURGO’s own
strategic interests as a founding entity is an inappropriate extraction of community capital.

The Cardano Treasury is an investment vehicle, not a corporate marketing slush fund. I urge EMURGO to renegotiate with their vendors, eliminate the management fee markup, and submit a leaner, more capital-efficient proposal. Until the pricing reflects open-market realities, my vote is NO.
