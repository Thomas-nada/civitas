I appreciate that this proposal is more compelling than a Cardano-only event because TOKEN2049 gives Cardano visibility in front of the broader crypto industry.  However, I still do not believe this is the best use of treasury funds at this point in time. While increased visibility is valuable, I feel Cardano needs to focus more heavily on its unique selling points and finding product market fit first.

In my view, marketing and event sponsorships are most valuable when they amplify strong momentum that already exists. Right now, I think we still need to spend more time building the infrastructure and business applications that give Cardano a clearer commercial story to tell when we show up at major industry events.

For that reason I am voting NO on this proposal.
