I evaluated the three proposals — Cardano Summit 2026, TOKEN2049 Platinum Sponsorship, and the TOKEN2049 Title Upgrade — as part of one broader strategic direction.

First, I strongly support Cardano having a presence at TOKEN2049. At this stage, I believe Cardano’s biggest challenge is no longer purely technology or research, but external adoption, business development, liquidity, institutional relationships, and attracting new builders into the ecosystem. TOKEN2049 represents one of the few global venues where over 25,000 participants — including investors, enterprises, exchanges, developers, and media — are already concentrated in one place. This makes it an extremely valuable opportunity for Cardano to expand beyond its existing ecosystem.

In particular, the Platinum Sponsorship proposal appears relatively practical and cost-conscious while still preserving the core strategic value. The builder stage, ecosystem booth presence, networking access, and builder ticket allocation provide direct opportunities for ecosystem exposure, relationship building, and potential onboarding. Given the current market conditions and the broader treasury environment, I believe this proposal has a reasonable chance of generating measurable external impact.

However, I am more cautious regarding the Title Upgrade proposal. While the keynote slot, additional branding exposure, and media positioning certainly have value, I do not believe Cardano’s highest priority right now is maximizing prestige or brand visibility alone. The ecosystem’s larger issue is converting attention into real adoption, partnerships, liquidity, and users. Because of this, I am not fully convinced that the additional cost of the upgrade delivers proportional ROI at the current stage.

Regarding Cardano Summit 2026, I understand and respect the overall vision behind the event. Community building, governance discussions, education, and ecosystem identity are all important for Cardano’s long-term health. However, I also believe there is a real risk of becoming too internally focused. Cardano already has strong technology, strong research, and a strong governance culture, but it still struggles with market penetration and external growth. In the current environment, I believe the higher priority should be attracting external participants rather than primarily reinforcing internal branding.

Overall, my position is:

TOKEN2049 Platinum Sponsorship → Support
TOKEN2049 Title Upgrade → Cautious / leaning unnecessary at this stage
Cardano Summit 2026 → Worth reconsidering if there is sufficient remaining NCL capacity. However, a further reduction in scope and budget would make the proposal significantly easier to support

For Cardano to reach the next phase, it must evolve from being simply a technically strong blockchain into an ecosystem that naturally attracts capital, enterprises, developers, and users. In my view, external connection and market expansion should be prioritized right now.
