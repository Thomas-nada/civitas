Socious votes Yes. This proposal funds Cardano's baseline presence at TOKEN2049 Singapore — 3,303,750 ADA (about $792,900) for the second-largest booth, a dedicated Cardano stage, conference access, and post-event coverage, framed around the ecosystem's builders. TOKEN2049 is one of the industry's highest-traffic venues, and a builder-centric presence there is a reasonable use of marketing budget.

As with the Summit proposal, the process matters. After community concern about a larger combined budget, EMURGO restructured this as an independent request and stepped the sponsorship down from "Title" to "Platinum", reducing the ask while keeping the core presence intact. EMURGO is headquartered in Singapore and has been a TOKEN2049 partner for several years, so execution risk is low.

Socious supports this baseline proposal. Its view on the separate "Title" upgrade is set out in that proposal's own rationale and is deliberately different — support for the baseline does not extend automatically to the premium tier.
