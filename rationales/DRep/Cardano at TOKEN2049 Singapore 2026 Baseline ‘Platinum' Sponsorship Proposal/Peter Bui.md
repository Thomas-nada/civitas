I am voting YES on the Token2049 base proposal.

This is still a significant treasury ask, so I do not take the vote lightly. In the current market, every ADA spent needs a clear reason, and event funding should be held to a high standard.

For me, the base Token2049 proposal is justified because it puts Cardano in front of the right audience at the right time.

Token2049 Singapore is one of the most important crypto and fintech events in the region. It attracts investors, exchanges, infrastructure providers, founders, institutions, funds, enterprise teams and decision makers who are actively looking at where the industry is going next.

Cardano needs to be visible in those rooms.

The ecosystem has spent years building core infrastructure. We are seeing momentum around stablecoins, Bitcoin DeFi, partner chains, real-world assets, governance, developer tooling and deeper integrations. If we want that work to turn into adoption, partnerships and liquidity, Cardano needs to show up where those conversations are already happening.

I also support the more practical approach of taking a strong but not excessive presence. The base proposal gives Cardano exposure, builder representation and a credible presence at the event without stretching the treasury ask further than necessary.

The success of this proposal should not only be judged by short-term on-chain metrics. A major industry event should be measured through qualified leads, builder participation, meetings, media reach, institutional conversations, follow-up partnerships and transparent post-event reporting.

My support is based on the expectation that the teams involved will publish clear outcomes after the event and show what was achieved with treasury funds.

For those reasons, I am voting YES.
