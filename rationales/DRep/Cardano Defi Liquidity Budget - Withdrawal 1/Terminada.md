Cayman entities, multisig contracts and audits add complexity, cost, and for many, contradict decentralisation goals. Cardano DeFi should emerge from real-world use cases and grow naturally rather than through treasury funded experiments. My view at this stage is that it would be better to fund builders to create real-world use cases and ecosystem infrastructure that makes liquidity valuable in the first place.
