The proposal funds the continued development of Tx3, an open-source framework providing standardized interfaces to Cardano protocols for both developers and AI agents. By reducing integration complexity and promoting interoperability across decentralized applications, it presents a reasonable case for advancing the long-term sustainability of Cardano under Article I - Section 1.

The proposal identifies an administrator, discloses previous Treasury and Catalyst funding, specifies audit and oversight arrangements, defines custody through dedicated treasury management smart contracts, provides transparent on-chain administration, and states (effective) compliance with the applicable Net Change Limit.

The proposal contains several forward-looking statements regarding AI adoption and Cardano's positioning within the broader blockchain ecosystem. While these are necessarily aspirational, they are presented as strategic objectives rather than established facts and therefore do not materially affect the constitutional assessment of the proposal.

Hence, I do not identify a clear constitutional violation and consider the proposal constitutionally compliant.
