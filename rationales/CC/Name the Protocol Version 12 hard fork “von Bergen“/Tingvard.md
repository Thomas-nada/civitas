# Tingvard

**Proposal:** Name the Protocol Version 12 hard fork “von Bergen“
**Vote:** Abstain
**Voter ID:** `646d1b3ac94568a422b687db6c47acdf849f1674982ae4f9a494be43`

---

This Info Action proposes naming the Protocol Version 12 hard fork “von Bergen” in recognition of Fabian von Bergen, also known as Zyroxa.

Fabian was a long-standing Cardano community member, SPO of Tempus Stake Pool, Cardano Ambassador, and a known voice on the Cardano Forum. The action seeks to honour his contribution to the ecosystem and preserve his name in Cardano’s history.

Tingvard does not judge this action constitutional or unconstitutional because it is an Info Action with no direct on-chain effect. It does not change protocol parameters, amend the Constitution, initiate a hard fork, withdraw treasury funds, or otherwise enact a binding ledger change.

The Constitutional Committee’s role is limited to assessing the constitutionality of governance actions with direct constitutional or ledger effect. This action expresses a community preference and symbolic naming proposal. It is therefore not appropriate for Tingvard to treat it as a constitutional question.

However, Tingvard supports the intention of the action.

Recognising people who have contributed meaningfully to Cardano is part of preserving the history and culture of the ecosystem. Fabian’s long-standing presence, honesty, and contribution to the Cardano community are worthy of respect.
