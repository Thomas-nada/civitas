"Name the Protocol Version 12 hard fork “von Bergen“" (gov_action1fzatpjn3e3r09mjzzfptznef9wxg8q4a5uraq04xvfjyhmfzhzfsqqgfc9h) is an Info Governance Action proposing that the Protocol Version 12 hard fork be named in salute and recognition of Fabian von Bergen, known to the community as Zyroxa, who passed away in January 2026. According to Article III, Section 1(4) of the Cardano Constitution, Constitutional Committee members are responsible for determining the constitutionality of "any proposed or contemplated actions contained within 'Info' actions." We state at the outset what this rationale is and is not: it is a finding about the constitutional format of this governance action, and it is not a judgment on the commemoration itself, which we address separately below.

Under Article II.6.1, all governance actions "shall follow a standardized and legible format before being recorded or enacted on-chain", and the required format includes a URL hosting a context document together with the hash of that document, where "The document hosted by such a URL shall be immutable and incapable of being altered after submission". This Action anchors its metadata at a plain HTTPS URL on a privately operated web server. We investigated before reaching a conclusion. As of our review, the document at that URL is live and its blake2b-256 hash exactly matches the hash recorded on-chain, so no alteration has occurred. The constitutional distinction, however, is not who operates the hosting. A URL that embeds the hash of the document it names, such as a content addressed IPFS URI or a commit hash URL, is self verifying and immutable in the constitutional sense: the identifier itself commits to the content, so what the URL names can never change, it can at most become unavailable. A location addressed URL names a place rather than a content commitment. Nothing in this Action's URL binds it to this document; whatever is placed at that location tomorrow answers to the same URL, and the document it hosts is therefore capable of being altered after submission. The constitutional requirement is not that alteration be detectable; it is that the document "shall be immutable and incapable of being altered after submission". This Action does not meet the format that Article II.6.1 requires.

This requirement is a deliberate feature of the Constitution now in force. The prior Constitution asked only that governance actions follow a standardized format "including a URL and hash" of the off-chain content; it imposed no immutability requirement on the hosted document, and under that text this Action would not have presented a constitutional problem. The Constitution ratified by the community and effective at epoch 609 added the immutability mandate expressly. Where the constitutional text has been strengthened by the community, we are bound to apply it as written. Content addressed hosting that satisfies the requirement is freely and widely available, and the on-chain governance ecosystem, including this Committee's own publications, uses it as a matter of course.

On the merits, which we do not reach as a matter of constitutional judgment, we wish to be plain: the contemplated naming aligns with Tenet 4, which emphasizes that community contributions should be recognized and recorded, and it fits squarely within the community tradition of naming hard forks after dedicated community members who have passed away. Fabian von Bergen was a community member since the Incentivized Testnet, the operator of the Tempus stake pool, a Cardano Ambassador from the earliest days of the program, and a critical, honest, and generous voice who introduced and guided many through the Cardano ecosystem. Nothing in this rationale should be read as diminishing that legacy or the community sentiment behind this Action.

A final observation completes the picture. The naming of hard forks rests with the Hard Fork Working Group, which has made the name subject to community governance. An Info Action has no direct on-chain effect, and the votes cast on this Action remain a register of community sentiment, imperfect instrument though it is. Nothing in this finding prevents the commemorative name from being adopted: if the Hard Fork Working Group judges this Action a valid show of community sentiment, the hard fork may yet carry Fabian's name, and that judgment lies with the Working Group.
