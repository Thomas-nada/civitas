Article II, Section 6(1) of the Cardano Constitution states "To ensure transparency in on-chain governance, proposed governance actions shall follow a standardized and legible format before being recorded or enacted on-chain. This format shall include a URL hosting a document that outlines additional context for the proposed governance action, and the hash of this document.
