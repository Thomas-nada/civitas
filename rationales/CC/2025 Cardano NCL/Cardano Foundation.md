This Info Governance Action (gov_action10k0unljvaej0kd89w7pn0zkgdx59c7xkl0x5q78dzvdtd73u0kmqq5xl5y5) proposes an NCL for the Cardano Treasury of 200 million ada, effective from the start of Epoch 532 to the end of Epoch 604. Its purpose is to seek DRep consensus on this limit, a prerequisite for subsequent budget and treasury withdrawal actions under the Constitution. The proposal defines the NCL as "the maximum amount of ada which may be withdrawn from the Cardano Treasury in a given period." While the Constitution uses the term "net change limit" (Appendix I, Section 3, TREASURY-01a; Art IV(3)) without prescribing a precise definition, this proposal's definition as a withdrawal cap is a clear interpretation upon which DReps can vote and is not, in itself, unconstitutional. 

## Constitutional Alignment:
This proposal demonstrates alignment with the constitutional framework:

- Article I, Section 1 (Tenets 8): The rationale for a more cautious NCL (200M ada) to allow governance processes to mature and to grow the Treasury supports responsible resource use and financial stability.
Article III, Section 5: The proposal meets the procedural standards contained in this section and additionally, provides comprehensive metadata linked via an immutable IPFS address (ipfs://QmRrGsZsP5R7cFYFzPXQ9vS2Ux7oTXb1ep1EciLdKqeDWY) with a verified hash, representing best practice in governance transparency and verifiability.
- Article IV, Section 3 and Appendix I, Guardrail TREASURY-01a: The action seeks consensus on a new NCL as envisaged by the provisions of the Constitution.
- Article VII, Section 1 and 4: The proposal appropriately uses the Info Action mechanism. This section of the Constitution specifies that Info Actions have no on-chain effect and are technically neither constitutional nor unconstitutional; thus, the CC cannot prevent their recording. 

Crucially, it allows CC members to express their view on whether the action's substance would be constitutional if enacted. Further, Article VII, Section 1, states that the CC must assess the Constitutionality of all governance actions, including all Info Actions, and the proposal contained therein.
