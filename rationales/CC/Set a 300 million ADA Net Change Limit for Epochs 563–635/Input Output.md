Furthermore, the proposal notes that the 300M ada NCL is in line with expected Treasury inflows during the specified period, which demonstrates consideration for Tenet 8: "The Cardano Blockchain shall not unreasonably spend resources".

The IOG ICC seat encourages robust community discussion and DRep scrutiny regarding the specific amount and duration of any NCL proposal.
