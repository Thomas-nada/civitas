I believe that the outlined process is a violation of the entire Article II - Section 1. 

1. Although not being explicitly said (but supported by the given example scenario, and the multiple references to the NCL as a target), the document proposes an Intersect-facilitated budget process as the *only* process permitting access to the Cardano treasury. Intersect being a member-based organisation, this contradicts the first paragraph which states that "no formal membership shall be required to use, participate in and benefit from the Cardano Blockchain."

2. Similarly, the suggested process promotes Intersect as the one central authority in matters of budget, thus discouraging any other effort to partake in the governance process. This contradicts the second paragraph which states that "Cardano Community members are encouraged to collaborate [..] to form organizations that support the Cardano Blockchain and the Cardano Community". 

Beneath it all, the budget is framed as a "Cardano" budget framework, whereas it is in fact an "Intersect" budget framework.
