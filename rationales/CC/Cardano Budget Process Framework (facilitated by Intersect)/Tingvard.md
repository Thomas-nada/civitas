This governance action is correctly submitted as an “Info” action under Article II, §6 and Article III, §1–§4 of the Cardano Constitution. Info actions create an on-chain record and provide information to the governance system but do not themselves enact changes to the protocol, parameters, treasury, or governance structure. The proposal therefore falls within the permissible scope of informational governance actions.This framework contains proposed actions that can affect future treasury withdrawals and therefore falls under Article III, §1, 4
The action satisfies the procedural requirements of Article II, §6. It provides a title, abstract, motivation, rationale, and supporting materials referenced through immutable hashes and URLs. These elements ensure that the off-chain documentation referenced by the governance action is verifiable and cannot be altered after submission, satisfying the constitutional requirement that governance actions provide sufficient context and supporting materials.
The proposal records a structured framework for the Cardano ecosystem budget process. The document outlines a multi-stage process covering strategic preparation, proposal submission, review cycles, prioritization, consolidation into treasury withdrawal actions, and subsequent execution and monitoring. As an Info action, this framework does not itself authorize treasury withdrawals and therefore does not trigger the requirements governing “Treasury Withdrawals” actions in Article II, §7. Instead, it records the procedural approach that may guide future budget cycles and the preparation of subsequent treasury withdrawal proposals.
The framework also recognizes the constitutional guardrails governing treasury withdrawals, including the requirement that withdrawals must not exceed the Net Change Limit (NCL) and must follow the governance processes defined by the Constitution. By explicitly acknowledging the need for an approved NCL and by stating that treasury withdrawal governance actions must follow the applicable constitutional processes, the framework is consistent with the treasury guardrails defined in Appendix I.
