# Cardano Japan Council

**Proposal:** Approve Cardano Foundation as New Managing Entity of Project Catalyst
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `725d4d44499865071536d54674b080322bd366be15338db221807b31`

This proposal is an Info Action seeking community approval to transfer the operational management of Project Catalyst from Input Output Global (IOG) to the Cardano Foundation. The body of this proposal meets the formal requirements for a Governance Action as stipulated in Article 2, Section 6, Paragraphs 1 and 2 of the Cardano Constitution and is presented as an immutable off-chain document. Therefore, as no conflicts with constitutional requirements have been identified, this proposal is deemed constitutional.
