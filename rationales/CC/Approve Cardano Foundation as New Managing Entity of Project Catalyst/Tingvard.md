# Tingvard

**Proposal:** Approve Cardano Foundation as New Managing Entity of Project Catalyst
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `646d1b3ac94568a422b687db6c47acdf849f1674982ae4f9a494be43`

---

This governance action is properly framed as an Info action. Info actions, as defined in Appendix I, Section 8 of the Cardano Constitution, have no direct effect on-chain other than creating an on-chain record. The proposal seeks community approval for the Cardano Foundation (CF) to assume the role of managing entity for Project Catalyst, replacing Input Output Group (IOG), in accordance with the statutes of the Catalyst Foundation Company (CFC). The proposal provides a clear abstract, motivation, and rationale, thereby satisfying the requirements for sufficient rationale under Article II, Section 6, §2. The objective of ensuring continuity of operations, minimizing delays for milestone reviews, and facilitating payouts for existing grant recipients aligns with Article I, Section 1, Tenet 9, which emphasizes fair and impartial treatment of all users, taking into account the collective desires of the Cardano Community, consistent with long-term sustainability and viability. The action also supports Article I, Section 1, Tenet 4 by recognizing and facilitating contributions by the Cardano Community.
