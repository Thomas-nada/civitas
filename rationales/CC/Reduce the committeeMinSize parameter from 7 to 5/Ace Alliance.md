# Ace Alliance

**Proposal:** Reduce the committeeMinSize parameter from 7 to 5
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `71aa5b3a9240a02a89c4e2839579ec5eb60c410af0a5bb483e1b8f04`

---

This Parameter Update action lowers the `committeeMinSize` protocol parameter from 7 to 5. As the proposer (Intersect, on behalf of the Civics Committee and the Technical Steering Committee, with the recommendation of the Parameter Committee) explains, the parameter sets the least number of registered, active Constitutional Committee members required for the governance system to ratify actions that depend on Constitutional Committee approval. The change does not alter the current number of committee seats, nor the maximum committee size, nor the membership of the sitting committee. Its sole effect is to widen the buffer between the minimum required seats and the seats actually filled, so that one or two resignations or term expirations do not render Parameter Changes, Constitution Updates, Treasury Withdrawals, and Hard Fork Initiations ineligible for ratification. We review the action for constitutionality only, not for the policy desirability of a smaller floor, and we apply identical scrutiny regardless of proposer.

**Article II.6.1 and II.6.2 (Governance Action Standards).** The action is anchored to an immutable IPFS document (`ipfs://bafkreidbxerdwxxntmmr6qvjvquyla3qbwhk3flfqjbm3d5lj2ubxfk3aq`, hash `8c7c510c1c959d5c587de268350ce9275af45a05f5774c15fd424b924e18e749`) and supplies a title, abstract, motivation, and rationale, including a comparison of voting arithmetic at committee sizes 7, 6, and 5 and an explicit reversion plan. The standardized-format and sufficient-rationale requirements are met.

**Article II.6.3 (Technical Review of Parameter Updates).** Parameter Update actions must undergo sufficient technical review and scrutiny under the Guardrails to ensure they do not endanger the security, functionality, performance, or long-term sustainability of the Cardano Blockchain. The change was processed through the Parameter Committee's Parameter Change Process and is documented in the public PCP-005 discussion. `committeeMinSize` is a governance parameter that governs ratification eligibility; it has no effect on node operation, block production, or transaction processing, so it presents no security, functionality, or performance risk.

**Appendix I.2 (PARAM-01).** `committeeMinSize` is a parameter explicitly named in the Guardrails, so it is eligible to be changed by a Parameter Update action; PARAM-01's prohibition on changing unlisted parameters is not implicated.

**Appendix I.2.1 (Critical Protocol Parameters: PARAM-05a, PARAM-06a).** `committeeMinSize` is enumerated among the parameters that are critical to the governance system, so the critical-parameter guardrails attach. PARAM-05a requires that DReps vote "yes" with collective support of more than 50% of active voting stake, a requirement enforced through the DRep voting thresholds for governance-parameter actions; the action is subject to and gated by that threshold. PARAM-06a provides that at least 90 days should normally pass between the publication of an off-chain proposal to change a governance-critical parameter and the submission of the corresponding on-chain action. This is a "should" guardrail, relaxable only for a Severity 1 or Severity 2 network issue, and it is satisfied here on its own terms: the off-chain proposal was published in the Cardano Forum on 2025-11-11, well over 90 days before on-chain submission, so no reliance on the relaxation clause is needed.

**Appendix I.2.5 (Governance Parameters: committeeMinSize, CMS-01 to CMS-03).** The proposed value of 5 satisfies every checkable guardrail for this parameter: it is not negative (CMS-01), it is not lower than 3 (CMS-02), and it does not exceed 10 (CMS-03). The change also sits squarely within the stated goals for managing governance parameters, namely ensuring governance stability and maintaining a representative form of governance: introducing an operational buffer directly serves stability by reducing the risk that a single departure halts ratification.

**Appendix I.2.6 (Monitoring and Reversion of Parameter Changes).** A specific reversion plan is required for each parameter change. The proposal provides one: `committeeMinSize` can be returned to 7, with the only practical impact arising if fewer than 7 members are seated at the time of reversion. This satisfies the reversion-plan requirement for a non-network parameter.

**Article III.2 (Composition and Terms).** The number of committee members and their term lengths are to be determined from time to time by Ada owners, with staggered terms to assure continuity. This action does not displace that determination: it sets a floor, not a target, and the proposer affirms that the elections process is intended to maintain a 7-seat committee. By preserving the system's ability to operate through brief periods of reduced membership, the lower floor is consistent with, rather than contrary to, the continuity that Article III.2 seeks to assure.

That sequencing is a coordination matter and raises no independent constitutional concern.
