"State of the Cardano Developer Ecosystem - 2026 Survey" (gov_action1wpjynage9uu4rg7rev2g2g2u0spvj5g3fe6lqq286m3dl5tdv47sqhysy4u) is an Info Governance Action submitted by the Cardano Foundation that invites developers building on Cardano to complete the fifth annual developer ecosystem survey, and invites other governance participants to share it with developers they know. The survey is offered in nine languages, requires no wallet or ada, and records its questions and answers on chain under the CIP-179 survey format, with the code that runs it and builds its report published as open source. The proposer states plainly that the action "doesn't change anything or ask for a decision," requests no withdrawal from the Cardano Treasury, and contemplates no later governance action. Under Article III.1.4, the Constitutional Committee is "limited to voting on the constitutionality of governance actions, including any proposed or contemplated actions contained within 'Info' actions." Our review therefore concerns whether this action meets the standards the Constitution sets for every governance action, not whether a governance action is the best channel for promoting a survey, which is committed to the Cardano Community and its DReps to decide, not to the Constitutional Committee.

Under Article II.6.1, governance actions "shall follow a standardized and legible format before being recorded or enacted on-chain," including a URL and hash of a context document that is "immutable and incapable of being altered after submission." The action anchors its metadata at a content-addressed IPFS URL, and the blake2b-256 hash of the document retrieved at that URL matches the hash recorded on chain. The document follows the CIP-100 and CIP-108 structure and names the Cardano Foundation as its author. It carries two fields beyond the standard ones, one linking the action to the on-chain survey record and one restating its deposit, return account, and action type, and neither impairs the legibility of the document. The document supplies the title, abstract, motivation, rationale, and references that Article II.6.2 requires, and its rationale explains why the proposer chose an Info Action to reach developers outside the usual developer channels.

Under Appendix I.8, "'Info' actions are not enacted on-chain. No guardrails are imposed on 'Info' actions." Article II.2.1 entitles ada owners to "access and participate in the on-chain decision-making processes of the Cardano Blockchain ecosystem," including "otherwise taking part in on-chain governance actions," and an Info Action that informs the Community and requests its engagement is a valid exercise of that right. Because this action contains no proposed or contemplated action, no further provision of the Constitution is engaged.
