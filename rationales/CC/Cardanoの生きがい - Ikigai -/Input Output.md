## 1. Summary

IOG votes “abstain” on this Info Action because expressions of feeling are not a matter for constitutional adjudication.

## 2. Rationale

The Governance Action under consideration is an expression of feeling. However, while ICC members may have individual positions regarding expressions of feeling submitted as Info Actions, such expressions do not implicate decision-making about other Governance Actions and there are no guardrails on Info Actions in the constitution (Appendix I, Section 8). For these reasons, the Governance Action under consideration is not a matter for constitutional adjudication.

Thus, based on the above, IOG abstains.

## 3. Further Considerations: Counterarguments

It might be argued that some Info Actions which do not implicate decision making about other Governance Actions should be considered matters for constitutional adjudication.

## 4. Further Considerations: Precedent

Info Actions are a mechanism for recording a vote on any information, with no binding on-chain effects. For this reason, there are no guardrails on Info Actions in the Interim Cardano Constitution (Appendix I, Section 8), as mentioned above.

Because Info Actions have no binding effect on-chain, many Info Actions may not be a matter of constitutional adjudication. To avoid setting precedents outside the proper context of those governance actions that have on-chain effects and are governed by the guardrails of the Interim Constitution, IOG will not vote on Info Actions that are not matters of constitutional adjudication going forward.

However, we want to reiterate the importance of the Info Action for developing consensus prior to constitutional adjudication and we encourage DReps and SPOs to make use of this tool.

We recognize that constitutional interpretation and decision-making for Cardano is a new field and conventions are still being established. IOG is committed to developing the practice of constitutional interpretation in partnership with the other ICC members during our tenure. We welcome any feedback from the wider community as we continue to evolve Cardano governance together.
