The governance action titled "2025 Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Budget - 7.5M ₳ for community builders" is unconstitutional due to failing to meet the criteria of Article IV, Section 2 which reads:

> Cardano Blockchain budgets shall specify a process for overseeing use of funds from Cardano Blockchain treasury withdrawals including designating one or more administrators who shall be responsible for such oversight.

Given that this proposal, as proposed, does not provide any *process for overseeing the use of funds from Cardano Blockchain treasury withdrawals*; we must find it to be unconstitutional.
