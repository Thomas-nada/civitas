Article II, Section 6(1) of the Cardano Constitution states "To ensure transparency in on-chain governance, proposed governance actions shall follow a standardized and legible format before being recorded or enacted on-chain. This format shall include a URL hosting a document that outlines additional context for the proposed governance action, and the hash of this document. The document hosted by such a URL shall be immutable and incapable of being altered after submission, and the content of every on-chain Governance Action must be identical to the final off-chain version of the proposed action.”

The governance action with ID "gov_action1znt2fr0chyye50nj0cdld2dvdjg3xsdfvq0fr37n43uehlvfkqdsqjnuq3n" and title “AURORA: Open infrastructure for bringing real credit markets to Cardano.” included a metadata document URL of https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fairway-global/aurora-proposal/refs/heads/main/data.jsonld, which is not immutable.

We therefore find this governance action **Unconstitutional**.
