This Info Governance Action (\`gov\_action1n5sn54mgf47a7men2ryq6ppx88kta4wvenz2qkl4f9v6ppje8easqxwm88m\`) proposes a budget of 1,150,000 ada to fund the maintenance and development of the GovTool platform (https://gov.tools/) for a period of 12 months.

**Constitutional Alignment**

This proposal aligns with the constitutional framework:

1. **Article IV, Section 1 (Budget Purpose & Period):** This section states that one or more budgets may be proposed for the "ongoing operation, maintenance and future development of the Cardano Blockchain ecosystem," including costs related to maintaining "decentralized, on-chain governance processes." It also expects budgets to cover a defined time period. The proposal's purpose, to fund governance tooling, supports the maintenance of governance processes. It specifies a clear duration of 73 epochs (12 months). The proposal's purpose and its defined period are compliant with the requirements of Article IV, Section 1\.  
2. **Article III, Section 5 (Procedural Requirements):** This section mandates that all governance actions follow a standardized format and include a verifiable "URL and hash of all documented off-chain content" along with "Sufficient rationale... and relevant supporting materials." The proposal provides a detailed rationale linked via an immutable IPFS address with a verified hash. It includes sections for Abstract, Motivation, Rationale, and supporting links to further documentation. The proposal meets the requirements of Article III, Section 5, providing a verifiable anchor to its main off-chain document.  
3. **Article IV, Section 2 (Administrator & Oversight):** This section requires that budgets "specify a process for overseeing use of funds... including designating one or more administrators." The proposal designates "Intersect" as the administrator and outlines a process where funds are distributed via smart contracts to the various builders in the GovTool consortium, with direction from the Governance Tools Working Group. The proposal specifies an administrator and a process for fund management, thereby meeting the basic requirements of Article IV, Section 2\.   
4. **Article IV, Section 4 & 5 (Audit & Fund Handling):** These sections mandate that funding requests make provisions for independent audits (Sec 4), dispute resolution (Sec 4), and that ada held by a budget administrator be delegated to the auto-abstain option (Sec 5). The proposal states an intent to use an external commitment from Intersect for audits. It is silent on dispute resolution and the auto-abstain delegation rule.  The requirements of Art. IV(4) and (5) do not specify whether these requirements apply at the budget info actions stage in the process or at the treasury withdrawal stage, and as such this budget info action does not contravene these provisions by containing little information in relation thereto. However, it is better practice to cover these points sufficiently at an early stage in budget Info Actions.
