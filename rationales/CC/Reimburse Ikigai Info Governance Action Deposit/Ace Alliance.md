"Reimburse Ikigai Info Governance Action Deposit." (gov_action105mjyzm3spjppny2m776lwk5jnsuu07uva9tz0yg5u4nkf770rvsql5raht) is a Treasury Withdrawal Governance Action requesting 103,000 ada to reimburse the 100,000 ada deposit posted by the submitter of the Info Governance Action "Cardanoの生きがい - Ikigai -" in September 2024, which the protocol did not return when that action expired because the stake credential named as the deposit return address had not been registered. The additional 3,000 ada is offered as compensation for staking rewards forgone since, at a stated rate of 2 percent per annum. The on-chain withdrawal destination is stake1uys93fhep4lc2u6lu0q09kcxayxzthasded35c0x0w60ugc9s0cm5, which the chain record confirms is the same reward address the original Ikigai action named as its deposit return address. This action is a resubmission of a materially identical Treasury Withdrawal that this Committee previously considered and found constitutional, and which expired at the close of epoch 643 without being ratified. The anchored metadata, its hash, the requested amount, and the withdrawal destination are unchanged from that submission, and nothing arising in the intervening period bears on the constitutional analysis, which carries forward in full.

Under Article II.6.1, governance actions "shall follow a standardized and legible format before being recorded or enacted on-chain", supported by a document that is "immutable and incapable of being altered after submission". The proposal anchors to a content addressed IPFS document at https://most-brass-sun.quicknode-ipfs.com/ipfs/QmSy2CiQhPBzzNLKDFVvMQaDb1FQDbmn9bJf6fVntVAQ1t whose blake2b-256 hash, abaf67cde8defc914712009094cd3805bcf8911d3fa79c6edfd6c975dcf9d471, matches the on-chain record, and the document supplies the title, abstract, justification, and supporting materials that Article II.6.2 requires.

Under Article II.7.1, a Treasury Withdrawal "must specify the terms of the withdrawal", including the purpose, the period for delivery, the relevant costs and expenses, and the circumstances under which the withdrawal might be refunded to the Cardano Treasury. The proposal specifies each of them: the purpose is reimbursement of the lost deposit, delivery is immediate and occurs as part of enactment, there are no associated costs or expenses, and there are no circumstances for a refund. That the withdrawal funds no program of activities does not leave Article II.7.1 unsatisfied. The provision requires that the terms be specified, and where the withdrawal is a single payment completed on enactment, the amount, the recipient, and the moment of transfer state those terms exhaustively. The disclosure requirement of Article II.7.2 is met by the express statement that the recipient has not previously received ada from the Cardano Treasury. The Net Change Limit required by Article II.7.3 has been set at 500,000,000 ada for the period spanning epochs 613 through 713, agreed by the DReps at the greater than 50 percent active voting stake threshold that guardrail TREASURY-01a requires, and the 103,000 ada requested here sits well inside the headroom remaining under that limit, on its own and in aggregate with the other withdrawals pending at the time of this review, satisfying guardrail TREASURY-02a. The request is denominated in ada, satisfying guardrail TREASURY-03a.

The audit allocation required by Article II.7.4, the administrator designation required by Article II.7.5, and the custody obligations of Article II.7.6 call for a different analysis given the self-executing character of this withdrawal. Article II.7.4 requires "an allocation of ada as a part of such funding request to cover the cost of periodic independent audits and the implementation of oversight metrics as to the use of such ada", and Article II.7.5 requires the designation of "one or more administrators responsible for monitoring how the funds are used, and ensuring the deliverables are achieved". This withdrawal is a single, automated, unconditional payment, completed in one transaction at enactment, to the address the recipient designated in 2024. No party holds the funds beforehand, no discretion is exercised afterwards, and there is no subsequent use of the funds to monitor. Article II.7.4 is satisfied because no ada need be set aside for periodic audits or for oversight metrics where the entirety of the disbursement is a single transaction that any ada holder can verify unilaterally on the public record. Article II.7.5 is satisfied because the protocol performs the monitoring and deliverable assurance function that the provision calls for: the deliverable is the payment itself, and the protocol both effects it and evidences it. The proposal names the DReps as Administrator. We do not adopt that designation, for the reasons set out in the Precedent Discussion below, and find instead that the protocol itself satisfies Article II.7.5 on these facts. Article II.7.6 attaches to ada "being held by an administrator prior to further disbursement to the Treasury Withdrawal Recipient", and no such holding occurs here, the funds moving directly from the Cardano Treasury to the recipient. We note for completeness that the destination is a key hash stake address, registered on chain and not delegated to any stake pool.

We emphasize that the treatment of Article II.7.4 through Article II.7.6 above is a very narrow exception, not a relaxation of the accountability architecture this Committee requires of Treasury Withdrawals generally. In the ordinary case a withdrawal funds a program of work across a delivery period, and the audit allocation, administrator, and custody provisions do essential work that no automated transfer can substitute for. This proposal is not that case. It restores a deposit the protocol did not return, in one transaction whose destination and amount are fixed by the action itself, and interposing an administrator, an audit engagement, or an oversight body would add cost without adding any constitutional protection.
