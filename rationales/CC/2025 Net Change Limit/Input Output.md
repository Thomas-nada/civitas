The Cardano constitution requires a Net Change Limit to be in place prior to the approval of a Cardano Blockchain ecosystem budget (Article IV, Section 3; TREASURY-01a), making the Net Change Limit a critical dependency of later budget and treasury actions which the Constitutional Committee is required to vote on (Article VII, Section 4). 

Only two significant constitutional constraints are relevant to consider. 

First, Info Actions (like all governance actions) are “expected to follow a standardized and legible format including a URL and hash of all documented off-chain content to the Cardano Blockchain” (Article III, Section 5). The “2025 Net Change Limit” Info Action meets this standard. 

Second, all governance actions should be evaluated in accordance with the Constitution's  Tenets. 

Section 4 of Article VII of the Constitution states:

Because "Info" actions have no on-chain effect and, accordingly, are neither constitutional nor unconstitutional, Constitutional Committee members may not prevent "Info" actions from being recorded on-chain. Members of the Constitutional Committee may nevertheless record a vote on-chain regarding an "Info" action in order to express their view on such "Info" action, including whether the suggested course of action proposed in such "Info" action, would be, in the view of such member, unconstitutional if it were to be enforced by on-chain mechanisms.

Accordingly, we are exercising our right under Article VII, Section 4, and it is our view that the “2025 Net Change Limit” Info Action would not violate constitutional principles if it were to be enforced by on-chain mechanisms, and, in setting a Net Change Limit, this Info Action seeks to satisfy a constitutional prerequisite for the passing of a budget. 

There are no other significant constitutional constraints on a Net Change Limit Info Action.
