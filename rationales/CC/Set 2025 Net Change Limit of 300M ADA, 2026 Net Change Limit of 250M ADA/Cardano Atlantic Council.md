The published rationale for this governance action states (emphasis ours):

> In accordance with the Constitution, this **action shall be considered ratified with a threshold of greater than 67%** of the delegated ada to DReps voting in favor.

the threshold for ratification of a Net Change Limit (NCL) governance action is defined in **Appendix I, Section 3, TREASURY-O1a** as follows:

> TREASURY-01a (x) A net change limit for the Cardano treasury's balance per period of time **must** be agreed by the DReps via an on-chain governance action with a threshold of greater than 50% of the active voting stake

The use of the language "**shall be considered ratified**" indicates an intent by the author(s) of this action to subvert the limit specified by the Constitutional Guardrails and thus makes this action *Unconstitutional*.
