# Cardano Japan Council

**Proposal:** 2025 Net Change Limit Extension
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `725d4d44499865071536d54674b080322bd366be15338db221807b31`

---

This governance action extends the term of the existing Net Change Limit (NCL) without modifying its total amount. As such, it remains fully compliant with the Treasury Guardrails defined in Appendix I, Section 3 — particularly TREASURY-01a(x) and TREASURY-02a(x) — which govern the application and continuity of a previously established NCL. The extension ensures continued treasury operations beyond the original expiration date and preserves the spending ceiling agreed upon by the Cardano Community. Therefore, we deem this governance action to be constitutional.
