# Cardano Curia

**Proposal:** 2025 Net Change Limit Extension
**Vote:** Yes
**Voter ID:** `84feba943c574d25984175cf8257959e6b3a1c64143d85e64fef6bd5`

---

This governance action extends the duration of the existing 2025 Net Change Limit without changing its approved amount. Under the Cardano Constitution, a valid Net Change Limit must be in effect for any treasury withdrawal to be constitutionally enacted. Extending the NCL ensures this required constraint remains continuously applicable and avoids a governance gap. The proposal preserves the originally approved spending ceiling and modifies only the period during which it applies, which is permitted under the Constitution.
