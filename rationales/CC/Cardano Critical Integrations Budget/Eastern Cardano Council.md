Article IV, Section 2 of the Cardano Constitution states "Cardano Blockchain budgets shall specify a process for overseeing use of funds from Cardano Blockchain treasury withdrawals including designating one or more administrators who shall be responsible for such oversight."

The governance action with ID "gov_action13a2dqgwxum7d6kjfprcs57cf9733ek2dkt5qnuhqd4ll5ntcwu7sqluwkxd" and title "Cardano Critical Integrations Budget", as a budget Info governance action, designates Intersect as the Administrator and specifies a "Steering Committee" as the process for overseeing the use of funds from the Cardano Blockchain treasury withdrawals."

We therefore find this governance action **Constitutional**.
