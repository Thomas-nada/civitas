This proposal satisfies the formal requirements for governance actions set forth in Article II, Section 6 (Governance Action Standards) of the Cardano Constitution, and is presented via an immutable off-chain document (IPFS). Furthermore, the proposal is submitted under Article II, Section 7 (Treasury Withdrawal Action Standards) and specifies the purpose of the funds, the implementation period, the breakdown of costs, and the conditions for returning unused funds. However, the proposal includes a contingency allocation to account for potential price fluctuations. Appropriate transparency in its implementation and reporting will therefore be important.
