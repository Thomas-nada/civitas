# Tingvard

**Proposal:** Reforming Treasury Governance
**Vote:** Abstain
**Voter ID:** `646d1b3ac94568a422b687db6c47acdf849f1674982ae4f9a494be43`

---

This governance action is an Info Action. It does not directly change protocol parameters, withdraw treasury funds, update the Constitution, initiate a hard fork, or otherwise create an immediate on-chain effect.

The proposal presents ideas for reforming Cardano treasury governance, including a more strategic budget process, dedicated budget structures, a strategic entity, an expert commission, and a staged process for Net Change Limit, budget, and treasury withdrawal decisions.

Tingvard notes that these are policy and governance-design proposals for community discussion. Any implementation would require further concrete governance actions, and possibly constitutional amendments, to be assessed on their own terms.

Because this Info Action has no direct on-chain effect, Tingvard does not judge it constitutional or unconstitutional as an operative governance action.
