Article III, Section 5 of the Cardano Blockchain Ecosystem Constitution mandates that: "prior to being recorded or enacted on-chain, all proposed governance actions are expected to follow a standardized and legible format including a URL and hash of all documented off-chain content to the Cardano Blockchain... The content of every on-chain governance action must be identical to the final off-chain version of the proposed action."

This provision is fundamental to the transparency, integrity, and verifiability of the on-chain governance process. It ensures that all participants in the Cardano community, including Delegated Representatives (DReps), Stake Pool Operators (SPOs), and the Constitutional Committee, can definitively ascertain and agree upon the exact off-chain details of the governance action under consideration.
