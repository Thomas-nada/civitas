The proposal titled "Cardano Treasury DeFi Liquidity Budget" is Unconstitutional due to the hash of the off-chain content not matching the on-chain version.

### Hash Mismatch

Article III, Section 5 of the Cardano Constitution states:

> … prior to being recorded or enacted on-chain, all proposed governance actions are expected to follow a standardized and legible format including a URL and hash of all documented off-chain content to the Cardano Blockchain.
>
> …
>
> The content of every on-chain governance action must be identical to the final off-chain version of the proposed action.

This requirement ensures transparency, prevents tampering, and maintains trust in the governnce process. Although Cardano Atlantic Council does recognize the error may not have been made by the proposal submitter and that inadequate tooling affected the process, we must declare this proposal as Unconstitutional and maintain precedent.

### Altering Thresholds

Following our own precedent (gov_action10ueqgzwenxr39le68n0se9peu92r7gm2846xwehh3u0ahc0qd0uqqyljxu5) in dissallowing individual proposers to adjust or modify ratification thresholds within the context of their actions, we find the following:

Article IV, Section 1 of the Cardano Constitution states:

> Existing budgets may be amended following the same process as provided in this Section 1.

Furthermore, with relation to budget actions, Appendix I, Section 3, TREASURY-04a states:

> Withdrawals from the Cardano Blockchain treasury **must not** be ratified until there is a Cardano Community approved Cardano Blockchain ecosystem budget then in effect pursuant to a previous on-chain governance action agreed by the DReps with a threshold of greater than 50% of the active voting stake

The language of this proposal suggests that it may be edited by submission of a subsequent governance action but specifies 67% thresholds at multiple points. This directly contradicts the two articles cited above and makes this proposal Unconstitutional.
