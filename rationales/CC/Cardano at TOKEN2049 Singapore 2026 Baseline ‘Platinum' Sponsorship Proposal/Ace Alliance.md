This Treasury Withdrawals Governance Action (TWGA) requests 3,303,750 ada (approximately USD 792,900 at an assumed rate of USD 0.24 per ada) to fund a ‘Platinum' tier sponsorship at TOKEN2049 Singapore on October 7 to 8, 2026. Our review concerns the constitutionality of the action as posted, not the merits of the underlying sponsorship strategy or the negotiated commercial terms.
