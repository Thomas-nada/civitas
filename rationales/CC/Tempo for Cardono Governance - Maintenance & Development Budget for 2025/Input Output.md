While this Budget Info Action is consistent with provisions of the Cardano Constitution for blockchain ecosystem budgets, questions may remain regarding its treatment of periodic audits, oversight metrics, delegation status, and the net change limit. We address these below.

Article IV, Section Four requires Budget Info Actions to provide for audits and oversight metrics.

“Any governance action requesting ada from the Cardano Blockchain treasury shall require an allocation of ada as a part of such funding request to cover the cost of periodic independent audits and the implementation of oversight metrics as to the use of such ada.”

Additionally, Article IV, Section Four requires provisions for dispute resolution in the governance of ada administered in such a budget.

“Contractual obligations governing the use of ada received from the Cardano Blockchain treasury pursuant to a Cardano Blockchain ecosystem budget shall include dispute resolution provisions.”

While neither of these provisions is accounted for directly in the Info Action rationale, the rationale does specify an administrator for its budget– Selfdriven – whose services include periodic audits and the implementation of oversight metrics.
